Recreation Financial Assistance (Brantford)

The City pays part of drop-in and registered program fees for children and youth 17 and under. The share depends on family size and gross income. Ontario Works, ODSP and refugee-status families get 100% with documents.

What it is

The City pays a share of fees, based on family size and income.

  • The City pays 75% if gross income is $0 to $22,000 for a family of 1 or 2.
  • The share drops to 50% and then 25% at higher income bands in the chart.
  • Bands run up to $51,900 for a family of 6 or more.
  • It covers most drop-in and registered programs, if funds and programs are available.

Good to know

Help is limited to two programs per person per season and two weeks of summer camp. Funds and programs must be available. Some restrictions apply. It is free to apply.

What you must meet

The program requires at least one of these:

  • Your family's gross annual income falls within the City's chart for your family size
  • You are on Ontario Works or ODSP, or your family has refugee status

The program is for children and youth 17 and under.

Amount or value

25%, 50% or 75% of program fees paid, or 100% on assistance

How to apply

  1. Check the financial assistance chart for your family size
  2. Fill out the online application, or apply at the front reception desk
  3. Pay your share of the fee when you register

What you may need

  • On Ontario Works or ODSP: a current pay stub and Child Tax Benefit showing the children's names
  • Others: an income tax form or Notice of Assessment, and a current pay stub
  • Or a pay stub and Child Tax Benefit, or an Employment Insurance stub and Child Tax Benefit

Practical tips

  • Assistance also depends on whether your family already got help this registration period.
  • The Arnold Anderson Sport Fund is separate. It gives youth up to $350, and the City does not approve it.
