Manitoba Pharmacare

Help with prescription costs for Manitobans whose income is seriously affected by high drug costs. Your deductible depends on your family income.

Manitoba is new here: please double-check before you rely on a detail

We added Manitoba in October 2026, while some of its programs are changing. Each program here comes from its official page, but some details are not published yet, so confirm with the program before you count on an amount or rule.

  • Manitoba is moving people with a disability from Employment and Income Assistance (EIA) to the new Manitoba Supports for Persons with Disabilities program, and that program's page does not publish payment amounts yet.
  • Since September 1, 2026, EIA covers only emergency dental care and points people to the federal Canadian Dental Care Plan.
  • Some income limits are old (home adaptation limits are from 2019) or change every year (Rent Assist and Winnipeg programs).
  • Winnipeg Transit Plus has proposed new eligibility rules that are not in effect.
  • City programs are listed for Winnipeg only so far.

What it is

Pharmacare sets a yearly deductible from your adjusted family income.

  • After you pay the deductible on eligible drugs, Pharmacare pays 100 per cent of eligible costs.
  • The deductible uses Canada Revenue Agency income from two years ago.
  • The deductible can be changed if family income dropped by more than 10 per cent.
  • You can pay the deductible in monthly instalments on your Manitoba Hydro bill.

Good to know

The benefit year runs April 1 to March 31. Not all drugs are eligible. Ask a pharmacist or use the Drug Formulary lookup.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You are eligible for Manitoba Health coverage.
  • Your prescriptions are not covered by other provincial or federal programs.

The program is for eligible Manitobans regardless of disease or age.

Amount or value

Yearly deductible from $100 (no maximum); then 100% of eligible drug costs

How to apply

  1. Fill out and sign the Pharmacare Application and Consent Authorization Form.
  2. Send it to Manitoba Health.
  3. Wait for a letter that gives your deductible amount.

What you may need

  • Pharmacare Application and Consent Authorization Form
  • Newcomers and new 18-year-olds: a signed statement of 2024 income

Practical tips

  • You can apply any time from April 1 to March 31.
  • You do not need to send receipts. The pharmacy system tracks your deductible.
  • Pharmacies may charge fees above what Pharmacare accepts, so ask your pharmacist.
