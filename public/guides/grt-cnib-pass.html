CNIB pass (Grand River Transit)

An annual pass for CNIB customers for unlimited use of conventional GRT buses and ION light rail. CNIB customers can ride free with a special CNIB EasyGO fare card.

What it is

CNIB customers can ride conventional GRT buses and ION light rail free with a special EasyGO fare card.

  • The pass is annual and costs nothing.
  • It provides unlimited use of conventional GRT buses and ION light rail.
  • The card has Braille and a small notch in the side to make it easier to identify by touch.
  • The source does not say MobilityPLUS is included.

Good to know

The source names conventional GRT buses and ION light rail. It does not say the pass includes MobilityPLUS.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You are a CNIB customer

The CNIB pass is listed at no cost.

Amount or value

Free unlimited travel on conventional GRT buses and ION light rail
