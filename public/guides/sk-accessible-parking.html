Accessible Parking Program (SaskAbilities)

A parking permit for people who cannot walk more than 50 metres unassisted without great difficulty or danger. It lets you use accessible parking spaces in any vehicle you ride in.

What it is

The permit lets you park in accessible spaces.

  • The permit is used in any vehicle carrying the permit holder.
  • It can only be used when the holder enters or exits the vehicle.
  • Other provinces and territories will recognize the permit, but you must follow local rules there.
  • Each person gets one permit.

Good to know

SaskAbilities runs the program. A health professional must confirm the medical details on the first application.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You cannot walk unassisted more than 50 metres without great difficulty or danger to your health and safety.

Legally blind people are also eligible, and no driver's licence is needed if you meet the medical criteria.

Amount or value

$10 fee each time a permit is issued

How to apply

  1. Fill in Section 1 in the name of the person with the disability.
  2. Ask a chiropractor, nurse practitioner, occupational therapist, physical therapist or physician to fill in Section 2.
  3. Mail it to the Saskatoon office, or bring it to a SaskAbilities location, with the $10 fee.

What you may need

  • Application form with your signature and your health professional's original signature
  • $10 permit fee

Practical tips

  • Emailed and faxed forms are not accepted.
  • The health professional may charge their own fee to complete the form.
  • Permanent permit holders can renew by filling in Section 1 only.
  • If you are denied, you can appeal in writing within 30 days.
