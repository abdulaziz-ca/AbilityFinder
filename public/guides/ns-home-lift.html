Home Lift Program

The program loans a floor or free-standing lift and a sling. The lift helps home care workers move you safely so you can stay in your own home.

What it is

A lift helps care happen safely at home.

  • It lifts, transfers and positions a person who cannot do so alone.
  • The Canadian Red Cross delivers the lift at a set date and time.
  • Your Care Coordinator or therapist requests the lift for you.

Good to know

There is no fee for the program. Home care may have a fee based on income, family size and care needed.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You live in Nova Scotia with a valid Health Card, or you have applied for coverage.
  • You get home care from an agency funded by the Department.
  • An occupational therapist or physiotherapist says you need a lift regularly.

Neither private insurance nor other public coverage rules you out.

What this guide can't tell you: whether you will be approved. The program decides, often using details we do not ask for.

How to apply

  1. You or your caregivers contact your Care Coordinator.
  2. An occupational therapist or physiotherapist completes an assessment.
  3. They send the assessment to the Canadian Red Cross.
  4. The Red Cross tells you the delivery date and time.

Practical tips

  • You can also contact the Canadian Red Cross at nsbedloans@redcross.ca.
  • A Care Coordinator can discuss your own situation.
