Access Pass (Penticton)

The Access Pass gives 25% off passes to the fitness room, pool or both. It is for people with a permanent cognitive or physical disability.

What it is

Access Pass holders get a lower price on recreation passes.

  • The discount is 25% off 10-ticket passes.
  • It works for the fitness room, the pool, or the combined pass.

Good to know

The discount also applies to promotional deals. It covers 10-ticket, 1, 3, 6 and 12 month passes.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You have a permanent cognitive or physical disability

Amount or value

25% off fitness room and pool passes
