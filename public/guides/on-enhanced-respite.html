Enhanced Respite for medically fragile children

Pays families back for respite care for a child under 18 who is medically fragile or technology dependent and needs care 24 hours a day. You claim the cost of covered respite services or supports.

What it is

Enhanced Respite gives families a break from round-the-clock care of a medically fragile child.

  • Gives back the cost of covered respite when you make a claim.
  • Respite can be in your home or outside your home, from local agencies.
  • Funding is for families caring for a child who depends on medical or technological equipment.
  • Extra funding was added on July 1, 2022 to support personal support worker wages.

Good to know

The services and supports covered are the same as those covered by the Special Services at Home program.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • Your child is under 18 years of age.
  • Your child lives at home.
  • Your child needs intensive care and constant monitoring on a 24-hour basis.
  • Your child is medically fragile or technology dependent, in one of the listed ways.

Listed ways include a ventilator, dialysis, IV drugs, tracheotomy care, suctioning, oxygen or tube feeding.

What this guide can't tell you: whether you will be approved. The program decides, often using details we do not ask for.

Amount or value

Up to $4,213 per child each fiscal year

How to apply

  1. Contact the ministry regional office nearest to you to apply.
  2. Arrange respite care with a local agency in or outside your home.
  3. Make a claim to get the cost of covered respite back.

Practical tips

  • Ask the regional office which respite supports are covered before you pay.
  • The same page also covers Special Services at Home, and Assistance for Children with Severe Disabilities.
