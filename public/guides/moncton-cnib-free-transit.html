Moncton · Transit

Free Codiac Transpo rides for CNIB cardholders

CNIB cardholders ride all Codiac Transpo buses for free. Show your valid CNIB card when you board.

What it is

This program gives you:

  • Free travel on all Codiac Transpo buses.
  • A free attendant pass for a companion who assists you.

Good to know

Companions who need to travel with someone with a disability can get a free attendant pass.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You hold a CNIB card.

Your CNIB card must be valid when you board.

Amount or value

Free rides on all Codiac Transpo buses

How to apply

  1. Get a CNIB card if you do not have one.
  2. Show your valid CNIB card to the driver when you board.
  3. Ask for an attendant pass at 140 Millenium Blvd if you travel with an assistant.

What you may need
