Saskatchewan · Health (for children)

Autism Spectrum Disorder Individualized Funding (ASD-IF)

Gives parents money each year to buy therapy and family supports for a child with an autism diagnosis. Up to $8,000 under age six, and up to $6,000 for ages six to 11.

What it is

ASD-IF pays for services that suit your child, chosen by you.

  • You pick from approved therapies and family supports.
  • Funding is up to $8,000 a year under age six.
  • Funding is up to $6,000 a year for ages six to 11.
  • A registry lists autism service providers near you.

Good to know

You choose from a list of approved services and expenses, then send in proof of what you spent.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • Your child has a diagnosis of Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD).
  • Your child is under age 12.
  • You can show proof of Saskatchewan residency.

Only one parent can apply; out-of-province diagnoses need Saskatchewan Health Authority review.

Amount or value

Up to $8,000 a year under age 6; up to $6,000 a year ages 6 to 11

How to apply

  1. If you think your child may have ASD, contact a local autism program to arrange an assessment.
  2. Gather your documents and apply online.
  3. Once approved, send proof of expenses with the Submit an Expense Online form.
  4. Renew each year, after sending all proof of expenses for the previous year.

What you may need

  • Social Insurance Numbers
  • Your child's Health Services Number
  • Proof of Saskatchewan residency (SGI photo ID, SaskPower, SaskEnergy or a utility bill from the last three months)
  • ASD Diagnostic Form
  • Direct Deposit Payment Request Form

Practical tips

  • Check the List of Eligible Services and Expenses before you spend.
  • Tell the Ministry of Social Services right away if your address or bank details change.
  • Private providers set their own fees, so ask them for rates.
