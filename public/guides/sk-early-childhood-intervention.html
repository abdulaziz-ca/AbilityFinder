Saskatchewan · Health (for children)

Early Childhood Intervention Program (ECIP)

Home-based specialized services for families of children with a developmental delay or disability, or at risk of one. No diagnosis is needed to start.

What it is

Services include:

  • Child assessment and developmental strategies.
  • Information and case co-ordination.
  • Transition support and referrals.

Good to know

ECIP is delivered in 14 regions by 12 community organizations and two school divisions.

What you must meet

The program requires at least one of these:

  • Your child has a developmental delay or disability.
  • Your child may be at risk of a developmental delay.

Delays may be cognitive, physical, social, emotional or in communication.

What this guide can't tell you: whether you will be approved. The program decides, often using details we do not ask for.

How to apply

  1. Find your region on the official page.
  2. Call your regional ECIP to refer your child.
  3. A health care professional or educator can refer your child, with your consent.

What you may need

  • No diagnosis is needed to start

Practical tips

  • You can start as soon as you notice a delay, even from birth.
  • The program gives you activities to do at home and connects you to services as your child moves into school or other programming.
