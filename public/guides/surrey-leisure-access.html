Surrey · Recreation

Leisure Access Program (LAP)

Surrey's recreation subsidy. Children, youth and seniors get a free year-long pass to every City recreation facility. Adults get three quarters off.

What it is

Surrey accepts several qualifying routes. Income-tax and Disability Tax Credit applicants must meet the family-income test and the line 12100, 12600, 20800 and 25400 limits on the City's page.

Good to know

Enrolment is valid for one year. If you apply with income tax documents, your family's combined net income must be less than the maximum for your family size in the table. This is line 23600 of your Notice of Assessment or Proof of Income Statement. A family is one or two married or common-law adults and their legal dependents aged 18 and under. In the year you are enrolled, course discounts have limits. For ages 0-18, the limit is up to $500 plus $500 at Semiahmoo Arts Society. For adults 19-59 and seniors 60+, it is up to $250 plus $250. Applicable day camps for children (0 to 12) and youth (13 to 18) get 75% off with no discount limits. A few specialty programs are not included.

What you must meet

The program requires at least one of these:

  • Income tax documents: your family's combined net income (line 23600) is less than the maximum for your family size in the table, and the City's other income-line limits are met
  • Client of the Ministry of Social Development
  • Disability Tax Credit applicants must meet the City's family-income and other income-line limits.
  • Senior receiving the Guaranteed Income Supplement
  • Child or youth in qualifying legal care, or a young adult supported by the Ministry's SAJE program.
  • Refugee, with a referral application completed through an approved settlement services agency

You must be a Surrey resident. The City says you may qualify if one of these situations applies. For the Disability Tax Credit route, your family's combined net income, after subtracting the Disability Tax Credit amounts the City's page lists, must be less than the maximum for your family size, and the City's other income-line limits must be met. The legal-care route covers a child or youth aged 0-18 in the legal care of the Ministry of Children and Family Development, Métis Family Services or Vancouver Aboriginal Child and Family Services Society, or a young adult aged 19-25 supported by the Ministry's SAJE program; enrolment covers the child or youth and one legal guardian.

How to apply

  1. Review the City's qualifying routes and select the one that applies
  2. Complete the Leisure Access Program application and copy the documents required for that route
  3. Submit online and wait for staff to schedule an appointment, or submit in person at a listed Surrey facility
  4. After approval, use the pass through the MySurrey account or call registration staff

What you may need

  • Application form
  • Government-issued photo identification for one adult
  • Proof of Income Statement for each adult in the family, when applying with income tax documents
  • Program confirmation, DTC information or referral documents for another qualifying route
  • Canada Child Benefit Notice, Goods and Services Tax Credit Notice or Canada Groceries and Essentials Benefit Notice (income-tax and DTC routes; MSD and GIS routes only if children aged 0-18 or a spouse in the family are not listed on the letter)

Practical tips

  • Translation and interpretation support are available free
  • As of September 1, 2026, income tax documents must be for the 2025 or 2026 tax year.
  • Ask whether a specialty program is excluded before you register

Timing

Online applicants are contacted for appointments on a first-come, first-served basis
