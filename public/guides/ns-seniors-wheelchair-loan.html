Seniors Community Wheelchair Loan Program

Lends new or recycled wheelchairs to Nova Scotians 65 or older who have a long-term disability or illness. An assessment checks the fit.

What it is

What to know

  • An occupational therapist or physiotherapist assesses you for fit.
  • A recycled chair is given if one can be adapted to your needs.
  • You keep the chair if you move into long term care.

Good to know

To find out if you qualify, you start by calling Continuing Care.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You live in Nova Scotia with a valid health card number.
  • You are 65 or older when you apply.
  • A therapist has assessed you.
  • A long-term disability, chronic illness or terminal illness means you need assistance.
  • Your household income is in category A of the home care income table.
  • Other programs, such as private insurance, do not cover you.

You must also sign a consent form for safe use of the wheelchair.

What this guide can't tell you: whether you will be approved. The program decides, often using details we do not ask for.

Amount or value

A new or recycled wheelchair on loan, manual or electric

How to apply

  1. Call Continuing Care to check if you qualify.
  2. Meet the therapist who arranges your assessment.
  3. Sign the consent form and receive your wheelchair.

What you may need

  • Nova Scotia health card number

Practical tips

  • Call again for a reassessment if your needs change.
  • In long term care, a transition plan helps you get a chair there.
