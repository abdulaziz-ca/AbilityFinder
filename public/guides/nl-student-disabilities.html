Student Aid for Students with Disabilities (provincial supports)

Extra student aid for post-secondary students with a verified disability. A provincial grant may cover disability-related costs above the federal services and equipment grant.

What it is

Supports you may qualify for include:

  • A Provincial Grant for High Need Students with Disabilities.
  • Extra weeks of funding to finish your program.
  • Full-time assistance with a smaller course load.
  • Extra funding for equipment, software, learning support or tutoring, through a federal grant.

Good to know

Only the Provincial Grant for High Need Students is provincial; the page also lists federal Canada Student Grants.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You have a verified disability that limits your studies or work.
  • The disability has lasted or is expected to last at least twelve months, or for life.
  • You apply for student aid through StudentAidNL.
  • You meet the academic performance rules.

What this guide can't tell you: whether you will be approved. The program decides, often using details we do not ask for.

Amount or value

Provincial grant for costs above the federal services and equipment grant (no dollar amount listed)

How to apply

  1. Submit the online StudentAidNL application and say you have a disability.
  2. Complete the Verification of Eligibility Form for Students with Disabilities.
  3. Your school applies for the federal services and equipment grant each study period.
  4. You are assessed for the provincial grant automatically when that application is reviewed.

What you may need

  • Verification of Eligibility Form for Students with Disabilities.
  • For a learning disability, a recent psycho-educational or comprehensive educational assessment.

Practical tips

  • You have access to 520 weeks of funding and may qualify for full-time aid with at least 40% of a full course load.
  • For a learning disability, the assessment must be within the last five years or done at age eighteen or older.
  • Proof of disability is usually needed only once.
  • Complete 40% of a full course load each study period to keep full-time aid.
