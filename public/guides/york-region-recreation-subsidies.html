Recreation subsidies (York Region)

York Region funds subsidies that let children from low-income families join recreation programs and camps run by their municipality, plus overnight camp funding for youth aged 12 to 17.

What it is

York Region funds recreation and camp subsidies for children from low-income families.

  • Positive Leisure Activities for Youth, known as PLAY.
  • Summer day camp funding.
  • Youth overnight camp funding for eligible youth aged 12 to 17.
  • The programs themselves are run by your municipal recreation department.
  • A subsidy does not guarantee a space in a program or camp.

Good to know

York Region does not guarantee space in programs or camps — a subsidy is not a place. Search on your city's site will not find this, because the Region funds it and the municipal recreation departments run the programs.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • The child is from a family with low income
  • You live in a York Region municipality

Three subsidies are offered: Positive Leisure Activities for Youth (PLAY), summer day camp funding, and youth overnight camp funding for eligible youth aged 12 to 17.

Amount or value

Subsidies for recreation programs, day camps and youth overnight camps

How to apply

  1. Check which of the three subsidies fits — PLAY, summer day camp, or youth overnight camp
  2. Apply through York Region
  3. Register with your municipal recreation department for the program or camp itself

What you may need

  • Information about your family's income

Practical tips

  • Looking on your city's website will not turn this up — York Region funds it and the city delivers the programs.
  • The subsidy does not hold a spot for you, so register for the program early as well.
  • It covers Aurora, East Gwillimbury, Georgina, King, Newmarket, Markham, Richmond Hill, Vaughan and Whitchurch-Stouffville.
