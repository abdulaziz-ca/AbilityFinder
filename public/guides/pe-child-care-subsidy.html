Prince Edward Island · Money (for parents)

Child Care Subsidy Program

Helps pay for child care and daycare for children up to and including age 12. You may get a full or partial subsidy.

What it is

How the subsidy works:

  • Government pays your centre's rate up to the maximum for your child's age and care type.
  • Costs paid by a parent or another organization are subtracted first.
  • Your provider is paid directly.
  • You pay any difference between actual cost and the subsidy.

Good to know

Page published March 25, 2024. The subsidy is paid directly to your child care provider.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You live in PEI and have a child aged 12 or under.
  • You are working, attending school or at home.
  • Your needs assessment and income support a subsidy.

What this guide can't tell you: whether you will be approved. The program decides, often using details we do not ask for.

Amount or value

Full or partial subsidy, set by a needs assessment

How to apply

  1. Try the online Child Care Subsidy Calculator.
  2. Call the subsidy office to set up an appointment.
  3. A needs assessment decides full or partial subsidy.

Practical tips

  • The program is flexible and offers a range of support options.
  • Ask about your situation if you are at home or in school.
  • Bring your questions to the appointment.
