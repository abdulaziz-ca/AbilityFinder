Disability Support Program

One-on-one help to make a plan for adults with a long-term disability who need help to support themselves or take part in the community.

What it is

Support can include:

  • Personal support in and outside the home, and respite care.
  • Help to take part in the community and to learn personal living skills.
  • Disability-specific transportation support.
  • Technical support and devices that meet policy guidelines.
  • Residential facility services and Meals on Wheels.
  • The Employment and Support Services Program.

Good to know

A financial assessment sets what you pay toward approved supports. It does not cover services other government programs offer.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You live in New Brunswick and are 19 to 64.
  • You have a long-term disability.
  • You need help to support yourself personally or to take part in the community.

Amount or value

Supports set by your plan; you may pay a share based on income

How to apply

  1. Start on your own or with help from family or friends.
  2. Or start with staff at Social Development or a community agency.
  3. Or get help from an independent facilitator from Inclusion NB.
  4. Apply online or call Social Development.

Practical tips

  • Your income is considered when setting your share of the cost.
  • A facilitator from Inclusion NB can help you apply.
