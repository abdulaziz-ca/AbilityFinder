Ambulance fee exemption

Some people do not pay the ambulance bill upfront because a provincial program covers it, including social assistance and Guaranteed Income Supplement recipients.

What it is

If you call 911 and need an ambulance to hospital, you receive an invoice in the mail.

  • For listed groups, the provincial program pays the bill.
  • There is no cost for transfers of an admitted patient from one hospital to another.
  • People with private insurance pay upfront, then claim the receipt from their insurer.

Good to know

The fee is $130.60 for New Brunswick residents and $650.00 for non-residents. Motor vehicle collision patients must pay.

What you must meet

The program requires at least one of these:

  • You are an entitled recipient of Social Assistance services.
  • You receive the Guaranteed Income Supplement.
  • You live in a nursing home and have been issued a yellow health card.
  • You are an Extra-Mural Program patient whose transport Extra-Mural requested.

Transport for Medical Assistance In Dying is also covered.

Amount or value

Resident fee is $130.60; listed groups do not pay it upfront
