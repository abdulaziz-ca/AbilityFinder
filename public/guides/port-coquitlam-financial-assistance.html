Financial Assistance for Recreation (Port Coquitlam)

Approved Port Coquitlam residents get a One City Pass for the calendar year. They can also take a scholarship of $200 per adult or $500 per child or youth. The scholarship gives a 75% discount on registered program fees.

What it is

A pass and an optional scholarship for registered programs.

  • A One City Pass is valid for each approved family member.
  • The scholarship is $200 per adult and $500 per child or youth aged 0 to 18.
  • You pay the other 25% of the program fee.
  • Specialty programs, personal training, rentals and events are not included.

Good to know

The scholarship cannot be used on programs you already registered for.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You are a Port Coquitlam resident
  • Your income is within the LICO table, or a Community Partner or adjudicator refers you

Family means a maximum of 2 adults plus children or dependents in the home.

Amount or value

One City Pass plus a $200 adult or $500 child scholarship

How to apply

  1. Fill out the online form, or download the paper form
  2. Provide proof of residency
  3. Provide a Proof of Income Statement or a Community Partner Reference Form
  4. Submit by email, mail or drop-off

What you may need

  • A bill from the past 90 days showing your name and Port Coquitlam address
  • A CRA Proof of Income Statement for adults in the family
  • Or the Community Partner Reference Form

Practical tips

  • A Notice of Assessment, T1 or T4 cannot replace the Proof of Income Statement.
  • January is busy. Staff reply within 10 to 14 business days.
  • The income limit is $31,264 or less for 1 person.
