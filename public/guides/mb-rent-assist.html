Manitoba · Housing

Rent Assist (not on EIA)

A monthly rent benefit for low-income Manitobans who rent in the private market and do not receive EIA. You may qualify even if you work.

Manitoba is new here: please double-check before you rely on a detail

We added Manitoba in October 2026, while some of its programs are changing. Each program here comes from its official page, but some details are not published yet, so confirm with the program before you count on an amount or rule.

  • Manitoba is moving people with a disability from Employment and Income Assistance (EIA) to the new Manitoba Supports for Persons with Disabilities program, and that program's page does not publish payment amounts yet.
  • Since September 1, 2026, EIA covers only emergency dental care and points people to the federal Canadian Dental Care Plan.
  • Some income limits are old (home adaptation limits are from 2019) or change every year (Rent Assist and Winnipeg programs).
  • Winnipeg Transit Plus has proposed new eligibility rules that are not in effect.
  • City programs are listed for Winnipeg only so far.

What it is

Rent Assist pays a benefit toward rent, based on your income and the market rent for your family size.

  • The benefit helps your household afford rent up to 80 per cent of median market rent.
  • Your benefit depends on household size, net income and whether you are over 55.
  • It also depends on whether you had CPP Disability benefits or the Disability Tax Credit.
  • It is paid monthly by direct deposit or cheque.

Good to know

Income limits apply. If you apply January to June, your tax return from two years ago is used. July to December uses last year's return.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You are a Canadian citizen or permanent resident, and at least 18.
  • You pay rent or room and board for unsubsidized housing.
  • Your net annual income is under $29,120 for a single person.
  • $33,920 for a single person 55+, or a person who qualifies for the Disability Tax Credit or CPP Disability.
  • Other limits are $38,720 for two people, $50,240 for three or four, and $60,768 for five or more.

You may not qualify if you rent from Manitoba Housing, own your home, or get another housing subsidy.

Amount or value

Based on your income and market rent for your family size; the page gives no set amount

How to apply

  1. Use the online Rent Assist calculator to estimate your monthly benefit.
  2. Contact Provincial Services to apply or to have a form mailed to you.
  3. Claim your rent on your income taxes each year.

What you may need

  • Your income tax return for the year used to assess you

Practical tips

  • Your eligibility is reassessed once a year from your tax return.
  • You may not qualify if you live in a First Nations community, student housing or a care facility.
  • You may not qualify if you are in Canada on a work or study permit.
