a-card — free OC Transpo travel if you are blind or partially sighted (Ottawa)

The a-card lets people who are blind or partially sighted ride OC Transpo free on every route except Para Transpo. You qualify with a CNIB card or an Attorney-General of Ontario Guide Dog photo ID card.

What it is

The a-card is free OC Transpo travel for people who are blind or partially sighted.

  • Free on all routes except Para Transpo.
  • Qualify with a CNIB card or an Attorney-General of Ontario Guide Dog photo ID card.
  • Para Transpo trips are not covered by the a-card.

Good to know

The a-card does not cover Para Transpo. If you use Para Transpo, look at the Access pass, or the Community Pass if you also receive ODSP.

What you must meet

You qualify if any of these apply:

  • You hold a CNIB card
  • You hold an Attorney-General of Ontario Guide Dog photo ID card

Proof is a CNIB one-piece photo ID card, or the Attorney-General of Ontario Guide Dog photo ID card.

Amount or value

Free travel on all routes except Para Transpo

How to apply

  1. Have your CNIB one-piece photo ID card, or your Attorney-General of Ontario Guide Dog photo ID card
  2. Take it to an OC Transpo Customer Service Centre to get the a-card

What you may need

  • A CNIB one-piece photo ID card, or an Attorney-General of Ontario Guide Dog photo ID card

Practical tips

  • The a-card does not cover Para Transpo — the Access pass or, for ODSP recipients, the Community Pass does.
