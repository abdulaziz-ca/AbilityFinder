Low-Income Seniors' Benefit

Gives $629 in annual financial support to eligible New Brunswick seniors who get certain federal Old Age Security benefits.

What it is

What to know about the 2026 benefit:

  • It pays $629 in annual financial support.
  • Spouses who live separately, such as one in a nursing home, can both get it.
  • Applications can be sent online or on forms from Service New Brunswick.

Good to know

The $629 is the 2026 amount. People may apply until Dec. 31.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You are 60 or older and live in New Brunswick.
  • You get the Guaranteed Income Supplement (you must be 65 or older).
  • Or you get the Allowance or the Allowance for the Survivor (you must be 60 to 64).

If both spouses get the Guaranteed Income Supplement and live together, only one benefit is paid.
