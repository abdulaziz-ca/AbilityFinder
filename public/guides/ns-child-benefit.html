Nova Scotia Child Benefit

A tax-free monthly payment for low- and modest-income families raising children. It is paid together with the Canada child benefit.

What it is

The benefit helps with the cost of raising children.

  • It is tax-free and paid monthly.
  • It is combined with the Canada child benefit in one payment.

Good to know

The amount applies from July 2026 to June 2027. The Province of Nova Scotia pays for this benefit in full.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You have a child under 18 years of age in your care.
  • Your adjusted family net income is below $34,000.

Between $26,000 and $34,000 of income, you may get part of the benefit.

Amount or value

You may be entitled to $127.08 a month for each child under 18 (July 2026 to June 2027)
