Leisure Access Program (White Rock)

White Rock gives low-income residents 50% off eligible recreation programs or memberships, up to $300 a year. Skating at Centennial Arena is free.

What it is

The program helps residents join recreation when money is tight.

  • It gives 50% off eligible programs or memberships, up to $300 per year.
  • It includes free public skating at Centennial Arena.
  • Annual and 20-session passes for Grandview Heights Aquatic Centre and South Surrey Indoor Pool are 50% off.

Good to know

South Surrey residents are not eligible. They can apply to the City of Surrey program instead.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You are a permanent resident of the City of White Rock
  • Your family income is at or below the limit for your family size

The limit is $26,926 for one person and $64,000 for six.

Amount or value

50% off programs and memberships, up to $300 a year
