Wellness and Recreation Access Program (Morinville)

WRAP helps low-income Morinville people and families pay for leisure centre memberships, fitness classes, children's programs, sports and FCSS camps. Qualified applicants can get up to $250 per person.

What it is

A subsidy for recreation and FCSS programs.

  • It covers Morinville Leisure Centre memberships.
  • It covers Town programs, such as children's sports and adult fitness classes.
  • It covers FCSS summer, PD day, and fall and spring break camps.
  • Local agencies with residential support may get Community Care day passes.

Good to know

You must renew every calendar year. The page says household income must meet the Low-Income Cut-Offs chart. Some applicants must also show AISH, Income Support or Seniors Benefits proof.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You are a resident of the Town of Morinville
  • Your household income is within the Low-Income Cut-Offs chart for your household size

Some applicants also give proof of AISH, Income Support or Seniors Benefits, or an FCSS social worker's assessment.

Amount or value

Up to $250 per person

How to apply

  1. Complete the application form
  2. Contact Morinville FCSS by email or phone
  3. Submit your documents with the application

What you may need

  • Alberta ID or a utility bill showing a Morinville address
  • A current tax assessment or the last 90 days of bank statements
  • If on AISH, Income Support or Alberta Seniors Benefits: proof of receipt

Practical tips

  • The income limit is $29,380 for 1 person and $77,751 for 7 people.
  • Community Recreation and FCSS programs are in the Community Program Guide.
