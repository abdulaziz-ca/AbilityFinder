Recreation Access Program (Prince Rupert)

Prince Rupert subsidizes recreation fees by 90% for children and youth and 50% for adults. Each eligible family member also gets 50 free admissions a year.

What it is

The program helps people in need use the Rec Complex at a lower cost.

  • The subsidy works toward memberships, visit passes or registered programs.
  • The 50 free admissions can be used for the gymnasium, swimming or skating.
  • Approval lasts one year and is reviewed each year.

Good to know

Funding is first come, first served until the budget is used. Eligibility is based only on financial need.

What you must meet

The program requires at least one of these:

  • You get a named provincial benefit such as disability assistance, social assistance or a Seniors Supplement
  • Your tax and benefit documents show financial need

You also need photo ID and proof you live in Prince Rupert.

Amount or value

90% off for children and youth, 50% off for adults

How to apply

  1. Check that you can show proof of financial assistance
  2. Get a referral letter if you need one
  3. Fill in the form, on paper or online
  4. Attach your documents
  5. Call to book an intake appointment if you want help

What you may need

  • Photo ID for all adults
  • Proof you live in Prince Rupert
  • Proof of assistance, or a Notice of Assessment for all adults and a Child Tax Benefit notice for dependents

Practical tips

  • Applications are kept confidential.
  • A missed program with no cancellation may affect later subsidy requests.
