Ontario Child Benefit

A monthly payment for low-income to moderate-income families to help with the cost of raising children. The Canada Revenue Agency sets the amount for each family.

What it is

The Ontario Child Benefit helps families with the cost of raising children, including a child with a disability.

  • Pays up to $1,760 per child per year, in monthly payments, if you are eligible.
  • The amount depends on your adjusted family net income on your annual tax return.
  • The number of children in your care who are under 18 also affects the amount.
  • The Canada Revenue Agency runs the benefit for Ontario.

Good to know

There is no separate application. The Canada Child Benefit application also assesses you, and you must file taxes each year.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You are a low-income to moderate-income family with children in your care.
  • You apply for the Canada Child Benefit through the Canada Revenue Agency.
  • You file your taxes every year, even if you have no income to declare.

The amount depends on your family net income and the number of children under 18 in your care.

Amount or value

Up to $1,760 per child each year

How to apply

  1. Send in your Canada Child Benefit application.
  2. Every year, file your taxes, even with nothing to declare.
  3. Wait for the Canada Revenue Agency to assess you for the Ontario Child Benefit and set your monthly payment.

What you may need

  • Your income tax return each year
