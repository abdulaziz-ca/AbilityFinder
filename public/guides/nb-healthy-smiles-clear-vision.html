New Brunswick · Health (for children)

Healthy Smiles, Clear Vision

New Brunswick's dental and vision plan for children of low-income families. It covers basic dental care, yearly eye exams, lenses and frames.

What it is

Family net income limits, based on Market Basket Measure calculations from June 2025:

  • 2 people: $35,683; 3 people: $43,702.
  • 4 people: $50,463; 5 people: $56,419.
  • 6 people: $61,804; 7 people: $66,756.
  • 8 people: $71,365.
  • Dental covers basic items such as regular exams, X-rays and extractions, plus sealants and fluoride.
  • Vision covers basic items such as a yearly exam, lenses and frames.

Good to know

Children need to be signed up for the federal dental plan to qualify for full coverage here.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You live in New Brunswick.
  • You have a dependent child aged 18 or younger.
  • Your child is enrolled in the federal Canadian Dental Care Plan (CDCP).
  • Your child has no other dental and vision coverage from a government program or private plan.
  • Your family net income is at or under the limit for your family size.

Income is checked against last year's NB income tax return or Notice of Assessment.

What this guide can't tell you: whether you will be approved. The program decides, often using details we do not ask for.

How to apply

  1. Check that your child is enrolled in the Canadian Dental Care Plan (CDCP).
  2. Download the application form, or pick up a paper copy at a Service New Brunswick centre.
  3. Paper copies are also at regional offices of the Department of Social Development.
  4. Mail or fax the completed form to Healthy Smiles, Clear Vision, 644 Main Street, P.O. Box 220, Moncton, NB E1C 8L3.
  5. The fax number is 1-506-867-4651.

What you may need

  • The completed application form
  • Last year's NB income tax return or Notice of Assessment (income is checked against it)

Practical tips

  • Children with dental and vision coverage through Social Development are moved over automatically.
  • Social Development clients do not need to apply.
  • For the CDCP, visit the Government of Canada page or a Service Canada Centre.
  • Questions about this plan go to Medavie Blue Cross.
