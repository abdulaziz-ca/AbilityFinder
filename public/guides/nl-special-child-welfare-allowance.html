Special Child Welfare Allowance

Money for families to buy items or services a child needs because of a physical or intellectual disability.

What it is

About this allowance:

  • It helps with the cost of services and supports.
  • Families can buy items and services needed because of the child disability.

Good to know

The monthly amount is set for each family by a financial needs test.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • Your child is under eighteen years old.
  • Your child has a physical or intellectual disability.
  • Your child lives at home with the family.
  • You pass a financial needs test.

Amount or value

Monthly amount set by a financial needs test
