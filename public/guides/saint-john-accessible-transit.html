Saint John · Transit

Saint John Accessible Transit

Rides for people with disabilities who cannot use regular public transit. A non-profit, Independence Plus Inc., runs the service.

What it is

This program gives you:

  • A 10-ride pass for $50 or a 20-ride pass for $90.
  • Service Monday to Saturday, with no service on Sunday.

Good to know

An attendant travelling with you pays nothing. Cancel at least 60 minutes ahead.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You have a disability.
  • You cannot use regular public transportation.

The page does not describe a registration process.

Amount or value

$5.50 each way, or 10 rides for $50

How to apply

  1. Call 506-648-0609 to book or cancel a ride.
  2. Be ready 5 minutes before your pickup time.
  3. Call 506-643-7004 for administration questions.

Practical tips

  • Rides within city limits cost $5.50 each way.
  • Drivers wait only 3 minutes.
  • Drivers cannot enter your home or help carry wheelchairs, so arrange an attendant.
  • Failing to cancel or being late costs the full fee, and three no-shows may lead to suspension.
