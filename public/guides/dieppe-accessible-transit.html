Dieppe Accessible Transit

Dieppe residents with a disability that stops them using public transit can book subsidized rides. The city pays the rest of the cost.

What it is

This program gives you:

  • $3 one way or $6 return within Dieppe.
  • $6 one way or $12 return to or from Moncton or Riverview.
  • Service Monday to Saturday 6:30 am to 9:30 pm, Sunday 9:30 am to 6 pm.

Good to know

Applications are reviewed case by case. Access can be denied, temporary or have conditions.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You live in Dieppe.
  • You use a wheelchair permanently, or can show a significant, lasting disability.
  • The disability does not let you use public transit safely.
  • You apply online or by phone.

The city decides each application case by case.

Amount or value

Trips from $3 one way in Dieppe, $6 to Moncton or Riverview

How to apply

  1. Apply online or call 506-877-7900.
  2. Give a valid email address, because decisions are sent by email.
  3. Wait for a decision. Applications are typically processed within four weeks.
  4. Book each trip with the provider at least 24 hours ahead.

What you may need

  • Proof of a significant, lasting disability, if you do not use a wheelchair permanently

Practical tips

  • The city may reassess, change or end your access later.
  • Some trips may be shared with other passengers.
  • You are told how many round trips you get each month.
