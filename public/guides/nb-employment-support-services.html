New Brunswick · Employment

Employment and Support Services Program

Agencies help adults in the Disability Support Program build life skills, join community activities and find and keep work that fits their skills.

What it is

The agencies can help you:

  • Gain life skills that promote independence.
  • Take part in volunteer, social and recreation opportunities.
  • Find and keep work that matches your skills and interests.
  • Work for appropriate wages in inclusive workplaces.

Good to know

An agency must be able to support you. The person referred must join the application and planning process.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You are approved for support from the Disability Support Program.
  • One of the 38 contracted non-profit agencies can support you.
