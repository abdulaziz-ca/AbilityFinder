ODSP employment supports and earnings rules

If you get Ontario Disability Support Program (ODSP) income support, you can work and keep your support and benefits. Extra benefits help with child care and start-up costs for a job, training or a business.

What it is

ODSP lets you earn money while keeping your income support.

  • Since February 1, 2023: up to $1,000 a month in net earnings with no reduction.
  • After the first $1,000, 25% of net earnings is also exempt.
  • The Work-Related Benefit gives up to $100 for each eligible family member who earns money.
  • The Employment and Training Start Up Benefit gives up to $500 in a 12-month period.
  • Up-front child care: the full cost of licensed care, or up to $600 per child in 12 months if unlicensed.

Good to know

You must report all earnings every month, even if they are exempt. If you miss a reporting deadline, your payments and benefits may stop temporarily.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You get ODSP income support.
  • You want to work, train or start your own business.

Family members can qualify for some benefits, with different age and schooling rules for each benefit.

Amount or value

Earn up to $1,000 a month without losing ODSP income support; Work-Related Benefit up to $100 per family member

How to apply

  1. Tell your caseworker about the money you and your family earn.
  2. Report your earnings each month through MyBenefits, the phone system, or the Employment/Training Income Report form.
  3. Return the report by the seventh day of the following month.
  4. Contact your caseworker to ask for the child care or start-up benefits, and send proof.

What you may need

  • Pay stubs
  • Business income and expense records, if self-employed
  • Letter of employment or training registration
  • Proof you need to pay for child care in advance

Practical tips

  • Report both gross and net pay.
  • If you earn nothing that month, put $0 on the form and still send it in.
  • If you stop working, tell your caseworker right away.
  • You do not need proof of employment costs to get the Work-Related Benefit; it is added automatically.
