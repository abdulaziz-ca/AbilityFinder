Yorkton Discounted Monthly Transit Pass

A discounted Yorkton monthly bus pass for people in provincially sponsored social assistance programs. You buy it at City Hall.

What it is

This pass costs less than the regular monthly pass.

  • The adult monthly pass costs $90 in 2026.
  • The discounted pass is sold at City Hall.

Good to know

The page does not name the programs or say what proof to bring. Ask City Hall.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:
