Access Transit (Swift Current)

A ride option in Swift Current for people with mobility issues who cannot use the regular Swift Transit bus stops. You phone Access Transit to arrange your trip.

What it is

Access Transit runs on these hours:

  • Monday to Friday, 8:00 am to 6:00 pm.
  • Saturday, noon to 6:00 pm.
  • Sunday, 8:30 am to 2:30 pm.

Good to know

SaskAbilities runs Access Transit and the regular bus in partnership with the City. There is no service on statutory holidays.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You have mobility issues, or you cannot walk to a bus stop or need accessibility accommodations.

The page says that if you have mobility issues, you may be eligible.

Amount or value

$3.00 per one-way trip
