Prince Edward Island · Health

Financial Assistance Drug Program

Covers the full cost of approved prescription and non-prescription medications for people approved for Social Assistance.

What it is

What the program covers:

  • Both prescription and non-prescription medications can be covered.
  • Medications must be on the PEI Pharmacare Formulary.
  • Some medications need special authorization.
  • Private insurance is billed first, then PEI Pharmacare covers what is left.

Good to know

Page published October 23, 2025. Medications under the High Cost Drug Program are also covered, effective June 1, 2023.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You have a PEI Health Card.
  • You have been approved under the Social Assistance Act.

What this guide can't tell you: whether you will be approved. The program decides, often using details we do not ask for.

Amount or value

All approved medication costs covered

How to apply

  1. Apply for Social Assistance.
  2. The Social Assistance Program submits your drug program application.
  3. Fill your prescriptions at any PEI community retail pharmacy.

What you may need

  • No separate documents are listed on the page.

Practical tips

  • Coverage is not retroactive.
  • Your eligibility ends the day you leave PEI to live elsewhere.
  • Social Assistance clients are enrolled in this program automatically.
