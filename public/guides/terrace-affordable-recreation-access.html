Affordable Recreation Access Program (Terrace)

Terrace gives a discount card for 50% off drop-in fees and short memberships. It is for people who get certain provincial or federal assistance or disability benefits.

What it is

A discount card lowers the price of drop-in visits and short memberships.

  • It gives 50% off drop-in fees.
  • It covers 10-visit, 1 month and 3 month memberships.
  • Buy memberships before the card expires.

Good to know

Fitness class registration, rentals, swimming lessons and other registered activities are not included.

What you must meet

The program requires at least one of these:

  • You get regular income assistance, PWD or PPMB benefits
  • You get Canada Pension Plan Disability
  • You get the Guaranteed Income Supplement or the Child Disability Benefit

Amount or value

50% off drop-in fees and short memberships

How to apply

  1. Pick up the application and letter at the Terrace and District Aquatic Centre
  2. Complete the form
  3. Take it to Service BC or Service Canada to verify your benefits
  4. Bring the paperwork back to the Aquatic Centre

What you may need

  • The completed application form
  • A Release of Personal Information form (HR0095) for provincial benefits
  • The standard benefit letter for CPP Disability, the Guaranteed Income Supplement or the Child Disability Benefit
  • Identification

Practical tips

  • Cards expire on a schedule: every six months for some benefits and yearly for PWD.
  • People on regular income assistance get cards for up to 3 dependent children too.
