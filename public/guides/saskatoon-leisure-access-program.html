Leisure Access Program (Saskatoon)

Low-income Saskatoon residents get unlimited drop-in admission at Leisure Centres and one registered recreation program per year.

What it is

The program includes:

  • Unlimited admission to drop-in programs at the Leisure Centres.
  • One registered recreation program per year.
  • Complimentary tickets to the Nutrien Playland rides at Kinsmen Park.
  • Single-use family passes to the Western Development Museum, while supplies last.
  • Free child minding for children six and under at three centres.

Good to know

Apply any time. You hear back by email in three to six weeks.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You live in Saskatoon.
  • Your household income before tax is at or below the limit for your household size.

Limits for 1 to 4 people: $27,478, $34,206, $42,053 and $51,058 (larger sizes on the page).

What this guide can't tell you: whether you will be approved. The program decides, often using details we do not ask for.

How to apply

  1. Fill out the online application, or pick up a paper copy at any indoor leisure centre.
  2. Attach last year's Notice of Assessment for each adult in the household.
  3. Attach a utility bill or government letter showing your current address.
  4. Wait for an email about your application.

What you may need

  • Notice of Assessment from the Canada Revenue Agency
  • Utility bill or government-issued letter with your address

Practical tips

  • Social service recipients use a different route, through the Social Services office.
  • Child minding is at Lakewood Civic Centre, Shaw Centre and Saskatoon Field House.
