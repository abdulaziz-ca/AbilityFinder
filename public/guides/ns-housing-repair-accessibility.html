Housing Repair and Accessibility Program

Helps low- and moderate-income homeowners pay for critical repairs and accessibility changes, like ramps and bathrooms. Each stream may fund up to $18,000.

What it is

What it helps with

  • Accessible stream: ramps, wider doors, chair lifts, bathrooms and kitchens.
  • Repair stream: structural, electrical, plumbing or heating hazards.
  • You can apply to either stream or both.
  • Costs for work done before approval are not eligible.

Good to know

Each stream may give $10,000 as a grant and $8,000 as a forgivable loan, if you meet the loan conditions. Larger projects may get a repayable loan of up to $20,000 across both streams.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You own and live in the home, and have lived there at least 1 year.
  • Household gross income is within the limits in the Program Guide.
  • You cannot pay for the work yourself; investments must be $25,000 or less.
  • Property taxes are paid up to date or on a payment plan for 3 months.
  • Property value is $360,000 or less in Halifax and East Hants, $280,000 elsewhere.

The accessible stream needs a household disability; the repair stream needs a home 5+ years old.

Amount or value

Up to $18,000 per stream; larger projects may get a repayable loan

How to apply

  1. Read the Program Guide and complete the application.
  2. Submit it with your supporting documents.
  3. The department confirms eligibility and an inspector visits.
  4. Get contractor quotes from the bid package and send them in.

What you may need

  • CRA tax slips and Notice of Assessment for last tax year
  • Property tax bills and receipts
  • Confirmation of Medical Condition Form, if applicable
  • Signatures of all owners and household members 19 or older

Practical tips

  • Do not start work before you are approved.
  • Emergency repairs, like no heat in winter, should take 1 to 3 days to review.
  • Non-emergency repairs should take 3 to 12 months.
  • There is no cost to apply.

Timing

1 to 3 days (emergency); 3 to 12 months (other)
