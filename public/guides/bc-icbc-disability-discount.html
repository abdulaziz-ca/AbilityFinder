ICBC Disability Discount (Basic Autoplan)

If you are eligible for the provincial fuel tax refund program for persons with disabilities, you may receive 25% off the basic part of your ICBC vehicle insurance.

What it is

The discount is applied through an Autoplan broker after the Ministry of Finance registers you in the Fuel Tax Refund Program for Persons with Disabilities.

Good to know

Register for the Fuel Tax Refund Program for Persons with Disabilities first. Then talk to an Autoplan broker about applying the discount to your policy.

What you must meet

You must be 16 or older. You may receive it if you are eligible for the Fuel Tax Refund Program for Persons with Disabilities — the same disability confirmations listed there apply.

Amount or value

You may receive 25% off ICBC basic Autoplan insurance (including electric vehicles) if you are eligible for the fuel tax refund program for persons with disabilities

At a glance

Difficulty
Easy
Application
Fuel-tax registration, then ask an Autoplan broker

How to apply

  1. Complete the provincial Fuel Tax Refund Program registration
  2. Wait for the Ministry of Finance registration confirmation
  3. Bring the confirmation and vehicle information to an Autoplan broker
  4. Ask the broker to apply the disability discount to eligible Basic Autoplan coverage

What you may need

  • Fuel Tax Refund Program registration confirmation
  • Vehicle registration and current Autoplan policy information
  • Identification requested by the broker

Practical tips

  • The discount applies to Basic Autoplan, not to every optional coverage charge
  • Ask the broker when the discount will start if your policy is already active
  • Electric vehicles can get the insurance discount even though they do not use fuel

Timing

Ask an Autoplan broker after provincial registration is confirmed
