Recreation Fee Assistance Program (RFAP)

Drayton Valley residents with gross household income below the Low Income Cut-Off get a 75% subsidized cost on Town programs at the Aquatic Centre and the Health and Fitness Centre.

What it is

The program lowers the cost of recreation for Drayton Valley residents with low income.

  • Aquatic Centre passes and swimming lessons are covered, as are children's camps of 4 or more days.
  • Fitness Centre passes, the indoor track and racquetball or squash passes are covered.
  • Passes can be yearly, 6-month, 3-month, monthly or a 10-visit punch pass.
  • A family is up to two adults and their dependent children, and you may apply for up to 7 people.

Good to know

Programs must be a Town program or activity that is 4 or more days or classes long, a camp, or a membership. Fundraising or uniform fees, travel to out-of-town competitions and programs of three days or less are not covered.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You live in the Town of Drayton Valley
  • Your gross household income is below the Statistics Canada Low Income Cut-Off
  • You send documents that prove income and residency for all adults in the household

The 2025 gross limits run from $30,526 for 1 person to $80,784 for 7 persons.

What this guide can't tell you: whether you will be approved. The program decides, often using details we do not ask for.

How to apply

  1. Download the application form, or pick one up at a Town recreation or civic location.
  2. Attach your income and residency documents to the form.
  3. Seal it in an envelope and drop it at the Community Services Centre, or mail or email it.
  4. Wait to be contacted. If approved, you get a letter listing the programs you can use.

What you may need

  • Proof of residence: a driver's license or utility bill with your legal land address, not a P.O. box
  • Alberta Health Benefits Card or Alberta Income Support Summary to verify income
  • Or a Notice of Assessment from the most recent tax year for every adult
  • Or your two most recent pay stubs or bank statements

Practical tips

  • You may need an appointment, depending on staff availability.
  • To get a copy of your assessment, call the Canada Revenue Agency and ask for it by mail.
  • Documents are needed for all adults in the household.
