Alberta Brain Injury Initiative

A network of agencies that helps adults with an acquired brain injury, and their families, find and use supports. A service coordinator looks at your needs and helps build a plan.

What it is

A service coordinator can

  • Give you information about brain injury and the supports that exist.
  • Assess what you need.
  • Work with you, your family and service providers on service plans and agreements.
  • Refer you to community supports and independent living skills development.
  • Help you find, apply for and coordinate benefits and services.

Good to know

Each agency serves set communities. Check the list on the official page to find the one that covers where you live.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You are an adult with an acquired brain injury.

Families of survivors can also get help from the service coordinators.

Amount or value

Service coordination and help finding supports. No dollar amount is published.
