Leisure Access Program (Duncan)

The Leisure Access Program gives economically disadvantaged residents annual access to drop-in programs at the Cowichan Aquatic Centre and Fuller Lake Arena. City of Duncan residents can apply.

What it is

The program covers drop-in use of two Cowichan area facilities for lower-income households.

  • Open to residents of North Cowichan, the CVRD, Duncan, Cowichan Tribes Reserve Lands and Ladysmith.

Good to know

North Cowichan runs the program for several areas, including the City of Duncan. The page gives no dollar value for the access.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You live in the City of Duncan or another listed area
  • Your gross household income is under the cutoff for your household size

Cutoffs run from $27,308 for 1 person to $72,268 for 7 or more; a household of 4 is $50,744.

How to apply

  1. Fill in the online application
  2. List every person in your household
  3. Upload photo ID and your 2025 Notice of Assessment, or use an agency referral
  4. Submit the form

What you may need

  • Current government-issued photo ID for each applicant over 18, with a matching address
  • 2025 Notice of Assessment, line 15000
  • Or a referral completed by a listed agency, with its stamp or business card

Practical tips

  • Total gross household income means all income before taxes from every household member.
  • Referral agencies are MCFD, Ministry of Housing and Social Development, Community Living BC, Cowichan Valley Youth Services and Cowichan Tribes Social Development.
  • Passes can be cancelled for breaking facility rules or the Code of Conduct.
