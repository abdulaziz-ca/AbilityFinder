Nova Scotia · Health (for children)

Provincial Preschool Autism Service

Services and support for preschool-age children with autism and their families across Nova Scotia. Support is matched to each child's strengths, needs and family priorities.

What it is

About the service

  • IWK Health, Nova Scotia Health, Hearing and Speech Nova Scotia and Autism Nova Scotia run it together.
  • Support is customized to each child and family.
  • Parents and caregivers are treated as knowing their child best.

Good to know

Services end when the child enters grade primary. The page says these services are evolving.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • Your child is of preschool age, before entering grade primary.
  • Your child has autism. If you have concerns about their development, talk to your care provider.

The page lists no further rules; call to ask what applies.

Amount or value

Needs-based autism services; no dollar value published

How to apply

  1. Talk to your care provider if you have concerns about your child's development.
  2. Ask about autism assessments through the IWK referrals page.
  3. Call the service or visit the IWK PPAS page.

Practical tips

  • Direct Family Support for Children can also be used for preschool children.
