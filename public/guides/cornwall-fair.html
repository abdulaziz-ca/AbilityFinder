Fee Assistance in Recreation, FAIR (Cornwall)

The City covers 75 percent of swimming lessons, City sports and other recreation programs for low-income residents. You pay 25 percent. It also covers pool memberships and public skating passes.

What it is

FAIR lowers what you pay for City recreation so cost is not a barrier.

  • You pay only 25 percent of eligible costs.
  • Swimming lessons and City sports are included.
  • Pool memberships and public skating passes are included.
  • Support stops once you reach $300 for the year.

Good to know

The $300 yearly cap applies. Each year on June 1 you send in the previous year's tax documents.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You live in the City of Cornwall
  • You are 18 or older to apply
  • Your yearly combined family income is at or below the Statistics Canada Low Income Cut-Off
  • You have not used up the yearly funding maximum

Amount or value

75% of costs covered, up to $300 a year

How to apply

  1. Fill in the FAIR client application form.
  2. Add ID for each family member and one proof of income.
  3. Add proof that you live in Cornwall.
  4. Email the full package to the City.
  5. Send last year's documents again each June 1.

What you may need

  • One photocopy of ID for each family member
  • One of these, showing your address: a Notice of Assessment for each adult, an Ontario Works or ODSP statement of deposit, a Canada Child Benefit notice, or a GIS notice
  • Proof of residency, such as a lease, utility bill or driver's licence

Practical tips

  • Canadian Tire Jumpstart can also help with youth sports costs.
