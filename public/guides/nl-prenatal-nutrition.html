Prenatal-Early Childhood Nutrition Supplement

Monthly money for extra food during pregnancy and until your child turns five. It is for people with low incomes.

What it is

What to know:

  • The benefit is paid monthly by direct deposit, with a mail option.
  • It is added to your child benefit payments once your child is born.
  • It continues until the month before your child turns five.

Good to know

A one time extra payment is made in the month of a child birth.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You are pregnant or have a child under five years old.
  • You live in Newfoundland and Labrador as a permanent resident.
  • You are eligible for the Child Benefit or you get Income Support.

What this guide can't tell you: whether you will be approved. The program decides, often using details we do not ask for.

Amount or value

$150 a month

How to apply

  1. If pregnant, call the Coordinator or complete the online application.
  2. If your child is born, apply for the Canada Child Benefit with the Canada Revenue Agency.
  3. Receive the supplement with your child benefit payments.

What you may need

  • The online application form, if you are pregnant.

Practical tips

  • Amount is $150 a month.
  • An extra one time $150 per child is paid in the birth month; multiple births get multiple payments.
  • The Coordinator can also share information on community supports.
