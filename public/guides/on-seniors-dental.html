Ontario Seniors Dental Care Program

Free, routine dental care for low-income Ontario seniors 65 or older. It covers check-ups, fillings, x-rays, tooth removal and treatment for infection, pain and gum disease.

What it is

The program pays for routine dental care for low-income seniors.

  • Covers check-ups, scaling, fluoride and polishing.
  • Covers repairing broken teeth and cavities, and x-rays.
  • Covers removing teeth or abnormal tissue, with anesthesia.
  • Covers treating infection, pain and gum conditions.

Good to know

Coverage ends on July 31 every year. Dentures are partially covered; some procedures have limits. You may still qualify if you also qualify for the Canadian Dental Care Plan.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You are 65 years of age or older.
  • You live in Ontario.
  • Your annual net income is $25,480 or less (single), or $42,290 or less (couple, combined).
  • You have no other dental benefits, apart from the Canadian Dental Care Plan.

Other benefits include private insurance, Ontario Works and the Ontario Disability Support Program.

What this guide can't tell you: whether you will be approved. The program decides, often using details we do not ask for.

How to apply

  1. Apply online or by mail.
  2. If you and your spouse both apply, send two separate applications.
  3. Wait for the welcome package and dental card by mail.
  4. Show your card at each dental visit.

What you may need

  • Your date of birth
  • A valid Ontario address
  • Your Social Insurance Number or Temporary Taxation Number
  • Proof you filed taxes last year (or a guarantor form if you did not file)

Practical tips

  • Your local public health unit can help you apply and find where to get care.
  • Care is available through public health units and partner community health centres.
  • Most clients are re-checked automatically each year; you are told by mail if you must re-apply.
