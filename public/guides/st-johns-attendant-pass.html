St. John's · Recreation

Attendant Pass

An Attendant Pass lets a support person go with you to events and activities for free or at a discount. A professional must verify your need.

What it is

Venues listed as accepting the pass:

  • Arts and Culture Centre
  • Facilities and events run by the City
  • Mary Brown's Centre
  • Metrobus
  • The Rooms
  • The Works/Aquarena

Good to know

Each venue sets the free or discounted rate. Certain restrictions may apply.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You live with a disability and need another person's support to join community activities.
  • A medical professional, social worker or disability agency representative verifies the request.

The pass belongs to the person who needs support.

Amount or value

Free or discounted entry for your support person, set by each venue

At a glance

Difficulty
Easy
Application
Fill in a form, add a photo and get it verified

How to apply

  1. Complete the application or renewal form in the name of the person who needs support.
  2. Ask a professional or agency who knows you to sign the verification.
  3. Add a photo.
  4. Send it by email, in person or by mail.

What you may need

  • Completed application or renewal application
  • A photo
  • Verification from a medical professional, social worker or disability agency representative

Practical tips

  • Other venues may accept the pass, so call ahead to confirm.
  • Apply by email to inclusion@stjohns.ca, in person at the Recreation Administrative Office, H.G.R. Mews Community Centre, 60 Blackler Ave, or by mail to P.O. Box 908.
  • Allow 2 to 4 weeks for processing.
  • Inclusive Services can also issue a pass for staff of agencies that support people.
