Leisure Access Program (RDCK, Nelson)

The Regional District of Central Kootenay gives a yearly subsidy credit to people in financial hardship. It gives 50% off adult and 75% off youth admissions and memberships, and 50% off programs. Nelson residents can apply.

What it is

A subsidy credit used for RDCK recreation.

  • Admissions and memberships are 50% off for adults and 75% off for youth.
  • Programs are 50% off the advertised rate.
  • CHEER events have 100% subsidized admission.
  • Discounts apply only while you have credit left.

Good to know

Apply with a Notice of Assessment or proof of government assistance. Assistance recipients get a second credit of up to $200 for adults and $300 for youth. The pass expires every May 31.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You live in the Regional District of Central Kootenay, which includes the City of Nelson
  • You are 19 or older, or a family applying together
  • You show proof of residency and one qualifying route

Routes are listed assistance, income below the Low Income Measure, or a social service letter.

What this guide can't tell you: whether you will be approved. The program decides, often using details we do not ask for.

Amount or value

$100 per adult or $150 per child credit a year

How to apply

  1. Complete the online application form
  2. Upload ID or a household bill
  3. Upload your proof of assistance or Notice of Assessment
  4. Wait for approval, then show your card at each purchase

What you may need

  • ID or a recent household bill showing your name and address
  • A confirmation letter for the assistance you get, or a Notice of Assessment
  • Or a letter from a pre-approved social service organization

Practical tips

  • Check your junk folder for the approval email.
  • The page recommends reapplying at least three weeks before May 31. The 2026 to 2027 form is on the page.
  • Expired passes get full rates, and fees are not refunded later.
