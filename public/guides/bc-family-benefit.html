British Columbia · Money (for parents)

B.C. Family Benefit

A tax-free monthly payment for families with children under 18. The CRA pays it together with the Canada child benefit and works out the amount from your tax return.

What it is

The CRA calculates the amount automatically from the tax returns you have filed.

  • You get the maximum if 2025 adjusted family net income is less than $30,176.
  • Between $30,176 and $96,562 you get a reduced amount of at least $775, $750 or $725 per child.
  • Over $96,562 the amounts are reduced by 4 percent of the extra income until they reach zero.
  • The monthly payment is the annual amount divided by 12.

Good to know

Amounts are for July 2026 to June 2027, based on 2025 income. Low-income single parents may get up to $500 more a year. Shared custody gets half the amount.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • Your family has a child under 18.
  • Your child is registered for the Canada child benefit.
  • You (and your spouse or common-law partner) file a T1 income tax return.

The amount depends on your family makeup and your adjusted family net income.

Amount or value

Up to $1,750 first child, $1,100 second child, $900 each added child a year (July 2026 to June 2027)

How to apply

  1. File your T1 tax return, and your spouse or partner files theirs.
  2. If your child is not registered for the Canada child benefit, apply for it through the CRA.
  3. The CRA uses that registration to decide your eligibility and pays you each month.

What you may need

  • Your T1 Income Tax and Benefit Return

Practical tips

  • Use the federal child and family benefits calculator to estimate your monthly amount.
  • If you move outside B.C., tell the CRA quickly to avoid an overpayment you would have to repay.
  • From July 2027 a disability supplement will be paid with this benefit for eligible children.
