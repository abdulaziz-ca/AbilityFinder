Prince Edward Island · Health

Provincial Dental Care Program

Covers exams, fillings, extractions, limited cleanings and removable dentures for PEI residents on social or disability assistance, or whose family income is under the program's thresholds.

What it is

What is covered:

  • An annual exam once per 12 months, and emergency exams as required.
  • Fillings, limited root canal treatment and extractions.
  • Removable dentures once every 10 years, though you may pay lab fees.
  • Sealants and fluoride have age limits.

Good to know

The brochure shows no date. The income table is in it, so confirm the current figures with the program.

What you must meet

The program requires at least one of these:

  • You get Social Assistance or Assured Income, including AccessAbility Supports.
  • Your family income is within the program's financial threshold for your family size.

You also need an active PEI Health Card.

What this guide can't tell you: whether you will be approved. The program decides, often using details we do not ask for.

How to apply

  1. Complete the application form, or call to request one.
  2. Fill it in and give consent for Health PEI to get your income from the Canada Revenue Agency.
  3. Return the signed original form to Dental Public Health.
  4. Get treatment at a private dental office or a Health PEI clinic.

What you may need

  • Completed and signed original application form.
  • Consent for Health PEI to get your financial information.

Practical tips

  • A one-person household at $22,014 or less gets 100% coverage, per the brochure table.
  • A one-person household up to $30,820 gets 20% coverage.
  • You can use private dental offices or Health PEI clinics.
