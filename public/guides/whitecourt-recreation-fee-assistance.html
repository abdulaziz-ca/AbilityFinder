Recreational Fee Assistance Program (Whitecourt)

A subsidy for adults and their dependents under 18 who cannot pay the full fee. It covers programs and memberships at the Allan & Jean Millar Centre. Applications are kept confidential.

What it is

The program helps people who cannot pay full fees use Allan & Jean Millar Centre programs.

  • Each family member may get help with 2 programs per session.
  • A 3 month membership may be covered instead.
  • You can apply again each year.
  • All applications are held in strict confidence.

Good to know

The page does not list income limits or the subsidy rate. Families must first apply to KidSport for programs it covers.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You live in the Town of Whitecourt or Woodlands County
  • Your circumstances meet the financial criteria for people unable to pay the full fee
  • You applied to KidSport first, if you need a program it covers

This is for adults and their dependents under 18.

What this guide can't tell you: whether you will be approved. The program decides, often using details we do not ask for.

How to apply

  1. Apply to KidSport first for any programs it covers.
  2. Go to Guest Services at the Allan & Jean Millar Centre to apply.
  3. Call Guest Services at 780-778-3637 for more information.
