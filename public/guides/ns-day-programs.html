Nova Scotia · Community supports

Community inclusion and day programs

The Disability Support Program funds day programs for adults and youth with disabilities. They offer skills training, supported employment, social outings and recreation. You refer yourself.

What it is

Options include

  • Skill development and training, such as computer training.
  • Supported employment with job coaching and resume help.
  • Social enterprise businesses with work experience.
  • Social and recreational activities, and a retirement stream for ages 55 and up.

Good to know

Each program has its own focus, and some have waitlists. Contact a provider to ask about an orientation.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You are an adult or youth with a disability.
  • You choose a Disability Support Program funded day program near you.

The page does not list further eligibility rules.
