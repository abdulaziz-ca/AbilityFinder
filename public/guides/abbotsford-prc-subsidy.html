PRC Subsidy Program (Abbotsford)

A recreation pass for Abbotsford recreation centres and 25% off some registered programs. It is for full-time Abbotsford residents whose family net income is under the City's low-income cutoff for their household size.

What it is

The subsidy gives lower-income Abbotsford households a recreation pass and discounts on some programs.

  • A recreation pass to City of Abbotsford recreation centres.
  • 25% off group swim and skate lessons; private lessons are excluded.
  • 25% off some neighbourhood and summer camps for ages 3 to 12, and other specified programs.
  • Net income limit: $22,361 for 1 person (from $5,000), $27,214 for 2, $33,888 for 3.
  • Net income limit: $42,278 for 4, $48,142 for 5, $53,390 for 6, $58,639 for 7 or more.

Good to know

The program runs May 1 to April 30 and you re-apply each year. Group swim and skate lessons qualify, but private lessons do not. Some camps and other programs qualify, and the list is updated each year.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You are a full-time Abbotsford resident
  • Your family net income after tax is within the City's low-income cutoff chart

Income proof is usually your 2025 Notice of Assessment (line 236); other proofs are listed.

Amount or value

Recreation pass plus 25% off listed programs

How to apply

  1. Compare your family's after-tax net income with the City's cutoff chart
  2. Fill in the application form with details for everyone in your household
  3. Take the form and your documents to City Hall, the Abbotsford Recreation Centre or the Matsqui Recreation Centre
  4. Wait for an email or letter telling you if you were approved
  5. If approved, go to the Abbotsford or Matsqui Recreation Centre to get your pass
  6. Re-apply each year

What you may need

  • Proof of Abbotsford residency dated in the last 3 months, such as a utility bill, a letter from your bank or a rental agreement
  • Valid government photo ID, such as a driver's licence, BC ID or passport
  • One of these as proof of income: a 2025 Notice of Assessment for every adult 19 or older, an Independent Youth Letter, or a letter from a Registered Social Worker
  • Canada Child Benefit Notice listing all dependent children, if children are on the application

Practical tips

  • A Tax Notice Summary is not accepted. You need the Notice of Assessment.
  • The City does not accept faxed or emailed applications.
  • New immigrants must visit Archway Community Services for an application form.
  • If you are denied, you get a letter in the mail with the reasons.
