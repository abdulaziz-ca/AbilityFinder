Specialized Access Program (Abbotsford)

Half-price drop-in admissions and passes at Abbotsford recreation centres. It is for full-time Abbotsford residents whose permanent disability a physician confirms. Support workers who are needed get in free.

What it is

This program lowers recreation centre costs for Abbotsford residents with permanent disabilities.

  • 50% off recreation centre drop-in admissions.
  • 50% off recreation centre passes.
  • Support workers who are required to assist you are admitted at no charge.

Good to know

There is no income test on the page. A physician completes a section of the form, so no medical copies are needed. The page does not give a renewal period.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You are a full-time Abbotsford resident
  • You have a permanent disability confirmed by a physician

Amount or value

50% off recreation centre drop-in admissions and passes

How to apply

  1. Download and complete the application form, and review the FAQ
  2. Ask a physician to complete the disability section of the form
  3. Take the form and your documents to City Hall, the Abbotsford Recreation Centre or the Matsqui Recreation Centre
  4. Wait for an email or letter telling you if you were approved
  5. Go to the Abbotsford or Matsqui Recreation Centre to get your Specialized Access Card

What you may need

  • Proof of permanent disability confirmed by a physician, on the application form only; no copies required
  • Proof of Abbotsford residency dated in the last 3 months, such as a utility bill, a letter from your bank or a rental agreement
  • Valid government photo ID of the applicant, or of the legal guardian if the applicant is a minor

Practical tips

  • The City does not accept faxed or emailed applications.
  • If you are denied, you get a letter in the mail with the reasons.
