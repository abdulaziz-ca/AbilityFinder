Accessible (Disabled) Parking Permit

A placard and permit for designated disabled parking spaces. It is for people with a significant mobility impairment.

What it is

The permit is for one person only.

  • It is not transferable and may not be loaned to anyone.
  • The placard must be clearly displayed in the vehicle.
  • The person it was issued for must be getting out of the vehicle at that stop.
  • You must obey all general parking laws.

Good to know

Apply at a Service New Brunswick centre. A physician, occupational therapist, nurse practitioner or physiotherapist must sign the application.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You have a significant mobility impairment, such as paralysis, lower limb amputation, or heart or lung disease.
  • This includes other health problems that make walking unassisted over 50 m in outdoor weather difficult.

Amount or value

No charge for the placard and permit

How to apply

  1. Download the application form (PDF).
  2. Ask your health professional to sign it.
  3. Take it to a Service New Brunswick centre.

What you may need

  • Application form signed by a health professional

Practical tips

  • Return the placard to Service New Brunswick if it is expired, no longer needed, or defaced.
