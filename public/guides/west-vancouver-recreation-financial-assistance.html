Recreation Financial Assistance Program (West Vancouver)

West Vancouver residents with low income and no significant savings or assets can get 50% off FitPasses and activities. Enhance West Van and Seniors' Activity Centre memberships are 100% subsidized.

What it is

A subsidy on community centre recreation.

  • 50% off FitPasses, up to a maximum per subsidy year.
  • 50% off activities, up to a maximum per subsidy year.

Good to know

FitPass subsidy is up to $362 per subsidy year, and activities up to $400. Birthday parties, personal training, private lessons and events are not eligible; other exclusions may apply. Review can take two to six weeks.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You are a West Vancouver resident, with proof for all family members
  • You are low income without significant savings or assets

Details are in the Qualifying for Financial Assistance handout.

Amount or value

50% off FitPasses and activities; some memberships free

How to apply

  1. Read the information handout to confirm eligibility
  2. Create a recreation account at activewestvanrec
  3. Complete and sign the RFAP application form
  4. Drop off the sealed package at the West Vancouver Community Centre front desk
  5. Register for an RFAP interview at drop-off

What you may need

  • The signed and dated application form
  • Supporting documents listed in the handout, including recent tax information
  • Copies of EI documents, if you received EI

Practical tips

  • Interview registration is not available online.
  • Do not email supporting documents.
  • Documents not collected within six weeks of your interview are shredded.

Timing

Two to six weeks to review
