Yorkton Access Transit

A shared door-to-door ride service for people with disabilities who cannot use regular public transportation. SaskAbilities runs it in Yorkton.

What it is

The City offers this service with SaskAbilities.

  • It helps you travel safely and independently within Yorkton.

Good to know

You must register ahead of time. The page does not give Access Transit fares. Yorkton transit is due to change in January 2027.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You have a disability
  • You cannot use regular public transportation
  • You register in advance

What this guide can't tell you: whether you will be approved. The program decides, often using details we do not ask for.
