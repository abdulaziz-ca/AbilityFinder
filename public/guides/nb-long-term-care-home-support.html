Long-Term Care Program (home support)

Seniors who need long-term help with daily activities may be eligible for subsidized services in their own home, a special care home or a nursing home.

What it is

Services include:

  • Home support helps with personal care, housekeeping and meal preparation at home.
  • Adult day centres give caregiver relief and social activities; seniors normally pay a small fee.
  • Special care homes give supervision and help with daily living.
  • Nursing homes are for people who are medically stable but need full-time nursing care.
  • Relief care gives families and caregivers a break, at home or in a care home.

Good to know

Needs are assessed by the Department of Social Development. Services are not covered by Medicare, and your income affects what you pay.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You are 65 or older.
  • You need help with daily activities, such as mobility, bathing or preparing meals, on a long-term basis.

An assessment of your needs comes first; the page says you may be eligible.

What this guide can't tell you: whether you will be approved. The program decides, often using details we do not ask for.

Amount or value

Subsidized services; what you pay depends on your income

How to apply

  1. Contact the Department of Social Development to ask for an assessment of your needs.
  2. Someone you trust can be with you during the assessment.
  3. The government may give financial help to people who cannot pay the full cost.

Practical tips

  • Receiving long-term care services is voluntary. It is your choice.
  • To order the Going to a Nursing Home booklet, call the Public Legal Education and Information Service of New Brunswick at (506) 453-5369.
