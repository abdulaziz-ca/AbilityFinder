Brandon Access Transit

Door-to-door rides in Brandon for people with disabilities who cannot use regular Brandon Transit. Fares are the same as regular transit.

Manitoba is new here: please double-check before you rely on a detail

We added Manitoba in October 2026, while some of its programs are changing. Each program here comes from its official page, but some details are not published yet, so confirm with the program before you count on an amount or rule.

  • Manitoba is moving people with a disability from Employment and Income Assistance (EIA) to the new Manitoba Supports for Persons with Disabilities program, and that program's page does not publish payment amounts yet.
  • Since September 1, 2026, EIA covers only emergency dental care and points people to the federal Canadian Dental Care Plan.
  • Some income limits are old (home adaptation limits are from 2019) or change every year (Rent Assist and Winnipeg programs).
  • Winnipeg Transit Plus has proposed new eligibility rules that are not in effect.
  • City programs are listed for Winnipeg only so far.

What it is

Access Transit gives you:

  • Door-to-door transportation within the City of Brandon.
  • Pick-up and drop-off at accessible building entrances.
  • Call back service for medical appointments only.
  • A 24-hour message service for cancellations.

Good to know

A doctor must sign the application. Incomplete forms are returned without processing.

What you must meet

The program requires at least one of these:

  • You need a mobility aid to perform daily tasks.
  • You have a visual impairment that stops you reading signs.
  • You cannot safely step up or down a 40 cm (16-inch) step alone.
  • You cannot walk 200 m (660 feet) from home to your destination alone.

You must be unable to use regular Brandon Transit services.

What this guide can't tell you: whether you will be approved. The program decides, often using details we do not ask for.

How to apply

  1. Fill in the application form (Form 1 of 2).
  2. Ask your Medical Doctor to complete and sign the assessment form (Form 2 of 2).
  3. Email both forms to accesstransit@brandon.ca, which is preferred.
  4. Or drop them at 723 Rosser Ave Unit A or City Hall, or mail them to 410 9th Street, Brandon MB R7A 6A2.

What you may need

  • Brandon Access Transit Application Form (Form 1/2)
  • Brandon Access Transit Assessment Form (Form 2/2), signed by your Medical Doctor

Practical tips

  • Both forms must be completed; incomplete forms are returned without processing.
  • Book return trips when you book your ride, and be ready 15 minutes before pick-up.
  • Trips are booked Monday to Friday, 6:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m.
