Provincial Home Repair Program

A forgivable loan for low-income homeowners to fix safety problems in their homes so they can stay in them longer.

What it is

Minor repairs that could threaten safety or force you to leave your home are eligible, in these areas:

  • Structural, heating and roofing repairs.
  • Water, electrical and septic repairs.
  • Energy efficiency repairs.

Good to know

Changed this fall; it now includes the former Home Energy Savings Program. It is for people who are not NLHC clients.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You own your home and have lived in it for at least five years.
  • Your combined yearly income is under the limit for your area.
  • You are not an NLHC client.

What this guide can't tell you: whether you will be approved. The program decides, often using details we do not ask for.

Amount or value

Forgivable loan up to $10,000 ($13,000 in Labrador)

How to apply

  1. Pick your nearest NLHC regional office and apply online, or print the form.
  2. NLHC gives the funding as a forgivable loan.
  3. See the fact sheet on the page for the full list of repairs.

What you may need

  • The page does not list documents.

Practical tips

  • Income limit is under $42,500 a year, or $65,000 in Labrador.
  • The loan is forgiven if you stay in the home for five years.
  • If you move out after three or four years, you may repay part of it.
  • You can apply again if you were funded more than five years ago.
  • The lifetime maximum is $30,000. No pre- or post-inspections are required.
