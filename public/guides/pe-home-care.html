Prince Edward Island · Home support

Home Care Program

Health care and support services in your own home, so you can live safely and with independence for as long as possible.

What it is

Home Care can include:

  • Nursing care such as health monitoring, injections and wound care.
  • Home support with daily activities such as bathing and dressing.
  • Physiotherapy and occupational therapy for daily living, devices and home changes.
  • Dietitian services, social work and palliative care.
  • Assessment for long-term care if you need a high level of nursing care.

Good to know

Services are based on assessed need for a defined period of time.

What you must meet

The program requires at least one of these:

  • You need support to keep living safely at home.
  • You left hospital and are recovering from surgery or an acute condition.
  • You have an ongoing health issue needing more care than you or family can give.

Services are provided based on assessed need.

Amount or value

Nursing, home support and therapy, for a set period of time

How to apply

  1. Fill out the Home Care Referral Form.
  2. Send it to your local Home Care office.
  3. Staff assess your needs; services are based on assessed need.

What you may need
