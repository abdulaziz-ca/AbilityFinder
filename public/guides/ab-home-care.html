Alberta · Home support

Home and Community Care (continuing care at home)

Health and personal care at home for people of any age, including help with dressing, bathing, meals and wound care. It also offers respite for caregivers.

What it is

Services can include help with

  • Dressing, eating and bathing.
  • Meal preparation and medication administration.
  • Wound care and respite.
  • Short-term care after an accident, injury or illness, or long-term care for disease, disability or aging.

Good to know

Services are meant to add to help from family, friends and community, not replace it. Day program clients pay a daily fee.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You live in Alberta.
  • A professional assessment finds that you have unmet care needs.

Age, diagnosis and how long you need support do not decide who can get services.

Amount or value

Publicly funded health and personal care. You pay for medications, nutrition products and long-term supplies.

Practical tips

  • Providers are Assisted Living Alberta, contracted providers, or someone you hire under self-managed care.
  • Programs such as Alberta Aids to Daily Living (AADL) may help pay for basic medical equipment and supplies.
  • Palliative care coverage may help with drugs, diabetic supplies and ambulance if you are diagnosed as palliative.
