Newfoundland and Labrador · Housing

Canada-NL Housing Benefit

A monthly rent benefit for households in severe core housing need. It can move with you if you stay in good standing with your landlord and the new home passes an NLHC inspection.

What it is

How the benefit works:

  • The benefit is paid to you, and you pay your landlord.
  • With your consent, NLHC can pay the landlord directly.
  • You can choose where to live, if the home passes an NLHC fire and life safety inspection.
  • The benefit is the gap between what you can pay and the lower of actual rent or average market rent.

Good to know

From an older NLHC question-and-answer sheet (uploaded 2023), so figures and availability may be out of date. There is no separate application; apply through the Rental Housing Program.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • Your household is in severe core housing need.
  • You may be eligible if your total household net income is at or under the limit for your area.

Selection depends on application date, waitlist codes and availability, and NLHC may offer a rental unit instead.

What this guide can't tell you: whether you will be approved. The program decides, often using details we do not ask for.

Amount or value

Monthly rent help; the amount depends on household income

How to apply

  1. Apply for the Rental Housing Program.
  2. If approved, find a home yourself or ask NLHC for a list of interested landlords.
  3. NLHC inspects the home before you move in.
  4. Get a transfer approved and the new unit inspected before any later move.

What you may need

  • The sheet does not list documents; NLHC uses your application information.

Practical tips

  • Income limit is $32,500 or less, or $65,000 in Labrador West and the North Coast of Labrador.
  • Your share of rent is 25 per cent of net income.
  • The sheet's example: net income of $20,000 and rent of $820 gives a monthly benefit of about $404.
  • Rent Supplement clients were to be converted to this program as agreements expire, 2023 through 2026.
  • Check current figures, and whether the benefit is open, with NLHC; this sheet was uploaded in 2023.
