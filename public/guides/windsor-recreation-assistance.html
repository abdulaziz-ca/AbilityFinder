90% Recreation Discount (Windsor)

The Pathway to Potential Recreation Assistance Program's 90% Recreation Discount subsidizes eligible Windsor-Essex residents for 90% off eligible recreation program registration fees before taxes.

What it is

The 90% Recreation Discount lowers eligible program registration fees for approved Windsor-Essex residents.

  • 90% off the program registration fee before taxes.
  • Funding covers program registration fees, but not equipment or material costs.
  • It extends to eligible programs and services delivered directly by municipal recreation departments and approved service organizations within Windsor-Essex County.
  • Payment is issued directly to the program provider, or you can request reimbursement if registration fees were paid in advance.
  • Program eligibility may differ across municipalities; contact your local municipal recreation department.

Good to know

Program eligibility may differ across municipalities. Apply or ask questions through your local municipal recreation department. Funding is first-come, first-served, and all approvals are determined by the local recreation department subject to local demands and budgets.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • Residents who have lived within Windsor-Essex County for at least 30 days and who are able to demonstrate residency
  • A combined household income that is at, or below, the Market Basket Measure (MBM) thresholds
  • A sustained sport, physical, cultural or artistic program — the City says these should run at least five weeks with at least one session per week — or a day camp or specialty camp, which must last at least five consecutive days for consideration

The programme page does not list the MBM thresholds; for further details it directs you to contact your local municipal recreation department. Competitive activities, travel teams, fitness centre orientations, personal training and private lessons are not eligible. Program eligibility may differ across municipalities.

Amount or value

90% off eligible program registration fees, before taxes

How to apply

  1. Ask your local municipal recreation department for the Market Basket Measure threshold for your family size — the programme page does not list it
  2. Check that the activity meets the duration rules and is not one of the excluded activities
  3. If a service organization runs the program, ask your local recreation department whether that organization is approved
  4. Gather your proof of residency and your combined-household-income documents — you must also have lived within Windsor-Essex County for at least 30 days
  5. Apply through your local municipal recreation department; funding is available on a first-come, first-served basis
  6. Use approved funding within the calendar year, and tell your municipality about changes to your financial, residency or other circumstances within 30 days of the change taking effect

What you may need

  • Preferred proof of income: a Canada Child Benefit notice, a Notice of Assessment, or proof of Ontario Works or Ontario Disability Support Program benefits. The Canada Child Benefit notice is preferred for 90% Recreation Discount applicants
  • One proof of residency: Ontario Driver's Licence, Ontario Health Card, Ontario Photo Card, current utility bill, property tax bill, lease or rental agreement, mortgage statement, or current bank statement
  • Additional documentation may be requested
  • If you cannot provide the required documents, or your circumstances recently changed in a way they do not reflect, a Letter of Endorsement may be used. It should be on professional letterhead, include the referring agency's contact information and the client's full name, outline the benefits the subsidy would provide, and indicate the barriers preventing application. Acceptance is at the discretion of the municipality

Practical tips

  • Funding is allocated to sustained sport, physical, cultural or artistic programs, which the City says should run a minimum of five weeks with at least one session each week. Day camps and specialty camps must last at least five consecutive days for consideration.
  • Competitive activities, travel teams, fitness centre orientations, personal training and private lessons are not eligible.
  • Funding is available per eligible person per calendar year, and approved funding must be used within that calendar year. It is not transferable from one year to the next.
  • All approvals are determined by the local recreation department and are subject to local demands and budgets.
  • Eligibility is non-transferable.
  • Ask your local recreation department whether a service organization is approved before registering.
