EquiPass — OC Transpo low-income discount (Ottawa)

A discounted OC Transpo pass for Ottawa residents whose family income is below the federal low income threshold, provided they do not already receive transportation benefits from Social Services.

What it is

EquiPass is OC Transpo's low-income fare discount.

  • $58.25 for a monthly pass, about half the $138.50 adult pass.
  • $1.75 per ride, against $4.10 for an adult.
  • You must not be receiving transportation benefits from Social Services.
  • You must use a Presto card — discounted fares are not available when paying cash.

Good to know

You cannot get EquiPass if you receive transportation benefits from Social Services. If you receive ODSP, look at the Community Pass instead — it is cheaper at $43.25 and includes Para Transpo.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • Your family income is below the low income threshold set by the Government of Canada
  • You do NOT receive transportation benefits from Social Services

You apply with a completed EquiPass Application and Consent Form plus a Notice of Assessment from the Canada Revenue Agency for the most recent taxation year. If you do not have a Notice of Assessment, other documentation can be used instead.

Amount or value

$58.25 a month, or $1.75 a ride — about half an adult pass

How to apply

  1. Complete the EquiPass Application and Consent Form
  2. Include your Canada Revenue Agency Notice of Assessment for the most recent taxation year, or other accepted documentation if you do not have one
  3. Set the EquiPass discount at an OC Transpo Customer Service Centre, or by calling OC Transpo Customer Service
  4. Buy the monthly pass up to 14 days before the month begins

What you may need

  • A completed EquiPass Application and Consent Form
  • A Canada Revenue Agency Notice of Assessment for the most recent taxation year, or other accepted documentation

Practical tips

  • If you receive ODSP, the Community Pass is cheaper at $43.25 and covers Para Transpo without a top-up.
  • A lawyer, notary, registered social worker or a member of a regulated health college can act as a guarantor on the application.
  • Paying per ride with Presto is capped at $12.25 a day and at the monthly pass price over a month.
