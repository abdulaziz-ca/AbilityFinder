Saskatchewan · Health (for children)

Family Health Benefits

Health benefits for low-income working families who pass an income test. They help pay for several health services for children and some for parents.

What it is

Children are covered for most dental services and more.

  • Children get eye exams once a year and basic eyeglasses.
  • Children get emergency ambulance, basic medical supplies and Saskatchewan Formulary drugs.
  • Parents get an eye exam every two years.
  • Parents have drug coverage with a $100 semi-annual family deductible and 35 per cent co-payment.

Good to know

The Ministry of Social Services decides eligibility. Call for a pre-assessment.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You are a low-income working family.
  • You meet the standards of an income test.

The page does not list income limits.

Amount or value

Children's dental, eye care, drugs; limited cover for parents

How to apply

  1. Call the Ministry of Social Services to complete a pre-assessment.
  2. Present your Saskatchewan Health Services card for any health service.

What you may need

  • Saskatchewan Health Services card

Practical tips

  • Some basic medical supplies for children need prior approval.
  • Parents or legal guardians may apply for more cover under the Special Support program.
