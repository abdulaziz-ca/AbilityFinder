Recreation Program Subsidy (Peterborough)

A yearly subsidy toward registration for sports, camps and recreation programs in Peterborough. People with a low income or on Ontario Works or ODSP can apply. Your income decides if you get $100 or $200.

What it is

The subsidy is paid straight to the program provider, or refunded if you already paid.

  • Children aged 17 and under can get up to $200 a year each.
  • Adults can get up to $200 a year for programs at City facilities.
  • Sports leagues, camps, dance, martial arts and skating lessons count.
  • You can apply for several programs and people at once.

Good to know

It covers registration fees only, not equipment or after-school programs. Programs outside City limits are not covered.

What you must meet

The program requires at least one of these:

  • You live in Peterborough and your household income is under the low-income cut-off chart
  • You live in Peterborough and receive Ontario Works or ODSP

The limit is $39,668 for a household of up to five for the full $200, and $39,669 to $43,993 for $100.

What this guide can't tell you: whether you will be approved. The program decides, often using details we do not ask for.

How to apply

  1. Check your household income against the chart.
  2. Upload your documents in the online application.
  3. Register for your programs, then pay anything above your subsidy.
  4. For other groups, give them the Verification of Subsidy letter.

What you may need

  • Notice of Assessment or Canada Child Tax Benefit statement for each adult, since pay stubs are not accepted
  • Statement of Assistance for anyone on Ontario Works or ODSP
  • Proof of a City of Peterborough address
  • The program name and start date

Practical tips

  • To be refunded, apply within 30 days of paying.
  • Staff can help with your application by email or phone.
