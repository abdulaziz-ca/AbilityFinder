Manitoba Supports for Persons with Disabilities (MSPD)

A Manitoba program for people with severe and prolonged disabilities who are financially eligible for income assistance. It started April 1, 2023.

Manitoba is new here: please double-check before you rely on a detail

We added Manitoba in October 2026, while some of its programs are changing. Each program here comes from its official page, but some details are not published yet, so confirm with the program before you count on an amount or rule.

  • Manitoba is moving people with a disability from Employment and Income Assistance (EIA) to the new Manitoba Supports for Persons with Disabilities program, and that program's page does not publish payment amounts yet.
  • Since September 1, 2026, EIA covers only emergency dental care and points people to the federal Canadian Dental Care Plan.
  • Some income limits are old (home adaptation limits are from 2019) or change every year (Rent Assist and Winnipeg programs).
  • Winnipeg Transit Plus has proposed new eligibility rules that are not in effect.
  • City programs are listed for Winnipeg only so far.

What it is

MSPD is a Manitoba program for people living with severe and prolonged disabilities.

  • The page says benefits and supports come in two types of assistance.
  • Some people are welcomed into the program right away.
  • Others apply, and staff decide whether all requirements are met.
  • Decisions can be appealed to the Social Services Appeal Board.

Good to know

The pages we read give no dollar amounts. Some people are instantly eligible. Others apply and are assessed. Decisions can be appealed.

What you must meet

The program requires at least one of these:

  • You have a severe, prolonged disability that limits daily living and qualify financially for income assistance.
  • Instant: EIA Disability (now Medical Barriers to Full Employment) plus a qualifying disability support.
  • Supports: Community Living disABILITY Services, a personal care home, or CPP Disability benefits.
  • Other Manitobans with severe and prolonged disabilities may apply and be assessed.

If you apply, staff look at your financial situation and the unique impact of your disability.

Amount or value

Not published on these pages. The page lists two types of assistance.
