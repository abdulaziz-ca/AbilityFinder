Moose Jaw Paratransit Service

A shared ride service from one accessible door to another. It is for people who cannot use regular transit because of a physical or mental challenge.

What it is

Paratransit is a shared ride service, not a taxi.

  • Drivers help you between the van and the door.
  • It does not run on Sundays or statutory holidays.
  • Regular subscription trips are available for work, school or medical visits.

Good to know

You must apply and be approved first. Regular tickets and passes do not work on Paratransit, so ask for Paratransit ones.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You live in Moose Jaw, or you are a registered visitor
  • A physical or mental challenge stops you using the regular bus
  • You complete an application form and are approved

Visitors can also register.

Application
Fill in a form and mail or email it, then wait for a decision.

How to apply

  1. Get the application form online, at City Hall or at the Kinsmen Sportsplex
  2. Send it to Moose Jaw Transit, 228 Main Street North, or email transit@moosejaw.ca
  3. Wait for the written decision, then book your rides

What you may need

  • The completed application form.

Practical tips

  • Eligibility is decided in consultation with professionals you designate.
  • Cancel a trip at least 24 hours ahead, or you may be charged.

Timing

Decision within 21 days of a complete application.
