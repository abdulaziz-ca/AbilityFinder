Developmental Services Ontario adult services

Developmental Services Ontario (DSO) is the way in to provincially funded services for adults with a developmental disability. Staff help you apply, assess your needs and connect you with available supports.

What it is

This page explains the services and supports for adults with a developmental disability and their families.

  • Helps you live, work and take part in your community.
  • Includes Passport funding, specialized clinical supports and Adult Protective Service Worker help.
  • Includes residential supports such as group living, supported independent living and the Host Family Program.
  • You can use part of your Passport funding for person-directed planning.

Good to know

Going through the application does not guarantee that you will receive services right away. Your risk-based priority is compared with everyone else waiting.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You have a developmental disability, confirmed by a registered psychologist or psychological associate.
  • You are at least 18 years old.
  • You live in Ontario.

These general rules come from the 2008 Act on social inclusion of people with developmental disabilities.

What this guide can't tell you: whether you will be approved. The program decides, often using details we do not ask for.

How to apply

  1. Contact your local Developmental Services Ontario office to apply.
  2. Meet with a DSO assessor for the application for developmental services and supports (ADSS).
  3. Meet again for the Supports Intensity Scale for adults (SIS-A), which measures the supports you need.
  4. Staff use a province-wide tool to score your level of risk and set your priority for services.

What you may need

  • Confirmation of your developmental disability from a registered psychologist or psychological associate

Practical tips

  • Each of the two meetings may take a couple of hours.
  • Family caregivers can share their needs in the application.
  • Private providers are not government-funded; check what they offer and charge before you choose.
