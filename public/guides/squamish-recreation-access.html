Recreation Access Program (Squamish)

Squamish residents in financial need get 50% off recreation services and registered programs. The card is valid for one year.

Good to know

Gross household income must be under the limit for your size (from $26,570 for one person, 2024 form). The form also mentions a minimum; ask the District.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You live in the District of Squamish
  • Your household income is under the limit for your household size
  • You have the original required documents

The page lists limits from $26,570 for one person to $70,298 for seven.

Amount or value

50% off recreation services and registered programs

How to apply

  1. Check the self-assessment on the form
  2. Complete the application form
  3. Bring it with your documents to Brennan Park Recreation Centre

What you may need

  • Government-issued ID for the main applicant
  • For each adult, a Notice of Assessment, proof of income, or an income assistance or disability statement
  • Proof of residency such as a lease or Canada Child Benefit notice

Practical tips

  • Approved agencies can take your application, including PearlSpace and Sea to Sky Community Services.
  • Staff can help if you lack the documents.
