North Battleford · Transit

Battlefords Handi-Bus

On-demand transportation for people who cannot use the regular transit service because of a disability. It runs Monday to Friday.

What it is

What the service is:

  • A specialized on-demand service for people with disabilities.
  • Jointly funded by North Battleford and the Town of Battleford.

Good to know

The page does not state a fare or a registration process.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You have a disability.
  • Because of it, you cannot use the regular transit service.
