Alberta Child Health Benefit

Health coverage for children under 18 in lower-income families. There is also a conditional route for 18- and 19-year-olds still in high school through grade 12.

What it is

No-premium prescription, dental, optical and other eligible health coverage for children under 18 in lower-income Alberta families. A child aged 18 or 19 can qualify only while living at home and attending high school through grade 12, with declaration AEHB3654.

Good to know

The applicant and family members must live in Alberta and be Canadian citizens or permanent residents. Income Support, AISH, and Non-Insured Health Benefits are excluded government coverage. Private plans are used first. They are not exclusions. For services covered by both ACHB and the Canadian Dental Care Plan, claims go to the Canadian Dental Care Plan first.

What this guide can't tell you: whether you will be approved. The program decides, often using details we do not ask for.

Amount or value

Covers eligible prescription drugs, basic and preventative dental care and eyeglasses for children in low-income households

How to apply

  1. Check the income guidelines and confirm the whole family's Alberta residency, citizenship or permanent-resident status, and other government health coverage.
  2. Complete the Alberta Child Health Benefit application.
  3. For an eligible 18- or 19-year-old living at home and attending high school through grade 12, also submit Declaration of 18 and 19 Year Old Dependent AEHB3654: https://cfr.forms.gov.ab.ca/Form/AEHB3654
  4. Submit the application with proof of income.

What you may need

  • Proof of family income
  • Your children's Alberta Health Care numbers
  • Declaration AEHB3654 for an eligible 18- or 19-year-old dependent

Practical tips

  • Income Support, AISH, and Non-Insured Health Benefits for First Nations and Inuit are excluded government health-benefit coverage.
  • Private or other health insurance is not an exclusion. Use it first. ACHB may cover remaining eligible costs.
  • If you also have the Canadian Dental Care Plan, claims for services covered by both plans go to the Canadian Dental Care Plan first. ACHB may then cover remaining eligible dental costs.

Timing

A few weeks to process.
