Accessible Parking Permit

A free permit that lets you park in zones marked with the blue wheelchair logo. You can ask for a temporary or a permanent permit.

What it is

Good to know about the permit:

  • It can be used in any vehicle where you are a passenger.
  • It cannot be loaned or transferred, and using one not issued to you is illegal.
  • Show it in the windshield with the number and expiry date visible.
  • Only one permit is issued per person.

Good to know

The permit goes to the person who needs it, not to a vehicle. You do not need a driver's licence or a vehicle.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • A doctor or nurse practitioner fills out your first application.
  • More medical information may be asked for to decide your application.

The page does not list medical conditions; the Registrar decides how long the permit lasts.

What this guide can't tell you: whether you will be approved. The program decides, often using details we do not ask for.

How to apply

  1. Get the application from a Motor Registration office or the website.
  2. Ask a doctor or nurse practitioner to fill it out the first time.
  3. Email it, fax it, or mail or drop it at any Motor Registration Office.
  4. Receive the permit by mail, or ask to pick it up at an office.

What you may need

  • Accessible Parking Permit Application completed by a doctor or nurse practitioner.

Practical tips

  • A temporary permit is valid for six months and a permanent one for five years; the Registrar decides the length.
  • Processing takes 5 to 10 business days, and may change with volume.
  • Renew a permanent permit online at MyGovNL; no new doctor visit is needed.
  • A temporary permit cannot be renewed; send a new application.
  • Email accessparkingpermits@gov.nl.ca or fax 709-729-4360.
