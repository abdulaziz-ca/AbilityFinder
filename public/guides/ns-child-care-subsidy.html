Nova Scotia Child Care Subsidy

The subsidy pays a portion of child care fees at licensed facilities, approved family home agencies and approved Before and After Programs.

What it is

The subsidy lowers what you pay for child care.

  • A caseworker assesses your application within 8 weeks.
  • An approved subsidy is backdated to the day your complete application arrived.
  • You can move the subsidy if your child changes approved location.

Good to know

The Department of Education and Early Childhood Development pays the subsidy directly to your child care provider.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You have a child 12 years old or younger in an approved child care setting.
  • Your assessed family income is under $70,000.

Your eligibility is reviewed at least once every year.

Amount or value

Pays part of your child care fees

How to apply

  1. Gather your documents and your partner or spouse's documents.
  2. Fill in the online application, which takes under 60 minutes.
  3. A caseworker may contact you with questions.
  4. The caseworker tells you when a decision is made.

What you may need

  • Most recent Notice of Assessment
  • Proof of residency status, if not a citizen
  • Pay slips or an employment letter, if you work
  • Proof of school enrollment, if a student
  • Custody agreement, if you share custody

Practical tips

  • The form is in English, so ask for help if you need it.
  • Each screen times out after 30 minutes.
  • Tell your caseworker about changes to income, address or custody.
  • Not reporting changes can mean repayment or a cancelled subsidy.
