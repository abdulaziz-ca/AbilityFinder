Recreation Fee Assistance Program (Winnipeg)

Winnipeg residents and newcomers with a family income below the low income cut-off can get a facility pass, a drop-in pass and an activity credit.

Manitoba is new here: please double-check before you rely on a detail

We added Manitoba in October 2026, while some of its programs are changing. Each program here comes from its official page, but some details are not published yet, so confirm with the program before you count on an amount or rule.

  • Manitoba is moving people with a disability from Employment and Income Assistance (EIA) to the new Manitoba Supports for Persons with Disabilities program, and that program's page does not publish payment amounts yet.
  • Since September 1, 2026, EIA covers only emergency dental care and points people to the federal Canadian Dental Care Plan.
  • Some income limits are old (home adaptation limits are from 2019) or change every year (Rent Assist and Winnipeg programs).
  • Winnipeg Transit Plus has proposed new eligibility rules that are not in effect.
  • City programs are listed for Winnipeg only so far.

What it is

You can get three types of benefit:

  • A facility pass for unlimited admission, on a rechargeable 10-visit pass.
  • A 10-visit pass for drop-in active living programs such as Zumba and Aquafit.
  • A $300 credit per person for registered Leisure Guide activities.
  • The credit cannot buy admissions, memberships, rentals, gift cards or POS items.

Good to know

The activity credit is valid for 12 months from approval and has no cash value.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You are a City of Winnipeg resident or a newcomer.
  • Your economic family income is below the low income cut-off (LICO).

Limits for 1, 2 and 3 people: $31,264, $38,922 and $47,851 (larger sizes on the page).

What this guide can't tell you: whether you will be approved. The program decides, often using details we do not ask for.

How to apply

  1. Fill out the Recreation Fee Assistance Program application and list your whole economic family.
  2. Check off the benefits you want.
  3. Add your supporting document and a daytime phone number.
  4. Send it by email to RecFeeAssist@winnipeg.ca, by mail, or in person at 395 Main St.
  5. If approved, finish setting up your leisureONLINE account.

What you may need

  • Application form (PDF)
  • An Employment and Income Assistance (EIA) case number
  • Or your most recent Canada Revenue Agency Notice of Assessment
  • Or a CPP Disability monthly statement of income
  • Or documents showing your landing date in Canada and UCI number

Practical tips

  • Apply before the Leisure Guide registration dates if you want the credit.
  • If approved, you register for activities yourself.
  • A social worker or support worker may be able to give you a referral.
  • You can also apply in person at any indoor pool or fitness and leisure centre.
  • The page says other considerations may be made for special circumstances.
