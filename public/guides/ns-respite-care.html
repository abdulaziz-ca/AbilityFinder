Nova Scotia · Home support

Facility Based Respite Care

Family caregivers can book a short stay for the person they care for in a licensed long-term care facility. Staff give care and meals during the stay.

What it is

Respite gives caregivers a planned break.

  • A stay can total up to 60 days within a calendar year.
  • From July 1 to October 1, no more than 30 days can be scheduled.
  • Beds are given on a first-come, first-served basis.

Good to know

There is a standard charge. You can ask for a reduced rate with an application and an income assessment.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • The person needs personal care and their regular caregiver is unavailable.
  • A Care Coordinator assesses their care needs and eligibility.

Emergency stays are possible if a caregiver is suddenly lost and no replacement is available.

Amount or value

Up to 60 days a year in a respite bed

How to apply

  1. Call Continuing Care to apply for a respite bed.
  2. A Care Coordinator checks care needs and eligibility.
  3. The Care Coordinator arranges the bed once eligibility is confirmed.

What you may need

  • Application form for a reduced rate

Practical tips

  • Call for the current charge, because it may change.
  • In an emergency, if necessary, a person can be admitted before the application is finished, which must then be completed right after.
  • There is also an option available for children.
