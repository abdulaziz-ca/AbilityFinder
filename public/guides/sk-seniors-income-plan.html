Seniors Income Plan (SIP)

A monthly benefit for seniors who have little or no income other than Old Age Security and the Guaranteed Income Supplement. No application is needed.

What it is

SIP tops up income for seniors who rely on OAS and GIS.

  • A single person at home gets up to $360 a month (rates effective July 1, 2023).
  • A married couple, both on OAS/GIS, gets up to $325 a month (rates effective July 1, 2023).
  • In a special care home, the maximum is $50 a month (rates effective July 1, 2023).
  • SIP ends at $4,560 yearly taxable income, not counting OAS/GIS, for a single person at home (Oct 1, 2026).

Good to know

Income cut-off levels are as of October 1, 2026. Maximum rates are effective July 1, 2023.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You are 65 or older and a permanent resident of Saskatchewan.
  • You receive full or partial OAS pension and GIS.
  • Your annual income is below a specified level.

Assets, OAS and GIS are not counted when judging eligibility.

What this guide can't tell you: whether you will be approved. The program decides, often using details we do not ask for.

Amount or value

Up to $360 a month at home (rates effective July 1, 2023)

How to apply

  1. File your income tax return each year.
  2. Apply for OAS and GIS through Service Canada.
  3. SIP is paid automatically if you qualify.

What you may need

  • Income tax return
  • OAS/GIS application

Practical tips

  • If you get the maximum GIS, you also get the maximum SIP.
  • More income lowers your GIS and SIP.
  • Eligibility is reassessed each year in July.
  • SIP clients with Saskatchewan Health coverage get extra health benefits.
  • Status Indian clients get health coverage from Health Canada.
