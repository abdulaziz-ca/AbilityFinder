Nova Scotia · Housing

Public Housing (Nova Scotia Provincial Housing Agency)

Rent-geared-to-income homes for households under the income limits for their area. Most pay 30% of income before taxes; a household with an adult and a dependent pays 25%.

What it is

Good to know

  • You can apply anywhere in Nova Scotia, not only where you live.
  • Some applicants can qualify for priority access with a signed referral form.
  • Income limits are set by the federal government through CMHC.

Good to know

Income Assistance recipients pay different rent rates. Contact the agency for details.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • Your gross household income is below the limit for your area and bedrooms.
  • Everyone in your household is a Canadian citizen or permanent resident.
  • You can live independently.
  • You live in Nova Scotia now.
  • Past tenants with arrears agree to a repayment plan.

Owners of suitable housing may qualify if they agree to sell within one year.

Amount or value

Rent is 25% or 30% of household income before taxes; Income Assistance rates differ

How to apply

  1. Check the income limit for your area and bedrooms.
  2. Apply through the online portal or at any District Office.
  3. Update your household and income details every year.

What you may need

  • Proof of Nova Scotia residency
  • Social insurance number
  • Proof of citizenship or permanent residency

Practical tips

  • District Offices are Metro, Western, Northern and Cape Breton.
  • Update your application when your household or contact details change.
