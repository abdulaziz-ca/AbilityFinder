Red Deer Transit & Recreation Fee Assistance

A reduced monthly transit pass plus help with City recreation fees. If you are on AISH, you may automatically qualify when you show the City your documents.

What it is

Two City of Red Deer programs.

  • The Transit Fare Assistance Pass drops a monthly pass to $34 no matter which type you'd normally buy (regular passes run $62–$75).
  • The Recreation Fee Assistance Program adds a Recreation Pass Card for drop-in use.
  • Plus, pending available funding, up to $200 a year per eligible person toward Safe & Healthy Communities program registrations.

Good to know

If you're on AISH or Income Support, you may automatically qualify when you provide the listed document. You may not need to send a Notice of Assessment. The transit pass is a permanent program (not a pilot). Both programs need renewing each year.

What this guide can't tell you: whether you will be approved. The program decides, often using details we do not ask for.

Amount or value

$34/month transit pass + up to $200/year per eligible person for recreation programs (pending available funding)

How to apply

  1. Apply online for Recreation Fee Assistance, or for the Transit Fare Assistance Pass — being approved for recreation also qualifies you for transit.
  2. If you're on AISH or Income Support, provide that document (Guaranteed Income Supplement qualifies for the transit pass only) — the City says you may automatically qualify.
  3. Otherwise attach your CRA Notice of Assessment so they can check you against the low-income line.
  4. Allow up to 10 business days (recreation applications may take longer due to high volume), then buy the $34 pass.
  5. Re-apply every year — approval expires.

What you may need

  • Proof you're on AISH / Income Support (or GIS, for the transit pass only) — or a CRA Notice of Assessment
  • Government-issued ID, and proof of residence if your ID does not show your current Red Deer address

Practical tips

  • One application can cover both. Being approved for Recreation Fee Assistance is itself a way to qualify for the transit pass.
  • Recreation funding is 'pending available funding'. Apply early in the year, not late.
  • Sorensen Station staff will help you apply in person if the online form is hard to use.

Timing

Transit pass: up to 10 business days. Recreation: the City says processing may take over 10 business days due to high volume.
