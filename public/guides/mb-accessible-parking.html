Manitoba · Daily living supports

Accessible Parking Permit (Manitoba Possible)

A permit lets you use designated accessible parking across Manitoba if you cannot walk more than 50 metres unassisted. A healthcare professional applies for you.

Manitoba is new here: please double-check before you rely on a detail

We added Manitoba in October 2026, while some of its programs are changing. Each program here comes from its official page, but some details are not published yet, so confirm with the program before you count on an amount or rule.

  • Manitoba is moving people with a disability from Employment and Income Assistance (EIA) to the new Manitoba Supports for Persons with Disabilities program, and that program's page does not publish payment amounts yet.
  • Since September 1, 2026, EIA covers only emergency dental care and points people to the federal Canadian Dental Care Plan.
  • Some income limits are old (home adaptation limits are from 2019) or change every year (Rent Assist and Winnipeg programs).
  • Winnipeg Transit Plus has proposed new eligibility rules that are not in effect.
  • City programs are listed for Winnipeg only so far.

What it is

The program is regulated by the province and run by Manitoba Possible.

  • A permit costs $15.
  • Motorcycle plate stickers are also available.
  • The permit works in any car if you, the holder, are also in it.
  • Lost, stolen or damaged permits are replaced for $6.

Good to know

Permanent permits last three years. Temporary permits last as long as your healthcare provider prescribes.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You are unable to walk more than 50 metres unassisted without great difficulty or danger to health and safety.
  • A regulated healthcare practitioner authorizes your application.

You can get a permit even if you do not own a vehicle.

Amount or value

$15 per permit

How to apply

  1. See your doctor, nurse practitioner, chiropractor, occupational therapist or physiotherapist.
  2. They complete the online application for you.
  3. Manitoba Possible contacts you for payment.

What you may need

  • Application completed online by your healthcare provider

Practical tips

  • Permanent permits are renewed without seeing a provider.
  • Temporary permits need a new application from your provider.
  • New residents need a Manitoba permit within three months.
