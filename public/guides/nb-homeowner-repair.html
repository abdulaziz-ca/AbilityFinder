New Brunswick · Home

Homeowner Repair Program

Loans for low-income homeowners and seniors for major repairs, accessibility changes for people with disabilities, and adaptations for seniors. Part of the loan may not have to be repaid.

What it is

Maximum forgivable loan by purpose

  • Homeowner repairs: up to $18,000, for households in core housing need with substandard housing.
  • Accessible modifications for disabled occupants: up to $15,000.
  • Landlords with a tenant with a disability may qualify for up to $15,000.
  • Seniors' home adaptations for daily living difficulties: up to $7,000.
  • The forgivable amount is based on the total cost of required repairs.

Good to know

Any repayable amount is at the provincial borrowing interest rate and can be repaid over up to 15 years. Call Housing NB to apply.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • Your household is low-income and you own and live in the home.
  • The home needs major repairs or lacks basic facilities.
  • Disability changes must be housing-related or give access to permanent basic facilities.
  • Senior adaptations must help you live independently for longer.

Repairs cover structural, electrical, plumbing, heating, fire safety or serious overcrowding.

Amount or value

Forgivable loan up to $18,000 (repairs), $15,000 (accessibility) or $7,000 (seniors' adaptations)

How to apply

  1. Check that your home needs one of the listed repairs or changes.
  2. Call Housing NB.
  3. Ask for the program that fits your need: repair, accessibility or seniors.

Practical tips

  • Assistance is a loan, and a portion may not have to be repaid.
  • Housing NB is open Monday to Friday, 8:15 a.m. to 4:30 p.m.
  • You can also email hnb.info@gnb.ca.
