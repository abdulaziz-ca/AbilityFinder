Rent Supplement (Manitoba Housing)

Helps low-income families, seniors and special needs households rent in private or non-profit housing. You pay rent based on income, and the province pays the rest.

Manitoba is new here: please double-check before you rely on a detail

We added Manitoba in October 2026, while some of its programs are changing. Each program here comes from its official page, but some details are not published yet, so confirm with the program before you count on an amount or rule.

  • Manitoba is moving people with a disability from Employment and Income Assistance (EIA) to the new Manitoba Supports for Persons with Disabilities program, and that program's page does not publish payment amounts yet.
  • Since September 1, 2026, EIA covers only emergency dental care and points people to the federal Canadian Dental Care Plan.
  • Some income limits are old (home adaptation limits are from 2019) or change every year (Rent Assist and Winnipeg programs).
  • Winnipeg Transit Plus has proposed new eligibility rules that are not in effect.
  • City programs are listed for Winnipeg only so far.

What it is

The province pays the gap between your income-based rent and the approved market rent.

  • Manitoba Housing pays the monthly supplement to the housing provider.
  • You pay your own portion of the rent as usual.
  • Units are in private rental housing and non-profit affordable housing.

Good to know

The page gives no income limit figure. Limits are set each year by CMHC as Program Income Limits.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • Your total household income is less than the Program Income Limits set annually by CMHC.
  • You are a permanent legal resident of Canada.
  • You are 18 years of age or older.
  • You do not get any other shelter allowance or rental subsidy.

The page names special needs households as a group the program serves.

What this guide can't tell you: whether you will be approved. The program decides, often using details we do not ask for.

Amount or value

You pay rent geared to income; the province covers the gap to the approved market rent

How to apply

  1. Ask for an application to be mailed, or pick one up at a Manitoba Housing Leasing Office.
  2. You can also send an application straight to a non-profit housing organization that offers the program.
  3. Check the program listing of non-profit housing organizations for availability.

Practical tips

  • Space depends on availability at each housing organization.
  • The program offices are open 8:30 AM to 4:30 PM, Monday to Friday.
  • Email RentSuppInfo@gov.mb.ca with questions.
