Nova Scotia Affordable Living Tax Credit

A tax-free credit for low- and modest-income people and families. It offsets the HST increase and is paid with the federal quarterly payments.

What it is

The credit adds income to help with everyday costs.

  • It is a tax-free amount.
  • It is combined with the quarterly payments of the federal CGEB.
  • The Province of Nova Scotia pays for it in full.

Good to know

The amounts apply from July 2026 to June 2027. The credit goes down as your income goes up.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You are a low- or modest-income individual or family.
  • The credit is reduced by 5% of adjusted family net income over $30,000.

The maximum is $255 for an individual or a couple, plus $60 for each child.

Amount or value

Up to $255 a year, plus $60 for each child (July 2026 to June 2027)

How to apply

  1. Read the CRA page for the Province of Nova Scotia.
  2. See the Canada Groceries and Essentials Benefit pamphlet RC4210.
  3. Payments come with your federal quarterly payments.

Practical tips

  • The CGEB was formerly the GST/HST credit.
  • A larger family can get $60 more for each child.
