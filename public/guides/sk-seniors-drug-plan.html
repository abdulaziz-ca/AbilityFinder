Seniors' Drug Plan

Eligible seniors 65 and older pay $25 for prescriptions on the Saskatchewan Formulary or approved under Exception Drug Status.

What it is

The plan caps what you pay for each eligible prescription.

  • Drugs must be on the Saskatchewan Formulary or have Exception Drug Status.
  • GIS clients keep a $200 and SIP clients a $100 semi-annual deductible, then a 35 per cent copayment.
  • There is no change for people with SAIL, palliative care, or SIP in long-term care.

Good to know

You pay the lesser amount if another program, such as Special Support, charges less.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You are a Saskatchewan resident aged 65 or older with a valid Saskatchewan Health card.
  • You qualify for the provincial age credit, based on net income (line 23600).
  • Your net income is $81,633 or less on your 2025 return, for 2027 coverage.

Seniors covered by federal programs, such as Non-Insured Health Benefits or Veterans Affairs, are not eligible.

What this guide can't tell you: whether you will be approved. The program decides, often using details we do not ask for.

Amount or value

$25 per eligible prescription

How to apply

  1. Choose Form A to let the CRA share your income, or Form B to send income documents.
  2. Mail, fax or email the form to the Seniors' Drug Plan.
  3. Wait for the confirmation of benefits letter.

What you may need

  • Seniors' Drug Plan application form, Form A or Form B
  • Income documentation, if you use Form B

Practical tips

  • You can apply up to two months before you turn 65.
  • If you apply before turning 65, coverage starts the first day of that birthday month.
  • Form A renews coverage automatically if you file your tax return each year.
  • Ask your physician or pharmacist about Exception Drug Status.
