Winnipeg · Transit

Winnipeg Transit Plus

Pre-booked shared door-to-door rides for people who cannot regularly use regular buses because of a physical disability. You must apply and be approved first.

Manitoba is new here: please double-check before you rely on a detail

We added Manitoba in October 2026, while some of its programs are changing. Each program here comes from its official page, but some details are not published yet, so confirm with the program before you count on an amount or rule.

  • Manitoba is moving people with a disability from Employment and Income Assistance (EIA) to the new Manitoba Supports for Persons with Disabilities program, and that program's page does not publish payment amounts yet.
  • Since September 1, 2026, EIA covers only emergency dental care and points people to the federal Canadian Dental Care Plan.
  • Some income limits are old (home adaptation limits are from 2019) or change every year (Rent Assist and Winnipeg programs).
  • Winnipeg Transit Plus has proposed new eligibility rules that are not in effect.
  • City programs are listed for Winnipeg only so far.

What it is

What the service offers:

  • Rides run within Winnipeg in cars, SUVs, accessible vans and mini-buses.
  • Drivers help you from the outer ground-floor door to the vehicle and back.
  • Rides are shared, so the first pick-up is not always the first drop-off.
  • Visitors registered with a paratransit service at home can apply for temporary service.

Good to know

Fares match regular transit, including reduced senior, youth and post-secondary fares.

What you must meet

The program requires at least one of these:

  • You need a wheelchair or scooter, ongoing or until a set date.
  • You cannot walk 175 metres outside, at all times or in winter only.
  • Your vision is 20/200 or less, or your visual field is under 20 degrees, even with lenses.
  • You have Alzheimer's or a related dementia that limits using regular buses with equal independence and safety.

Some approvals cover only dialysis trips or last until a set date.

Shared door-to-door rides at regular transit fares

How to apply

  1. Fill in the application online, or download the printable form.
  2. Staff contact you by letter within 30 business days of receiving it.
  3. You will likely attend the office for a functional assessment.
  4. You are told in writing whether you are approved or denied.

What you may need

  • Completed application form
  • Home paratransit registration number (visitors only)

Practical tips

  • Visitors should apply at least 7 to 10 business days before the trip.
  • Pay with cash (exact change), tickets, a bus pass or fare payment from account.
  • The City is considering new eligibility rules, but they are only proposed and not in effect.
