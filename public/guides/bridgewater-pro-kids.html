PROKids recreation fee help

PROKids is a Town of Bridgewater program. It helps children and youth whose families cannot afford sport, recreation or cultural activities.

What it is

Family income limits listed on the page:

  • 2 person family: under $39,721 a year.
  • 3 person family: under $48,832 a year.
  • 4 person family: under $59,288 a year.
  • 5 person family: under $67,244 a year.
  • 6 person family: under $75,839 a year.
  • 7 person family: under $84,436 a year.

Good to know

Only Town of Bridgewater residents can use this form.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You live in the Town of Bridgewater and your child cannot join an activity because of cost.
  • Your total family income (line 15000) is under the limit for your family size.
  • You show your Notice of Assessment or a Community Services or Indigenous Assistance endorsement.

The page gives no age rule beyond children and youth.

Amount or value

Covers some or all of the registration fee for a sport or recreation activity

How to apply

  1. Open the PROKids page and get the application form.
  2. Count your family size: parents in the household plus children 18 or younger.
  3. Add your most recent Notice of Assessment for each parent or guardian, or an endorsement.
  4. Ask Town of Bridgewater Recreation staff how to submit the form.

What you may need

  • Most recent tax year Notice of Assessment, one for each parent or guardian
  • Or an endorsement from the Department of Community Services or Indigenous Assistance

Practical tips

  • Lunenburg District and Chester District residents use their own municipality's form.
  • KidSport and Canadian Tire Jumpstart are other options for help with fees.
