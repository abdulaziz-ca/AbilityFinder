P.R.O. Kids recreation fee help

P.R.O. Kids is a charitable program run by the City of Saint John for low-income families. It pays costs of organized, registered recreation.

What it is

This program pays for:

  • Registration fees.
  • Equipment costs.
  • Transportation costs related to the activity.

Good to know

The page gives no age rule or income limit. Ask the City about both.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You are in a low-income family.
  • You need help paying for organized, registered recreation.

The page does not state an income limit or an age rule.

Amount or value

Pays registration, equipment and transportation costs
