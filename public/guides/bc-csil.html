British Columbia · Home support

Choice in Supports for Independent Living (CSIL)

Your health authority sends you money each month so you can hire and manage your own home support workers. For people with a significant physical disability who want more control.

What it is

CSIL gives you control over who you hire, when services happen and how tasks are done.

  • You become the employer and manage hiring, supervising and how the funds are spent.
  • Funding is the hourly rate times the home support hours your assessment says you need each month.
  • Your monthly contribution is taken out of the funding you receive.

Good to know

You pay a daily rate based on income, and no more than $300 a month if you or your spouse has earned income. You pay no daily rate if you get GIS, support and shelter allowance, or War Veterans Allowance.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You are assessed as needing home support services as part of your care plan.
  • You are assessed as having high physical care needs and a physical disability, and are medically stable.
  • Your assessed needs can be met within CSIL, within available resources.
  • You agree to pay the assessed client rate.
  • You can safely manage CSIL, or a support group or representative acts as your employer.

You must also meet the general eligibility criteria for home and community care services.

Amount or value

Monthly funds from your health authority; minimum $38.19 an hour (April 1, 2024)

How to apply

  1. Contact your health authority's home and community care office, or ask a health care professional to refer you.
  2. Have your health authority assess your care needs and your finances.
  3. Agree on funding in a written agreement between you and the health authority.
  4. Hire and supervise your own care providers and manage the funds.

What you may need

  • The page does not list documents. Ask your responsible assessor what to bring.

Practical tips

  • Read the CSIL Online Workbook to see what being an employer involves.
  • Tell your responsible assessor about any earned income during your financial assessment.
  • If the daily rate would cause serious financial hardship, you can ask for a temporary reduction.
  • If you cannot manage CSIL alone, a client support group or a representative can act as employer.
