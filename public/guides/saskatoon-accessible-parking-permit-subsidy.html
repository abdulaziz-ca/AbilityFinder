Saskatoon Accessible Parking Permit Subsidy

Low-income Saskatoon residents with a SaskAbilities placard can buy the City's Accessible Parking Permit at 50 percent off. You must be approved first.

What it is

The permit lets you park on the street in pay parking zones without paying at a pay station.

  • You can park up to 4 hours in regular pay spaces, accessible zones and loading zones.
  • The regular permit costs $100 plus GST a year.
  • The permit does not work in parking lots, residential zones or bus stops.

Good to know

You need subsidy approval before you buy the permit. The prorated price is $25 plus GST from May 1.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You are a Saskatoon resident
  • You have a valid and current SaskAbilities Accessible Parking placard
  • Your household income is below the Low Income Cut-Off (LICO)

What this guide can't tell you: whether you will be approved. The program decides, often using details we do not ask for.

How to apply

  1. Fill in the online form, or print the form and mail or bring it to City Hall
  2. Wait for your subsidy approval
  3. Buy your permit at City Hall

What you may need

  • Your provincial disability placard and a piece of ID, when you buy the permit.

Practical tips

  • Permits expire at the end of October each year.
  • Questions go to parkingsubsidy@saskatoon.ca.
