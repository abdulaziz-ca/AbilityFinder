Regina Paratransit

Scheduled shared door-to-door rides for people with a disability who are restricted in using regular buses some of the time. You must register first.

What it is

There are five types of registration:

  • Unconditional, for Regina residents who need Paratransit all the time.
  • Temporary, for a short-term disability such as a broken leg.
  • Winter, issued from November 1 until March 31.
  • Visitor, for people visiting Regina.
  • Conditional, for certain times or conditions, such as after dark.

Good to know

Fares match regular transit. You must be registered before you can ride.

What you must meet

The program requires at least one of these:

  • You have trouble walking or wheeling to a bus stop.
  • You have trouble following directions or using regular buses.
  • You are easily confused or disoriented.
  • You have a risk of falling.
  • You cannot board a regular bus on your own.
  • You tire easily.

You must be restricted in at least one of these ways.

Amount or value

Shared door-to-door rides at regular transit fares

How to apply

  1. Complete the electronic application to create your rider profile.
  2. Staff review your eligibility and may contact the professionals you listed.
  3. You may be asked to attend an in-person interview.
  4. Once registered, book trips by phone, email or the app, up to seven days ahead.

What you may need

  • Completed application with professionals listed

Practical tips

  • Evening calls are easier, since the lines are busy during the day.
  • An unconditional registration ends if you do not ride for 18 months.

Timing
