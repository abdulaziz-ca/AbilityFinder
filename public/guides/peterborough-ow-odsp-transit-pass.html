Subsidized City Bus Pass (Peterborough, OW and ODSP)

A subsidized monthly Peterborough Transit pass for adults on Ontario Works and for people on ODSP. A 30-day pass costs $49.

What it is

The pass lowers the price of monthly City bus travel for people on Ontario Works or ODSP.

  • You buy it in person at the Peterborough Transit bus terminal.
  • Both city and county residents on these programs can use it.

Good to know

Ontario Works clients may be eligible if they do not get transportation money from their Case Manager.

What you must meet

The program requires at least one of these:

  • You are an adult on Ontario Works in the City or County and get no travel money from your Case Manager
  • You are an ODSP client living in the City or County of Peterborough

Amount or value

30-day City bus pass for $49

How to apply

  1. Take your statement of assistance for the current month and personal ID.
  2. Go to the Peterborough Transit bus terminal at 190 Simcoe St.
  3. Buy the 30-day pass for $49.

What you may need

  • Your Ontario Works or ODSP statement of assistance for the current month
  • Personal identification

Practical tips

  • Ontario Works clients can ask their Case Manager about the pass.
