Saskatchewan Housing Benefit

A monthly benefit for renters whose rent and utilities take a large share of their income. The amount depends on household size and the share of income spent on shelter.

What it is

The benefit is co-funded by the federal and Saskatchewan governments.

  • Spending 35 to 45 per cent of income on shelter: $210, $270 or $330 a month.
  • Spending over 45 per cent: $270, $330 or $390 a month, by household size.
  • The higher amounts apply to households with one or more dependants.
  • A Supportive Housing Stream and a Seeking Safety from Interpersonal Violence Stream also exist.

Good to know

Amounts are effective July 1, 2026. Two other streams help people in vulnerable living situations.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You rent your home and spend 35 per cent or more of before-tax household income on rent and utilities.
  • Your household has less than $300,000 in assets.
  • Your before-tax household income is at or below the limit for your household.

Limits are $43,100 (single or couple), $52,900 (one dependant), $63,800 (two or more dependants) a year.

What this guide can't tell you: whether you will be approved. The program decides, often using details we do not ask for.

Amount or value

$210 to $390 a month (effective July 1, 2026)

How to apply

  1. Complete the online application, or download the PDF application. You only need one.
  2. If you use the PDF, send it with required documents by email, fax or mail.
  3. Wait for Saskatchewan Housing Corporation to contact you if it needs more information.

What you may need

  • The completed application
  • The documents the application asks for

Practical tips

  • You are not eligible if you get shelter support from another Saskatchewan assistance or training program.
  • You are also not eligible if you rent from a housing authority under the Social Housing Program.
  • Sponsored newcomers, people incarcerated in a facility, and full-time post-secondary students are not eligible.
  • Allow extra time if you apply by mail.

Timing

If you hear nothing within 14 business days, contact the housing corporation.
