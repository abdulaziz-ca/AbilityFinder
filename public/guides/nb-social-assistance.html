Social Assistance Program (Income Assistance)

Money for basic needs like food, rent, utilities and clothing when your household has low income. It may also help with child care, transportation and prescription drugs.

What it is

Income Assistance can also come with other help.

  • Other benefits include health cards, the Emergency Fuel Benefit and Medical Transportation.
  • Emergency Special Benefits are also offered by the department.
  • People on Income Assistance may get help with learning, work and transitional case plans.

Good to know

The amount depends on total household income. Social Development adds up income from everyone in the household.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • Your total household income is under the rate for your family type, so you are likely eligible.

If you worked recently and may get Employment Insurance, check those benefits too.

What this guide can't tell you: whether you will be approved. The program decides, often using details we do not ask for.

Amount or value

Depends on household income and family type

How to apply

  1. Gather your address, rent or mortgage costs, and details for each household member.
  2. Call the screening and intake line to answer questions about whether you may be eligible.
  3. If eligible, you get an appointment to complete the application package.

What you may need

  • Mailing address and residence address if different
  • Rental or mortgage costs
  • Name, birthdate, Medicare number and Social Insurance Number for each household member
  • Income and assets for each household member

Practical tips

  • Bring the checklist of documents the department lists to your appointment.
  • Assets means what each family member owns, like a car or a house.
