Low-Income Bus Pass (Saskatoon)

Low-income Saskatoon residents can buy a monthly bus pass at a reduced rate. You qualify using last year's Notice of Assessment.

What it is

The monthly prices are in the tips below.

  • A $5 activation fee applies to a new reloadable pass card.

Good to know

You are accepted for one year at a time and must requalify after that.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You live in Saskatoon.
  • Your household income on last year's Notice of Assessment is under the limit.

Limits for 1 to 4 people: $27,478, $34,206, $42,053 and $51,058 (larger sizes on the page).

Amount or value

Adult $66.40 a month; high school $47.20 a month

At a glance

Difficulty
Easy
Application
Form by mail with tax and address proof

How to apply

  1. Fill out the online application, or get a paper copy at a leisure centre or the Transit Customer Service Centre.
  2. Include your Notice of Assessment and a utility bill with your address.
  3. Mail it to Community Development, 3130 Laurier Drive, Saskatoon SK S7L 5J7.
  4. Take the returned form to the Customer Services Centre, 226 23rd Street East, to buy the pass.

What you may need

  • Notice of Assessment from the Canada Revenue Agency
  • Utility bill with your current address

Practical tips

  • Low-income monthly pass prices: adult $66.40, high school $47.20.
  • The page says the Notice of Assessment is the only tool used to qualify you.
  • Rates stay the same after the July 1, 2026 fare change.

Timing

Three to four weeks
