St. John's · Recreation

Adult recreation financial subsidy

The City of St. John's can pay all or part of the fee for its adult recreation programs. It is for residents over eighteen and looks at need.

What it is

What the page says about this subsidy:

  • It applies to programs run by the City.
  • The City can approve all or part of the fee, based on need.
  • Requests are considered based on program availability.

Good to know

Applications are taken all year and depend on program availability. You need proof of residency and proof of income.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You live in St. John's and are over eighteen.
  • Your need, judged by Statistics Canada's Low Income Cut Off, supports a subsidy.
  • You meet the requirements of the City program you want.

Applications may be approved for a full or partial subsidy.

What this guide can't tell you: whether you will be approved. The program decides, often using details we do not ask for.

Amount or value

Full or partial subsidy for City of St. John's adult programs

How to apply

  1. Open the Financial Supports page and get the adult application form.
  2. Fill in the form completely.
  3. Attach the documents the form asks for.
  4. Send it in at any time of year.
  5. Wait for the City to tell you the result.

What you may need

  • Proof of residency showing your name and address, such as a utility bill or driver's license
  • Proof of income from all sources, such as a Canada Revenue Agency Notice of Assessment for the previous year

Practical tips

  • The page asks you to allow 7-10 business days for a reply.
  • The page's example is from 2022 (2021 income), so the tax year to use now is not stated there.
  • For Notice of Assessment, call 1-800-959-8281.
  • Email inclusion@stjohns.ca if you need help with the form.
