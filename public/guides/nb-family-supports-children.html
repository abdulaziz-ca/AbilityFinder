Family Supports for Children with Disabilities

Helps families meet the unmet needs of a child with a disability, with a social worker. Examples are medical equipment, relief care and home support.

What it is

Examples of what the program can help with:

  • Medical equipment, medical transportation and incontinence supplies.
  • Child care costs that exceed typical day-care or after-school costs.
  • Relief care and home support services.
  • Family support workers for social inclusion and skill-building.
  • Therapeutic-based recreation, and a health card for families without health insurance.

Good to know

The program is voluntary. Families work with a social worker. Families who have the means make a financial contribution.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You are a family with a child or children who have a disability and unmet needs.

The page does not list further eligibility criteria.

How to apply

  1. Call Social Development to refer your own family.
  2. Or ask a professional working with your child to call for you.
  3. Choose Social Worker Managed or Family Managed case management.

Practical tips

  • Family Managed means you get the funds directly to arrange the services.
  • The Family Support Plan outlines your child's unmet needs.
