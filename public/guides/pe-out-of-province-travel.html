Prince Edward Island · Health

Out-of-Province Travel Support

Helps pay bus tickets for medical care outside PEI that Health PEI approved. The subsidy is 100% at $35,000 household income or less, 50% from $35,001 to $50,000.

What it is

How it works:

  • The subsidy applies to Maritime Bus fares.
  • An essential escort is a parent or guardian of a child, or a person needed for safe travel.
  • Household income counts both the applicant and the spouse.
  • Maritime Bus is wheelchair accessible, with 72 hours notice.

Good to know

This is from the program's application form, which shows no date. Confirm current rates with the program.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • Health PEI has approved your out-of-province medical services.
  • You live in PEI and hold a valid Personal Health Number.
  • Your net household income is $50,000 or less.
  • Your travel costs are not covered by other non-provincial government help.

The applicant must be 18 or older, and the form lets you request an essential escort.

What this guide can't tell you: whether you will be approved. The program decides, often using details we do not ask for.

How to apply

  1. Get approval from Health PEI for the out-of-province service.
  2. Fill in the application form and sign the consent.
  3. Attach the Notice of Assessment for you and your spouse.
  4. Send it by email, fax, mail or online.

What you may need

  • Canada Revenue Agency Notice of Assessment for you and your spouse.
  • Signed application form with declaration and consent.

Practical tips

  • Incomplete applications are returned.
  • Send new assessments if more than 12 months pass.
  • Tell the program if your household income changes.
