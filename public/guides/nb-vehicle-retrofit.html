Vehicle Retrofit Program

Helps pay for accessibility features in a vehicle, like lifts, ramps, hand controls and special seating. You pay the taxes.

What it is

The program can cover some of the cost of:

  • Wheelchair or scooter lifts and ramps.
  • Changes to the roof, floor and doors.
  • Special-needs seating and hand controls.
  • Wheelchair tie-downs and passenger-restraint systems.
  • Other items may also be considered.

Good to know

Individuals can apply for new changes every eight years. Organizations can apply every five years.

What you must meet

The program requires at least one of these:

  • You are a New Brunswick resident with a physical disability.
  • You are a registered non-profit, municipality or company providing transportation for people with disabilities.

Amount or value

Up to 80% of eligible costs, to a maximum of $8,000

How to apply

  1. Fill out the online application form (PDF).
  2. Mail it to Ability NB, Suite 102, 440 Wilsey Road, Fredericton, NB, E3B 7G5.
  3. Ability NB reviews it and tells you the decision by mail.

What you may need

  • Completed application form

Practical tips

  • Sales tax on some features may be refunded by the Department of Finance.
  • Ability NB can help with your application: info@abilitynb.ca.
