Self-Managed Care Program

Adults with a physical disability hire their own care providers and make their own care plans. Funding pays for hiring, training, managing and paying them.

What it is

You are the boss of your own care team.

  • Care providers help with bathing, dressing, preparing meals and taking medication.
  • The Department does not set required qualifications for providers.
  • A Care Coordinator can recommend changes to your funding.

Good to know

You cannot hire family members as your care providers. You may appoint an unpaid Care Manager to act for you.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You are 19 years or older.
  • You have a physical disability and are in stable health.
  • You need help with routine activities of daily living.
  • You can fully take part in decisions about your own care.
  • You can enter a legal agreement with Nova Scotia Health Authority.

What this guide can't tell you: whether you will be approved. The program decides, often using details we do not ask for.

How to apply

  1. Call Continuing Care to learn about the program.
  2. Go to the Self-Managed Care Orientation Session.
  3. Sign the legal agreement and hire your care providers.

Practical tips

  • A client guide explains what you need to do.
  • Funding can be stopped if you or your Care Coordinator ask within 30 days.
  • Continuing Care may stop funding if you break your agreement.
