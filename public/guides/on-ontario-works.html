Ontario Works

Money for living costs such as food and rent, plus health benefits, for people in financial need. In most cases you take part in employment-related activities to get the money.

What it is

Ontario Works helps people in financial need with living costs.

  • Pays money toward living expenses, including food and rent.
  • Includes health benefits for you and your eligible family members.
  • You may get more if you have a spouse or child.
  • You may get extra money, for example for transport to medical appointments.

Good to know

The Canada Disability Benefit is exempt as income and does not affect your Ontario Works eligibility or payment. You may get more for family members or other benefits.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You need financial assistance for food and housing.
  • You are in financial need.

A worker checks your income and assets; if found not eligible, you have 30 days to ask for a review.

What this guide can't tell you: whether you will be approved. The program decides, often using details we do not ask for.

Amount or value

Up to $733 a month for basic needs and shelter if you are single

How to apply

  1. Apply online (20 to 30 minutes), by phone, or at your local Ontario Works office.
  2. An intake worker reviews your application and may ask for more documents.
  3. You are told within four business days if you are approved and how much you may receive.
  4. Your Ontario Works office schedules an appointment to make a plan for employment-related activities.

What you may need

  • Social insurance numbers, if applicable
  • OHIP cards
  • Birth certificates
  • Immigration papers
  • Tax returns
  • Banking information and bank statements
  • Proof of income, shelter costs and Ontario residency

Practical tips

  • One application covers you and the immediate family who live with you.
  • All family members on the application should be with you when you start, so they can consent.
  • A plan for work activities may be waived for reasons such as an illness or caregiving responsibilities.
  • If you have a disability, one application can be used for Ontario Works or the Ontario Disability Support Program.
