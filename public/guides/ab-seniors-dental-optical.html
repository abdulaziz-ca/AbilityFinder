Dental and Optical Assistance for Seniors

Help paying for basic dental care and prescription glasses for lower-income seniors 65 and older. You must first be approved for Seniors Financial Assistance programs.

What it is

What is covered

  • Dental: exams, x-rays, cleaning, fillings, extractions, root canals, gum treatment and basic dentures.
  • Not covered: crowns, bridges, braces, implants, fluoride and cosmetic work.
  • Optical: prescription glasses, sunglasses, lenses, contact lenses, frames and repairs.
  • Not covered: eye exams, eye surgery, cataract surgery lenses and magnifiers.

Good to know

Dental coverage may not cover the full fees that dentists charge. Ask your dentist to request pre-authorization from Alberta Blue Cross first.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You are 65 or older.
  • You are an Alberta resident and lived here at least 3 months before applying.
  • You are a Canadian citizen or admitted to Canada for permanent residence.
  • You are approved for Seniors Financial Assistance programs.
  • Your annual income is within the program limits.

For 2026–27, coverage needs 2025 total income of $0 to $32,690 (single) or $0 to $65,380 (couple).

What this guide can't tell you: whether you will be approved. The program decides, often using details we do not ask for.

Amount or value

Dental: up to $5,000 every 5 years. Glasses: up to $230 every 3 years.

How to apply

  1. Apply for Seniors Financial Assistance programs. Skip this if you already did.
  2. Show your Alberta health card at the dental or optical office and say you are enrolled.
  3. Ask the office to bill Alberta Blue Cross directly.
  4. If they cannot, pay the bill and send in a reimbursement claim.

What you may need

  • Alberta health card
  • Receipts and a completed claim form, if you claim a reimbursement

Practical tips

  • Unused dental money does not carry into the next 5-year period. Unused optical money does not carry into the next 3-year period.
  • Claim within 12 months of the expense or it will not be paid.
  • For services covered by both plans, claim from the Canadian Dental Care Plan first.
  • If cataract surgery changes your prescription, you may get funding for another set of glasses. You need a medical note.

Phone

Alberta Blue Cross claims: 1-800-661-6995
