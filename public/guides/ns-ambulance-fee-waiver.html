Nova Scotia · Health

Ambulance fee waiver

People with a low income can ask to have an ambulance fee waived. There is a time limit to apply.

What it is

Related fees on the page

  • Residents with a health card pay a lower fee for medically essential transport.
  • Mobility-challenged residents pay less for trips to medical appointments.
  • Transfers between approved facilities cost residents nothing.

Good to know

Decisions are based on Statistics Canada's Low Income Measure. If the fee causes hardship, you are offered a repayment schedule.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You got an ambulance bill and cannot pay it because your income is too low.
  • You apply in time, no later than 90 days after the bill date.

Your income is checked against Statistics Canada's Low Income Measure.

Amount or value

Fee waived if your income is too low to pay it

How to apply

  1. Download the Ambulance Fee Assistance Program Form and the income limits.
  2. Call the billing office to ask where to send the completed form, within the 90 days.
  3. Call the billing office with questions.

What you may need

Practical tips

  • The fee for residents with a health card was $146.55, as of April 1st, 2015.
  • You can appeal a fee within 30 days of the bill date.
