Access Pass, Disability (Maple Ridge)

A discount on City of Maple Ridge recreation for residents with a diagnosed temporary or permanent disability. You get 50% off admissions and 25% off program fees, up to $150 a year. It is for the person with the disability, not the family.

What it is

The Disability Access Pass lowers admission and program costs for Maple Ridge residents with a diagnosed disability.

  • 50% off single, 10-pass, 20-pass, 1-month and 3-month admissions.
  • The pass is valid for one year from the date of approval.
  • A medical or para-medical provider can verify your disability on the form.

Good to know

The page lists no income test for this pass. The pass lasts one year from approval and you re-apply each year.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You live in Maple Ridge (ask the City how this applies if you hold a study or visitor visa)
  • You have a diagnosed temporary or permanent disability, with documents or third-party verification

The pass is for the person with the disability only, one applicant per application.

Amount or value

50% off admissions; 25% off programs up to $150 a year

How to apply

  1. Choose how to apply: online, on paper at the Maple Ridge Leisure Centre, or through a third party
  2. Fill in the Individuals Diagnosed With Temporary or Permanent Disability form
  3. Upload or hand in your documents, or have a third party complete the adjudicator section
  4. Wait for an email or phone call about your application
  5. Pick up your membership card at the Maple Ridge Leisure Centre or Albion Community Centre
  6. Re-apply each year

What you may need

  • An address-related bill from the last 3 months. PO Box addresses are not accepted
  • Proof of disability, such as BC PWD designation, Disability Tax Credit approval, CPP Disability letter, Veterans Affairs or WorkSafeBC documents
  • Not needed if a third party verifies you: proof of address

Practical tips

  • Not discounted, among others: private swimming lessons, high performance training camps, contracted or partner programs over $299.99, licensed afterschool care and employment certification.
  • The page's list of disability documents is cut off at the end, so ask staff if yours is not listed.
  • Online registration applies your discount automatically. Email Registration@MapleRidge.ca for your first sign-in link.
  • Applications needing extra screening can take up to 14 days.
