Home Repair Program - Adaptations for Independence

A forgivable loan of up to $23,000 to make a home more accessible for a person with a housing-related disability. For owners with low incomes, and rental owners who rent to low-income households.

What it is

This program helps pay to make a home accessible.

  • The loan is forgivable and up to $23,000.
  • Homeowner income maximums by bedrooms: $58,100, $71,300, $86,300, $103,500 (July 1, 2026).
  • The asset level is $300,000 for all households.
  • Rental monthly maximum rents: $1,262, $1,550, $1,875, $2,250 (July 1, 2026).

Good to know

Repairs done before written approval do not qualify. Work must be finished within six months of approval.

What you must meet

The program requires at least one of these:

  • You own and live in the home, and a household member has a housing-related disability.
  • Or you rent the modified units to low-income households that include a person with that disability.
  • Household income and assets are at or below the limits set by Saskatchewan Housing Corporation.

Income and rent limits are dated July 1, 2026; a health practitioner must identify the changes.

What this guide can't tell you: whether you will be approved. The program decides, often using details we do not ask for.

How to apply

  1. Download the Homeowner or Rental application form.
  2. Complete it and mail, deliver or email it to Saskatchewan Housing Corporation.
  3. Wait for written approval before any work starts.
  4. Finish the work within six months of approval.

What you may need

  • Completed Repair and Renovation Application Form
  • Modifications identified by a qualified health practitioner
  • Required supporting documents listed on the form

Practical tips

  • You pay any costs above the approved amount.
  • Liens or judgments on the property title can affect eligibility.
