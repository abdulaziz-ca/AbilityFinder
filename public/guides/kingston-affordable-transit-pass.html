Kingston Affordable Transit Pass

A discounted, renewable monthly Kingston transit pass. You get it through the City's Municipal Fee Assistance Program (MFAP), which is income-based. You only need to apply once to MFAP.

What it is

MFAP gives low- and moderate-income households access to City services at no cost or a lower cost. The transit pass is one of them.

  • You can get a discounted renewable monthly transit pass.
  • If you recently started a part-time or full-time job, the transit employment program gives a free two-month pass.
  • MFAP also covers other things, such as free access to fitness centres, public skates and public swims through SPARK.

Good to know

The page does not give the pass price. Funding is first-come, first-served until the yearly budget runs out. Ask the City about the price and Kingston Transit's fares and passes page.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • MFAP compares your household income with its limit for your household size
  • You apply to the Municipal Fee Assistance Program (MFAP)

MFAP uses the after-tax Low-Income Measure plus 15 percent; the 2026 limits are in the tips below.

What this guide can't tell you: whether you will be approved. The program decides, often using details we do not ask for.

How to apply

  1. Compare your household income with the 2026 limits in the tips
  2. Create a MyKingston account and apply online. You can also email mfap@cityofkingston.ca or apply in person at Housing and Social Services, 362 Montreal St.
  3. See the City's Transit Fares and Passes page for the discounted monthly pass

What you may need

  • The page does not list documents. Ask the City what to bring

Practical tips

  • 2026 MFAP income limits (after tax): $34,793 for 1 person, $49,205 for 2, $60,263 for 3, $69,587 for 4, $77,800 for 5, $85,225 for 6, $92,054 for 7, $98,410 for 8, $104,380 for 9 and $110,020 for 10.
  • Funding is first-come, first-served, so apply early.
  • Questions: mfap@cityofkingston.ca or 613-546-2695 ext. 4906.
