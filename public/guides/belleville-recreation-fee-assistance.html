Recreation Fee Assistance (Belleville)

A discount on City of Belleville recreation programs and facility visits. Your household income sets the rate. Approval lasts from July 1 to June 30.

What it is

Approved households pay a lower rate on eligible City programs without reapplying for each one.

  • Eligible programs may include camps, group swim and skate lessons, and aquafit sessions.
  • Eligible programs may also include session passes, drop-ins and facility memberships.
  • Aquatic leadership courses, such as Bronze Cross, may be eligible.
  • The rate is 25, 50 or 75 percent off, set by your income.

Good to know

Private, semi-private and low-ratio lessons, personal training, exam fees and add-ons are not covered. You renew every year. An appointment is needed only every other year unless your address or other details change.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You live in Belleville
  • Your total family income is within the City's chart limit for your family size

The City's chart gives the 75, 50 and 25 percent income limits for families of 1 to 7.

What this guide can't tell you: whether you will be approved. The program decides, often using details we do not ask for.

Amount or value

25%, 50% or 75% off City recreation program fees

How to apply

  1. Fill in the application online, or call 613-966-4632 to ask for a form.
  2. Wait for an email with instructions to book an appointment.
  3. Expect a call within 10 business days to confirm the appointment.
  4. Bring your documents to the appointment.
  5. Register for eligible programs at your approved rate.

What you may need

  • Photo ID showing your address, for each household member 18 and over
  • Proof of income for everyone in the household, such as a Notice of Assessment, ODSP or Ontario Works statement
  • Proof of residency, such as a property tax bill or rental agreement

Practical tips

  • To reapply, apply online or in person and hand in documents at the Quinte Sports & Wellness Centre.
