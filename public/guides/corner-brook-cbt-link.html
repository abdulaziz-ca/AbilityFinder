Corner Brook · Transit

CBT Link accessible transit

CBT Link is Corner Brook's door-to-door accessible transit. It is for residents whose disabilities prevent them from using the regular transit system.

What it is

Service details from the page:

  • Trips are available anywhere within city limits during operating hours.
  • Approved attendants or support persons ride free with an eligible rider.
  • Approved riders book through the Blaise Transit App or by phone.

Good to know

Standard transit fares apply unless you qualify for the Free Transit Pass Program. Approved attendants ride free.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • Your disability prevents you from using the regular transit system.
  • You are approved and registered before you ride.
  • A healthcare professional completes the medical section of the application.

Incomplete applications may delay processing.

Amount or value

Door-to-door accessible rides anywhere within Corner Brook city limits

How to apply

  1. Call to request an application or download the form.
  2. Print it and fill it in completely.
  3. Ask a healthcare professional to complete the medical section.
  4. Submit all pages.

What you may need

  • Printed application form with all pages
  • Medical section completed by a healthcare professional

Practical tips

  • Corner Brook Transit services are not available on statutory holidays.
  • Book rides in the Blaise Transit App or call 709-289-3124.
  • Only approved riders should pick Reduced Mobility in the app.
  • The page lists the regular adult cash fare as $3.25.
