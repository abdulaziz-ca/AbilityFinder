Manitoba · Health (for children)

Children's disABILITY Services (CDS)

Supports for families raising a child with a developmental or physical disability, to help meet some of the extra needs. A diagnosis is required.

Manitoba is new here: please double-check before you rely on a detail

We added Manitoba in October 2026, while some of its programs are changing. Each program here comes from its official page, but some details are not published yet, so confirm with the program before you count on an amount or rule.

  • Manitoba is moving people with a disability from Employment and Income Assistance (EIA) to the new Manitoba Supports for Persons with Disabilities program, and that program's page does not publish payment amounts yet.
  • Since September 1, 2026, EIA covers only emergency dental care and points people to the federal Canadian Dental Care Plan.
  • Some income limits are old (home adaptation limits are from 2019) or change every year (Rent Assist and Winnipeg programs).
  • Winnipeg Transit Plus has proposed new eligibility rules that are not in effect.
  • City programs are listed for Winnipeg only so far.

What it is

CDS helps families care for their children at home, in their own communities.

  • Since February 4, 2025, children living in First Nation communities may also use CDS.
  • For children 5 and under, autism diagnoses from community pediatricians are accepted.
  • While your child is eligible, a worker may stay involved until age 18 or until you no longer need services.

Good to know

Written confirmation of the child's diagnosis must be included with every application.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • Your child is under 18 and lives in Manitoba with birth, adoptive or extended family.
  • Your child has a medical diagnosis of one of the listed conditions.

The full list of qualifying diagnoses is in the tips below.

What this guide can't tell you: whether you will be approved. The program decides, often using details we do not ask for.

How to apply

  1. Fill out the Referral and Intake Application form.
  2. Send it with a diagnostic assessment or medical report to your regional CDS office.
  3. If your child qualifies, a CDS worker connects with you to talk about needs and goals.

What you may need

  • Referral and Intake Application form
  • Diagnostic assessment or medical report
  • Written confirmation of the diagnosis

Practical tips

  • Qualifying diagnoses: intellectual disability; developmental delay; autism spectrum disorder; lifelong physical disability with significant functional limitation in mobility; or a high probability of developmental delay due to a pre-existing condition.
  • Schools, medical professionals and child care facilities may refer you.
  • You can also apply directly.
