Paradise · Transit

Accessible Transit Project

The Town of Paradise covers the cost of taxi rides within town for residents with a disability. You are assessed by a health practitioner first.

What it is

How the program works:

  • Registered riders use Newfound Cabs at no cost within town boundaries.
  • The taxi company bills the Town directly.
  • The driver applies the program discount when you show your ID card.

Good to know

The Town pays for the assessment. Outside Paradise, the Town covers only the part of the trip inside the town.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You live in Paradise and have a physical, cognitive, sensory or visual limitation.
  • A health practitioner at Body Quest Health and Wellness assesses you.
  • You apply to the Town using the application form.

Participants get a Town-issued identification card.

What this guide can't tell you: whether you will be approved. The program decides, often using details we do not ask for.

How to apply

  1. Download and complete the application form.
  2. Email it, drop it off at Town Hall or mail it to the Town.
  3. Attend the assessment at Body Quest Health and Wellness.
  4. Show your ID card to a Newfound Cabs driver when you ride.

What you may need

  • Completed application form

Practical tips

  • Email info@paradise.ca, drop off at Town Hall or mail to 28 McNamara Drive.
  • The assessment is at 15 McNamara Drive.
  • Contact Newfound Cabs each time you need a ride.
