Saskatchewan · Health

Cochlear Implant Processor Replacement Program

Help toward the cost of a replacement external sound processor for a cochlear implant, for children and adults. You pay an $840 co-pay per processor.

What it is

Priority is based on these criteria:

  • Your current processor is within the five to seven year replacement time.
  • Your current processor has been deemed obsolete by the manufacturer.
  • Wanting newer technology while your processor works is not covered.

Good to know

Meant for people without coverage through other programs. Typical replacement is once every five to seven years, or the processor's lifespan.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You have a valid Saskatchewan health card.
  • Your sound processor was provided through the Saskatchewan Health Authority (used to set priority).
  • You meet the clinical indicators for a replacement.

Clients are prioritized by greatest need; people with no sound are a priority.

What this guide can't tell you: whether you will be approved. The program decides, often using details we do not ask for.

Amount or value

You pay an $840 co-pay per processor; the program is cost-shared

How to apply

  1. Contact the Saskatchewan Health Authority about a processor replacement.
  2. Clients must meet the clinical indicators and are assessed based on greatest need.

What you may need

  • Valid Saskatchewan health card

Practical tips

  • Implants other than cochlear implants are not covered.
  • The program started on December 1, 2022.
  • The program is administered by the Saskatchewan Health Authority under the direction of the Ministry of Health.
