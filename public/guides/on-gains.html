Guaranteed Annual Income System (GAINS)

A monthly, non-taxable payment for Ontario seniors with low income. It is paid on top of any federal OAS and GIS payments you may get.

What it is

GAINS tops up the income of low-income seniors in Ontario.

  • Paid about the 25th of each month, from July to June.
  • Your payment can change during the year if your private income or marital status changes.
  • The benefit year runs from July 1 to June 30 of the next year.
  • Tables on the page show your amount by age and marital status.

Good to know

If you had no private income in 2025, you get the maximum amount. Different income limits apply if your spouse is under 65 or cannot get Old Age Security.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You are 65 years or older.
  • You have been a Canadian resident for 10 years or more.
  • You have lived in Ontario for 12 months, or for 20 years in total since age 18.
  • You receive the federal Old Age Security (OAS) pension and Guaranteed Income Supplement (GIS).
  • Your private income is up to $4,416 (single) or $8,832 (couple).

Private income can include a workplace pension, the Canada Pension Plan and bank interest.

Amount or value

Up to $92 a month for the 2026 benefit year (July 1, 2026 to June 30, 2027)

How to apply

  1. If you already get Old Age Security and the Guaranteed Income Supplement, you do not have to apply.
  2. File your income tax return every year by April 30, even with no income to report.
  3. Or complete a Guaranteed Income Supplement application and send it to your nearest Service Canada office.
  4. Sign up for direct deposit through Service Canada.

What you may need

  • Your income tax and benefit return

Practical tips

  • If you disagree with a decision, contact the Ontario Ministry of Finance.
  • You can file a Notice of Objection within 90 days of the written decision being mailed.
