FAIR Play Program

Burnaby gives every eligible family member a recreation credit and a free annual Be Active Pass. The pass is in addition to the credit. It is not paid for out of the credit.

What it is

Each approved family member receives both a Be Active Pass and recreation credit. The City accepts income-assistance, income, child-disability, refugee or current CUAET, and approved-agency-referral routes.

Good to know

You can apply once a year, and the credit is valid for a year. Unused credit is removed when it expires and has no cash value. The FAIR Play Program replaced the older Recreation Credit Program on March 1, 2024.

What you must meet

The program requires at least one of these:

  • You receive federal or provincial government income assistance.
  • Your combined family income is within the FAIR Play income scale.
  • You apply for a child with a disability and currently receive the Child Disability Benefit. This application is only for your child.
  • You are a government or privately sponsored refugee.
  • You have current Canadian Ukraine Emergency Travel Authorization (CUAET).
  • You have an approved agency referral; no household application is required.

You must also live in Burnaby.

Amount or value

$250 credit per youth, adult or senior, $500 per child, plus an annual Be Active Pass for each family member

How to apply

  1. For a household application, download and print the FAIR Play application or pick up a copy at a Burnaby recreation or culture facility
  2. Choose the qualifying route and gather the supporting documents listed on the form
  3. For a household application, complete the household information and submit using the City's instructions
  4. For an approved agency referral, no household application is required
  5. Instead, your approved referral agency worker can complete and submit a FAIR Play Agency Referral Form directly to the FAIR Play Office on your behalf
  6. After approval, use the Be Active Pass and apply the recreation credit during online or staff-assisted registration

What you may need

  • Completed FAIR Play application for the household application route
  • FAIR Play Agency Referral Form submitted by your approved referral agency worker instead of a household application for the agency-referral route
  • Proof of Burnaby residence
  • Income, assistance, child-disability, refugee, current CUAET or agency-referral documentation for the chosen route

Practical tips

  • Burnaby Community Services can help with translation, documents and the application
  • Credit expires after one year and cannot be paid out as cash
  • During online registration, check that the recreation-credit box is applied to the balance

Timing

Apply once per year; ask the City for current processing time
