WorkBC Employment Services

BC's free public employment service. Help finding work, possible funding support for training, and possible financial supports, with services built specifically for people with disabilities.

What it is

WorkBC centre services and possible funding:

  • Free employment services including job-search help, work experience and wage subsidies.
  • Funding support for training and employment-related disability supports may be available, typically for clients with an employment plan.
  • A WorkBC counsellor reviews eligibility and builds the plan with you.

Good to know

If you have a disability, job search help includes working with employers to shape or customize jobs to meet your needs. Funding for assistive technology is covered by a separate program in this catalog: WorkBC Assistive Technology Services.

What you must meet

Check whether you are eligible by applying online at apply.workbc.ca. Staff can support you in completing your application.

What this guide can't tell you: whether you will be approved. The program decides, often using details we do not ask for.

Amount or value

Free employment services, including work experience and wage subsidies. Funding support for training, transportation, dependent care and employment-related disability supports may be available, typically for clients with an employment plan.

How to apply

  1. Get a Basic BCeID or set up online access with a BC Services Card
  2. Register for Online Employment Services and select WorkBC Employment Services
  3. Complete the application, including work status, barriers and the WorkBC centre you want to use
  4. Upload requested documents and communicate with the centre through the secure portal
  5. Meet an employment counsellor to confirm eligibility and create a personal action plan

What you may need

  • Valid Social Insurance Number
  • Information showing you are legally allowed to work in B.C.
  • Work and education history
  • Information about disability-related employment barriers if requesting specialized supports

Practical tips

  • You do not need a phone number or a fixed mailing address to submit the online application
  • Centre staff can help you complete the application
  • People with disabilities can qualify in more work or student situations. These include being at risk of losing a job or being in the final year of school
  • Financial supports are typically for clients who are case managed through WorkBC. That means you have an employment plan and work with an employment specialist.
  • Dependent care covers childcare while you are job searching.
