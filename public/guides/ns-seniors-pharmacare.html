Nova Scotia · Health

Seniors' Pharmacare Program

Helps seniors with the cost of prescription drugs and some supplies if they have no other drug coverage. You pay a yearly premium and copayment, and the province covers the rest.

What it is

How it works:

  • It pays for drugs, supplies and related services listed in the Nova Scotia Formulary.
  • Your Health Card becomes your Pharmacare Card once enrolled.
  • The premium is based on household income; a calculator is on the page.
  • There is no cost to apply, but there is an annual premium.

Good to know

Both the premium and the copayment have annual maximums. Some members may get a reduced or waived premium.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You are 65 or older and live in Nova Scotia.
  • You have a Health Card that is not expired.
  • You do not have prescription drug coverage under other programs or plans.

Each senior applies individually, regardless of marital status.

What this guide can't tell you: whether you will be approved. The program decides, often using details we do not ask for.

Amount or value

Annual premium based on household income, with annual maximums

How to apply

  1. Review the Seniors' Pharmacare Program Guide.
  2. Complete the form online and check which supporting documents it needs.
  3. Submit the form and documents. You can apply any time.

What you may need

  • Health Card number for you and your spouse, if applicable.
  • Social Insurance Number for you and your spouse, if applicable.
  • Signatures from all adults, if applicable.

Practical tips

  • Renew by 1 April each year. Renewing on time is your responsibility.
  • It should take 1 to 2 weeks to hear back, and longer if more information is needed.
  • You should get an application package before your 65th birthday.
