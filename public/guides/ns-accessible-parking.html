Accessible Parking Permits and Plates

Permits and licence plates let you park in spaces set aside for people with disabilities. Permits have no fee. Plates carry regular registration fees.

What it is

What you get

  • A permit or plate that allows parking in any space for people with disabilities.
  • Permits can be permanent or temporary.
  • Permits are issued at all Registry of Motor Vehicles locations.
  • Plates suit passenger, light commercial and motorcycle vehicles.

Good to know

A qualified medical practitioner must certify your application. The doctor may charge a fee. Plates are for vehicles where the principal driver has the disability.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • A qualified medical practitioner certifies your application.
  • For plates, the principal driver of the vehicle is the person with the disability.

Permanent and temporary permits are available.

Amount or value

Permit: no fee

How to apply

  1. Ask a qualified medical practitioner to certify your application.
  2. For a permit, apply at any Registry of Motor Vehicles location.
  3. For plates, apply in person at a Registry of Motor Vehicles location or by mail to the Processing and Distribution Centre.
  4. Motorcycle plates go by mail, fax or drop-off.

What you may need

  • Medical practitioner certification
  • Statement of Insurance form for plates
  • Identification (see the Identification Requirements on the page)

Practical tips

  • Insurance information is needed each time plates are issued or renewed.
  • Motorcycle plates cannot be paid for with cash.
  • Faxed motorcycle applications must be paid by credit card.
