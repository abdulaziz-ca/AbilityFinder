Recreation Access Program and LEAP (Comox Valley)

LEAP gives Comox Valley residents with lower incomes 52 free drop-in visits a year and a $350 subsidy per family member. Courtenay residents also get RAP discounts on registered programs.

What it is

One application covers both programs for lower-income Comox Valley residents.

  • LEAP drop-in visits work at participating Comox Valley recreation centres.
  • LEAP: a $350 subsidy per family member for registered programs.
  • RAP for Courtenay residents: children get 75% off, up to $450 a year.
  • RAP: youth get 75% off, up to $400 a year.
  • RAP: adults get 50% off, up to $350 a year.

Good to know

RAP is for Courtenay residents only; LEAP is regional.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You live in the Comox Valley
  • Your household income is within the Statistics Canada after-tax low-income measure (LIM-AT)

RAP is for City of Courtenay residents; other Comox Valley residents use LEAP.

What this guide can't tell you: whether you will be approved. The program decides, often using details we do not ask for.

How to apply

  1. Download the combined Recreation Access and LEAP 2026 application form
  2. Complete it
  3. Take it to the Lewis or Florence Filberg centre with your documents
  4. Wait for staff to contact you when your recreation cards are ready

What you may need

  • Proof of Comox Valley residency, such as a utility bill or rental receipt
  • A B.C. Services Card for you and each family member applying

Practical tips

  • Drop-ins include swimming, fitness, the Wellness Centre, parent and tot programs, The LINC Youth Centre and skating.
  • A program summary sheet is on the page.
