Prescription Drug Program: Assurance Plan

Help for people and families whose eligible drug costs are high compared to their income. Your share depends on income and drug costs.

What it is

About this plan:

  • You pay a co-payment based on your income and drug costs.
  • Eligibility and co-payment are reassessed every six months.

Good to know

Eligibility and your share are checked again every six months.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You live in Newfoundland and Labrador.
  • Your eligible drug costs are above a set share of your net income.

The share depends on income, as listed in the tips.

What this guide can't tell you: whether you will be approved. The program decides, often using details we do not ask for.

How to apply

  1. Complete the application form, from a pharmacy or physician office.
  2. Or call and ask for a form by mail.
  3. Send the form with the needed documents.
  4. A letter of eligibility is mailed to you.

What you may need

  • Application form and the documents it asks for.

Practical tips

  • Drug costs must exceed 5% of net income if you earn below $40,000.
  • The share is 7.5% for $40,000 to under $75,000 and 10% for $75,000 to under $150,000.
  • Example: $30,000 income and $5,000 drug costs in the previous year means a 30% co-payment.
  • The letter arrives within 7 business days in 90 per cent of cases.
