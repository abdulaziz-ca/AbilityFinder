Nova Scotia Home Care

Home Care brings nursing and home support to your home through Nova Scotia Health. A Care Coordinator assesses what you need and what you can get.

What it is

Home Care helps you stay in your home and community.

  • Home support covers personal care, respite and essential housekeeping.
  • Nursing covers dressing changes, catheter care, IV therapy and palliative care.
  • The assessment sets the services you are eligible for.

Good to know

Home Care adds to the help you already have from family, friends or community. It does not replace it.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You live in Nova Scotia and need help with care at home.
  • A Care Coordinator assesses you and decides which services you get.

Costs depend on your income and the services you need; some services, such as nursing, are free.

How to apply

  1. Call Continuing Care to talk about Home Care.
  2. A Care Coordinator arranges a time to meet and assess you.
  3. The Care Coordinator confirms costs during the assessment.

Practical tips

  • Fees depend on your income and the care you need, as the page says.
  • In some areas you may be put on a wait list.
  • Tell your Care Coordinator if your needs change.
  • The Care Coordinator can link you to meal programs and adult day programs.
