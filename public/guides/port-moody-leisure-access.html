Leisure Access Program (Port Moody)

Port Moody residents in financial need get 50% off admissions, fitness passes and some programs. The card lasts up to one year.

What it is

The Leisure Access Card gives half-price recreation for the whole family.

  • 50% off single drop-ins, fitness passes and skating or aquatics passes.
  • 50% off up to four registered programs per family member each year.
  • 50% off up to two weeks of all-day summer camp per person.
  • Free skate and helmet rentals.

Good to know

Your family income must fall between a minimum and a maximum that depend on family size. Free skate and helmet rentals are included.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You live in Port Moody
  • Your family income is above the minimum and below the maximum for your family size

For one person, income must be from $5,000 to $23,861.

Amount or value

50% off admissions, fitness passes and some programs

How to apply

  1. Complete the Leisure Access application form
  2. Attach the checklist documents
  3. Take it to the Recreation Complex or Kyle Centre
  4. Have a photo taken for each family member
  5. Reapply each year, at least a month before expiry

What you may need

  • Government photo ID
  • Utility bill in an adult's name
  • GST credit notice
  • Child benefit notices if you have dependents
  • Cheque stub from your income source
  • Notice of Assessment for each income earner

Practical tips

  • Private lessons and party packages are not included.
  • Students living at home must include their family's income.
