Halton SPLIT transit subsidy (Burlington, Oakville, Milton)

A Halton Region subsidy that pays part of your local bus tickets or monthly passes on Burlington, Oakville or Milton Transit. It is for Halton residents with a low income, including people on Ontario Works or ODSP.

What it is

SPLIT helps Halton residents with low incomes pay for local travel.

  • Use it for bus tickets or monthly passes from Burlington Transit, Oakville Transit or Milton Transit.
  • It also covers Handi-Van and Care-A-Van, but not GO Transit, taxis or ride-sharing.
  • You can transfer into Hamilton or Peel Region if you finish the trip within two hours.

Good to know

If you qualify, you are eligible for one year, then you re-apply. The page does not give the subsidy amount. Ask the Region what it covers for your age group and city.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You live in Halton
  • You are a student, adult or senior with a low income, or you get Ontario Works or ODSP

In Halton Hills the subsidy is for ActiVan or the Taxi Scrip Program instead of buses.

Amount or value

Covers part of the cost of bus tickets or monthly passes

How to apply

  1. Check your eligibility with the SPLIT online tool. You do not need an account to check
  2. Apply online with a Halton 311 account, or call 311
  3. Send proof of address, income, date of birth and status in Canada within 10 business days, or the application is closed
  4. Staff contact you if they need more information, then tell you if you qualify
  5. Buy your pass or tickets: Burlington Transit at Burlington GO Station (2101 Fairview St.), Milton Town Hall (150 Mary St.) or Oakville Transit (430 Wyecroft Rd.)

What you may need

  • Proof of address for you and your spouse, such as a driver's licence or utility bill
  • A Notice of Assessment for every adult in your household
  • Proof of status in Canada and date of birth for you, your spouse and your children

Practical tips

  • Only apply when your documents are ready. Missing documents close the application after 10 business days.
  • Upload documents online, mail them to Halton Regional Centre (1151 Bronte Road, Oakville) or drop them off in person.
