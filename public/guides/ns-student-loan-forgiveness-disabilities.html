Nova Scotia · Education

Student loan forgiveness for students with disabilities

Students who need more time to finish a program because of a disability can have loans forgiven for more years of study. You do not apply.

What it is

How it works

  • It works differently for degrees than for diplomas or certificates.
  • It builds on the regular loan forgiveness programs.
  • The department checks you when you graduate.

Good to know

All students are automatically assessed for loan forgiveness when they graduate.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • Nova Scotia Student Assistance counts you as a student with a disability.
  • You studied full-time at a reduced rate of 40% to 60% of a standard course load.
  • You finish a degree within 10 years or a diploma or certificate within 6 years.

Other rules match the regular Nova Scotia Student Loan Forgiveness Program.

Amount or value

Loans forgiven for more years of study; no dollar amount published

How to apply

  1. Check that you qualify as a student with a disability under Student Assistance criteria.
  2. Finish your program within the time limit.
  3. The department assesses you at graduation.

Practical tips

  • Check the regular forgiveness rules for your type of program.
