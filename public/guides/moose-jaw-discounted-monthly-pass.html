Moose Jaw Discounted Monthly Bus Pass

A discounted Moose Jaw monthly bus pass for people on social assistance. You show proof that you are in an eligible program when you buy it.

What it is

The City partners with the Ministry of Social Services to sell monthly passes at a lower price.

  • The discounted monthly pass costs $25.
  • The fares table lists the discounted price for fixed route and paratransit.

Good to know

The pass looks the same as other passes. Proof of the program must be shown at the time you buy a pass.

What you must meet

The program requires at least one of these:

  • You are in Saskatchewan Income Support (SIS)
  • You are in the Saskatchewan Employment Incentive (SEI)
  • You are in Saskatchewan Assured Income Disability (SAID)
  • You are in the Saskatchewan Employment Supplement (SES)

Your proof must show your name, the program name and the date for the month you buy.

Amount or value

$25 monthly pass

How to apply

  1. Get proof of your program, such as a cheque stub, program letter or bank statement
  2. Buy the pass in person with your proof
  3. Ask the Transit office if you have questions

What you may need

  • A cheque stub, a letter from the program or a bank statement with your name, the program name and the date for the month of purchase.

Practical tips

  • Passes are sold at City Hall, Yara Centre and Kinsmen Sportsplex.
