Ontario Electricity Support Program (OESP)

A monthly credit on your electricity bill for lower-income households. The amount depends on how many people live in the home and your combined after-tax household income.

What it is

The OESP puts an ongoing monthly credit directly on your electricity bill.

  • For example, a 4-person home with $65,000 a year after tax gets a $40 credit each month.
  • The credit chart covers household after-tax amounts up to $71,000 a year.
  • Higher credits apply for Indigenous households, electric heating, or certain medical devices.
  • Credits last 2 years before you reapply, or 5 years if you are 65+ or get a CPP disability pension.

Good to know

Households using certain electricity-intensive medical devices may get a higher credit, between $52 and $113 a month. People on Ontario Works or ODSP qualify automatically and apply through their caseworker.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You are the customer of an electricity utility, or a unit sub-meter provider bills you for your own meter.
  • Your household size and combined after-tax income are within the program's levels.

Ontario Works and ODSP recipients automatically qualify and must contact their caseworker to apply.

What this guide can't tell you: whether you will be approved. The program decides, often using details we do not ask for.

Amount or value

$35 to $75 a month on your bill; $52 to $113 for some households

How to apply

  1. Gather your electricity bill, the names and birthdates of everyone in your home, and SINs for adults
  2. Apply online at OESP.ca, by mail, or with help from a free intake agency
  3. Sign the consent form that lets the CRA verify your household income for the program
  4. Wait about 4 to 6 weeks for a decision; the credit appears on your bill about 6 to 8 weeks after approval

What you may need

  • Your electricity bill
  • Names and birthdates of everyone living in your home, as registered with the CRA
  • Social Insurance Numbers or temporary tax numbers for household members 18 and older

Practical tips

  • If you have not filed a tax return recently, or your situation changed, apply through an intake agency and bring proof of income.
  • If you already get OESP, you must reapply to get a higher credit under the newer income levels.
  • If you qualify for OESP, you may also apply for emergency grants through the Low-income Energy Assistance Program (LEAP).
