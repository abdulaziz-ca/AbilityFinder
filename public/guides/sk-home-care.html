Saskatchewan · Home support

Home Care and Individualized Funding for Home Care

The Saskatchewan Health Authority provides home care, such as nursing, homemaking and meals, based on assessed need. Some services have fees based on income, and you can apply for a subsidy.

What it is

Home care helps people stay independent at home.

  • Services include assessment, nursing, homemaking, personal care, respite and meals.
  • Nursing assessment, therapy and volunteer services are free to clients.
  • Fees for some services depend on your income and services used.
  • Individualized funding does not cover nursing or therapies.

Good to know

Individualized funding lets you arrange and manage your own support services if you are assessed as eligible.

What you must meet

The program requires at least one of these:

  • You need home care and the health authority assesses your need for services.
  • For individualized funding, you must be eligible for home care support services and need long-term supportive care.
  • For individualized funding, you must also be able to manage the funding and willing to accept the responsibilities.

The health authority decides your services and funding level after an assessment.

Amount or value

Services based on assessed need; fees based on income

How to apply

  1. Contact the Saskatchewan Health Authority Home Care Program in your community.
  2. They assess your care needs.
  3. They set the type of service and funding level.
  4. For individualized funding, sign an agreement with the health authority.

What you may need

  • Signed individualized funding agreement, if you choose that option

Practical tips

  • Individualized funding means you hire, schedule and pay staff yourself.
  • You must have a backup care plan that does not rely on home care.
  • Hiring family members is not permitted.
