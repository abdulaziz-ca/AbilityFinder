Program and Admission Fee Subsidy (Pitt Meadows)

Pitt Meadows residents with a low income can get discounted recreation program and admission fees for one year. You pay part of each fee.

What it is

Council Policy C083 sets up a fee subsidy for local residents who cannot afford full fees.

  • It covers program fees and admission fees.
  • Approval lasts one year, and you can reapply each year.
  • Everyone still pays a portion of the fee.

Good to know

The policy does not state the discount amount. Temporary residents, including people on study or work permits, are excluded.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You live in Pitt Meadows
  • Your income is at or below the federal low-income cut-off
  • Your documents show at least $5,000 of income

Visitors and people on a study or work permit are excluded.

What this guide can't tell you: whether you will be approved. The program decides, often using details we do not ask for.

How to apply

  1. Gather proof that you live in Pitt Meadows
  2. Gather proof that your income meets the cut-off
  3. Complete the Fee Subsidy Application Form
  4. Reapply each year with the same documents

What you may need

  • Proof of local residence
  • Income documents showing you meet the cut-off and have at least $5,000 of income

Practical tips

  • Business class immigrants, investors and entrepreneurs are also excluded.
