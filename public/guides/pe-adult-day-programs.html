Prince Edward Island · Community supports

Adult Day Programs

A safe, supervised social outlet for seniors, and a break for caregiving families. Lunch and snacks are included.

What it is

What the programs offer:

  • Activities such as arts and crafts, games and theme-based activities.
  • Some locations offer personal care services.
  • Caregivers get time to run errands, work or rest.
  • A French-language program is offered in Abram Village.

Good to know

A coordinator assesses your eligibility, and you pay for your own transportation.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • The page describes the program as being for seniors.
  • A coordinator completes an assessment to confirm you are eligible.

The page does not list other criteria.

What this guide can't tell you: whether you will be approved. The program decides, often using details we do not ask for.

How to apply

  1. Contact the Home Care office in your area.
  2. You, family, a care provider or a family doctor can refer you.
  3. A coordinator completes an assessment of your eligibility.

What you may need

  • Home Care Referral Form.

Practical tips

  • Hours are generally 8 a.m. to 4 p.m. on set days.
  • Souris and St. Peters Bay (902) 687-7096; Montague (902) 838-0786.
  • Charlottetown (902) 368-4790; Summerside and Abram Village (902) 888-8440; Alberton (902) 859-8730.
  • Contact the program of interest for more details.
