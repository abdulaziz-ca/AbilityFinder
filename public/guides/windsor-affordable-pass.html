Affordable Pass Program (Windsor)

A fee subsidy for Windsor-Essex residents and households living on a low income. For approved applicants, Transit Windsor publishes a $63 adult or $43.50 youth 30-day bus pass, before taxes.

What it is

The Affordable Pass Program lowers bus-pass costs for approved Windsor-Essex residents living on a low income.

  • Transit Windsor's APP prices are $63 for an adult 30-day pass and $43.50 for a youth 30-day pass, before taxes.
  • Transit Windsor's regular Smart Pass prices are $124 for an adult aged 20 to 59, $85 for a youth aged 13 to 19, and $63 for a senior aged 60 and over.
  • Transit Windsor charges $3.95 for a single adult, senior or youth fare, including a 2-hour transfer.
  • The APP adult 30-day price and Transit Windsor's regular senior 30-day price are both $63, so the APP is worth less to a rider who is already 60 or over.
  • Other municipalities may have different eligibility criteria; contact your local municipal transit department if you live elsewhere in Windsor-Essex.

Good to know

These are Transit Windsor's prices. Eligibility criteria may differ across municipalities, so if you live elsewhere in Windsor-Essex contact your local municipal transit department. Funding is first-come, first-served, and all approvals are determined by the local transit department subject to local demands and budgets.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • Residents who have lived within Windsor-Essex County for at least 30 days and who are able to demonstrate residency
  • A combined household income that is at, or below, the Market Basket Measure (MBM) thresholds

The programme page does not list the MBM thresholds; for further details it directs you to contact your local municipal transit department. International students, full-time post-secondary students and foreign workers are not eligible, although exceptions may apply within municipalities. Eligibility criteria may differ across municipalities.

How to apply

  1. Ask your local municipal transit department for the Market Basket Measure threshold for your family size — the programme page does not list it
  2. Gather your proof of residency and your combined-household-income documents — you must also have lived within Windsor-Essex County for at least 30 days
  3. Apply through your local municipal transit department; funding is available on a first-come, first-served basis
  4. If Transit Windsor approves you, get the APP pass at the Windsor International Transit Terminal, 300 Chatham Street West — it is available only there, with proof of identification
  5. Tell your municipality about any change to financial, residency or other circumstances within 30 days of the change taking effect

What you may need

  • Preferred proof of income: a Canada Child Benefit notice, a Notice of Assessment, or proof of Ontario Works or Ontario Disability Support Program benefits
  • One proof of residency: Ontario Driver's Licence, Ontario Health Card, Ontario Photo Card, current utility bill, property tax bill, lease or rental agreement, mortgage statement, or current bank statement
  • Proof of identification when getting a Transit Windsor APP pass at the Windsor International Transit Terminal
  • Additional documentation may be requested
  • If you cannot provide the required documents, or your circumstances recently changed in a way they do not reflect, a Letter of Endorsement may be used. It should be on professional letterhead, include the referring agency's contact information and the client's full name, outline the benefits the subsidy would provide, and indicate the barriers preventing application. Acceptance is at the discretion of the municipality

Practical tips

  • Funding availability is based on a first-come, first-served basis.
  • Access to the discount is available per eligible person for a 12-month period from the date of approval.
  • All approvals are determined by the local transit department and are subject to local demands and budgets.
  • If you do not report a change to financial, residency or other circumstances within 30 days of it taking effect, the provider reserves the right to terminate eligibility.
  • Eligibility is non-transferable. Passes cannot be sold, transferred, traded, given away or shared.
  • The Transit Windsor APP pass is available only at the Windsor International Transit Terminal, 300 Chatham Street West, with proof of identification.
