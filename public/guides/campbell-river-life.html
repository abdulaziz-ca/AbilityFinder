L.I.F.E. Program (Campbell River)

The L.I.F.E. card gives Campbell River residents who face financial challenges 52 free drop-in admissions. It also gives 50% off fees for three City recreation programs.

What it is

The L.I.F.E. card lowers recreation costs for Campbell River residents with limited funds.

  • 52 free drop-in admissions, including Centennial Pool, the weight room and drop-in classes.
  • 50% off fees for three City of Campbell River recreation programs.
  • Some exceptions apply to the discount, such as Summerside Express summer camps.
  • Every family member can have one LIFE card.

Good to know

Some programs are excluded, including Summerside Express summer camps. Each family member can get a card. Cards last one calendar year.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You live in Campbell River
  • You meet the income requirements on the application form

The income limits are on the application form, not on the page.

Amount or value

52 free drop-ins and 50% off three programs

How to apply

  1. Get the form at the Sportsplex or Community Centre, or print it from the page
  2. Fill in the whole form
  3. Hand it to office staff at the Sportsplex or Community Centre with your proof documents
  4. Show your card whenever you want the reduced fee

What you may need

  • Proof of residency with your current address, such as a driver's licence or current utility bill
  • Proof of income, such as a Notice of Assessment, EI, disability or pension stubs

Practical tips

  • Applications that are not filled in fully are not accepted.
  • A card is cancelled if the information on the form is false.
  • Cards are not transferable and have no cash value.
