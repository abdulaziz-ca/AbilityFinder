Prince Edward Island · Health

Generic Drug Program

Limits what you pay for eligible generic prescriptions if you are under 65 and have no drug insurance.

What it is

What the program covers:

  • It covers eligible generic prescription drugs on the provincial formulary.
  • Some drugs are approved by special authorization only.
  • Drugs not on the PEI Pharmacare Formulary are not covered.
  • Manual claims are allowed only if a PEI pharmacy cannot dispense the drug.

Good to know

Page published June 25, 2026. Brand name, diabetes, narcotic and controlled, compounded and over-the-counter drugs are not covered.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You have no private drug insurance coverage.
  • You are under 65.
  • You live in PEI as defined by the Drug Cost Assistance Act.
  • You have a valid PEI Health Card and an approved application.

What this guide can't tell you: whether you will be approved. The program decides, often using details we do not ask for.

Amount or value

Out-of-pocket cost capped at $19.95 per generic prescription

How to apply

  1. Complete the online application, or fill in the paper form.
  2. Hand in the paper form at Access PEI, a pharmacy, a doctor's office or a pharmacare office.
  3. You need an approved application to be covered.

What you may need

  • Completed Generic Drug Program application form.

Practical tips

  • The most you pay per eligible generic prescription is $19.95.
  • Coverage is not retroactive; past purchases are not repaid.
  • Diabetes drugs are covered through a different public program.
