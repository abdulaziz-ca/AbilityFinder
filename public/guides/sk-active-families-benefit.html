Saskatchewan · Money (for parents)

Active Families Benefit

A refundable provincial tax benefit toward sport, recreation and culture fees for your child. The amount is higher for a child eligible for the federal Disability Tax Credit.

What it is

The benefit helps with registration or membership fees for a child's activity.

  • Up to $400 per child if your child qualifies for the Disability Tax Credit.
  • The activity must include instruction and supervision.
  • The activity must need registration and a paid registration or membership fee.
  • The provider must be located in Saskatchewan.

Good to know

For 2024 and earlier, it was up to $150 per child, or $200 if eligible for the Disability Tax Credit, with income of $60,000 or less.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You are the parent or legal guardian of a child 18 or younger at the end of the tax year.
  • Your family's adjusted income is $120,000 or less.
  • Your child took part in an eligible sport, recreation or culture program.

If parents live apart, only the parent who mainly cares for the child can claim.

Amount or value

Up to $300 per child, or up to $400 per child who qualifies for the federal Disability Tax Credit (2025 and later)

How to apply

  1. File your Saskatchewan personal income tax return for the year you are claiming.
  2. Complete the Active Families Benefit section when you file.
  3. Keep your receipts for six years in case you are asked to check your claim.

What you may need

  • Receipts for registration or membership fees (keep them; do not send them with your return)

Practical tips

  • Claim the fee in the year you pay it, not the year the activity takes place.
  • Only one parent can claim a child in a tax year, and each expense only once.
  • Equipment, uniforms, school-curriculum or child-care-hours activities, and fees paid with fundraising credits are not covered.
  • For help with the form, call the Canada Revenue Agency at 1-800-387-1193.
