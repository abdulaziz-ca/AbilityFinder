Community Bus Pass (Cornwall)

An affordable Cornwall Transit pass for people who get ODSP or Ontario Works. There are 175 passes, given first come, first served. The pass also works on Specialized Service if you have qualified for it.

What it is

A lower-cost pass for people on ODSP or Ontario Works.

  • Qualified Specialized Service users can use it on that service too.

Good to know

You need documents each time you buy a pass. The page does not give the price. Contact Cornwall Transit to ask.

What you must meet

The program requires at least one of these:

  • You get benefits from the Ontario Disability Support Program
  • You get Ontario Works assistance

Amount or value

Affordable bus pass; 175 passes available

At a glance

Difficulty
Easy
Application
In-person visit with proof

How to apply

  1. Go to the Cornwall Transit office at 863 Second St W
  2. Show your photo ID and proof of benefits
  3. Buy the pass

What you may need

  • Photo ID
  • An original or photocopied Ontario Works or ODSP cheque stub, or a drug or dental card

Practical tips

  • Bring proof every time you buy a pass.
