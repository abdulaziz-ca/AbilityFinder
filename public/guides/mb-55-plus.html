Manitoba · Income support

55 PLUS Program

Manitobans aged 55 and over with lower incomes can get a quarterly payment. The maximum is $161.80 for a single person and $173.90 for each person in a couple.

Manitoba is new here: please double-check before you rely on a detail

We added Manitoba in October 2026, while some of its programs are changing. Each program here comes from its official page, but some details are not published yet, so confirm with the program before you count on an amount or rule.

  • Manitoba is moving people with a disability from Employment and Income Assistance (EIA) to the new Manitoba Supports for Persons with Disabilities program, and that program's page does not publish payment amounts yet.
  • Since September 1, 2026, EIA covers only emergency dental care and points people to the federal Canadian Dental Care Plan.
  • Some income limits are old (home adaptation limits are from 2019) or change every year (Rent Assist and Winnipeg programs).
  • Winnipeg Transit Plus has proposed new eligibility rules that are not in effect.
  • City programs are listed for Winnipeg only so far.

What it is

This income supplement adds to the income of lower income older adults.

  • Payments are mailed four times a year, in late April, July, October and January.
  • The Junior Component covers people with partial benefits at lower incomes.
  • The Senior Component depends on family make-up and OAS benefit levels.
  • The benefit year runs from April 1 to March 31.

Good to know

Partial benefits go to singles with income up to $9,746.40 and couples up to $16,255.20. Apply anyway if you are slightly above.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You are 55 or older and live in Manitoba.
  • You have a valid Manitoba Health registration number.
  • Your net income from last year is within the allowable ranges.
  • You do not get EIA, unless you get only its health benefits.

If you get federal Old Age Security benefits, your eligibility is worked out for you.

Amount or value

Up to $161.80 a quarter (single)

How to apply

  1. Check if you already get OAS; if so, you do not need to apply.
  2. If not, download the application form.
  3. Print, complete and bring it to the office or fax it.

What you may need

  • 55 PLUS application
  • Direct deposit form

Practical tips

  • You can apply at any time during the year.
  • Without OAS benefits, you must apply again every year.
  • Some deductions from gross income are allowed.
