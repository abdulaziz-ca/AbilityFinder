Manitoba · Employment

Employability Assistance for People with Disabilities (EAPD)

Employment services for adults with disabilities who want to prepare for, find and keep a job. Help comes through a vocational counsellor or a designated agency.

Manitoba is new here: please double-check before you rely on a detail

We added Manitoba in October 2026, while some of its programs are changing. Each program here comes from its official page, but some details are not published yet, so confirm with the program before you count on an amount or rule.

  • Manitoba is moving people with a disability from Employment and Income Assistance (EIA) to the new Manitoba Supports for Persons with Disabilities program, and that program's page does not publish payment amounts yet.
  • Since September 1, 2026, EIA covers only emergency dental care and points people to the federal Canadian Dental Care Plan.
  • Some income limits are old (home adaptation limits are from 2019) or change every year (Rent Assist and Winnipeg programs).
  • Winnipeg Transit Plus has proposed new eligibility rules that are not in effect.
  • City programs are listed for Winnipeg only so far.

What it is

EAPD offers a wide range of employment-focused services for adults with disabilities.

  • It helps you prepare for work, get a job and keep it.
  • Supported Employment Agencies help find real work in integrated settings.
  • Agencies also give you and your employer ongoing support.
  • The aim is work paying minimum wage or better, or the standard pay for the position.

Good to know

The page gives no dollar amount. Who serves you depends on your type of disability.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You live in Manitoba and have an intellectual, psychiatric, learning or physical disability.
  • You are 16 years of age or older.
  • You can legally work in Manitoba on a permanent basis.
  • You show a willingness to prepare for, get and keep a job.

The page does not say how the disability must be shown.

How to apply

  1. Find your service line by disability type on the program page.
  2. Intellectual, psychiatric or learning disability: email EAPD@gov.mb.ca to reach a vocational counsellor.
  3. Visual disability: contact Vision Loss Rehabilitation Manitoba.
  4. Spinal cord injury: contact SCI Manitoba.
  5. Other physical disability, including Deaf or hard of hearing: contact Manitoba Possible.

Practical tips

  • Employment and Training Centres deliver services under your Vocational Plan.
  • 211 Manitoba is an online database of government, health and social services.
