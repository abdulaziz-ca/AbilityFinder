Newfoundland and Labrador · Health (for children)

Direct Home Services Program

Free home visits to teach skills to young children who show, or are at risk for, significant developmental delay. Includes an autism program.

What it is

About the program:

  • A child management specialist visits the home to teach skills and behaviour strategies.
  • Visits are weekly for six months and then every two weeks, depending on need.
  • Children are reassessed every six months.
  • An intensive behaviour program is offered for children with autism up to Grade three.

Good to know

A diagnosis of autism spectrum disorder by a qualified professional is needed for the intensive program.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • Your child is an infant or preschool age.
  • Your child shows, or is at risk for, significant developmental delay.
  • A parent or caregiver must be present at home visits and commit to working on the program.

The intensive autism program also needs a diagnosis from a qualified professional.

What this guide can't tell you: whether you will be approved. The program decides, often using details we do not ask for.

Amount or value

No cost to the family

How to apply

  1. A family or service provider refers the child to the regional health authority.
  2. A specialist visits within one month to explain the program and check eligibility.
  3. Visits start when a space opens.

Practical tips

  • Refer as soon as a delay is suspected.
  • A service provider needs the consent of the family to refer.
