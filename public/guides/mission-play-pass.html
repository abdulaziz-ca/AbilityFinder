PLAY Pass (Mission)

The PLAY Pass gives free admissions to the Mission Leisure Centre. You qualify with a low income or by being on income assistance. There is a small admin fee.

What it is

The PLAY Pass removes the entry cost at the Leisure Centre for lower-income residents.

  • Free admissions to the Leisure Centre.
  • Based on the CRA low income threshold or income assistance.
  • The page does not say how many free visits you get.

Good to know

The fee is $5 for an individual and $10 for a family. Processing can take up to three weeks.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You have lived in Mission for at least 3 months
  • Your income is at or below the CRA low income thresholds, or you get Income Assistance

Income Assistance means the provincial ministry or the Federal Social Development Program.

How to apply

  1. Apply online at mission.ca/playpass or in person at the Leisure Centre
  2. Pay the admin fee when your application is approved
  3. Wait up to three weeks for processing
