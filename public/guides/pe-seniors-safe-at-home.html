Seniors Safe @ Home Program

Pays for minor home changes that help low and moderate income seniors with daily living stay in their home. This brochure lists a lifetime grant of up to $5,000, set by your income.

What it is

What the grant covers:

  • Changes must be housing related and permanently installed.
  • They must improve access to basic facilities or physical safety.
  • The grant is $5,000 at $35,000 income or less, down to $1,000 at $47,001 to $50,000.
  • Portable devices are not eligible.

Good to know

This brochure shows no date. The July 2021 PEI renovation brochure lists up to $10,000 for this program, so confirm the current amount and income limits.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You are 60 or older on the date you apply.
  • Your combined income is $50,000 or less.
  • You, your spouse or a family member owns the home. Rentals are not eligible.
  • The property is your principal residence.
  • The changes relate to your loss of ability and keep you safe at home.

You must be a Canadian citizen or landed immigrant and PEI permanent resident, not at the maximum grant.

What this guide can't tell you: whether you will be approved. The program decides, often using details we do not ask for.

How to apply

  1. Use the Self Assessment Guide to pick the changes you need.
  2. Fill out the application form.
  3. Send it in with your documents.
  4. Finish the changes within 90 days of approval, then send invoices for payment.

What you may need

  • Canada Revenue Agency Statement of Consent, or your latest Notice of Assessment.
  • Two detailed estimates for the changes.

Practical tips

  • Changes made before the approval date are not eligible.
  • The program works on a first come, first served basis.
  • You can apply more than once, up to the lifetime maximum.
