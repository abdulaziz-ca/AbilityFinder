Region of Waterloo · Transit

MobilityPLUS taxi coupons (Grand River Transit)

MobilityPLUS customers can buy a taxi coupon book for $30. It holds $60 in $5, $2 and $1 coupons. The coupons have punched holes to show each value.

What it is

MobilityPLUS customers can buy $60 of taxi coupons for $30.

  • Each book contains $5, $2 and $1 coupons, with holes punched to identify each value.
  • Coupons do not expire and are not refundable.
  • Up to 30 books may be purchased between January 1 and December 31.
  • Seasonal customers may buy only during active registration periods.
  • Coupons are personal, cannot be resold or transferred, and lost or stolen coupons are not reimbursed. Proven abuse results in privileges being revoked.

Good to know

Coupons are for the MobilityPLUS customer's personal use. They cannot be resold or transferred. Proven abuse results in privileges being revoked, and lost or stolen coupons are not reimbursed. Coupons do not expire and are not refundable.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You are a MobilityPLUS customer who lives and travels in Cambridge, Kitchener and Waterloo
  • You are not a MobilityPLUS North Dumfries or Kiwanis Transit customer (they are not eligible for taxi coupons)

Customers may purchase up to 30 coupon books between January 1 and December 31. Customers with seasonal eligibility may only buy during active registration periods.

What this guide can't tell you: whether you will be approved. The program decides, often using details we do not ask for.

How to apply

  1. Buy by phone at 519-585-7597 ext. 7347, Monday to Friday from 8:30 a.m. to 4 p.m.; a credit card is required
  2. Or buy in person at a GRT customer service location
  3. Or mail a cheque payable to Region of Waterloo to 250 Strasburg Rd., Kitchener, ON N2E 3M6, write your MobilityPLUS ID number on the front, and allow two weeks
  4. When booking a taxi, tell the company you are using taxi coupons and say if you use a mobility device so it can arrange a suitable vehicle

What you may need

  • Your MobilityPLUS ID number for mail orders
  • A credit card for telephone orders, or a cheque payable to Region of Waterloo for mail orders

Practical tips

  • Orders of three books or fewer go by Canada Post. Orders of four or more go by courier between 9 a.m. and 5 p.m. You must be home to accept the delivery.
  • The taxi companies named are Golden Triangle Taxi in Cambridge, and City Cabs, United Taxi and Waterloo Taxi in Kitchener and Waterloo.
  • Keep your coupons safe. Lost or stolen coupons are not paid back.
