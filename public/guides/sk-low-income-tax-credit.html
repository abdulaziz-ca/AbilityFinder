Saskatchewan Low-Income Tax Credit

A refundable, non-taxable payment for Saskatchewan residents with low and modest incomes. You do not need to pay income tax to get it. There is no application.

What it is

For July 1, 2025 to June 30, 2026, the maximum amounts are:

  • $429 each for the basic adult and the spousal or equivalent component.
  • $169 per child, to a maximum of $338 per family.
  • Up to $1,196 a year for families earning less than $38,590.

Good to know

The benefit is reduced by 2.88 per cent of family net income above $38,590. Amounts are indexed to inflation each year.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You file an income tax return as a Saskatchewan resident.
  • You meet the income and family criteria.
  • The Canada Revenue Agency decides if you are entitled.

You do not need to owe income tax to get the credit.

Amount or value

Up to $1,196 a year for families earning less than $38,590 (July 1, 2025 to June 30, 2026)

How to apply

  1. File your income tax and benefit return.
  2. The CRA works out if you are entitled and tells you.
  3. The CRA pays the credit every three months.

What you may need

  • Your income tax and benefit return

Practical tips

  • There is no need to apply for this credit.
  • It is paid with the federal Goods and Services Tax Credit.
