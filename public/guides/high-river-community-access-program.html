Community Access Program (High River)

A CAP card gives lower-income High River residents half price on Town programs and on admissions and passes at the Bob Snodgrass Recreation Complex and Cargill Field House. It also gives a free library card and local discounts.

What it is

A card with recreation discounts and local business discounts.

  • 50 per cent off all programs in the Community Program Guide.
  • 50 per cent off admissions and passes at the two recreation facilities.
  • A free High River library card.
  • Discounts at some local health, music, thrift and pharmacy businesses.

Good to know

Apply at the Town of High River's FCSS Resource Centre, and renew your card by June 30 every year.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You live in High River
  • Your household income is at or below the limit for your household size

The limits run from $45,000 for 1 person to $72,500 for 7 people.

Amount or value

50% off programs and recreation admissions

How to apply

  1. Pick up an application at the FCSS Resource Centre, 251 9 Avenue SW
  2. Bring your ID, plus proof of where you live and what you earn
  3. Renew the card by June 30 each year

What you may need

  • Proof you live in High River, such as ID or utility bills
  • ID for household members, such as health cards or birth certificates
  • A current Notice of Assessment, or 3 months of bank statements

Practical tips

  • Pharmacy discounts are for store-front items only, not prescriptions.
  • Staff are at the Resource Centre Monday to Friday, 9:00am to 4:00pm.
