Niagara Transit Benefit Pass

A discounted $50 monthly bus pass from Niagara Region Transit. It is for Niagara residents on Ontario Works or ODSP, or who qualify as low income. Full-time post-secondary students are not eligible.

What it is

This program gives eligible Niagara residents a discounted monthly bus pass.

  • The pass costs $50 a month.
  • It is for individuals and families on Ontario Works, ODSP, or who qualify based on low-income criteria.
  • You pay for the pass yourself.
  • If you use the Transit app, the approval lets you buy the $50 monthly pass in the app each month for one year.

Good to know

Niagara Region must approve low-income applications before you buy a pass. It will not reimburse passes you already bought. The page does not give the low-income limits. Ask the Region how they apply to you.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You are a Niagara resident
  • You get Ontario Works or ODSP, or you qualify as a low-income individual or household
  • You are not a full-time post-secondary student

OW and ODSP recipients have qualified automatically since July 1, 2025; others apply for an income check.

What this guide can't tell you: whether you will be approved. The program decides, often using details we do not ask for.

How to apply

  1. If you get Ontario Works or ODSP, you already qualify. Get your monthly payment statement from the mail or online through MyBenefits
  2. If you do not get OW or ODSP, apply online at owbenefits.niagararegion.ca. You can also call 905-641-9960 ext. 6072, fax 905-984-6759, or mail the Ontario Works Discretionary Benefit Team at 234 Bunting Rd., St. Catharines, L2R 6Z4
  3. A staff member will contact you to review your application
  4. If you applied on low income, wait for Niagara Region to approve you before you buy a pass. You then get a Transit Benefit Pass Approval Form for each eligible family member
  5. Get verified in person with your ID and your benefit statement or approval form. For the Transit app, go to the St. Catharines Bus Terminal or the Welland Bus Terminal and give a unique email address. For a physical pass, go to a Transit Benefit Pass outlet
  6. Pay $50 for the monthly pass

What you may need

  • One valid ID for each household member if you apply on low income: birth certificate, driver's licence, health card or citizenship document
  • Proof of income if you apply on low income, such as bank statements, pay stubs or pension stubs
  • If you get OW or ODSP: a recent benefit statement or proof of enrolment. OW clients can ask their caseworker for a proof of eligibility letter if they cannot get it online
  • If you applied on low income: the approved letter from Niagara Region's Social Assistance and Employment Opportunities office, or the Transit Benefit Pass Approval Form
  • A valid ID for each purchase. It must match your verification documents

Practical tips

  • Apply and get approved before you buy a pass. The Region does not reimburse passes you already bought.
  • At a pass outlet, only the verified person can buy the pass. Bring your documents and ID each time.
  • Be ready to pay when you get verified.
  • In the Transit app, the pass is not available until that month's payment has been made.
  • For help, email info@niagaratransit.ca or call 1-833-678-5463.
