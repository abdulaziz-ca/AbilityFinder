Designated Parking Permit

A permit lets you park in spaces set aside for people with disabilities. ResourceAbilities runs the program for the province.

What it is

What you get

  • A permit to park in designated spaces across the Island.
  • Permanent and temporary permits are available.

Good to know

The page does not list a fee or say how long a permit lasts. Ask ResourceAbilities when you apply.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You are unable to walk more than 75 metres without serious difficulty or danger to safety or health.
  • A medical professional signs your application.

Temporary permits are also available.

How to apply

  1. Ask a medical professional to sign the application.
  2. Bring the signed application to a ResourceAbilities office.
  3. Renew online each year if you held a permit the year before.

What you may need

  • Application signed by a medical professional

Practical tips

  • Renewals for 2027 open on or after November 10, 2026.
  • If you did not have a permit last year, contact the office to renew.
