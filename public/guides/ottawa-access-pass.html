Access pass — OC Transpo discount for Para Transpo customers (Ottawa)

A discounted monthly OC Transpo pass for registered Para Transpo customers. It gives unlimited travel on the O-Train and all buses except Para Transpo. It takes about a third off the cash fare on Para Transpo trips.

What it is

The Access pass is OC Transpo's discounted monthly pass for Para Transpo customers.

  • $43.25 a month.
  • Unlimited travel on the O-Train and all non-Para Transpo buses.
  • Saves one third of the cash-fare price on each Para Transpo trip.
  • A $2.75 top-up is charged for each Para Transpo ride.
  • ParaPay and ParaPass cannot be used on OC Transpo buses or the O-Train.

Good to know

Access pass holders pay a $2.75 top-up for each Para Transpo ride. If you also get ODSP, compare it with the Community Pass. The Community Pass costs the same $43.25 and includes Para Transpo with no top-up.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You are registered as a Para Transpo customer

You will be asked for your Para Transpo registration number. Discounted fares cannot be used when paying cash.

What this guide can't tell you: whether you will be approved. The program decides, often using details we do not ask for.

How to apply

  1. Register as a Para Transpo customer and note your registration number
  2. Set the Access discount at an OC Transpo Customer Service Centre, or by calling OC Transpo Customer Service
  3. Buy the monthly pass up to 14 days before the month begins

What you may need

  • Your Para Transpo registration number

Practical tips

  • If you also get ODSP, check the Community Pass first. It costs the same and has no Para Transpo top-up.
  • Fare capping means that paying as you go with Presto never costs more than $12.25 in a day. In a month, it never costs more than the monthly pass price.
