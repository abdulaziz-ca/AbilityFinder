New Brunswick · Housing

Public Housing (Housing NB)

Housing NB owns rental homes across the province for families and individuals with low-to-moderate incomes. Applicants are chosen based on demonstrated need.

What it is

Income ceilings by household

  • One bedroom, single person or couple: $45,000 in the eight major cities, $50,500 in rural areas.
  • Two bedrooms, living with another person: $57,500 urban, $59,500 rural.
  • Three bedrooms, living with more than 1 other person: $63,000 urban, $67,500 rural.
  • Social housing also includes not-for-profit and cooperative housing.

Good to know

Some exceptions to the income limits may apply. Call Housing NB to ask about your situation or to apply.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • Your household income is under the ceiling for your area and home size.
  • You live in crowded or inadequate housing.
  • Or you pay under 30% of income for rent, but adequate housing would cost 30% or more.
  • Or you pay 30% or more of income for rent, and adequate housing would too.

You must be in at least one of the three housing situations.

What this guide can't tell you: whether you will be approved. The program decides, often using details we do not ask for.

Amount or value

Income ceilings from $45,000 (urban) or $50,500 (rural) for a one-bedroom home

How to apply

  1. Check the income ceiling for your area and the bedrooms you need.
  2. Check you are in one of the three housing situations.
  3. Call Housing NB to apply.

Practical tips

  • Rural means living outside city limits.
  • Housing NB is open Monday to Friday, 8:15 a.m. to 4:30 p.m.
  • You can also email hnb.info@gnb.ca with questions.
