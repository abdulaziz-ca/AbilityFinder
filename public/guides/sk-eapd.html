Employability Assistance for Persons with Disabilities (EAPD)

Pays for disability-related supports that help you prepare for, find and keep work, or take needed training or education. Employers hiring you may also get help with accommodation costs.

What it is

EAPD helps people with disabilities move into work.

  • It funds supports to take part in training or education for work.
  • It funds supports to get and keep a job.
  • Funding may pay for an assessment of your disability's impact.
  • Employers may get help with costs like job coaching and technical supports.

Good to know

The page gives no dollar amount. Funding covers disability-related costs only.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You need specialized supports because of a disability to prepare for, get or keep work.
  • You are legally entitled to work in Canada and Saskatchewan.
  • You are at least 16 years of age.
  • You will give a self-declaration of your disability.
  • You will develop an approved career action plan or service plan.

The disability may be learning, developmental, mental health, physical or other, as you declare it.

Amount or value

Funding for disability-related costs; no amount published

How to apply

  1. Contact SaskJobs Career Services to learn about the program.
  2. Get your career action plan or service plan approved.
  3. The plan lists your career goals and needed supports.
