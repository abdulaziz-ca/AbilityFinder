Leisure Access Program (Prince George)

The Leisure Access Program gives 52 free public swims. After that it gives 75% off swim passes and 75% off swimming programs, up to a $50 credit. It is for Prince George residents with limited income.

What it is

The program lowers swimming costs for residents on a limited income.

  • 52 complimentary public swimming admissions.
  • 75% off all swim passes once the 52 free admissions are used.
  • 75% off swimming programs, such as lessons, up to a $50 credit.
  • Income under: $26,127 for 1 person, $32,525 for 2, $39,986 for 3.
  • Income under: $48,550 for 4 people, $55,064 for 5, $62,103 for 6.

Good to know

The program covers a variety of aquatic activities. Referral agencies can submit applications for their clients.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You live in Prince George (the City says “permanent residents of Prince George”)
  • Your annual income is below the Statistics Canada Low Income Guidelines, or you get a BC Seniors Supplement below them

Income limits are on the page by household size, from 1 to 6 people.

Amount or value

52 free swims, then 75% off swim passes

How to apply

  1. Fill in the LAP Participant Application form, online or on paper
  2. Get a paper copy at the Canfor Leisure Pool or P.G. Aquatic Centre if you prefer
  3. Include proof of yearly household income and photo ID
  4. Or ask an approved referral agency to apply for you with the LAP Referral Agency Application

What you may need

  • Proof of yearly household income
  • Photo ID

Practical tips

  • Applications are processed Monday to Friday, 9 a.m. to 4:30 p.m.
  • Processing can take up to 10 days, but it is typically a day or two on weekdays.
  • Agencies that are not yet approved can email leisureaccessprogram@princegeorge.ca to ask about becoming a referral agency.
