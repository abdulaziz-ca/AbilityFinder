Children's Rehabilitation Services (Children's Treatment Centres)

Therapy and family supports for children and youth with physical or developmental disabilities or communication difficulties. You do not need a formal referral.

What it is

Children's Treatment Centres and Surrey Place in Toronto offer these services.

  • Physiotherapy helps restore, maintain and make the most of a child's mobility and well-being.
  • Occupational therapy helps with self-care, productivity and leisure.
  • Speech and language therapy helps with oral motor and communication function.
  • SmartStart Hubs in Toronto give families one entry point to assessment and services.
  • Family supports include respite services and parent coaching.

Good to know

Children's Treatment Centres also give these services in schools to students in Ontario public schools. Students in publicly funded schools may use them until they finish school.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You are a child or youth up to 19 years old, or up to 21 if you are in school.
  • You have a physical disability, a developmental disability, or communication difficulties or disorders.

A formal referral is not needed, but education or health professionals may refer you.

Amount or value

Physiotherapy, occupational therapy and speech and language therapy

How to apply

  1. Find your area in the table of Children's Treatment Centres on the page.
  2. Contact your local centre, or Surrey Place in Toronto, to talk about your child's development.
  3. The centre can explain which services your child should get and how to reach them.
  4. If you already know what your child needs, you can contact providers directly.

What you may need

  • A formal referral is not required

Practical tips

  • Contact Empowered Kids Ontario (EKO) for more information about services and locations.
  • Children from birth until school may also qualify for preschool speech and language services.
  • You can also call your local ministry regional office for more information.
