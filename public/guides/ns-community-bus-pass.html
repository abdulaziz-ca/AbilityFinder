Community Bus Pass (Halifax and Cape Breton)

A free yearly bus pass for income assistance and Disability Support Program clients in the Halifax and Cape Breton regions.

What it is

Who else can get a pass

  • Cape Breton: spouses of Income Assistance clients and children 5 to 21.
  • Halifax: spouses and children 13 to 21 not in the Student Transit Pass Program.
  • Children 12 or younger ride Halifax Transit free; children 5 or younger ride Transit Cape Breton free.

Good to know

Eligible family members may get one too. The page covers only these two municipalities.

What you must meet

The program requires at least one of these:

  • You get Employment Support and Income Assistance and live in Halifax or Cape Breton Regional Municipality.
  • You are in the Disability Support Program and live in either municipality.

Spouses and some children of Income Assistance clients can also get a pass.

What this guide can't tell you: whether you will be approved. The program decides, often using details we do not ask for.

Amount or value

Free annual bus pass

Application
Visit an office for a photo; the pass is mailed.

How to apply

  1. Go to a department office in your municipality with your family members.
  2. The department takes your photo for the pass.
  3. The department mails the pass to your home.

Practical tips

  • Offices are open Monday to Friday, 8:30am to 4:30pm.
  • Tell your caseworker first if your address will change soon.
  • The photo is used only for your bus pass.
