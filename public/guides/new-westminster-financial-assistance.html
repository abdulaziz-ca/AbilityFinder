Financial Assistance Program (New Westminster)

Eligible New Westminster residents get a 50% discount on eligible registered programs, the Active 10 Visit pass and the Active 30 Day pass. You qualify by a social service agency referral or household income under $50,000.

What it is

The program reduces fees for New Westminster residents who show a referral or lower household income.

  • Every family member receives 1,000 credits; they reduce fees by up to 50% on eligible purchases.
  • Credits work through staff or your online account, and expire December 31.
  • $2 drop-in admissions are open to everyone, but they get no extra discount.

Good to know

Each family member gets 1,000 credits. They expire on December 31 each year, and you can then re-apply. Credits are non-transferable and have no cash value. You can buy one pass at a time.

What you must meet

The program requires at least one of these:

  • A social service agency completes the Financial Assistance Referral Form for you
  • Your household income is below $50,000

Either route also needs proof of New Westminster residency.

What this guide can't tell you: whether you will be approved. The program decides, often using details we do not ask for.

How to apply

  1. Choose a route: an agency referral, or proof that household income is below $50,000
  2. Bring your documents and proof of residency to Recreation Facility customer service staff during office hours
  3. Staff create accounts for you and eligible household family members
  4. Use your credits through staff or your online account
  5. After credits expire on December 31, apply again

What you may need

  • Proof of New Westminster residency
  • Agency route: the Financial Assistance Referral Form completed by a social service agency
  • Income route: a CRA tax assessment or a federal Child Benefit Notice, dated within the past 12 months

Practical tips

  • Registered programs are not eligible if they involve a third-party operator, are already subsidized, are private or semi-private lessons, or involve transportation costs.
  • An agency can also email the referral letter, and staff will contact you.
  • Credits expire December 31; you can then re-apply.
