Regina Discounted 31-Day Transit Pass

A $28 Regina Transit 31-day pass for people on SAID, SIS, SES or SEI. The City runs it with the Saskatchewan Ministry of Social Services.

What it is

The City of Regina and the Ministry of Social Services offer this pass to eligible low-income earners in Regina.

  • The pass costs $28 and covers 31 days.

Good to know

Call the Transit Information Centre to find out how to get the pass. Your first discounted pass must be bought at the Transit Information Centre.

What you must meet

The program requires at least one of these:

  • You are in Saskatchewan Assured Income for Disability (SAID)
  • You are in Saskatchewan Income Support (SIS)
  • You are in the Saskatchewan Employment Supplement (SES)
  • You are in the Saskatchewan Employment Incentive (SEI)

Amount or value

$28 for a 31-day pass

How to apply

  1. Check that you are in one of the four provincial programs
  2. Call the Transit Information Centre to ask how to get your pass
  3. Buy your first discounted pass at 2124 11th Avenue

What you may need

  • The page does not list documents. Ask the Transit Information Centre what to bring.

Practical tips

  • The Transit Information Centre is open Monday to Friday, 8:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m.
