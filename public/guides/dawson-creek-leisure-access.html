Leisure Access Program (Dawson Creek)

Funding for low-income Dawson Creek residents to pay for classes, drop-ins and events. You may get a drop-in punch card or credit toward a registered program. The amount depends on limited yearly funds.

What it is

Help paying for classes, drop-ins and events.

  • Approved drop-in funding is used with a punch card at the front desk.
  • For registered programs, you tell staff to use your funding on the fee.
  • If you are approved for $30 on a $50 class, you pay the other $20.

Good to know

Funding is not guaranteed, and the amount can be small. New applicants are considered before repeat applicants. Applications are considered on the first day of each month.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You are a permanent resident living within city boundaries for at least three months
  • Your family income is at or below the Statistics Canada Low-Income Cut-Off

Amount or value

Funding toward drop-in and program fees; amount varies

How to apply

  1. Print the individual or group form, or pick one up at the Kenn Borek Aquatic Centre or Culture & Recreation front desk
  2. Fill out the main applicant section
  3. Ask a Trusted Partner to fill out the bottom section
  4. Email the form or drop it off at the aquatic centre or Kin Arena

What you may need

  • The completed application form
  • The bottom section completed by a Trusted Partner, verifying income and residency

Practical tips

  • A Trusted Partner is a social service agency, government agency or non-profit you work with.
  • Applicants are told by mail, up to three weeks after the deadline.
  • You can apply again, but funding is limited each calendar year.
