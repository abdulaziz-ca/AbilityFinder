Home Energy Low-income Program (HELP)

Households with an income of $35,000 or less are eligible for free air-sealing. Other features are a programmable thermostat, a low-flow showerhead, bulbs and a furnace cleaning voucher (up to $80 value).

What it is

What you get:

  • A tradesperson does comprehensive air-sealing at no cost.
  • A programmable thermostat and a low-flow showerhead are installed.
  • A voucher covers a free furnace cleaning worth up to $80.
  • The form says you cannot apply again for the same property for 5 years.

Good to know

This is from the program's application form, which shows no date. Confirm current rules with efficiency PEI.

What you must meet

The program requires at least one of these:

  • Your annual household income is $35,000 or less.
  • The form also lists receiving the National Child Benefit Supplement.
  • The form also lists receiving the Guaranteed Income Supplement.

Registered in your name, or a year-round rental where you pay all heating; lived in November to April.

What this guide can't tell you: whether you will be approved. The program decides, often using details we do not ask for.

How to apply

  1. Confirm you are eligible using the terms and conditions.
  2. Fill out the form completely.
  3. Mail it with originals of your proof of income and your latest electric bill.
  4. You can also drop it off at an Access PEI office.

What you may need

  • Your most recent GST, National Child Benefit Supplement or Guaranteed Income Supplement notice.
  • Original July GST Credit Notice as proof of income.
  • Maritime Electric bill showing last year's use.

Practical tips

  • Incomplete forms delay or disqualify your application.
  • The furnace voucher has no cash value and cannot be transferred.
  • The owner can allow or deny any contractor access.
