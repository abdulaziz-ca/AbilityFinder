Affordable Bus Pass (Chatham-Kent Transit)

A 30-day rolling pass with unlimited rides on urban routes for Chatham-Kent residents with a low family income. You can load 30, 60 or 90 days at a time.

What it is

The pass gives low-income residents a fixed price for unlimited urban bus rides.

  • It starts the first time you scan it and runs for 30 days in a row.
  • It works on Chatham and Wallaceburg urban routes.
  • It also covers specialized transit.

Good to know

The pass also covers specialized transit in Chatham and Wallaceburg. The card cannot be passed to someone else.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You live in Chatham-Kent
  • Your yearly combined family income is at or below the Statistics Canada before-tax low-income measure

The limit depends on your family size, and ODSP clients can show current income statements.

What this guide can't tell you: whether you will be approved. The program decides, often using details we do not ask for.

Amount or value

30-day unlimited urban transit pass for $41.99

How to apply

  1. Fill in the application form.
  2. Gather a tax notice and ID for each family member.
  3. Submit everything to the Chatham-Kent Civic Centre.
  4. Load 30, 60 or 90 days onto your card after you are approved.

What you may need

  • A signed application form
  • A current Canada Revenue Agency Notice of Assessment for every family member with income
  • Identification for you and each family member, such as a birth certificate, license, passport or health card

Practical tips

  • The pass costs $41.99.
  • The Affordable Bus Pass Policy on the transit site has more detail.
