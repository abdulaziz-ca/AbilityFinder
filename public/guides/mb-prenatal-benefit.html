Manitoba · Money (for parents)

Manitoba Prenatal Benefit

A monthly payment for six months to help you buy healthy food while pregnant. The amount depends on your income.

Manitoba is new here: please double-check before you rely on a detail

We added Manitoba in October 2026, while some of its programs are changing. Each program here comes from its official page, but some details are not published yet, so confirm with the program before you count on an amount or rule.

  • Manitoba is moving people with a disability from Employment and Income Assistance (EIA) to the new Manitoba Supports for Persons with Disabilities program, and that program's page does not publish payment amounts yet.
  • Since September 1, 2026, EIA covers only emergency dental care and points people to the federal Canadian Dental Care Plan.
  • Some income limits are old (home adaptation limits are from 2019) or change every year (Rent Assist and Winnipeg programs).
  • Winnipeg Transit Plus has proposed new eligibility rules that are not in effect.
  • City programs are listed for Winnipeg only so far.

What it is

The benefit pays a monthly income supplement and links you to pregnancy supports.

  • It is a monthly supplement for six months.
  • It comes with a referral to community Public Health Services and resources.
  • It helps you connect with prenatal care early in pregnancy.
  • It includes a referral to a Healthy Baby Community Support program.

Good to know

You must apply while you are pregnant. Income assistance cheques are not affected by this benefit.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You are pregnant.
  • You live in Manitoba.
  • Your net family income is less than $32,000 a year.

Amount or value

Up to $162.82 a month for six months, based on income

How to apply

  1. Choose an application option: the online E-Form, or a printable form.
  2. On EIA or MSPD: use the shorter Registration Form, by mail or in person.
  3. Not on EIA or MSPD: use the full Application Form, by mail or in person.
  4. Include a note confirming your pregnancy.

What you may need

  • A note confirming your pregnancy
  • SIN application form, if you have no Social Insurance Number
  • Extra form if you are 18 or younger and have not filed a tax return

Practical tips

  • Forms are also at medical offices, community agencies and government offices.
  • Or pick one up at the Healthy Baby Prenatal Benefit Office, 100-114 Garry Street, Winnipeg.
  • Use the lower-income form if this year's income is much lower than your last return.
