Newfoundland and Labrador · Housing

Rental Housing Program

Low-income rental housing for households that cannot find suitable, affordable rental housing on the private market. This includes people with disabilities.

What it is

NLHC rents housing to many groups, including:

  • Families with low income and seniors.
  • Non-elderly single people and Aboriginal people.
  • Individuals with disabilities and others in need of housing.
  • Non-profit groups also offer housing; your regional office can give you a list.

Good to know

NLHC rents housing directly and also works with non-profit groups. The program is cost-shared by the provincial and federal governments.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • Your total household income falls within certain income levels.
  • Your home is adequate but you pay over 30 per cent of income on rent and heat.
  • Or your home is unsuitable and suitable housing in your area would cost 30 per cent or more.

The page lists these tests without saying how they combine; ask your regional office.

What this guide can't tell you: whether you will be approved. The program decides, often using details we do not ask for.

Amount or value

Rent set at 25 per cent of net monthly income in non-profit housing (unless Income Support pays your rent)

How to apply

  1. Choose your region and apply online, or print the form.
  2. Contact your nearest regional office with questions.
  3. Ask the office for a list of non-profit housing groups in your area.

What you may need

  • The page does not list documents.

Practical tips

  • Tenants whose rent is paid through Income Support are not on the 25 per cent rule.
  • Rents in non-profit housing are based on tenant income.
  • Federal and provincial subsidies cover the difference in non-profit housing.

  • Regional offices: Goose Bay 709-896-1920; Grand Falls-Windsor 709-292-1000.
  • Regional offices: Marystown 709-279-5375; Stephenville 709-643-6826.
