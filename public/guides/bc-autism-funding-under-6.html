Autism Funding: Under Age 6

Ending: new applications until March 2027; the program ends March 31, 2027

Direct yearly funding to buy autism intervention services (behaviour consultants, therapists, supervised behaviour interventionists) for children under 6 with an autism diagnosis.

What it is

Normally, up to 20% of the allocation can go to training, travel and equipment (TTE). For the final aligned funding period ending March 31, 2027, BC increased the allowable TTE portion to 50%. Requests must not exceed 50% of a child/youth’s annual funding amount for benefit plans that are 12 months or less. For benefit plans that are 13 months or longer, the limit is 50% on the adjusted portion, with the first 12 months being available at 20%. Families are encouraged to check their TTE balance on MyFamily Services (MyFS) or call Autism Funding to confirm their balance (Toll Free: 1 877 777-3530).

Good to know

Autism Funding accepts new applications until March 2027 and ends March 31, 2027. The ministry says it will contact all recipients about their eligibility to transfer to the BC Children and Youth Disability Benefit ahead of the transition. Final aligned funding periods may be prorated to March 31, 2027.

What you must meet

Child under age 6 with an autism spectrum disorder diagnosis meeting BC standards (BCAAN or qualified private assessment)

What this guide can't tell you: whether you will be approved. The program decides, often using details we do not ask for.

What it covers

Up to $22,000 per year per child covers:

  • Professionals listed on the Registry of Autism Service Providers (RASP)
  • Behaviour interventionists supervised by a RASP professional
  • Family counselling or therapy (providers do not need to be on RASP)
  • Employment-related costs when you hire staff
  • Administrative costs up to $100/month

How to apply

  1. Get an autism diagnosis through the BC Autism Assessment Network (BCAAN, free via physician referral) or a private assessment meeting BC standards
  2. Register for a Basic BCeID account to apply online, or complete the paper Application for Autism Funding
  3. Email the application and supporting documents to mcf.autismfundingintake@gov.bc.ca or submit through your local CYSN office
  4. Sign the funding agreement, then choose providers (RASP-listed professionals, supervised behaviour interventionists, or family counselling providers) and submit invoices or reimbursement claims

What you may need

  • Proof of age and residency (for example a birth certificate or BC Services Card)
  • BCAAN assessment: the BCAAN Clinical Outcome (PANTER) Form (BCAAN assessment reports are optional)
  • Private (non-BCAAN) assessment: form CF0904 and a diagnostic report showing the ADOS-2 and ADI-R were used, signed by the assessor, with the speech-language and psychological evaluation findings
  • Assessment outside B.C.: form CF0905 and a copy of the original diagnostic report or assessment information
  • Basic BCeID account for online applications
  • Assessment outside B.C.: a new assessment may be required if the BC specialist cannot confirm that the previous diagnosis meets the DSM-5 criteria

Practical tips

  • Intervention services must come from providers on the RASP registry, or from behaviour interventionists supervised by a RASP professional. Family counselling or therapy providers do not need to be on RASP.
  • The normal training, travel and equipment limit is 20%, but BC raised it to 50% for the final aligned funding period ending March 31, 2027. Requests must not exceed 50% of a child/youth’s annual funding amount for benefit plans that are 12 months or less. For benefit plans that are 13 months or longer, the limit is 50% on the adjusted portion, with the first 12 months being available at 20%. Families are encouraged to check their TTE balance on MyFamily Services (MyFS) or call Autism Funding to confirm their balance (Toll Free: 1 877 777-3530).
  • Approved services and purchases must be delivered by March 31, 2027; direct-payment supporting documents are due May 31, 2027. Submit invoices and reimbursement requests within 6 months of each service or purchase; for those made on March 31, 2027, the last day is September 30, 2027
  • From July 2026 the ministry contacts Autism Funding families about moving to the new Disability Benefit ($6,500 or $17,000/year). You keep your current funding until your transition date.
  • These professionals may be eligible to provide family counselling or therapy: Canadian Certified Counsellors, Registered Clinical Counsellors, Registered Marriage and Family Therapists, Registered Psychiatrists, Registered Psychologists, and Registered Social Workers or Registered Clinical Social Workers. Contact Autism Funding or Autism Information Services BC for more information.

Timing

New applications are accepted until March 2027. The program and final aligned funding periods end March 31, 2027; later paperwork deadlines do not extend the service-delivery date.
