Prince Albert Access Transit

A door-to-door ride service for people who cannot use the public transit system. It has ramps and lifts for wheelchairs. The Community Service Centre runs it.

What it is

The Community Service Centre can help you get where you want to go.

  • Access Transit is a door-to-door service.
  • Vehicles have ramps and lifts for wheelchair passengers.

Good to know

Rides must be booked ahead. The page does not give fares, hours or how to register.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You cannot use the public transit system
  • The reason is a permanent or temporary disability

What this guide can't tell you: whether you will be approved. The program decides, often using details we do not ask for.

Door-to-door rides with ramps and lifts
