New Brunswick · Daily living supports

Self-Managed Support

This option lets you manage your own services and get a monthly payment to cover them, if you are already in either program.

What it is

With this option you take charge of your care.

  • You coordinate, manage and direct the services in your plan.
  • You can hire family members who do not live in the residence.
  • You can carry over unused funding within the same calendar year.
  • Your social worker reviews your plan and spending at year end.

Good to know

You hire and direct your own providers. Keep detailed records of how you spend the funds.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You are in the Disability Support Program or the Long-term Care Program.

Amount or value

A lump sum paid at the start of every month

How to apply

  1. Tell your social worker you are interested.

Practical tips

  • The department recommends a separate bank account for these funds.
  • You arrange services, hire and train staff, and check their skills.
