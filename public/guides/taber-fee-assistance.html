Fee Assistance Program (Taber)

A 50% discount on Community Centre drop-in programs and summer kids day camps. It also gives 50% off Aquafun Centre membership, drop-ins and lessons. Some restrictions apply.

What it is

Half price on many Town recreation fees.

  • 50% off Community Centre drop-in programming and summer kids day camps.
  • 50% off Aquafun Centre membership and drop-ins.
  • 50% off lessons and courses, with some restrictions.

Good to know

It is for residents of the Town of Taber and the Municipal District of Taber who are below the income thresholds. The card lasts one year, and you can reapply through FCSS.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You live in the Town of Taber or the Municipal District of Taber
  • Your income is below the low-income thresholds
  • FCSS confirms you are eligible

The page sends you to the Fee Assistance Procedure for the thresholds.

Amount or value

50% off recreation programs and Aquafun Centre fees

At a glance

Difficulty
Medium
Application
FCSS application, then card pickup

How to apply

  1. Fill out the application form and apply through FCSS
  2. If eligible, get a referral letter from FCSS
  3. Bring the letter to the Aquafun Centre reception desk
  4. Staff issue each family member a fee assistance card

What you may need

  • The application form
  • The FCSS referral letter

Practical tips

  • Staff try to keep your use of the program confidential.
  • KidSport and Jumpstart are other programs listed on the page.

Timing
