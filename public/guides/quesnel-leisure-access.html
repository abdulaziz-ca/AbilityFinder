Leisure Access Card (Quesnel)

Quesnel gives 50% off public swimming and skating admissions and 25% off programs. It is for people on income assistance or with a low household income.

What it is

The card lowers costs at the Arts and Recreation Centre and Quesnel Arenas.

  • The swimming and skating discount is for children and adults.
  • 25% off programs at the Arts and Recreation Centre and the arenas.

Good to know

The card is valid for one year from the day the application is accepted. Show both your Resident Card and your Leisure Access Card.

What you must meet

The program requires at least one of these:

  • You get income assistance from the provincial ministry
  • Your household income is at or below the limit for your family size

The limit is $24,486 for one person and $64,797 for seven or more.

Amount or value

50% off swimming and skating, 25% off programs

How to apply

  1. Complete the application form
  2. Have proof of financial need ready
  3. Submit the form with your proof
  4. Renew with a new application when the card is about to expire

What you may need

  • A signed Release of Personal Information form, or your latest Notice of Assessment
  • A Seniors Supplement card or a current Employment Insurance form also works

Practical tips

  • New applicants and renewals both fill in a new application.
  • The program is paid for by North Cariboo Parks and Recreation taxes.
