Prescription Drug Program: 65Plus Plan

Coverage of eligible prescription drugs for residents 65 and older who receive both federal OAS and GIS benefits.

What it is

About this plan:

  • The plan covers eligible prescription drugs.
  • Plan members also qualify for the Ostomy Subsidy Program.
  • The program repays part of the cost of ostomy items.

Good to know

No application is needed.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You are sixty-five or older.
  • You live in Newfoundland and Labrador.
  • You receive Old Age Security and the Guaranteed Income Supplement.

What this guide can't tell you: whether you will be approved. The program decides, often using details we do not ask for.

Amount or value

Eligible prescription drugs covered; dispensing fee up to $6

How to apply

  1. Make sure you get both federal senior benefits.
  2. Service Canada notifies the department.
  3. A drug card is issued automatically.

What you may need

  • For ostomy items, original prescription receipts.

Practical tips

  • You pay a dispensing fee of up to $6.
  • The Ostomy Subsidy repays 75% of the retail cost of benefit ostomy items.
