Affordable Access Program (Regina)

Regina residents with a household income under set limits get reduced rates on recreation programs and on adult and youth transit passes.

What it is

Once approved, you get:

  • Single admissions at 50 per cent off the regular price.
  • Leisure Passes at 80 per cent off the regular price.
  • Class registration at 80 per cent off, up to $160 off per family member.
  • Adult and Youth transit passes at 20 per cent off the regular price.

Good to know

Approval lasts until the end of each year. You must re-apply every year.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • Your household income is under the limit for your household size.

Limits for 1 to 4 people: $27,478, $34,206, $42,053 and $51,058 (larger sizes on the page).

Amount or value

50% to 80% off recreation; 20% off transit passes

How to apply

  1. Fill out the Affordable Access Program Application Form.
  2. Add the required income verification.
  3. Submit it in person at the North West Leisure Centre, Sportplex or Sandra Schmirler Leisure Centre, or by email, fax or mail.
  4. If approved, buy transit passes at the Transit Information Centre, 2124 11th Avenue.

What you may need

  • Completed application form
  • Income verification

Practical tips

  • Processing takes about three weeks on average, so apply early.
  • Passes that are already discounted (Discount, Post-Secondary, Senior) do not get the extra 20 per cent off.
