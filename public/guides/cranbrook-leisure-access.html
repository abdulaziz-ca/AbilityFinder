Leisure Access Program (Cranbrook)

The Leisure Access Program gives Cranbrook residents with limited income 75% off regular swim rates and free transit passes. Children under seven swim free with an adult.

What it is

The program lowers swim and transit costs.

  • 75% off regular swim rates for public swims, Aquafit and joint mobility classes.
  • Free public swims for children under seven, with an adult.
  • Free transit access through monthly transit passes.

Good to know

Transit passes are handed out monthly, cannot be transferred and are not replaced if lost or stolen. Benefits last one year, then you must re-apply.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You live in Cranbrook
  • Your household income is at or below the limit for your family size

Limits run from $20,848 for 1 person to $55,172 for 7 people; a family of 4 is $38,740.

Amount or value

75% off swim rates and free transit

How to apply

  1. Check your family size against the income chart
  2. Contact one of the four adjudicating agencies listed on the page
  3. Meet the adjudicator, who checks your information and fills out your application
  4. Wait about a week, then book an appointment with Recreation & Culture to register
  5. Show residency and ID at that appointment

What you may need

  • Government-issued photo ID with your current address
  • Proof of address in one adult's name, such as a utility bill, bank statement or rental contract
  • Notice of Assessment or T1 General for each adult, or a Release of Information from MSDSI
  • Canada Child Benefit notice if children are on the application

Practical tips

  • The adjudicators are Salvation Army, Community Connections Society, Interior Health Mental Health Programs and MCFD.
  • Release of Information forms are at Service BC, 101-100 Cranbrook Street N.
  • If you do not qualify, check the pool schedule for Free Family Swims.
