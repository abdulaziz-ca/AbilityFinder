Personal Care Home Benefit (PCHB)

A monthly payment for seniors who live in a licensed personal care home. It is the difference between a $3,500 monthly income threshold and your monthly income.

What it is

PCHB helps with the cost of living in a licensed personal care home.

  • The amount depends on your income and marital status.
  • Benefits are paid from the date the ministry receives your application.
  • You have up to 60 days to send your documents.
  • Recipients send income documents before the next benefit year starts.

Good to know

Married or common-law applicants give both incomes, and half of the combined household income is counted.

What you must meet

You must meet all of these:

  • You are 65 years of age or older.
  • You are a Saskatchewan resident.
  • You live in a Saskatchewan licensed personal care home.
  • You receive an Old Age Security pension.
  • Your monthly income is below specified levels.

Approved clients do not re-apply every year but must send income documents before September.

Amount or value

Monthly top-up to a $3,500 monthly income threshold

How to apply

  1. Review the Application Guide and the list of licensed personal care homes.
  2. Download and complete the application form.
  3. Send documents within 60 days to the Client Service Centre in Regina.
  4. By mail, email or fax; contact details are on the application page.

What you may need

  • Signed application form
  • Direct deposit form
  • GIS Entitlement letter, or most recent tax return and a bank statement showing OAS/GIS deposit
  • Responsible Person Consent Form (if required)
  • Confirmation of Admission Form (if required)

Practical tips

  • Apply as soon as you meet the eligibility rules.
  • A spouse sends their own GIS letter or tax return and bank statement, if applicable.
