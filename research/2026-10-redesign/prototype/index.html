Canada Disability Benefit

A monthly payment for adults aged 18 to 64 who are approved for the Disability Tax Credit and have a lower income.

Up to$204.20 a month
Effort15–30 minutes
First paymentMonth after approval

You may qualify if

  • ✓You are 18 to 64You told us 60 to 64.
  • ✓You are approved for the Disability Tax CreditYou told us you are approved.
  • ?You and your spouse filed your 2025 tax returnWe did not ask. The benefit is based on that return.

How to apply

  1. Make sure your DTC is approved and your taxes are filed.
  2. Apply online, by phone, by mail, or at a Service Canada office.
  3. Add direct deposit so payments arrive automatically.
Official wording from canada.ca
"Benefit amounts are based on your adjusted family net income. The benefit amount will start to decrease after your adjusted family net income reaches a certain threshold."
