Questions people ask

Still unsure? Free local organizations can help you apply or appeal.

Is AbilityFinder a government website? ＋

No. It is a free, independent tool. Every program links to the official government page, and you always apply there.

Will it tell me if I'm approved? ＋

No. Each program makes its own decision, often using medical or income details we do not ask for. We show which programs are worth checking and what each one still needs.

Where do my answers go? ＋

Nowhere. They stay in your browser on this device. We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which does not use cookies and never sees your answers.

Do I need my documents to start? ＋

No. It helps to know your province, your city and roughly your household income. Your results include a checklist of documents for later.
