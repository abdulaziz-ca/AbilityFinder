Canada Disability Benefit

A monthly payment for adults aged 18 to 64 who are approved for the Disability Tax Credit and have a lower income.

How to apply

  1. Make sure your DTC is approved and your taxes are filedBoth are required before payments can start.
  2. Apply online, by phone, by mail, or at a Service Canada officeOnline is fastest. Someone you trust can help you.
  3. Add direct depositService Canada asks everyone to use it, because it is the fastest way to get paid.

Documents to have ready

DTC approval on file with the CRA
Your most recent tax return filed
Banking info for direct deposit

Questions

Do I need the Disability Tax Credit first?＋
Yes. Get the DTC approved first; it is required.
What is the $150 supplement?＋
Starting in fall 2026, eligible recipients may receive a separate fixed $150 lump sum toward the cost of getting the DTC. You do not apply for it separately.
