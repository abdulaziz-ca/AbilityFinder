 Eligibility for the New Brunswick Prescription Drug Program
 The New Brunswick Prescription Drug Program (NBPDP) provides prescription drug benefits to eligible residents of New Brunswick.

 Overview
 New Brunswick residents with a valid Medicare card who are eligible for the New Brunswick Prescription Drug Program (NBPDP) include seniors, those living in nursing homes, adult residential facilities, correctional facilities, those with specific medical conditions and Social Development clients. 
Drugs covered under the program are listed in the New Brunswick Formulary.
The NBPDP is administered in accordance the Prescription Drug Payment Act and its regulations.

 Adult residential facilities
 Individuals who reside in an adult residential facility are eligible for drug coverage through this plan. Eligibility is determined by the Department of Social Development. 
There are no fees or co-payments associated with this plan.
- Long-Term Care Services for Seniors
 Children in the care of the Minister of Social Development and special needs children
 Children in care of the Minister of Social Development and special needs children are eligible for drug coverage through this plan. Eligibility is determined by the Department of Social Development. 
There are no fees or co-payments associated with this plan.
 Correctional Services
 Individuals incarcerated in provincial correctional facilities who do not have existing drug coverage are eligible for drug coverage through this plan. Eligibility is determined at the facility.
There are no fees or co-payments associated with this program.
 Cystic fibrosis
 Individuals who have been diagnosed with cystic fibrosis may apply for either of the following public drug plans if they do not have coverage for any portion of the cost of cystic fibrosis drugs from any other drug plan. 
Cystic Fibrosis Plan
The Cystic Fibrosis Plan covers certain cystic fibrosis drugs. Prior to registration, please call 1-800-332-3692 to confirm that the prescribed drug is covered under the plan.
There is an annual premium of $50 and a co-payment per prescription. The co-payment is 20% of the prescription cost, up to a maximum of $20. There is a maximum co-payment amount of $500 per family unit per plan year (July 1 to June 30). To apply for the Cystic Fibrosis Plan, a completed Cystic Fibrosis Plan Application Form (PDF 601 KB) must be submitted to:

New Brunswick Drug Plan
The New Brunswick Drug Plan covers a broad range of drugs, including cystic fibrosis drugs. The premium and maximum co-payment amounts for the New Brunswick Drug Plan are based on annual family income. 
To learn more about or apply to the New Brunswick Drug Plan, visit Enrol in the New Brunswick Drug Plan.

 HIV/AIDS
 Individuals diagnosed with HIV infection and who have been registered in the plan by a physician are eligible for coverage of certain antiretroviral drugs used to treat HIV/AIDS. 
If the applicant has existing drug coverage with another drug plan, the applicant must submit a letter from their existing drug plan confirming that the required drugs are not listed on the plan’s formulary.
The HIV/AIDS Plan has an annual registration fee of $50 and a co-payment. The co-payment is 20% of the prescription cost, up to a maximum of $20. The co-payment ceiling is $500 per family unit, per plan year (July 1 to June 30).

 Seniors
 Various plans offer prescription drug coverage to seniors in the province. Seniors who receive the Federal Guaranteed Income Supplement (GIS) from Employment and Social Development Canada (ESDC) are eligible for coverage by the New Brunswick Prescription Drug Program (NBPDP) – Seniors plan. 
Seniors who do not receive the Federal GIS from ESDC can apply for the New Brunswick Drug Plan, an income-based plan that covers uninsured New Brunswickers.
NBPDP Seniors plan
New Brunswick seniors are eligible to apply for drug coverage if they:
- are 65 years of age or older
- are a permanent resident of New Brunswick
- have a valid New Brunswick Medicare card
- do not have prescription drug coverage from another plan
The Application Package (PDF 1.2 MB), which includes the Seniors Drug Plan Application Form (PDF 613 KB), is automatically sent to every New Brunswick resident 60 days before they turn 65.

 Social Development clients
 Individuals who hold a valid health card issued by the Department of Social Development are eligible for drug coverage through this plan. Eligibility is determined by the Department of Social Development.
Individuals on this plan are required to pay a co-payment of $4.00 for each prescription for adults (18 years of age and over) and $2.00 for children (under 18 years of age), up to a maximum of $250 per family unit in a fiscal year.
 Privacy
 Upon enrolment in any of the New Brunswick Drug Plans, applicants must consent to the collection, use and disclosure of the personal information required to administer the Plans and provide beneficiaries with eligible drug benefits.
Requests to designate a substitute decision maker may be provided via the Substitute Decision Maker Request form (PDF 544 KB) or by providing a copy of the Certificate of Appointment of Committee or the Enduring Power of Attorny.
The New Brunswick Drug Plans will only release a beneficiary’s personal health information to a third party if the beneficiary has completed a Release of Information to Third Party Consent form (PDF 572 KB).
