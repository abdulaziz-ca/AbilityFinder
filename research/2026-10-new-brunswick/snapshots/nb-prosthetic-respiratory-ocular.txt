 Persons with disabilities
 Information for people with disabilities, including related to housing options, help at home, financial assistance, assistance with employment, managing medical issues and more.

 Family supports for children with disabilities program
 The Family Supports for Children with Disabilities Program assists families to address the unmet needs of the child or children with the disability by providing the required support and resources.

 Training and employment support services (TESS) for persons with disabilities

 Employment and support services program
 Learn about services to help people living with a disability find employment, develop skills and make connections in the community that best suit their needs, interests and talents.
 Help at home and with housing
 - 
 Disability support program
 This program provides personalized, flexible support for adults with disabilities to increase their independence and connection to the community.
 - 
 Self-managed support
 This service option gives individuals in the Disability Support Program or the Long-Term Care Program flexibility and greater independence by allowing them to manage their own services.
 Medical assistance
 - 
 Mobility and adaptive equipment loan program
 The program loans specific mobility and adaptive equipment to eligible clients to support them in their daily activities and enable them to live and be cared for safely.
 - 
 Ocular Prosthesis Program
 This program provides funding to enable residents in need to have access to the services of a qualified ocularist.
 - 
 Orthopedic program
 The program assists eligible clients with the coverage of orthopedic items which are not covered by other agencies or private health insurance plans.
 - 
 Ostomy / incontinence program
 The program assists clients with coverage for ostomy, catheterization and incontinence supplies which are not covered by other agencies or private health insurance plans.
 - 
 Prosthetic program
 The program assists clients with coverage for specific prosthetic services that are not covered by other agencies or private health insurance plans.
 - 
 Respiratory program
 The program assists clients with coverage of respiratory equipment and supplies which are not covered by other agencies or private health insurance.
