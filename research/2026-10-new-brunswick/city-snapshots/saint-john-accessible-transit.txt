Saint John Accessible Transit
 Image 
Saint John Accessible Transit, operated by Independence Plus Inc., a non-profit organization, provides transportation to people with disabilities who are unable to use regular public transportation.  
Hours of Operation
 Monday, Wednesday, Friday
 7 a.m. – 7 p.m.
 Tuesday, Thursday
 7 a.m. – 9 p.m.
 Saturday
 10:30 a.m. – 7 p.m.
 Sunday
 No service
Saint John Accessible Transit service is available for all Sea Dogs games.
Fares
 Within city limits
 $5.50 each way 
 Pass for 10 rides
 $50.00
 Pass for 20 rides
 $90.00
 Private bookings
 $75 per hour
There is no charge for an attendant traveling with the passenger.
To book a ride, cancel a ride (at least 60 minutes notice required), or ask questions, call 506-648-0609. 
Note
 - Please be ready 5 minutes before your scheduled pick up. Drivers can only wait for 3 minutes. 
 - Drivers can’t enter the residence of passengers or assist in carrying wheelchairs. 
 - Saint John Accessible Transit passengers requiring assistance should make arrangements with an attendant prior to their pickup to ensure they can get on the bus safely.
 - Failure to cancel, or being late, will result in a full fee charge. After three ‘no shows’, passengers may be suspended from using Saint John Accessible Transit service.
 - Regularly scheduled passengers who will not require service for a period of time must call one week in advance to reschedule.
 - Service dogs are the only animals permitted on the bus and they must be on a leash.
