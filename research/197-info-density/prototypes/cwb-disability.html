Canada Workers Benefit — Disability Supplement

If you work and earn a lower income, this adds an extra disability top-up to your tax refund.

Am I eligible?Lower-earning workers approved for the DTC.
How much?Extra refundable tax credit on top of the base benefit
How do I apply?Canada Workers Benefit details

Received with your tax refund (or as advance payments).

Eligibility

You must meet ALL of these:

  • You are approved for the Disability Tax Credit (DTC).
  • You have employment income.
  • You must be a resident of Canada throughout the year, be 19 or older on December 31 or live with your spouse, common-law partner or child, and have net income below the level set for your province or territory. You are not eligible if you were a full-time student for more than 13 weeks in the year unless you have an eligible dependant, if you were confined to a prison or similar institution for at least 90 days during the year, or if you do not have to pay tax in Canada because you are an officer or servant of another country, such as a diplomat, or their family member or employee.

What you get

Extra refundable tax credit on top of the base benefit
