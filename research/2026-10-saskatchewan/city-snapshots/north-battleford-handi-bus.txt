 Handi-Bus
Battlefords Handi-Bus is a specialized on-demand transportation service for persons with disabilities. This program is available to anyone who, due to a disability, cannot use the regular transit service.
This service operates Monday to Friday.
For more information or to book a ride, call 306-445-7797.
North Battleford Public Transportation hours
North Battleford Public Transportation runs Monday to Friday.
- Green route
- 8 a.m. to 11 a.m.
- Blue route
- 1 p.m. to 4:30 p.m.
The last stop for each route is at the Discovery Co-op Mall.
Fares
Exact change is needed for single-ride fares. 
There are five locations you can buy monthly passes:
- City Hall - 1291 101st Street
- The InnovationPlex: The Battlefords CO-OP Aquatic Centre or the NationsWEST Field House
- Discovery CO-OP Pharmacy
- Battlefords Transit office - 762 109th Street

 North Battleford Public Transit
North Battleford Public Transit is 100% funded by the City of North Battleford. These buses are operated by Battlefords Transit System, which also operates Battlefords Handi-Bus.
The Battlefords Handi-Bus is jointly funded by the City of North Battleford and the Town of Battleford.
