 Affordable Access Program
 The City offers reduced rates for recreation programs and transit passes to ensure all residents can be included through the Affordable Access Program.
 Reduced Rates
Affordable Fun
Upon approval, single admissions can be purchased for 50 per cent off the regular price and Leisure Passes for 80 per cent off the regular price. Participants can also register for classes at 80 per cent off the regular price, to a maximum of $160 discount per family member. The City has hundreds of great recreation and leisure programs to choose from such as swimming, arts and crafts, and sport and fitness. Find them in our Leisure GuideOpens in new window.
Affordable Transit
Transit Passes can be purchased at 20 per cent reduced cost of regular price for Adult and Youth. Already discounted passes do not qualify (Discount, Post- Secondary, Senior) for the additional 20 percent off. When approved, passes must be purchased from the Transit Information Centre (2124 11th Avenue).

How to Apply
To apply, please fill out the Affordable Access Program Application FormOpens in new window. Make sure to include the required income verification along with your application form. Submit your application one of four ways:
- In person at the North West Leisure Centre, Sportplex or Sandra Schmirler Leisure Centre
- Hard copies of the form are available at City neighbourhood centres and major recreation centres
- Email to affordableaccess@regina.caOpens in new window
- Fax to 306-777-6831
- Mail to the City of Regina Affordable Access Program, 2476 Victoria Ave, 6th Floor, PO Box 1790, Regina, SK S4P 3C8
The average time to process an application is three weeks, so get your application in early.
