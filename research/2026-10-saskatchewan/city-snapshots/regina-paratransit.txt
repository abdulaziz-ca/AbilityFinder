 Paratransit
 Regina Paratransit is a shared ride transportation service for persons experiencing a disability who are restricted in using fixed route transit some of the time. The service is a scheduled door-to-door service for those who are registered.
 Qualifications for Paratransit
To qualify for Paratransit, someone must be restricted in one or more of the following:
- Ability to walk or wheel to a bus stop.
- Ability to identify landmarks, follow directions or navigate fixed-route transit.
- Easily confused or disoriented.
- Risk of falling.
- Unable to board fixed-route transit independently.
- Tires easily.
 Register for Paratransit
There are five types of registrations. People registering for Paratransit must meet the following eligibility criteria:
Unconditional
An unconditional registration is granted if a person requires the continuous use of Paratransit and is a Regina resident. If Paratransit is not used in an 18-month period, the unconditional registration will be deactivated and you will need to re-register.
Temporary
A temporary registration is granted if the person is temporarily disabled (e.g. broken leg or hip replacement surgery) and unable to use fixed route transit for a limited time period. Temporary registrations can be extended if required.
Winter
Winter registrations are for people who only require Paratransit in the winter and are issued from November 1 until March 31 of each year.
Visitor
Paratransit is available for people visiting Regina. You must provide a city address as a pick-up location. Visitor registration does not expire and is valid every time you visit Regina.
Conditional
A conditional registration allows people to travel on Paratransit at certain times of the day and under certain conditions (e.g. after dark).

If you cannot complete the electronic application, you can call 306-777-7726, and someone will assist you.
Upon receiving your application, Regina Paratransit Service will review your eligibility and may consult with the professionals you listed in your application form. You may also be asked to attend an in-person interview. Most applications are reviewed within two weeks; however, delays may occur if additional information requested is not received promptly.
Note: You must be registered before you can use Paratransit services.
 Book Your Trip
Trips are booked on a first-come, first-served basis, depending on service availability. You can book your trip up to seven days in advance.
When determining your pick-up time, remember that:
- There is a 20-minute pick-up time frame (e.g., if your trip is for 9 a.m., Paratransit will arrive between 9:00 and 9:20 a.m.).
- A one-way ride may take up to 60 minutes to complete, depending on who else may be on the paratransit bus at that time and where everyone is going.
You can book your trip online via email at paratransit@regina.caOpens in new window by calling Regina Paratransit Service at 306-777-7007 during the hours listed below, or by using the Regina Paratransit app available on Goole PlayOpens in new window and the App StoreOpens in new window. 
Advance, Charter, Group and Subscription Bookings
- Monday to Saturday from 9 a.m. to 8 p.m.
- Sunday from 9 a.m. to 6:30 p.m.
There are no advance bookings on statutory holidays.
Same-day Bookings
- Weekdays and Saturday from 7:30 a.m. to 11 p.m.
- Sunday and statutory holidays from 8:30 a.m. to 6:30 p.m.
You may find it easier to book your trip during the evenings since the telephone lines are quite busy during the day. Also, you can only book one trip per call when the telephone lines are busy.

 Fares and Passes
Paratransit fares and passes are consistent with conventional transit. Operators will collect your fare when you board the bus. For a trip to more than one place, you must book separate trips and pay separate fares.
You can purchase your rides or passes from any transit agent, as well as the Transit Information Centre.
 Change or Cancel Your Trip
Change: Call Regina Paratransit Services at 306-777-7007 as soon as possible to allow staff time to reschedule/reroute operators.
Cancel: Use the Regina Paratransit app or leave a message at the Regina Paratransit 24-hour Cancellation Line at 306-777-7007. Provide your name and the date and time of the trip you are cancelling. Please give as much notice as possible when cancelling your trip.
Operators cannot change or cancel the destination or pick-up time of your trip.
