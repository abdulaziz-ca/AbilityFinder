SWIFT TRANSIT...WELCOME ABOARD!
Swift Transit travels on a scheduled, fixed route, picking up and dropping off customers at designated bus stops at key locations and near places like senior centres, shopping malls, major attractions and Cypress Regional Hospital.
Swift Transit Hours of Operation: RED LINE – Monday to Saturday: 7:00 AM to 7:00 PM (last run beings at 6:00 pm). OUT OF OPERATION FOR STATUTORY HOLIDAYS
Cost:
• $3.00 for one-way trip – please have exact change
• $30.00 for a 10 trip punch pass - available at City Hall OR from bus driver if you have exact change
• $75.00 Monthly Pass – available for purchase at City Hall
Children ages 6-12 can ride for $1.00 each way and the service is free for children under 6 years old. Children under 6 years of age must be accompanied by a responsible attendant at all times.
If you cannot walk to a bus stop or require accessibility accommodations:
If you have mobility issues, you may be eligible to use Access Transit services. Call Access Transit at 306-770-2263 to make arrangements. The fare for a one-way trip is $3.00.
Access Transit Hours of Operation:
Mon-Fri 8:00am to 6:00pm
Saturday 12:00pm to 6:00pm
Sunday 8:30am to 2:30pm
STATUTORY HOLIDAY INFO: NO SERVICES
Operating Partners:
A partnership between the City of Swift Current and the SaskAbilities has been established, with SaskAbilities operating both the Access Transit and public transit system.
This partnership provides opportunities for local adults and children with disabilities and enhance their independence and participation in the community.
If you have additional questions or concerns: Please call 306-770-2263 or email swifttransit@saskabilities.ca and we will be happy to assist you.
