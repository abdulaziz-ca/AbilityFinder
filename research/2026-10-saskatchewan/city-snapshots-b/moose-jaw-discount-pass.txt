Transfers are included with your cash fare. Transfer tickets are eligible for use when an immediate transfer to another bus at the downtown transfer point is required to reach the final destination. Please advise your bus driver at the time of boarding if you require a transfer, and they will issue you a transfer ticket.
**Discounted monthly bus passes are available to those on social assistance under the Saskatchewan Employment Incentive, Saskatchewan Assured Income Disability, Saskatchewan Employment Supplement or Saskatchewan Income Support. Proof of participation in one of the above programs must be presented at the time of pass purchase.
***A family pass discount of $10.00 per pass is available when an adult is purchasing two or more passes for family members within the same household. Identification/health cards are required for all household members at the time of purchase.
Fare Options
Passengers may board using cash, tickets or monthly passes.
Transit operators do not carry change, nor do they sell tickets or passes. To avoid delays, have the exact fare ready upon boarding if paying cash.
Bus passes and tickets are available from:
 - Main Floor at the City Hall– 228 Main Street North (8:30 am-4:30 pm)
 - Yara Centre – 1220 High Street W (during regular hours of operation)
 - Kinsmen Sportsplex– 855 MacDonald Street (during regular hours of operation)
New in 2022: The Hotspot App can now be used to purchase regular Transit passes!
You can now purchase single bus tickets, 10-bus tickets, monthly bus passes, or seniors' annual bus passes directly from your mobile device. Skip the trip to City Hall to purchase a bus pass and never worry about losing your pass again, as it will be kept safely in your phone. 
Visit https://htsp.ca/how-it-works/ or scan the QR code below to download the app to your mobile device today!
Getting set up for Hotspot is easy!
Bus Passes & Tickets are non-transferable and non-refundable.
Monthly Passes
Use a monthly pass for convenience and economy. The pass provides unlimited rides for a full calendar month. Passes must be shown upon boarding, and they may only be used by the person whose signature appears on the back of the pass. Transit personnel have the right to confiscate monthly passes they feel are being used fraudulently.
Monthly passes are also available for Paratransit clients. All Paratransit clients must be registered (see Paratransit section for details).
Annual Senior Citizen Passes
An annual transit pass is available to Moose Jaw residents over the age of 60 years. The annual pass may be purchased in December of the preceding year.
Discounted Passes
For those on social assistance, the City of Moose Jaw has partnered with the Ministry of Social Services to make monthly passes available at a discounted rate. Current proof of participation in an eligible program is required -  a cheque stub, a letter from the program or a bank statement that clearly states the customer’s name, the program name and the date for the month of pass purchase will be accepted. The following programs are eligible:
SIS - Saskatchewan Income Support
SEI - Saskatchewan Employment Incentive
SAID – Saskatchewan Assured Income Disability
SES – Saskatchewan Employment Supplement
There is no visual distinction on the passes, however, proof of participation in one of the above programs must be presented at the time of purchase.  For more details, contact the Transit office at 306.694.4488.
