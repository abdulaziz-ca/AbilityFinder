 Accessible Parking Permit
 Program Details
The City of Saskatoon Accessible Parking Permit Program is an optional program allowing users with a valid SaskAbilities placard to park on the street for free in pay parking areas. The program strives to provide a convenient on-street parking option in pay parking zones without having to pay at a pay station for each session. 
The City Accessible Parking Permit is a hologram sticker attached to a valid SaskAbilities parking placard. The placard must be hung from the inside rear-view mirror and should be visible from outside of the vehicle. 
The permits expire every year at the end of October. Permits for the new permit year are available for purchase at the beginning of October. 
Questions about the Accessible Parking Permit Program can be emailed to parking.permits@saskatoon.ca. 
Parking Locations
The Accessible Parking Permit is valid on the street in pay parking zones only. The permit is not valid in the following locations:
- Public or private parking lots, including City, University, Airport, or Hospital lots
- Residential Parking Zones or other special parking zones
- Private property
- Bus Stops
- No Parking or No Stopping areas
Parking Time Limits
Users with a valid Accessible Parking Permit may park on the street without paying for up to 4 hours in:
- Any regular pay parking space
- Accessible parking zones
- Loading zones
Permit Cost
- Annual cost of $100+gst
- Pro-rated cost of $50+gst (effective May 1)
Replacement permits will be provided free of charge. 
Purchasing an Accessible Parking Permit
Permits are available for purchase at City Hall during regular business hours (8 a.m. to 5 p.m., Monday to Friday, excluding statutory holidays). Please bring your provincial disability placard and a piece of I.D.. 
Accessible Parking Permit Subsidy
The City has launched a subsidy program to help off-set the cost for Saskatoon residents who qualify as low income. The subsidy allows qualified users to purchase a parking permit at a 50 percent discounted rate:
- Annual cost of $50+gst
- Pro-rated cost of $25+gst (effective May 1)
To apply for the subsidy:
- Fill out the online application form at the bottom of the page; or
- Print and fill out the application form found under "Related Documents" and either mail the form or bring it in to City Hall.
Please Note: You must receive your subsidy approval in order to purchase your permit at the reduced fee.
Questions about the parking permit subsidy may be emailed to: parkingsubsidy@saskatoon.ca.
