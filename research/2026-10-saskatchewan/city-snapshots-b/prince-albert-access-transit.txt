 Access Transit & Shuttles
 Section Menu
 If you cannot use the public Transit system because of a permanent or temporary disability, the Community Service Centre can help get you where you want to go through the Access Transit service. Access Transit is a door-to-door service, with ramps and lifts for wheelchair passengers that must be pre-booked.
Seniors
If you are over 60 years old and need door to door service you can use the Community Service Center's Seniors Transportation service. Rides are pre-booked through the Community Service Center at least 24 hours ahead.
Shuttles
The companies listed below provide shuttle transportation services. Dates, times, and prices vary. Please contact these services directly for more details. Shuttles offer a flat rate for trips and trips must be booked at least two hours in advance.
