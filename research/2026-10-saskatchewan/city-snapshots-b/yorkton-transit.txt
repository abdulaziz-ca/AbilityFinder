 Yorkton Transit Is Changing
January 2027
Instead of following set routes and schedules, riders will be able to book trips when and where they need them. The new transit service will include a mobile app, online and phone booking options, new vehicles, simpler fare passes, and one service that combines regular and accessible transit.

 The City offers three types of transit services based on the rider's needs.
These are services are provided in partnership with the City of Yorkton, SaskAbilities and Society for the Involvement of Good Neighbours (SIGN). This partnership provides opportunities for local adults and children with disabilities. Call 306-621-6580 or email SaskAbilities with any questions.
 Yorkton Transit
 Yorkton Transit provides safe, reliable and affordable transportation on scheduled, fixed routes. It runs on a one-hour route with the start of each half-hour at the Yorkton Legacy Co-op Grocery Store.

 Access Transit
 Access Transit is a shared ride, door-to-door service for people with disabilities who are unable to use regular public transportation. Operated by SaskAbilities, this service helps individuals travel safely and independently within Yorkton. Riders must register in advance to use the service.

SIGN Senior Mobility
 A safe and affordable ride service for Yorkton residents aged 55 and over (who aren’t wheelchair‐bound). SIGN provides clean, smoke‑free vehicles and helpful drivers. With a small fee for rides, funded by donations and the City, it makes trips to appointments, shopping, or social outings easy.

 Rider Etiquette
 To make everyone's trip with Yorkton Transit better, please abide by the following rules:
- Be ready and waiting at the stop several minutes ahead of time;
- Be ready to board or disembark as soon as the bus stops;
- Refrain from eating or drinking on the bus;
- Refrain from wearing scented products;
- Take any garbage with you when you leave;
- Keep aisles clear from bags and purses by placing on your lap or between your feet;
- Please use headphones when listening to music;
- Please keep conversations and cell phone volume quiet;
- Please do not distract the driver.
