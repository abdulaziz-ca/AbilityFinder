 Saskatchewan Housing Benefit
 Saskatchewan Housing Benefit is a monthly benefit that helps eligible Saskatchewan renters with their shelter costs (rent and utilities). The Saskatchewan Housing Benefit is co-funded by the Government of Canada and the Government of Saskatchewan under the National Housing Strategy.

 2. Eligibility
 Renters are eligible for the benefit if they:
 - Spend 35 per cent or more of their annual before-tax household income on shelter costs (rent and utilities);
 - Have less than $300,000 in household assets; and
 - Have an annual before-tax household income at or below:
 Single and couples
 Single or couple with one dependant
 Single or couple with two or more dependants
 Annual Income Limit
 $43,100
 $52,900
 $63,800
Renters are not eligible for the benefit if they: 
 - receive shelter support from another Government of Saskatchewan income assistance or training program;
 - rent from a housing authority under the Social Housing Program;
 - are a sponsored newcomer to Canada; or
 - are incarcerated in a federal or provincial facility or a community training residence; or
 - are a full-time post-secondary student.
There are two additional SHB streams that provide financial support for people in vulnerable living situations: the Supportive Housing Stream and the Seeking Safety from Interpersonal Violence Stream.

 3. How to Apply
 Complete the Saskatchewan Housing Benefit application using one of the following options. Both applications are the same. You only need to complete one application.
Saskatchewan Housing Benefit Online Application
or
Download the PDF application
If you choose to apply using the PDF form, you can either fill out the application on your computer or print it and complete it by hand.
Once you have completed the PDF application, forward it along with all required documents to Saskatchewan Housing Corporation (SHC) by: 
 - Email: SaskHousingBenefit@gov.sk.ca; 
 - Fax: 1-306-798-3110; or
 - Mail: 11th Floor - 1920 Broad St, Regina, SK, S4P 3V6
SHC will contact you if additional information is required. If you do not hear from SHC within 14 business days, please contact SHC at SaskHousingBenefit@gov.sk.ca or at 1-844-787-4177.
Please allow additional processing time if you are mailing your application.
