 Corner Brook Transit
 Corner Brook Transit Hours 
 Regular Fixed-Route Bus Service 
 Monday to Friday, 7:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. 
 Download the Transit Schedule (PDF)  
 Open Interactive Transit Map 
 Evening and Weekend On‑Demand Service
- Monday to Friday: 6:00 p.m. to 11:00 p.m.
- Saturday and Sunday: 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.
- Please be advised that Corner Brook Transit services are not available on statutory holidays.
 How to Book 
 Book your trip using the Blaise Transit App , available through mobile app stores or online at www.blaisetransit.com .
 You can also book by phone: 709-289-3124 
 Important information when creating a Blaise Account 
 Only riders approved for Accessible Transit should select “Reduced Mobility” in the app. If you use regular transit services and are not registered for Accessible Transit, do not select this option.
 Accessible Transit (CBT Link) 
 CBT Link is Corner Brook’s accessible, door-to-door transit service for residents whose disabilities prevent them from using the regular transit system.
 Eligibility 
 All riders must be approved and registered before using the service.
 How to Apply 
 To apply: 
- Call 709-637-1666 to request an application, or
- Download the application form ( PDF ).
 Please note: 
- The application must be printed and completed in full.
- A healthcare professional must complete the medical section of the form.
- All pages must be submitted. Incomplete applications may delay processing.
 Booking a Ride 
 Approved riders can book through the Blaise Transit App or by calling  709-289-3124 
 Service Details   
- Trips are available anywhere within Corner Brook city limits during operating hours.
- Approved attendants or support persons ride free of charge when accompanying an eligible rider.
- Standard transit fares apply unless the rider qualifies for the Free Transit Pass Program.
 Free Transit Passes For Eligible Residents
 The City of Corner Brook provides free transit passes to eligible residents through funding from the Government of Newfoundland and Labrador.
 Eligible Residents 
- Individuals receiving income support
- Youth receiving youth services
- Seniors receiving the Guaranteed Income Supplement (GIS)
 How to Access Passes 
 Income Support and Youth Services Recipients 
- Provincial program representatives will contact eligible participants directly.
 Seniors Receiving GIS 
- Pick up passes at City Hall.
- Bring government-issued photo ID and proof of GIS benefits.
- To obtain proof of GIS from Service Canada, call: 1-800-277-9914 
 Transit Fares and Passes
 Payment Options 
- Cash Fare — Exact change required.
- Transit M‑Card — Reloadable card for ride cards and monthly/semester passes. Click here for more information regarding the M-Card
 Regular Cash Fares 
- Adult — $3.25 
- Children under 12 — $2.00 
- Preschool with adult — FREE 
 E-Rides 
- Adult 5‑Ride — $13.50 
- Senior 5‑Ride (65+) — $12.50 
- Student 5‑Ride — $12.50 Student ID required 
- Student 50‑Ride — $55.00 Student ID required 
 E-Passes 
- Adult Monthly Pass — $80.00 
- Senior Monthly Pass (65+) — $60.00 
- Student Semester Pass — $220.00 Valid Sept–Dec 31 or Jan–Apr 30 . Student ID required 
 Passes can be renewed: 
- Online through the Corner Brook Transit Registration and Reloading Site 
- In person at City Hall Customer Service  Monday to Friday, 8:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. 
 Where to Purchase
- City Hall — Paper Passes & M‑Cards
- City Pharmacy — Paper Ride Cards Only
- Grenfell Campus Bookstore — M‑Card
