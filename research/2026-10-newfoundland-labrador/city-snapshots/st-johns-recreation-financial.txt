 Financial Supports
 Section Menu 
 Financial support may be available for individuals and families facing financial barriers to participate in recreation and leisure programs. The type of financial support is based on, age, program type, family income and family size.
 Children's Programs
 Financial assistance is available for residents of St. John's who are applying for the  After School or Preschool Programs and camps , including spring and summer camps for children under 16 years of age. Learn more about how to apply below
 Adult Programs
 Financial Assistance is available for residents of St. John's who are applying for City of St. John's Programs
 The Adult Program Financial Assistance is for St. John's residents who are over 18 years of age. Learn more about how to apply below. 
 REAL Program
 The REAL Program helps children and youth access sports, arts, recreation, and other community programs by providing financial support to eligible families. Check out our REAL Program webpage for more information. 
 How to Apply - Financial Subsidy for Children's Programs
 Eligibility
 Applications for financial support for City of St. John's programs are considered based on level of need and determined by  Statistics Canada's Low Income Cut Off . Applicants must meet the program requirements (such as age, designated school, etc.).
 Applications  must be completed in full and include:
- Proof of residency for St. John's which clearly identifies the applicant's name and address. Acceptable documents include a utility bill, driver's license, etc.
- Proof of income from all sources such as:
- Canada Revenue Agency Notice of Assessment for the previous year (applications submitted in 2022 are based on 2021 income): Call 1-800-959-8281
- Canada Child Tax Benefit Notice: Call 1-800-387-1193
 Deadlines
 Deadline dates occurring on a weekend are extended to the following Monday.
- Spring Camp - February 1
- Pre School and After School Care - March 1
- Summer Camp - March 17
 Application Process
- One application per program is required
- Applications may be approved for full or partial subsidy based on need.
- Applicants will be notified once the application is processed.
- Please allow 7-10 business days for us to reply.

 How to Apply - Financial Subsidy for Adult Programs
 Eligibility 
 Applications for financial support for City of St. John's programs are considered based on level of need and determined by Statistics Canada's Low Income Cut Off. Applicants must meet the program requirements.
 Deadline
 Applications are accepted on an ongoing basis and are considered based on program availability.
 Application process
 Applications for adults  must be completed in full and include:
- Proof of residency for St. John's which clearly identifies the applicant's name and address. Acceptable documents include a utility bill, driver's license, etc.
- Proof of income from all sources such as: Canada Revenue Agency Notice of Assessment for the previous year (applications submitted in 2022 are based on 2021 income): Call 1-800-959-8281
- Applications may be approved for full or partial subsidy based on need.
- Applicants will be notified once the application is processed.
- Please allow 7-10 business days for us to reply.
