 Attendant Passes
 Section Menu 
 An Attendant Pass is a card that is issued to a person living with a disability that requires the support of another person (or persons) to be able to access events and activities in their community. This need has been verified by a professional during the application process.  
 The pass enables an attendant or support person to accompany a person requiring support to activities and/or events for free or at a discounted rate determined by the accepting venues. 
 The attendant pass belongs to the person who requires assistance. 
 Inclusive Services can issue an Attendant Pass for staff of agencies or organizations who support individuals requiring support. Contact us to learn more about the attendant pass by calling 709-576-4556 or email  inclusion@stjohns.ca .
 Apply
 Complete the  application  or  attendant pass renewal application  in the name of the person requiring support. A medical professional, social worker or representative of a disability agency must verify the request. Please include a photo.
 Submit the application in one of the following ways:
- by email:  inclusion@stjohns.ca 
- in person: Recreation Administrative Office, H.G.R. Mews Community Centre, 60 Blacker Ave
- by mail: Inclusion and Accessibility, P.O. Box 908, St. John's NL, A1C 5M2
 Please allow 2 to 4 weeks for processing. Certain restrictions may apply.
