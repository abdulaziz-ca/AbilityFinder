 Health and Community Services > For Seniors > Community Support Services 
 Seniors who wish to remain living at home can access various community support programs offered through the regional health authorities. Two of these include:
- Home Support Program 
- Special Assistance Program 
 The cost of these programs are subsidized for individuals who have been assessed as eligible by staff of the regional health authority.
 For further information on these and other community support services in your region contact your regional health authority.
