 Newfoundland and Labrador offers a range of financial supports to help seniors access health care and live well in their communities. These programs are designed to reduce financial barriers and promote independence, dignity, and well-being.
 Key supports include:
- Aging Well at Home Grant: Up to $400 per year for low-income seniors to help with services like snow clearing, grocery delivery, and prescription drop-offs. Aging Well Grant 
- Caregiver Benefit: $400 per month for eligible caregivers providing unpaid support to seniors with complex needs. Caregiver Benefit 
- Financial Subsidies for Home Support, Personal Care Homes, and Long Term Care (based on eligibility criteria). Income Based Financial Assessment Policy Manual for Long Term Care and Community Support Services
