 Seniors > Seniors Health Care > Special Assistance Program 
 The Special Assistance Program (SAP) provides basic medical supplies and equipment to help individuals in the community with their daily living needs.
 Who Can Access the Program?
 To be eligible, individuals must:
- Live in the community (not in a long-term care home).
- Require medical supplies or equipment to assist with activities of daily living.
- Meet the program’s financial eligibility criteria.
 What does the Program Cover?
- Medical supplies (e.g., dressings, catheters, incontinence products)
- Oxygen and related equipment
- Orthotics (e.g., braces, burn garments)
- Mobility and support equipment (e.g., wheelchairs, walkers, commodes)
