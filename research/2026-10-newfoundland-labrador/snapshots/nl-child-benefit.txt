 Social Supports and Well-Being > Income Support > The Newfoundland and Labrador Child Benefit (NLCB) 
 Français 
 This is a provincial tax credit for families whose income is less than $38,193 and have children under 18 years of age
 If your net family income is below $20,397, you will receive the full benefit; however, there will be a reduced amount if your family income is between $20,397 & $38,193. This amount is based on your family income from the previous year. Payments run from July to June and are issued by the Canada Revenue Agency (CRA).
 In addition, you can receive the Prenatal-Early Childhood Nutrition Supplement , ($150 a month) if your child is below the age of five or you are pregnant.
 For information on federal benefits, visit the Child Benefit FAQ or the federal Child & Family Benefits section at CRA .

 Quick Facts – Newfoundland and Labrador Child Benefits
- It helps approximately 9,000 low income families.
- Became effective in July 1999.
- The amount received is based on family size and income.
- Families leaving Income Support for work keep their NLCB and other Child Benefits.
- This benefit does not affect Income Support entitlement.
- It is combined with federal child benefits into one monthly payment.
- Parents must file an income tax return each year to verify eligibility for the NLCB and Canada Child Benefit (CCB).
