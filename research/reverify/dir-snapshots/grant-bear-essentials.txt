Bear Essentials Bear Essentials is a family support program that assists with the costs of health-related travel and equipment for Island kids. The program is designed for families that struggle to afford the associated costs or that have barriers limiting their ability to access health care or equipment for their child. Bear Essentials is a referral-only program. A health care professional familiar with a child's needs and family circumstances must complete the online application on their behalf, with the consent of the applicant's parent or primary guardian. Eligibility criteria To be eligible for Bear Essentials, applicants must meet all of the following conditions: Age: 0 to 19 years old (up to 20th birthday) Location: Live on Vancouver Island or the surrounding islands Health status: Have a medical condition, health challenge, or disability confirmed by a health care professional Financial need: Require financial support to cover the cost of the item(s) in the application Funding limit: Have not exceeded the lifetime Bear Essentials maximum of $15,000 Review the full program guidelines for eligibility and learn how to apply.
