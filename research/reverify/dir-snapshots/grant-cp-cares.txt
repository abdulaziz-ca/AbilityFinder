CP CARES PROGRAM FUNDING ELIGIBILITY   a. Paid member of the CPAA for a minimum of 3 months with a completed Member Intake Form b. Application submitted with ALL supporting documents listed below:          i. Letter of diagnosis          ii. Support letter from a neutral party, not connected to the application          iii. Confirmation of efforts and other fundraising or funding opportunities approached and approved          iv. 3 quotes from vendors, should the funding cheque be issued directly to the vendor as a payment for the product or service. Talk to the vendor if they are accepting 3rd party payment prior to applying, as CPAA is not able to issue a cheque to the applicant prior to the purchase. OR         v. Receipts or proof of purchase, for reimbursement, if the product/services has been paid for already.  Purchases must be done within the last 6 months of the date of the application. CPAA members are permitted to apply once every 12 months, counting from their last application date, but the CPAA is not required to guarantee the funding.   FUNDING PRIORITIES & LIMITS Available support is not guaranteed and subject to change. Applications will be accepted and reviewed for one of the following per member: ​ Basic daily needs Mobility and/or adaptive equipment including adapted bikes Treatments and therapies not covered by other funding programs and benefits Technology including computers, laptops,  tablets, speech devices, adapted electronic devices ​   About the CP Cares Program The CP Cares Program assists Albertans with disabilities to achieve better quality of life by providing support with different levels of needs:  The CP Cares Program is open to paid members of the CPAA living with various disabilities to receive financial support, as an assistance with offsetting costs of basic daily needs supplies, adapted equipment and technology, programs,  educational courses, treatments and therapies, and items that would improve their quality of everyday life. In order to benefit from this program individuals must be a paid member of the CPAA for at least 3 months and have completed a Member Intake Form. Please note that you can only apply to the CP Cares Program once per year for a maximum request of up to $1,500, based on funds available.  For a complete list of funding maximums, please refer to the Application Form.  To join as a member, please click the button below. The CP Cares Program is funded entirely through the CPAA’s own fundraising efforts, the UCP's Bellows fund and donations from generous members of our community.    Questions or Care Packages request?
