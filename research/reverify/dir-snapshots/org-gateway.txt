SUPPORTING 4000 PEOPLE EVERY YEAR  Employment Resource Centre  Family Resource Centre HOW WE HELP Gateway Support  JOB SEEKER SUPPORT Job seekers receive one-on-one assistance to discover their interest fields, discover a place to work and find a job that fulfills them. Employees and employers receive ongoing support throughout the entire working relationship.  FAMILY SUPPORT Gateway supports people with disabilities and their families and fosters more inclusive communities. Our supports and services are as unique as the people we serve. Each individual is unique in their needs. We can help.  EMPLOYER SUPPORT In partnership with employers, Gateway develops meaningful, paid employment relationships that benefit both workers and employers. Our process involves thorough discovery with each job seeker, and full exploration with every potential employer.  WORKSHOP CALENDARS We offer workshops for families, job seekers, employers, and anyone interested in joining an inclusive community. Workshops cover topics such as transitioning to adulthood, as well as workshops specific to families and staff that use Family Managed Services (FMS). All workshops are free. Real people.
