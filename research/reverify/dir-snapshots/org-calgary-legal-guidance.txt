CLG offers free legal guidance to people who cannot afford a lawyer or who do not have access to paid services. Calgary Legal Guidance (CLG) plays an important role in Alberta’s justice sector and increases access to justice through free legal programs and services to thousands of people every year. All programs and services are designed to support Calgarians who face financial and other barriers to accessing legal services. As a non-profit organization, CLG offers a wide range of legal supports, including legal information, advice, and assistance in areas not provided by Legal Aid Alberta, as well as connections to community resources to strengthen an individual’s justice outcomes. CLG offers a number of volunteer clinics and programs that provide ongoing legal education, advice, assistance and navigation.
