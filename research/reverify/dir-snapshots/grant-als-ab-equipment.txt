About 300 – 400 Albertans and 3,000 Canadians live with ALS today. It’s a rare disease, but its impact is devastating and far-reaching. Last year, the ALS Society of Alberta supported over 450 clients and their loved ones. Dealing with a diagnosis? We can help with equipment and support. Through our provincial Equipment Loan Program, we are able to assist people living with ALS by lending power mobility devices, assistive communication devices, and other medical equipment. The Equipment Loan Program is free of charge to ensure support is readily available when our clients need it most. In order to make each day the best day possible for people living with and affected by ALS, we provide home visits, support groups, information, referrals and support for youth through The Debi Lefaivre ALS Youth Champions Fund. The ALS Society of Alberta also provides services in the Northwest Territories when required.
