Easter Seals Alberta has been a cornerstone of support, inclusion, and empowerment for Albertans living with disabilities and medical conditions. Since our beginnings in 1951, in all our versions and names, we have made a great contribution to fostering independence, enriching lives, and breaking down barriers through a wide range of programs. Through community-driven support and fundraising, Easter Seals has helped generations build confidence, participate fully in community life, and access the tools they need to thrive. Our legacy is one of compassion, resilience, and a more inclusive Alberta for all.
