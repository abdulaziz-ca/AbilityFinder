The program is designed for families that struggle to afford the associated costs or that have barriers limiting their ability to access health care or equipment for their child. Bear Essentials is a referral-only program . A health care professional familiar with a child’s needs and family circumstances must complete the online application on their behalf, with the consent of the applicant’s parent or primary guardian. If you have questions, see our FAQ or contact us . Eligibility criteria To be eligible for Bear Essentials, applicants must meet all of the following conditions: Age: 0 to 19 years old (up to 20th birthday) Location: Live on Vancouver Island or the surrounding islands Health status: Have a medical condition, health challenge, or disability confirmed by a health care professional Financial need: Require financial support to cover the cost of the item(s) in the application Funding limit: Have not exceeded the lifetime Bear Essentials maximum of $15,000 Review the full program guidelines for eligibility and learn how to apply. Apply now FAQ How do I apply? Applications for funding must be completed by a health professional. A health professional is anyone that knows the child’s health journey and can provide documentation to support the request. Examples: Social worker, public health nurse, early intervention specialist, audiologist, program navigator/coordinators, midwives and physicians/medical office assistants. However, if a health professional or family has questions about the process or are trying to navigate multiple funding sources, our Bear Essentials team is available for information and navigation support. What can I apply for? The Bear Essentials application guidelines are updated on April 1 of each year. This document outlines what is eligible for funding and how funding decisions are made. If you have any questions about applying, you can also contact the Bear Essentials Program Navigators at 250-940-4948 or bearessentials@islandkidsfirst.com . Is prenatal travel covered? The Bear Essentials program does not provide funding for any type of prenatal travel (e.g. prenatal relocation or prenatal appointments). Can families be reimbursed for past travel or previously purchased equipment? No. Applications must be received and approved in advance of travel or equipment purchase. What can I expect after a funding application has been submitted? The Bear Essentials team reviews applications during business hours Monday through Thursday, 8:30-4:30 pm. The program is not designed to address emergency funding needs. One of our Program Navigators will be in touch within five business days for travel applications. Equipment applications are not reviewed immediately and may take two to three weeks before an initial response. The Bear Essentials team reviews equipment-related funding requests bi-weekly. Applications may be deferred based on budget constraints. We work to provide updates on the status of these applications in a timely manner. Can I expect to get 100% of my expenses covered? The Bear Essentials program is designed to provide partial or supplementary funding to support families with challenges accessing health care or health care equipment for their child. Each application is individually reviewed and assessed, and maximum amounts are detailed in our guidelines . Can travel funding be applied separately for two caregivers? Supplementary travel funding covers one caregiver and the applicant. Approved funds can be split between two caregivers if requested. Once I’ve received Bear Essentials funding, do I need to reapply for future travel or equipment needs? A new application from a health professional is required for any future travel or equipment requests, even if you’ve received funding in the past. If you have questions about reapplying, please don’t hesitate to contact us. If you have questions about your application or support, please contact the Program Navigators at 250-940-4948 or bearessentials@islandkidsfirst.com Where does the money for Bear Essentials come from? The Bear Essentials program and our Homes Away From Home (Jeneece Place and Q̓ʷ alayu House) are supported entirely by our generous community of donors. Our Program Navigators work in collaboration with other funding sources to co-fund applications when and where available. I still have a few questions about Bear Essentials funding.
