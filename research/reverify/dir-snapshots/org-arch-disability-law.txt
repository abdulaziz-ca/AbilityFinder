ARCH is dedicated to defending and advancing the equality rights, entitlements, fundamental freedoms, and inclusion of persons with disabilities with low income in Ontario. ARCH is primarily funded by Legal Aid Ontario. ARCH provides a range of legal services directly to persons with disabilities. Learn more about ARCH’s legal services .
