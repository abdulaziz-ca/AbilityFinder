Child Grant Eligibility We are here when families need us Our support programs are designed to provide comprehensive assistance for BC children with disabilities and complex health needs. Eligibility Requirements To qualify for our grants, families must meet the following criteria: The grant must be for a child who is 0-19 years of age. (Note: for autism assessment, the benefitting child must be 4 years or under.) The child must be a resident of B.C. or Yukon and must have resided here for a minimum of three months. The child must have a valid Health Care Card. The grant must be for a child with a qualified medical or developmental need recognized by a designated professional. (This does not apply to private autism and psychoeducational assessments or mental wellness requests) The family must have total household income of $85,000 or less. Please Note: Families can apply for up to two grants during Variety’s fiscal year October through September, with one application and one set of supporting documents. Type 1 Diabetes support has different eligibility requirements. Maximum individual grant amounts per fiscal year (Oct-Sept) Adaptive & mobility equipment: $4000 Van conversion: $7,500 Mental wellness counselling: $2,500 Specialized therapies: $2,500 Autism and psychoeducational assessments: $4,000 Tutoring bursary for learning disabilities: $1,500 Tuition (school fees): $4,000 Type 1 Diabetes Support is based on your PharmaCare Maximum Family Deductible. FAQ: An application can be started by a parent/caregiver, healthcare professional, social worker, support worker, teacher etc. but must be signed and submitted by the primary parent/caregiver. Most requests for child grants are considered year round, but all grants expire on September 30. Exceptions: Tuition is reviewed August 1 to October 31 only. Variety requires a new application and supporting documents annually. Where the Government can fund the item requested (partial or full), Variety requests that you apply to the Government for that particular item first. Variety will consider top ups, by providing funding for items that the Government or private healthcare plan will not cover fully. Not funded: services or equipment not directly related to a child's complex needs; medicines/prescriptions, eye glasses or dental, MSP premiums or deductibles; alternative or naturopathic treatments or those not provided in person; anything paid wholly by government; behavioural intervention/consultation; autism services for children under six receiving over $22,000 per year in government funding (except music therapy); childcare, respite; warranties, insurance, maintenance, batteries, repairs; administrative fees; electronic devices incl. apps or software; travel costs; non-adaptive sports equipment; residents outside B.C. or Yukon; reports or consultations; autism assessments for children age 5 and over; more than one autism/psychoeducational assessment per child; expenses initiated before submission or written approval; providers without certification; non-Canadian distributors or services outside Canada.
