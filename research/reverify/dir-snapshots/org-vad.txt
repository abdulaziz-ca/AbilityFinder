The DOORS are LOCKED – 780-488-9088 CURRENT EVENTS DEAM – Disability Employment Awareness Month Every October, Disability Employment Awareness Month (DEAM) shines a spotlight on the importance of creating inclusive workplaces where people with disabilities have equal opportunities to succeed. It is a time to celebrate the valuable contributions that employees with disabilities make to our communities while encouraging employers to remove barriers to employment. Despite the skills, education, and experience that many people with disabilities bring to the workforce, barriers to employment continue to exist. These barriers may include inaccessible workplaces, misconceptions about disability, limited accommodations, or hiring practices that unintentionally exclude qualified candidates. The good news is that accessibility benefits everyone. When employers embrace inclusive hiring practices, they gain access to a larger talent pool, improve workplace culture, increase innovation, and build stronger, more diverse teams. Many workplace accommodations are simple, low-cost, or even free to implement, yet they can make a significant difference in an employee’s ability to thrive. At the Voice of Albertans with Disabilities (VAD), we believe that employment is about more than earning a paycheck—it is about independence, dignity, purpose, and full participation in community life. We continue to work alongside employers, community organizations, and government partners to promote accessibility, disability awareness, and inclusive employment practices across Alberta. IDPD Community Resource Fair 2026 Every year on December 3, communities around the world recognize the International Day of Persons with Disabilities (IDPD). This important day promotes awareness of disability issues, celebrates the contributions and achievements of persons with disabilities, and encourages everyone to help build communities where accessibility, inclusion, and full participation are part of everyday life. At Voice of Albertans with Disabilities (VAD), IDPD reflects the heart of the work we do throughout the year. It is an opportunity to celebrate progress, connect people with valuable supports and services, and continue conversations about removing barriers for persons with disabilities. Join Us for VAD’s 2026 IDPD Community Resource Fair! To celebrate IDPD, VAD is once again bringing community members, service providers, organizations, advocates, and people with disabilities together for our IDPD Community Resource Fair. Thursday, December 3, 2026 Mill Woods Town Centre 2331 66 Street NW, Edmonton 11:00 AM – 3:00 PM The Resource Fair provides an opportunity to discover programs, services, resources, and supports available throughout Edmonton and Alberta. Visitors can meet directly with community organizations, ask questions, learn about available services, and make valuable connections—all in one welcoming space.
