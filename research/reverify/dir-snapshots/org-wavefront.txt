WHY CHOOSE US GET IN TOUCH Access changes everything — Helping people connect, communicate, and thrive Since 1956, Wavefront Centre for Communication Accessibility has been creating more inclusive communities through hearing healthcare, communication accessibility, community outreach, and research. As a charitable non-profit and social enterprise, every service you choose helps expand access and strengthen programs that support people who are Deaf, DeafBlind, and Hard of Hearing. From expert hearing healthcare and accessibility services to seniors outreach, employment support, and DeafBlind services, our team is here to help people connect, communicate, and thrive. Across our three locations, our experienced clinicians provide personalized recommendations and evidence-based care to help you hear with confidence. LEARN MORE Accessibility Services Access ASL On-Demand Interpreting Access ASL provides instant access to professional ASL interpreters, helping organizations communicate effectively with Deaf customers, employees, and community members. Connect in seconds with no advance scheduling, using any smartphone, tablet, or computer to support accessible, inclusive interactions wherever communication happens. Flexible subscription plans make it easy for organizations of all sizes to improve customer service, support employees, and create more accessible workplaces while meeting accessibility commitments. LEARN MORE Sign Language Interpreting Professional ASL services for clear, inclusive communication Our Registered Sign Language Interpreters bridge communication gaps between American Sign Language (ASL) and English for people who are Deaf, DeafBlind, and Hard of Hearing. Wavefront Centre provides real-time interpretation for medical, legal, educational, and professional settings, both in-person and remotely. With expertise in diverse communication strategies, our interpreters meet rigorous qualifications and ongoing training to ensure accurate, culturally appropriate services for any occasion.
