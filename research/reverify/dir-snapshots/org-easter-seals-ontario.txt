Easter Seals Ontario - Services Discover the services and supports we provide for children, youth, and young adults with disabilities, and their families. Let's Get Started . STEP 1 Register Your Child Your child must be registered as an Easter Seals Ontario client to apply to the Equipment Funding Program or to attend an Easter Seals Ontario camp. Please complete and submit a Child Registration Application. (If your child has previously registered with Easter Seals Ontario, you can proceed to STEP 2.) How to Register Your Child Child Registration Application STEP 2 Access Equipment Funding Easter Seals Ontario offers funding for essential mobility and accessibility equipment for children and youth who are registered with our organization. Children and youth who are registered with Easter Seals Ontario may be eligible to attend camp programs and provided with funding towards the cost. Additional Services We Offer Easter Seals Ontario Camps Easter Seals camps – Camp Merrywood and Camp Woodeden – offer the rare gift of accessible and inclusive experiences, specially tailored for kids, youth, and young adults with physical disabilities. At camp, barriers disappear and campers can embrace new challenges, build confidence, and discover what they are truly capable of achieving. Camp Information Incontinence Supplies Grant and Top Up Grant Easter Seals Ontario administers two government funded programs. These programs are fully funded and criteria is set by each Ministry. You can find more information and how to apply to either program below. IGprogram.EasterSeals.org Scholarships Families of children with physical disabilities often incur significant additional expenses, making it challenging to send their child to post-secondary school. This is why we are so grateful to our donors who generously provide financial support to allow these students to pursue their academic dreams through the Easter Seals Ontario Scholarship Program. More Information Provincial Ambassador Program Provincial Ambassadors have the opportunity to speak in front of audiences at major Easter Seals events, be interviewed by media for television, radio and newspaper coverage, meet-and-greet celebrities, meet other Easter Seals youth and past Ambassadors, and attend unique and fun events. The role is an excellent opportunity for youth to develop public speaking skills, gain confidence, acquire new skills and more.
