ABOUT US In the spotlight What’s happening now at Kids Cancer Care YOU’RE HELPING ARTEM FIND HIS WAY BACK TO THE CLASSROOM! At just two and a half years old, Artem’s childhood changed when he was diagnosed with a rare and aggressive brain tumour. After three years of surgeries, chemotherapy, radiation and three bone marrow transplants, his family arrived in Canada. Cancer-free but facing a new language, anxiety and his first experience of school at age six, Artem needed support to find his place and rebuild his confidence. Because of you, he didn’t have to do it alone. Your support helped Artem take his first steps into the classroom, giving him the confidence to learn, belong and reclaim the childhood cancer interrupted.
