Please email recreation@brampton.ca​ should you have any questions. ​ ActiveAssist What is ActiveAssist? ActiveAssist is a fee subsidy program offered by the City of Brampton, designed to help low-income families and individuals in Brampton participate in Recreation programs. Confidentiality is always maintained. This assistance is given in the form of a credit to your account. Subsidy credits can be used towards&#58; Most registered programs Drop-in programs Memberships *some exclusions apply What Do I need to Apply? Provide proof of residency (eg. utility bill, lease agreement, driver’s license, etc). Provide proof of total family net income. Provide proof of legal responsibility for all dependents listed on the application. How Much Do I Get? Once your application is received and approved, your account will be activated. Staff will apply the subsidy amount to the primary account holder as a single lump sum payment in the amount of $275 for each eligible member of the family (e.g. An approved family of four (4) would receive $1,100.00). Once approved, you qualify for the program for 2 years. You will receive an approval confirmation with important information about the program and registration. Please Note&#58; Expired ActiveAssist subsidy allocation will not be automatically renewed for you. If your ActiveAssist subsidy has expired and you would like to renew, please re-apply using the links in the "Complete an Application" section. A member of our team will connect with you once we have received your completed application. ​ ​​ Complete an Application There are three easy ways to apply for ActiveAssist&#58; Online (must attach requ​ired documentation with application) Pick up or drop off an application form at any Brampton Recreation Centre By mail to&#58; ActiveAssist Brampton, 2 Wellington St. W., Brampton, ON L6Y 4R2 To download a fillable pdf. application form, please click here​ ​.
