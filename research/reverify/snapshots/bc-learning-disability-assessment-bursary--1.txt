What is available? Am I eligible? How do I apply? What is available? Up to $3,500 is available to eligible students. If you have a learning disability, you need a recent learning disability assessment to determine eligibility for services and/or equipment. Am I eligible? You may be eligible if you: qualify for student funding through StudentAid BC are enrolled in full-time or part-time post-secondary level courses at a designated public post-secondary institution in B.C. have been recommended to have a learning disability assessment by the accessibility services office at your school How do I apply? Please contact the accessibility services office at the post-secondary institution you are planning to attend.
