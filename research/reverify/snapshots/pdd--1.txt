Skip to On this page Skip to Explore pages in Explore pages in: Persons with Developmental Disabilities (PDD) Disability Services office locations Eligibility Supports and services How to apply How to get services Resolve concerns Appeal a decision How to stay safe Resources Introduction The Persons with Developmental Disabilities (PDD) program helps eligible adults plan, coordinate and access services to live as independently as they can in their community. The program is based on the Persons with Developmental Disabilities Services Act and Regulations . Get help to navigate and access disability supports and services in Alberta from Family resource centres . In focus PDD – Eligibility Adults must meet a number of criteria to be eligible for the Persons with Developmental Disabilities (PDD) program. PDD – How to apply Follow these steps to apply for the Persons with Developmental Disabilities (PDD) program. PDD – Supports and services The Persons with Developmental Disabilities (PDD) program helps eligible adults with planning and services to meet personal needs and goals. PDD Policy Manual The Persons with Developmental Disabilities (PDD) Policy Manual guides and directs the delivery of the PDD program. It contains information for staff, community service providers, and Individuals with developmental disabilities and their supporters.
