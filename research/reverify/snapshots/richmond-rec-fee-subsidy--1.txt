Financial hardship is defined as when a person has difficulty paying for basic living costs such as food, clothing and housing, and does not have savings or other financial resources. RFSP participants receive financial support for many registered and drop-in parks, recreation and cultural programs offered by the City of Richmond and Community Associations and Societies. For residents who qualify, the RFSP provides: Unlimited free admission to many drop-in programs and services A 90 per cent discount on most registered programs: Up to a maximum of $300 per year in subsidy for children and youth (18 years and under) Up to a maximum of $100 per year in subsidy for adults and seniors (19+ years) Select opportunities at the Richmond Olympic Oval The following Rack Card provides a brief overview of the program: RFSP Rack Card (English) RFSP Rack Card (Chinese) Program Application Applications are now being accepted for the 2026−2027 program year (Sep 1, 2026–Aug 31, 2027). Contact the RFSP office at subsidy@richmond.ca or 604-247-4909 for application support and more information. If you are a newcomer from Ukraine wishing to apply for support, contact the subsidy office. Apply for RFSP 2026-2027 Program Eligibility To be eligible for the RFSP, individuals/households must live in Richmond AND be experiencing financial hardship as determined by one of the following two methods. Individuals/households who are receiving government financial assistance from one of the following programs: Youth Agreements Strengthening Abilities and Journeys of Empowerment (SAJE) Income Assistance Disability Assistance Resettlement Assistance Rental Assistance Program (RAP) Shelter Aid for Elderly Renters (SAFER) Guaranteed Income Supplement (GIS) Canada Disability Benefit (CDB) OR Individuals/households who do not receive government financial assistance but meet the following financial limits: Family Net Income (line 23600): Family Size Maximum 1 $26,442 2 $37,395 3 $45,799 4 $52,884 5 $59,126 6 $64,769 7 $69,959 Other Financial Limits Type Maximum Family Size 1 Family Size 2+ Interest and Investment Income (Line 12100) $250 $500 Rental Income (Line 12600) $1,000 $2,000 Capital Gains (Line 12700) $500 $1,000 RRSP Contributions (Line 20800) $1,000 $2,000 Resources The Instructions for Required Documents offer step-by-step instructions to locate the supporting documents required to apply to the program: Instructions for Required Documents - English Instructions for Required Documents - Simplified Chinese Instructions for Required Documents - Traditional Chinese The Frequently Asked Questions provides answers to commonly asked questions about the program, such as who can submit an application, how to get required documents, and when participants can begin to use their subsidy: Frequently Asked Questions - English Frequently Asked Questions - Simplified Chinese Frequently Asked Questions - Traditional Chinese In-person application support is available by appointment. Contact the Registration Call Centre at 604-276-4300, Monday to Friday, 8:30am-5:00pm (excluding statutory holidays) to schedule a 15-minute appointment. Appointments will be held at Richmond City Hall (6911 No. 3 Road). How to Submit Completed applications and supporting documents can be submitted in one of these ways: Email: subsidy@richmond.ca Mail: Recreation Fee Subsidy Program, Parks, Recreation and Culture Division, 6911 No. 3 Road, Richmond, BC V6Y 2C1 In-person: at any City of Richmond recreation facility, the Richmond Cultural Centre or Richmond City Hall. Note: In-person application support (by appointment only) is available at City Hall, 6911 No. 3 Road, Richmond, BC. Please call 604-276-4300 for a 15-minute appointment, no drop-in session.
