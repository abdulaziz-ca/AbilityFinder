Beware of scams There is no fee to apply or renew your coverage for the CDCP. Be careful of scams sent by mail, phone calls, text messages, or emails that ask for your personal, banking or credit card information. Watch out for ads and pop-ups that are not from official Government of Canada websites. If you are concerned about the legitimacy of any CDCP communication, please contact us .
