The CWB has two parts: a basic amount and a disability supplement. Sections Who is eligible Depending on your income and other circumstances, you may be eligible for the Canada Workers Benefit. How much you can get The amount you can expect to receive for the Canada Workers Benefit depends on your income and your province or territory of residence. How to claim You can claim the Canada Workers Benefit when you file your tax return.
