You may qualify for up to $25,000 in forgivable loans to keep your home in good repair or install accessibility features . Who is eligible You may qualify based on: Your income Assessed value of your home (Maximum of $600,00) (MPAC Assessment) Review the fact sheet for more information on eligibility and for a list of projects that could be covered. Call 519-575-4400 or email us if you have any questions. Ontario Renovates Fact Sheet Email us Examples of repairs Repairs and rehabilitation Examples of eligible repairs and rehabilitation include, but are not limited to: Building exterior such as roofs, walls or foundations Doors and windows Plumbing Fire safety Electrical systems Heating systems Mold remediation Accessibility modifications Examples of eligible accessibility modifications include, but are not limited to: Ramps Handrails Chair and bath lifts Height adjustments to counter tops Cues for doorbells/fire alarms Bathroom modifications How to apply 1) Download and complete the Ontario Renovates application form 2) Submit your completed form by email, fax, mail or in person. Email : OntarioRenovates@regionofwaterloo.ca Fax : 519-575-4026 Send by mail or drop off in person at one of the following Regional offices: 20 Weber St. E., Kitchener, ON N2H 1C3 150 Main St., Cambridge, ON N1R 6R1 99 Regina St. S., Waterloo, ON N2J 4V3 Ontario Renovates Application Form To receive a loan this year you will need to apply before December 1. A limited amount of funding is available each year. We will set up a home inspection if you qualify. If you need repairs right away because of a water leak or other urgent issue, call 519-575-4400 . This program is funded by the federal and provincial governments through the Ontario Priorities Housing Initiative (OPHI). You can also find out if you may be eligible for funding for building affordable secondary suites, available through the Ontario Renovates program.
