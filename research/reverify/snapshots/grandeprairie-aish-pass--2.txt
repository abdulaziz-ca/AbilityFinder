All youth in the City of Grande Prairie, aged 17 years and under, are entitled to free transit with a valid Youth SUPERPASS card. FAQ&#039;s Transit Fares What is the SUPERPASS? The SUPERPASS is a new, electronic smart card that can be loaded and reloaded to pay your transit fare. It is designed to make your transit commute easier and more convenient. How does the SUPERPASS work? SUPERPASS cards can be purchased either at one of our vendors or at a City of Grande Prairie facility. The initial fee for a SUPERPASS card is $5.50. After initial purchase of your SUPERPASS card, simply reload your card online or by visiting one of the City of Grande Prairie facilities. When you board a Grande Prairie Transit bus, simply tap your SUPERPASS against the card reader located on the electronic fare-box next to the driver. Hold it until it approves your pass and find your seat. If you use a SUPERPASS, no paper transfer is required. The SUPERPASS automatically creates a 120-minute countdown in its program, so if you swipe it again within this timeframe it won’t deduct a fare. If you pay with cash (coin only), kindly ask the Transit Operator for a transfer. Transfers are valid for 120 minutes after the transfer is issued. Do I need to purchase a new SUPERPASS each month? No. Once you have a SUPERPASS, you simply need to reload your card by purchasing either a new monthly pass fare or ride fare. How do I reload a SUPERPASS? Purchasing and re-loading a new monthly pass or rides onto your SUPERPASS can be done easily online or in-person. To do it online visit superpass.cityofgp.com and follow the simple, step-by-step instructions. A credit card is required. Re-loading your SUPERPASS on-line can take up to 24-48 hours to become active. To do it instantly in-person, visit any participating City of Grande Prairie facility (City Hall, City Service Centre, Eastlink Centre, and Muskoseepi Park) or at the downtown Co-Op. You will have to bring your SUPERPASS with you to complete the transaction. Your card will be updated immediately. Please note, third party vendors such as Shoppers Drug Mart are unable to re-load a SUPERPASS. Only pre-loaded SUPERPASS cards are available at these locations. What does it mean to register a SUPERPASS? With the new SUPERPASS you are now able to register it online and create an account with Grande Prairie Transit. Creating this account and registering your SUPERPASS gives you access to view your remaining balance and reload it online. Registration also means that in the event you lose your card, we are able to transfer your existing balance to a new SUPERPASS and cancel the one that was lost. Register online at superpass.cityofgp.com or by visiting participating City of Grande Prairie facilities (City Hall, City Service Centre, Eastlink Centre, and Muskoseepi Park). How do I keep track of my SUPERPASS balance? To keep track of your balance online at any time, register your SUPERPASS with a Grande Prairie Transit account superpass.cityofgp.com . If you wish to keep your SUPERPASS unregistered, a message will flash on a display each time you tap your card for fare payment with your remaining balance. What happens if I lose my SUPERPASS? We highly recommend registering your SUPERPASS so that in the event you lose it, we can assist you better. When a registered SUPERPASS goes missing, attend any City facility noted below with appropriate identification. We can then simply transfer your remaining balance to a new SUPERPASS and cancel the missing one. Please note, this transaction can only be completed at a participating City of Grande Prairie facility (City Hall, City Service Centre, Eastlink Centre, City on 99 and Muskoseepi Park). A fee of $5.50 will be charged to replace a lost SUPERPASS. Note that there are no refunds for lost cards and that only those cards which are registered will have the ability to have your unused value transferred to a new card. Are purchased fares refundable? Passes and rides are non-refundable. The City of Grande Prairie is not responsible for lost or stolen transit monthly passes or rides. What data is being collected via the SUPERPASS? The SUPERPASS program allows Grande Prairie Transit to vastly improve our methods of data collection. For the first time ever, we will be able to access data such as fare category, travel times, routes used and stop characteristics. This will provide exceptional value as we seek to improve our service, design new routes and analyze system performance. The SUPERPASS does not collect any personal identification or contact information. Grande Prairie Transit will never track an individual’s SUPERPASS use history or share individualized data with third parties. Only aggregate data will be analyzed for system planning. What if I receive my regular bus pass through Assured Income for the Severely Handicapped (AISH), the Transit Access Program (TAP) or my school? If you currently use an AISH, the Transit Access Program (TAP) or school pass, keep in touch with your normal pass vendor to confirm what changes you’ll need to be aware of. When were fares last increased? Starting January 1, 2026 This increase stems from Council's budget strategy developed during budget deliberations and aligns with Bylaw C-1395: Fees, Rates and Charges. Youth transit fares remain free with a valid youth SUPERPASS card, providing Grande Prairie’s young residents the convenience of accessing community spaces like recreational facilities, the Prairie Mall and Eastlink Centre, as well as to and from school. This decision supports the City’s commitment to maintaining an accessible, innovative and sustainable transit system while addressing rising operational costs. What is the overall increase to fares? The fare adjustment includes an 8-10 per cent increase in some fare products with no change to daily cash fares. How can I provide feedback about the rate increase? We value your input. Feedback can be shared by emailing info@cityofgp.com so your feedback is tracked and can be passed to the appropriate contact.
