When you open a plan, you may also get grants and bonds from the Government of Canada to help with your long-term savings. Sections How much you could get in grants and bonds Family income and amount of contributions determine how much matching grants and bonds you could get. How to open a plan A plan can be opened for an eligible beneficiary directly at a financial organization. Who can open a plan and apply You apply for grants and bonds when you open a plan. Make contributions and watch savings grow Make contributions to your plan to ensure growth. If you lose Disability Tax Credit approval What you can and can't do in an RDSP when the beneficiary has lost DTC approval. Withdraw money from your plan Withdraw amounts from the plan at least 10 years after the last time you received a grant or a bond. Transfer or close a plan Plans can be transferred from one financial organization to another. Closing a plan may involve repayments. When a beneficiary dies, there may be a repayment of grant and bond. Any money remaining in the plan after the repayment will go to their estate.
