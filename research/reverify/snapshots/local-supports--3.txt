In most of Canada, the free and confidential service can be accessed 24 hours a day, in more than 150 languages, by phone, chat, text, and web. 211 connects people to the right information and services, making their pathway to care and resources a guided and trusted one. The 211 service is provided across Canada by United Way Centraide Canada and a national network of 211 Service Providers. 211 is available by phone, chat, text and website. You do not have to give your name or personal details to ask for information. Dial 2-1-1 to connect to community services near you.
