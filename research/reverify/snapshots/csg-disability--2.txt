Application processing times The majority of applications are processed within 30 days. Applications under review Some applications require a manual review to determine eligibility for student aid. Reviews may be initiated due to funding restrictions, academic progress, information discrepancies, or other eligibility criteria. At times, we may ask students for missing information or verification documents. We encourage students to respond to information requests as quickly as possible. Reviews are processed in the order they are received. If you’ve already applied, please do not close your application and reapply. This will move your application to the back of the queue and delay the processing time. Applications requiring a manual review may take up to 45 days to process.
