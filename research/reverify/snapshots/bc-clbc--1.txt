Community Living BC funds a range of services and also helps eligible individuals find opportunities to connect to their communities. What funded support people get, and when, depends on how urgent their situation is and the amount of funding CLBC has at the time of the request. Supports to Participate in Your Community CLBC funds supports to help you get a job, learn new things, and enhance your quality of life by connecting to your community. Supports to Live in Your Home CLBC funds different types of supports to live in your own place, with a roommate or family in shared living, or in a staffed home. Support for You and Your Family’s Well-being Wellness support gives you services or funding to pay for things to help make daily responsibilities easier. Support to Connect to Community CLBC can support you to find ways to share your strengths and connect with your community in ways that make sense for you. Other Government Support If you are eligible for, or exploring CLBC supports, a number of other government agencies, organizations and ministries may also be able to assist you.
