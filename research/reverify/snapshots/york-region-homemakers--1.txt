Homemakers can help with laundry, meals and light housekeeping. Eligibility To be eligible for Homemakers Services you must: Be temporarily or permanently ill or disabled Meet financial need requirements How to Apply To apply for in-home homemaker services, please call 1-877-464-9675 or email @email .
