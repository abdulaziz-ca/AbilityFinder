My Self Serve - Home Ministry of Social Development and Poverty Reduction Skip to main content Skip to navigation Accessibility Statement Please call us at 1-866-866-0800 if you need help registering for or accessing My Self Serve Welcome to My Self Serve Yes, I have a MySS account Sign in No, I do not have a MySS account Create an account How to apply for or return to assistance My Self Serve (MySS) provides online access to income and disability assistance for residents of British Columbia. If you are not currently in receipt of income or disability assistance, My Self Serve will guide you through the Application for Assistance. If you need help with your application contact the ministry at 1-866-866-0800 . If you are currently in receipt of income or disability assistance, My Self Serve will allow you to securely access your current information online. For example, you can view personal messages from the ministry, submit your monthly report, and upload forms.
