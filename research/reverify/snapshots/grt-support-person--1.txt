The support person providing assistance will not pay a fare. Eligibility The program is open to anyone who, due to a disability, cannot travel independently on conventional or specialized transit service. The support person can be a family member, friend, paid attendant or volunteer - anyone 13 years of age or older - who travels with you to assist you in using transit. Applying Complete the Transit Support Person online application Call GRT customer service to have a form mailed to you Pick up a form in person at any GRT customer service location If you are currently enrolled in the Personal Assistant for Leisure Activities (PAL) program through the City of Kitchener , City of Waterloo or City of Cambridge , you do not have to have your health care professional fill out your application, you can provide your PAL card number on your application. If you are not enrolled in the PAL program, the application should be completed by a registered health-care professional with one of the following designations: Physician Nurse practitioner Nurse Psychologist/psychiatrist Physiotherapist Occupational therapist Optometrist/ophthalmologist Social worker with the Ontario College of Social Workers and Social Service Workers Orientation and mobility specialist Developmental services resource centre service coordinator Other professionals approved by the Region Any fees charged by a health-care professional will not be reimbursed by GRT. Submit the completed form to Grand River Transit, 250 Strasburg Rd, Kitchener ON N2E 3M6. GRT will send you a letter within 14 calendar days of receiving your application indicating whether you have been approved. Using the program on MobilityPLUS When you book a trip and plan to travel with a transit support person, inform the reservationist when you call or add additional passengers and select 'Approved Support Person' when you book your trip online. A MobilityPLUS operator's daily itinerary indicates when a customer is travelling with a support person. Using the program on GRT buses and ION trains You will need an EasyGO fare card . GRT will add an ‘electronic flag’ to the card to indicate you are approved for the Transit Support Person program. When you tap your fare card to pay your fare the operator will see that you are approved to travel with a support person. If you already have an EasyGO fare card, call Customer Service so we can add this flag to your existing card.
