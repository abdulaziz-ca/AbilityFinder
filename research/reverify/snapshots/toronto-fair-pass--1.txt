Upload Additional Documents About the Program The Fair Pass Transit Discount Program provides a full year of savings on TTC and Wheel‑Trans trips by offering a 36 per cent discount on adult single fares, applied directly to the rider’s PRESTO card. Eligible residents receive a 12‑month discount on their PRESTO card, saving $1.20 on every TTC adult single fare. The discounted single fare is $2.10 instead of the regular adult fare of $3.30. Additionally, with TTC Monthly Fare Capping, Fair Pass clients will pay no more than $98.70 per month for TTC travel after 47 paid trips in the same month. Fair Pass Fare Cost per ride $2.10* Savings per ride $1.20 Monthly Fare Cap (47 paid rides per month)** $98.70 Previous Monthly Pass (now discontinued)** $123.25 *Amounts are subject to change and based on TTC fares using PRESTO payment. **For more information, visit TTC Fare Capping and TTC Fares and Passes. In 2016, Toronto City Council voted to establish the Fair Pass Program as part of the Toronto Poverty Reduction Strategy , outlined in the staff report Fair Pass: Transit Fare Equity Program for Low-Income Torontonians . The Fair Pass Transit Discount Program is being implemented in phases and is subject to annual funding by Toronto City Council. Who Can Apply To apply you must: Be between 20 and 64 years of age* Live in the City of Toronto Have a PRESTO Card Number Have a total after tax family income (for all family members over the age of 18) below 75 per cent of the the Low-Income Measure After-Tax (LIM-AT) threshold Provide one of the following as proof of income: Income documents for you and any family members 18 years old or older, as outlined in the Required Documents tab OR Program identification number for recipients of: Ontario Works (OW) Ontario Disability Support Program (ODSP) Toronto Children’s Services Child Care Fee Subsidy *If you are between the ages of 13 and 19, or 65 and up, see TTC Fare Types for details about youth, student and seniors fares. Income Eligibility The program uses 75 per cent of the Statistics Canada’s Low-Income Measure After-Tax to determine income eligibility. Residents currently receiving assistance from Ontario Works (OW), Emergency Assistance or Ontario Disability Support Program (ODSP) meet the financial eligibility criteria All other residents, including Child Care Fee Subsidy recipients, meet the financial eligibility criteria based on their family size, if their net annual family income is below: Family Size* Total Annual Income for Family 1 person $ 22,691 2 persons $ 32,090 3 persons $ 39,302 4 persons $ 45,383 5 persons $ 50,739 6 persons $ 55,582 7 persons $ 60,035 8 persons $ 64,181 9 persons $ 68,074 10 persons $ 71,756 *Family size is defined by the Statistics Canada Census Family definition which includes up to two generations of immediate family members who are living together. **Total annual net income will only be calculated for family members aged 20 to 64. The income threshold is 75 per cent of the Statistics Canada’s Low-Income Measure After Tax (LIM-AT). LIM-AT changes annually based on the latest available data provided by Statistics Canada. The LIM-AT was updated in February 2026 to reflect LIM-AT 2023 data. Required Documents You do not need to provide any proof of income documents if you are receiving financial support from any of the following programs: Ontario Works (OW) Ontario Disability Support Program (ODSP) Toronto Children’s Services Child Care Fee Subsidy Proof of Income If you are not actively receiving services from the programs above, proof of income is required for all household family members. If your first name, last name and address is also displayed in your income document and matches your application; no additional documents are required to verify identity and address. Acceptable proof of income documents: Canada Revenue Agency (CRA): Current year Notice of Assessment or Proof of Income Statement (must include name, address, tax year and page with line 23600 income) Employment Insurance (EI): My Current/Recent Claim page from your My Service Canada Account Canada Pension Plan (CPP): Payment Information page from your My Service Canada Account Old Age Security (OAS), Guaranteed Income Supplement (GIS) and Ontario Guaranteed Annual Income System (GAINS) you must submit current year Notice of Assessment to prove your income Pay stub(s): for the last 30 days Letter of Support: If you do not have income or do not have proof of income documents, a signed letter from a representative of a social service provider or religious institution that lists all household family members and verifies address and family income (see Letter-of-Support-Guidelines-and-Template for further details). Proof of Address You must live in the City of Toronto to be eligible for the Fair Pass Discount. For individuals who have no fixed residential address or cannot safely disclose their address (see Access TO ), you may provide a letter of support with an alternative Toronto address such as where you access services (e.g. a local shelter, community space, hospital or government office). If the address displayed in your income document (or letter of support) matches the address you provide in your application, no additional documents are required. If your income document does not include your address, one proof of address document for the household is required. You do not need to provide proof of address documents for every family member. Acceptable proof of address documents (if required): Ontario Driver’s License Ontario Photo Card Current Bank Statement Current Utility Bill Lease/Rental Agreement Current Child Tax Benefit Statement Mortgage Statement Property Tax Bill Current Canadian Pension Plan Statement Current Old Age Security Statement *City staff will assess whether submitted documentation is sufficient for verification to determine eligibility. You may be notified by email requesting for additional document verification if it is deemed insufficient. How to Apply You are encouraged to apply online for the Fair Pass Transit Discount. The Human Services Integration Office also supports applications by phone for Social Assistance recipients, access to language interpretation and paper applications upon request by calling 416-338-8888. To Apply Online Provide your PRESTO card number Provide an email address Provide your Toronto address Enter information for yourself and each family member living with you, if applicable (spouse or partner, child(ren), or parent(s) and sibling(s)) Program identification number if you are an active recipient of Ontario Works (OW Member ID), Ontario Disability Support Program (ODSP Member ID) or Toronto Children’s Services Child Care Fee Subsidy (Household/ Client File Number) OR Proof of income documentation (see Required Documents ). Program specific details (names of all family members) Receive an email upon application submission with a confirmation number Receive an email notification about the outcome of your application If your first name, last name and address is displayed in your income document and matches your application; no additional documents are required to verify identity and address. If your application has incorrect or missing documents, you will receive an email with a unique upload token to re-submit documents within 30 days. Please note that any missing documentation will increase the processing time of your application. Apply for Fair Pass If you require assistance or have questions about the application, please call the Human Services Integration Office at 416-338-8888. To Apply by Phone If you apply by phone, you must: Provide your PRESTO Card Number Provide your Toronto address* Provide information for yourself and each family member living with you, if applicable (spouse or partner, child(ren), or parent(s) and sibling(s)) Provide income information for yourself and all immediate family members aged 20 to 64 Provide Required Documents for yourself and all immediate family members aged 20 to 64 within 30 days If you provide an email address, you will be emailed a Document Submission link where you must enter your unique token number and confirmation number prior to uploading your required documents If you do not provide an email address, you will be provided with a fax number during your phone application where you must fax your required documents. You must provide a phone number where you can be contacted regarding the outcome of your application Once the required documents are received, staff will review and assess your eligibility. If you provide an email address, you will receive an email notification about the outcome of your application. If you do not provide an email address, staff will contact you at the phone number you provide during your application to inform you of the outcome of your application. To Apply In-Person If you are unable to apply online or by phone, you can book an in-person appointment at one of the following City locations between 9 a.m. and 4 p.m. Please book an appointment as space is limited. In Person Appointment Booking Locations Regent Park Community Centre ( 402 Shuter St. ) Keele Community Hub ( 1652 Keele St. ) Scarborough Civic Centre ( 150 Borough Dr. ) How to Use Your Discount Activating Your Discount You will receive an email notification once the Fair Pass discount is applied to your PRESTO card number. Once you receive the email, you can start riding with the discount right away. Using Your Discount The PRESTO card is not transferable and is intended for the approved Fair Pass discount cardholder only. The discount will only work on Toronto Transit Commission (TTC) vehicles, including Wheel-Trans. The discount is not valid for any travel with a PRESTO card on other transit systems or the UP Express. The discount will save you 36 per cent on each adult PRESTO fare. As of September 1, 2026, the TTC will replace monthly passes with a monthly fare capping system. Once you reach the monthly fare cap of 47 paid trips, any additional TTC travel for the rest of that month will be free. For more information, visit TTC Fare Capping . Maintaining Your Discount Your Fair Pass discount is valid for 12 months and you may be required to submit a new application before your discount expiry date (see Annual Eligibility Review and Reapplication section). If your PRESTO card number or email address has changed, or you are being charged a full fare when using the TTC, please contact the Human Services Integration Office by calling 416-338-8888. All Fair Pass clients are encouraged to register their PRESTO card by creating a PRESTO account . With an account, you can confirm your discount and how long it is valid, load and check funds and report a lost card. Physical PRESTO Card Once you receive the email that your renewal was successful, your card will be ready to use. Digital PRESTO Card Number If you have a digital PRESTO card number (PRESTO in Mobile Wallet), the discount will automatically be applied and your card will be ready to use right away. Annual Eligibility Review and Reapplication Starting August 1, 2026, the Fair Pass Transit Discount Program will return to the eligibility review process adopted by City Council when the program was established. Reviewing eligibility helps ensure the Fair Pass discount continues to be provided to eligible residents and that clients receive important program updates. During the COVID-19 pandemic, Fair Pass discounts were renewed automatically as a temporary measure. Reviewing eligibility helps ensure the Fair Pass discount continues to reach eligible residents and that the City has up-to-date contact information to share important program updates. Do You Need to Reapply If you are actively receiving Ontario Works (OW), Ontario Disability Support Program (ODSP) or Toronto Children&#8217;s Services Child Care Fee Subsidy (CCFS) benefits, you may not need to reapply. The City will review the information already on file to confirm your eligibility. If we need more information, we will contact you and share the next steps. When a New Application is Required Some Fair Pass clients will be required to submit a new application to confirm their ongoing eligibility for the program. Clients who need to complete a new application will be notified 45 days before their discount expiry date and provided with information on next steps. To avoid interruptions in their discount, clients should submit any required application before their expiry date. If you are not actively receiving these benefits, you may be required to submit a new application to continue receiving your Fair Pass discount. How Will You Know if You Need to Take Action If you need to reapply or provide additional information, we will contact you 45 days before your Fair Pass discount expires. If you have an email address on file, you may receive an email with information about your eligibility status and any next steps. If you do not have an email address on file, we may contact you using other contact information available in your account. To help ensure you receive important updates, please keep your contact information up to date. How to Submit a New Application If you are asked to submit a new application to confirm your ongoing eligibility, you can complete the application using any of the following methods. Online Apply for Fair Pass By Phone Call 416-338-8888 In Person (Walk-in) Scarborough Civic Centre – Tuesdays, 9 a.m. to 4 p.m. Regent Park Community Centre – Wednesdays, 9 a.m. to 4 p.m. Keele Community Hub – Thursdays, 9 a.m. to 4 p.m. You can also book an appointment for in-person services. For more information, please see How to Apply. Your PRESTO Card The Fair Pass discount can be applied to only one PRESTO card number. Toronto residents must provide their PRESTO card number to complete a Fair Pass application. How to Get a PRESTO Card Number You can choose to get a physical PRESTO card or a digital PRESTO card (PRESTO in Mobile Wallet). Visit PRESTO for more information, including how to add your PRESTO Card to Google Wallet or add your PRESTO Card to Apple Wallet . Physical PRESTO Card You can buy a new physical PRESTO card for $4 and load funds through Customer Service Outlets . Digital PRESTO Cards (PRESTO in Mobile Wallet) Make sure that you have the Apple Wallet or Google Wallet app installed on your mobile device. If you already have a physical PRESTO card, you can use the PRESTO app to convert it into a Mobile Wallet Card. You can get a new PRESTO in Mobile Wallet card through the PRESTO App , your Apple Wallet or your Google Wallet. If you get a new PRESTO in Mobile Wallet card through the PRESTO app while signed into your PRESTO account, it will be added to your account right away. If you get a new PRESTO in Mobile Wallet through Apple Wallet or Google Wallet, it will be an anonymous card until you register it to your PRESTO account using the PRESTO app. For more information or support, please visit the PRESTO Help Centre . How to Activate a New Physical PRESTO Card Only physical PRESTO cards need to be activated. You have the following options to activate your physical PRESTO card: Activate your card through your PRESTO account Activate your card using the PRESTO Virtual Assistant , or Activate your card by calling PRESTO at 1-877-378-6123 and following the automated instructions. Once you activate it, your PRESTO card will be ready to use right away. Replacing a Physical PRESTO Card When replacing your physical PRESTO card, PRESTO can transfer your old card’s remaining balance and your Fair Pass discount to your new card if you have a My PRESTO Account. You must still notify the Human Services Integration Office of your new PRESTO card number before your discount expires. If you do not have a PRESTO Account, you will need to call the Human Services Integration Office at 416-338-8888, so the Fair Pass discount can be applied to your new card.
