What is available? Am I eligible? How do I apply? What is available? Up to $20,000 per loan year is available to eligible students for services and equipment like: Notetakers Tutors Interpreters Technical aids Am I eligible? You may be eligible if you: qualify for a federal student loan are enrolled in a full-time or part-time studies at a designated post-secondary institution have a permanent disability , or a persistent or prolonged disability as defined by the Canada Student Financial Assistance Program have exceptional education related costs for services or equipment due to your disability Please see the Canada.ca site for full eligibility requirements. How do I apply? Visit How to apply for disability funding to learn more.
