Find out how to get and use a handyPASS and Taxi Saver vouchers, along with helpful guidelines to make using them easy. If handyDART cannot meet your travel request, the Taxi Saver program gives you another way to get around in Greater Victoria. Many riders use a mix of fixed route, handyDART, and Taxi Saver depending on their trip. To use Taxi Saver, you must: Be a permanently registered handyDART customer Be 12 years of age or older Have a handyPASS Taxi Saver Taxi Saver vouchers help cover the cost of one time taxi trips when handyDART is not available. Available only to permanently registered handyDART riders Provides a 50 percent subsidy on taxi fares Requires a handyPASS to use Open and print this Taxi Saver guide (PDF) . Booking a Taxi Saver Trip Call one of the participating local taxi companies listed below to book your trip. Yellow Cab is no longer part of the Taxi Saver program. We appreciate your patience as service options adjust. Contact the Taxi Saver Office Call 250-995-5618 Email taxisaver@bctransit.com How to Purchase Taxi Saver Coupons You need a handyPASS to buy and use Taxi Saver coupons. Eligible riders can buy: An $80 package of coupons for $40 One package per month Coupons in $1, $2, and $5 values By Mail Send your order with: Your handyPASS number A return address A cheque for $40 payable to BC Transit Mail to: BC Transit Taxi Saver Program 520 Gorge Road East PO Box 9861 Victoria, BC V8W 9T5 How to Use Taxi Saver Coupons Book your taxi directly with a participating company. Confirm they accept Taxi Saver vouchers. When booking: Tell the dispatcher it is a Taxi Saver trip Let them know if you need a wheelchair accessible vehicle Use coupons to pay the metered fare. If the fare is $5.80, you pay $5.00 in coupons and $0.80 in cash. Taxi drivers do not give change for vouchers. Participating Taxi Companies BlueBird Cabs*: 250-382-4235 Sidney Taxi*: 250-656-6666 Victoria Taxi: 250-383-7111 WestShore Taxi: 250-478-7888 Westwind Taxi (Western Communities): 250-474-4747 BlueBird Cabs and Sidney Taxi offer wheelchair and scooter accessible vehicles. Let the dispatcher know if you need an accessible ride.
