Contact Us Online Services Sitemap Expand Search Recreation Access Program Section Menu In 2018, the Community Social Needs Assessment identified that more than 20% of residents were facing financial barriers to participation in recreation. This spurred a proposal for a program to remove financial barriers to recreation services for residents with modest income. In March 2019, the City of Lloydminster launched the Recreation Access Program. This program is income-based, accessible, and designed to remove some of the financial barriers associated with using City of Lloydminster Recreation and Cultural facilities . It allows eligible Lloydminster residents to access these facilities at a reduced cost. Benefits of the Recreation Access Program Once issued with a Recreation Access Program (RAP) card, individuals are entitled to the following benefits: $2.00 Adult (18+ years) admission Servus Sports Centre Bioclean Aquatic Centre/Co-op Community Outdoor Pool Lloydminster Museum + Archives $1.00 Child and Youth (0-17 years) admission Servus Sports Centre Bioclean Aquatic Centre/Co-op Community Outdoor Pool Lloydminster Museum + Archives 75% reduction of membership costs Servus Sports Centre Bioclean Aquatic Centre/Co-op Community Outdoor Pool 2 for 1 Ticket Vic Juba Community Theatre *Please visit Vic Juba Community Theatre to access shows that qualify for RAP Qualifications for the Recreation Access Program Applicants must be a resident of Lloydminster and meet ONE of the following qualifiers on an annual basis: 1. Your household income is at or below the indicated thresholds as listed below. Households include main applicant’s spouse/partner and eligible dependent children 17 years of age or younger. 2025- 2028 Income Threshold Number of household members 1 Person 2 Persons 3 Persons 4 Persons 5 Persons 6 Persons 7+ Persons Gross annual income (See notice of assessment line 15000) $32,111 $39,975 $49,145 $59,667 $67,675 $76,323 $84,978 The City of Lloydminster used Alberta’s Provincial Minimum Gross to calculate the base income threshold. For every additional dependent, $4,973 is added to the total. The additional amount is based on Alberta’s Adult Health Benefits Program. 2. You (and dependents, if applicable) are a recipient of provincial disability benefits: Assured Income for the Severely Handicapped (AISH) Saskatchewan Assured Income for Disability (SAID) 3. You are a Permanent Resident, Temporary Foreign Worker, Student Visa, or Status Refugee who has been in the country for less than 18-months. Eligibility as a newcomer is capped at ONE (1) application during the eighteen (18) month period. NOTE: The residency requirement does not apply to First Nations, Metis, and/or Inuit applicants. However, they must still meet the income eligibility. Supporting Documents Proof of Income Please submit a copy of ONE of these documents with your application. A copy of the document MUST accompany your application for each member of your household 18 years or older. Canada Revenue Agency Notice of Assessment A copy of your Notice of Assessment for the current or previous year. Previous years Notice of Assessment will be accepted until May 31 of the current year. Assured Income for the Severely Handicapped (AISH) A copy of your AISH Health Benefits Card (that has not expired), listing all dependents if applicable. Saskatchewan Assured Income for Disability (SAID) A copy of your statement of benefits and deductions from the Government of Saskatchewan from within the last 3 months. Permanent Residence Card (For those who have been in Canada less than 18 months) A copy of your permanent residence card from Citizenship and Immigration Canada. A copy of your Confirmation of Permanent Residence from Citizenship and Immigration Canada. Temporary Foreign Workers (For those who have been in Canada less than 18 months) A copy of your work permit visa from Citizenship and Immigration Canada. Student Visa (for those who have been in Canada less than 18-months) A copy of your student visa from Citizenship and Immigration Canada. Status Refugees (For those who have been in Canada less than 18 months) A copy of your Refugee Protection Claimant Document from Citizenship and Immigration Canada. Proof of Address Please provide a copy of ONE of the following documents with your application: A copy of a government issue ID card that has your name and address on it. A copy of a utility, telephone, or cable bill with your name and address dated within the last 30 days. A copy of a government document with your name and address dated within the last 30 days. A copy of a signed lease agreement that has your name and address on it. Residency Exemption for First Nations, Metis, and/or Inuit applicants A copy of your Certificate of Indian Status card from Indian and Northern Affairs Canada. A copy of your Certificate of Treaty Status card from the Government of Canada. Note: P.O. Boxes or Rural Route Addresses are not accepted. Approval Terms Standard approvals are set for a one-year term. AISH and SAID applications are approved for a three-year term. Applicants will need to notify the Social Programs & Services Department should there be any change with their status or disability benefits. Once RAP card expires, applicants are required to reapply by filling out the application form and submitting their supporting documents. Applicants can reapply one month before their RAP cards expire. Applicants who have an active ban at any City of Lloydminster operated recreation and cultural facilities are automatically disqualified from the program. Submit Your Application Fill out the Recreation Access Program application form . Paper copies of the form can also be picked up at City Hall or any Lloydminster Recreation and Cultural facility . Submit your signed application with supporting documents by email, mail, or drop off. You can mail or drop off applications to: City of Lloydminster Attn: Social Programs & Services 4420 50 Avenue Lloydminster, AB T9V 0W2 Email scanned or clearly photographed applications to fcss@lloydminster.ca Recreation Access Program Frequently Asked Questions What are some alternative verification methods? A letter from an Alberta or Saskatchewan Registered Social Worker. A letter from a local non-profit organization. Letter must be on letterhead and dated within the previous 30 days. The letter should outline that the RSW is aware of the income threshold eligibility, and that the household/individual meets the eligibility requirements. What happens to the financial information I provide? All supporting documents are reviewed and then shredded and deleted after approval of RAP cards. Please only submit copies as you will not get them back. If approved, how long until I get my card? Applications are typically processed every Friday of the week. Approval letters and RAP cards are then mailed to the recipient at the address provided in the application. If I forget/lose my RAP card, can they look me up on the computer at the recreation facilities to get the discount? No, records of RAP cardholders are maintained by the Social Program and Services Department and not released to recreation facilities. If you lost your card, you will not be able to receive the discounted rate. It is the applicant’s responsibility to request for a replacement card. There are no fees associated with requesting for a replacement RAP card. Please contact the Social Programs & Services at 780-875-6184 ext. 2908 or email fcss@lloydminster.ca . Does the Recreation Access Program include discounts for swimming lessons, sports clinics, or arts and craft classes? No, the Recreation Access Program is only applicable for drop-in or memberships at select city-operated facilities but there are non-profit organizations that provides financial assistance to eligible families to access recreation and cultural activities. Arts and Culture Sports Creative Kids Saskatchewan Give them a call: 1-855-277-9469 Send them an email . Check out their website . Provides financial assistance for children and youth, ages 4-19 years, to participate in art, dance, music, theater, and cultural activities. Alberta and Saskatchewan residents of Lloydminster are eligible to apply. JumpStart Give them a call: 1-844-937-7529 Send them an email . Check out their website . Financial assistance for children, ages 4-18, in registered sporting programs that are minimum 5 days in length. Kid Sport Lloydminster Give them a call: 1-800-319-4263 Send them an email . Check out their website . Financial and equipment assistance for children, ages 5 to 18 years, in registered sporting programs. If I am approved, how long will it take to get my card? Standard mail can take up to 7 to 10 business days. Applicants have the option to pick up their approval letters and RAP cards at City Hall on Fridays, if their application package is complete and all supporting documents were submitted. For more information, please call 780-875-6184 Ext. 2908 or email fcss@lloydminster.ca .
