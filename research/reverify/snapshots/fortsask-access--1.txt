Eligible individuals and families can apply for support to enjoy activities, stay active, and connect with the community. If your application is approved and you choose to participate, you will receive: An Annual Multi-Facility Membership Now offered at a 100% discount Valid January 1 – December 31 Provides full access to both Harbour Pool and the Dow Centennial Centre, including drop-in programs (Childminding not included) Registered Programs for Children & Youth Eligible for up to 4 registered programs or a maximum value of $245. Eligibility Requirements There are two requirements to be eligible for this program: The applicant must be a resident of the City of Fort Saskatchewan. Proof of residency is required. The applicant must be receiving funding from: an approved Government program (such as: AISH, CPPD, FSCD or Income Support); OR have a combined household income at or under the amounts listed on the Statistics Canada Low Income Thresholds. Proof of income verification is required.
