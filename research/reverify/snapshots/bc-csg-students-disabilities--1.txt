What is available? Am I eligible? How do I apply? What is available? $2,800* $2,000 per program year (August 1 to July 31). *Note: the above Canada Student Grant amount is temporarily increased by 40% from 2019/20 pre-pandemic levels until July 31, 2027. Am I eligible? You may be eligible if you: qualify for a federal student loan are enrolled in full-time or part-time studies at a designated post-secondary institution have a permanent disability , or a persistent or prolonged disability as defined by the Canada Student Financial Assistance Program Please see the Canada.ca site for full eligibility requirements. How do I apply? Visit How to apply for disability funding to learn more.
