Leisure Access Program brochure and application form Leisure Access Program brochure PDF file (170 KB) Leisure Access Program self-refer application form PDF file (252 KB) The Leisure Access Program (LAP) supports low-income residents of Vancouver by providing free and reduced-fee access to programs and services at Park Board facilities, including community centres. Once your application is approved, a Leisure Access membership will be added to your OneCard and linked to your ActiveNet account. This membership allows you to register for eligible programs and access services at reduced rates. Your LAP pass is for your use only The Leisure Access Pass is for your personal use only and cannot be shared or transferred. Staff will take your photo and add it to your OneCard account to use the pass. Children and youth aged 16 and under require a signed OneCard photo consent form PDF file (81 KB) from a parent or guardian.
