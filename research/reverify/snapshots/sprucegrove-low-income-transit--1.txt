Transit Transit Fares Transit Fares Fares Local service is within and between Spruce Grove, Stony Plain, and Parkland County. Commuter service is to/from Edmonton. This does not include transfers to ETS. If you transfer to/from local on-demand transit to/from commuter transit, you only pay the commuter fare. Fare is valid for 90 minutes. You can pay with an Arc card, debit/credit, or cash. Each individual rider (over the age of 12) needs their own form of payment. Local Fare Commuter Fare Single fare $3 $6.25 Arc - monthly fare cap $75 $135 Arc - youth (18 and under) monthly fare cap $45 $100 Arc - subsidy monthly fare cap (Low Income Transit Pass) $25 $50 U-Pass holders Free Free CNIB ID holders Free Free Children aged 12 years and under (when accompanied by a paying passenger) Free Free Attendants of individuals requiring support (when accompanied by individual needing support) Free Free Regional fares Regional products are available for any transit agency within the Edmonton region. Regional fare Arc - regional 90 minute ticket $8.00 Arc - regional 24 hour ticket $16.00 Arc Arc is the Edmonton region’s electronic fare payment system. Transit users in Edmonton, St. Albert, Strathcona County, Fort Saskatchewan, Leduc, Spruce Grove (including Stony Plain and parts of Parkland County), and Beaumont can use the same transit payment to pay for each system's fare. Get the best fare rate by using the same card, every time you tap. Whether it’s your Arc card, digital or physical credit or debit card—to ensure you get the best rate. To learn more about Arc, or if you have questions or concerns, visit myarc.ca or call the Arc call centre at 1-888-302-0001. You can also submit your questions through Arc's online form . How to use fare products Using Arc cards & tickets Using an Arc Card Tap your Arc card on the validator when entering and exiting buses and LRT platforms to ensure you are charged the correct fare. Each individual rider (over the age of 12) needs their own form of payment. Add money into your account - you can load as little as $4 Register your Arc card online to protect your funds if your card is lost or stolen Using credit & debit cards (Arc Tap to Pay) Using Arc Tap to Pay Tap your physical or digital credit or debit card on the validator when entering and exiting buses and LRT platforms to ensure you are charged the correct fare. Each individual rider (over the age of 12) needs their own form of payment. Riders who use the same payment method each time will continue to benefit from daily and monthly standard adult fare caps. You are not required to set up an account for your digital credit or debit card, unless you use a fare concession, which will require you to update your payment option in your account at myArc.ca . Using cash Exact change is required when paying with cash on all buses . Operators do not carry change Fare caps Riders have monthly fare caps. The caps mean riders won't pay more than a maximum each month based on their account profile type and the service type which they travel. Each region sets their own fare caps. You must use the exact same form of payment for those fares to contribute to your fare cap. Once you reach your monthly fare cap, you ride free for the rest of the month in the region. For Spruce Grove Transit: Once you pay $75 in local or commuter fares, you ride locally for free for the rest of the month. Once you pay $135 in local or commuter fares, you ride local, commuter, and regional transit for free for the rest of the month. Where to buy Arc products You can purchase an Arc Card online or at retail locations , located in the gift card section. If you'd like to purchase a card locally in Spruce Grove or Stony Plain, visit the following locations: Border Paving Athletic Centre - 9 Tri Leisure Way Heavy Metal Place - 50 Westwind Drive Stony Plain Town Hall - 4905 51 Ave. Low Income Transit Pass The Low Income Transit Pass Program (LITP), funded through a Government of Alberta Grant, helps to support residents to access public transit and connect with their community. All Spruce Grove, Stony Plain, and Parkland County residents are eligible. Subsidized rates are offered for both Local and Commuter monthly passes. Newcomers to Canada (who arrived in Canada within the last three months) are eligible for three months of fully subsidized passes (either Local or Commuter passes), after that, individuals will be transitioned to the subsidized rate, as applicable. Application information To be eligible, applicants’ household income (from Line 15000 on your CRA Notice of Assessment) must not exceed the Low-Income Cut-Off level determined by Statistics Canada +25%. Applications to access the program can be completed year-round. Proof of address and household income are required. Spruce Grove Residents Applications can be completed at: 414 King Street, Spruce Grove (Community Social Development) Call 780-962-7618 or email CSD@sprucegrove.org Stony Plain Residents Applications can be completed at: 107, 4613-52 Avenue, Stony Plain (Community and Social Development) Call 780-963-8583 or email connect@stonyplain.com U-Pass The Universal Transit Pass, commonly referred to as a U-Pass, is provided, for a fee, to students attending the University of Alberta, MacEwan University, NAIT and NorQuest College. How to use U-Pass The U-Pass is accepted by Spruce Grove Transit and is fully transferable to all other transit systems in the Capital Region, including Edmonton, Beaumont, Leduc, Strathcona County, Fort Saskatchewan and St. Albert. Arc U-Pass cards are issued by participating post-secondary institutions. NAIT students can use their One Card to tap on/off buses and the LRT. Contact your post-secondary institution to get your U-Pass. Attendants of individuals requiring support Mandatory attendants of individuals who require support are allowed to ride for free on Spruce Grove Transit when accompanying a person who needs assistance. Qualifications Those requiring support must complete the Needs Assessment for Applicants Requiring a Mandatory Attendant. The applicant will receive a registration card if it is determined that a mandatory attendant is required. The person requiring support will be required to pay the transit fare and they may be asked to show their registration card, which will allow their attendant to ride for free. This applies to Spruce Grove’s on-demand local service and commuter service . For more information please call 780-962-7634 ext. 193. Questions?
