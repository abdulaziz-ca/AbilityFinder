Cochrane Connect Card The Cochrane Connect Card helps you save money for the things that matter most. The card provides low-income households access to services and programs with a discount. Program application & eligibility Eligibility for the Cochrane Connect Card program is based on financial and situational assessment. See the following table for household income eligibility: Household composition Annual income 1 person $54,500 2 people $65,000 3 people $77,000 4 people $85,000 5 people $96,500 (maximum) To apply for the program, you must book an appointment with a resource worker and provide the following documentation for all adults in the household. Financial information Canada Revenue Agency Notice of Assessment (with line 15000), or AB Works, EI or AISH confirmation (income support) Proof of address Mortgage or lease agreement, or Current bill (showing name and address, dated within three months) Apply now Families with children under the age of 18, book an appointment with Family Resource Network (see contact information below) Individuals or families with children 18+, older adults or rural residents, book an appointment with Family and community Support Services (see contact information below). Cochrane Connect Card program discounts: GST is NOT included, prices subject to change, can not be used in combination with other subsidies or discounts. FAQs Open all Do I need to reapply every year? Yes. You must reapply to confirm you are still eligible for the program. How do I reapply? To reapply, you must provide a Notice of Assessment from your previous year’s tax return. If you need assistance with tax preparation, you can inquire about our Volunteer Tax Program. Residents who have low income and simple tax situations are eligible.
