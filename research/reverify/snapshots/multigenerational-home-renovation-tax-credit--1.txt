If you are eligible, you can claim this credit for certain renovation expenses to create a self-contained secondary unit. The secondary unit must allow a senior or an adult who is eligible for the disability tax credit to live with a qualifying relative. You can claim up to $50,000 in qualifying expenditures for each qualifying renovation that is completed. The tax credit is 14.5% of your costs, up to a maximum of $7,250, for each claim you are eligible to make.
