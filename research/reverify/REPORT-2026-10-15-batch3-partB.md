# Snapshot re-verification report — 2026-10-15, batch 3, part B

## Method and scope

Same method as `REPORT-2026-10-15-batch2.md`. `public/data.js` was evaluated in a Node `vm` with `{window:{}, document:{}, console}` and `BENEFITS` exported (as in `scripts/check-readability.js`); it was not grep-parsed. The `unmet`/`short` text of every key in each record's `requires` was read from the `REQS` object in `public/app.js`. Only the snapshots in `research/reverify/snapshots/` (fetched 2026-10-14, re-fetched 2026-10-15; headers carry the 10-14 timestamps) were treated as truth. Records covered: aadl, airdrie-fair-access, bc-icbc-disability-discount, bc-pharmacare-plan-c, bc-pwd-designation, bc-supported-child-development, bc-workbc-employment-services. Filenames below are relative to `research/reverify/snapshots/`.

Facts are distinct assertions, not appearances; repeats across amount, summary, note, eligibility, requiresNote, steps, documents, tips and gates are consolidated. **CONFIRMED** gives the supporting snapshot sentence. **DIFFERS** gives the sentence and exactly what differs (number / boundary / hedge / dropped condition / scope). **NOT ON PAGE** means absent from the supplied snapshots, not wrong; linked pages and PDFs are not treated as read. Hedges (may / up to / usually / expected) are checked in both directions. Generic advice with no factual claim is not counted. No wording is proposed.
