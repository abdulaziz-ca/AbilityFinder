 Community Inclusion and Day Programming
 The Disability Support Program (DSP) supports adults and youth
 with disabilities to participate in meaningful activities, and in
 being a part of their community. Day programs funding by DSP are
 offered by non-profit organizations in all regions of Nova Scotia
 in a variety of settings. A list of available providers is
 included below.
 Day programs support people in many aspects of their lives. They
 value the choices and self-determination of participants. They
 offer a range of programming, activities, and supports and are
 flexible to meet a wide range of needs. Some activities and
 supports offered include skill development and training, job
 preparation and supported employment, social outings and
 recreational activities, as well as personal care. Some activities
 are based in the program location while others are in various
 community places. Some of these programs operate small businesses
 where people with disabilities can learn on the job skills and
 have employment opportunities.
 Program Options
 Skill development & training:
 - Learning or enhancing skills to increase independence
 - Community awareness and safety
 - Computer training
 - Job-specific skills
 - Classroom, community, or job settings
 Supported employment:
 - Real work, fair pay
 - Job exploration and job coaching
 - Resume and interview preparation
 - Employer relationships
 Social enterprise businesses:
 - Work experience and training
 - Work opportunities
 Social & recreational activities:
 - Group activities/outings
 - Individual activities
 - Community participation
 Retirement (55+ years):
 - Health and wellness (workshops)
 - Recreation, community activities
 - Social opportunities
 - Volunteering support
 Please note all Day Programs do not provide all of these
 components. Each program is unique and has its own focus and
 activity offerings. See the list below for a program in your
 community.
 Programs are self-referral. You do not need to be referred by
 anyone. Some programs have waitlists. You can contact the program
 directly to find out more or to arrange an orientation.
