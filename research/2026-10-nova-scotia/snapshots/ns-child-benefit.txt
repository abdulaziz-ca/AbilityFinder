 Other Benefits
 These are some of the benefits outside of Employment Support and
 Income Assistance (ESIA) that may be able to help you.
 Canada Child Benefit (CCB)
 This benefit is a tax-free monthly payment from the federal
 government made to eligible families to help with the cost of
 raising children under 18 years of age.
 Heating Assistance Rebate Program (HARP)
 This program helps with the cost of home heating.
 Nova Scotia Affordable Living Tax Credit (NSALTC)
 This credit is a tax-free amount for low-income and modest-income
 individuals and families.
 Nova Scotia Child Benefit (NSCB)
 This benefit is a tax-free payment from the provincial government
 made to eligible families to help with the cost of raising children
 under 18 years of age.
 Poverty Reduction Credit (PRC)
 This credit is a tax-free amount paid to Nova Scotian individuals
 and couples, without children, living in poverty.
 More Support
 211 Nova Scotia may also be able to help you. It is a free,
 confidential service that can connect you to thousands of programs
 and services offered by local community groups, non-profits and
 government departments across Nova Scotia.
