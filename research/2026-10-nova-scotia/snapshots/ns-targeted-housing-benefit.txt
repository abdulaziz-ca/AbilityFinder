 Canada-Nova Scotia Targeted Housing Benefit
Homeowners
 Apply for a homeowner’s supplement: Canada-Nova Scotia Targeted Housing Benefit
Homeowners can apply for help to stay in their home if they pay more than 50% of their pre-tax (gross) household income on housing costs.
Renters
 Apply for a rent supplement: Canada-Nova Scotia Targeted Housing Benefit
Renters can apply for help with the cost of their rent.
