 Low-Income Fare Transit Program (LIFT)
 Section Menu 
 Starting August 22, all regular Transit routes will be replaced with new ones. School routes will continue to be available. Learn more . 
 About LIFT
 The Low Income Fare Transit program aims to reduce financial barriers to transit for eligible individuals and increase access to essential services.   This program provides a monthly pass for conventional transit for a nominal fee of $10, and a 75% discount on 10 and 25-ride passes for Specialized Transit 
 Eligible LIFT applicants must apply by completing the online submission form or one of the alternative application options listed below. If you need assistance with your application, please contact Pulse at 780-743-7000. 
 Online Submission Form
 Eligibility
 The applicant must: 
- Be a resident of Wood Buffalo
- Be 18 years old or older *
- International students and their dependent families are not eligible for the program based on the Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) financial requirements to obtain a study permit.
- Sponsored individuals and families are ineligible based on the formal sponsorship agreement with Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) in their first year of resettlement.
- Individuals residing in the region under the Temporary Foreign Worker Program are ineligible.
- Have a total household gross income equal to, or less than, the income ranges below:

 Application
- One completed Application Form
- Submit ONE of the following for EACH household member 18 years and older:
- Assured Income for Severely Handicapped (AISH) Form
- Alberta Health Benefit or Statement of Income Support
- Resettlement Assistance Program or Refugee Protection Claimant Document (RPCD)
- Canada Revenue Agency Notice of Assessment
- Letter from a Registered Social Worker (RSW)/Case Worker issued within the last 30 days and listing all household members. Only when no other documents are available. The letter should state that the RSW is aware of the Low-Income Eligibility and that the household meets the eligibility requirements.
- Individuals on the By-Name list or accessing housing support from Community Plan on Homelessness (CPH) serving agencies.
 How to Apply
 Submit your application and attach all supporting documents:
- Online:  Apply Now 
- Contact:  Pulse 
- By email: community.services@rmwb.ca 
- By mail: 
- Regional Municipality of Wood Buffalo / ATTN: LIFT
9909 Franklin Ave
Fort McMurray, AB T9H 2K4
- In person: 
- Drop off application form at Jubilee Building, 9909 Franklin Ave
- Your nearest Rural Municipal Office 
 Print Application 
 Approval
- You will be notified within 7-10 business days.
- Applicants must submit a complete application.
 Purchasing your discounted bus passes
- After your application is approved, purchase your $10 conventional transit pass every month from your local municipal office, at either:
- 1st Floor of Jubilee Building, 9909 Franklin Ave (if you live in Fort McMurray or Fort McKay)
- Your nearest Rural Municipal Office (if you live in a southern rural community)
- Bring your ID and approval letter when you pick up your monthly transit pass.
- Eligible individuals who require SMART Bus services can purchase their discounted 10 and 25-ride passes from SMART Bus operators.
 Additional Information
- You only need to submit one application per household.
- All household members (applicant, partner/spouse and dependent children living in the same residence) are eligible for the program's benefits.
- Income thresholds are subject to change based on Statistics Canada Low-Income Cut offs.
- To calculate your total household income eligible for the program:
- Take the number from Line 15000 on your CRA Notice of Assessment
- Add the number from Line 15000 on the CRA Notice of Assessments of all your household members.
- Lost, stolen or damaged passes will not be replaced.
 Local Community Partners - Offers and Discounts
- The LIFT Program connects qualified applicants with discounts and offers to programs and services in the community so that they can participate more fully in their community and have an increased sense of belonging and well-being. Only one application is required to assess Eligibility for the discounted programs.
- Keyano Theatre has partnered with the LIFT program to offer 80% discounted rates on select shows for eligible participants.
- 
 The Wood Buffalo Recreation Support Program is offering a 60% discount on Family and Individual memberships at the Syncrude Sport and Wellness Centre, all Regional Recreation Corporation of Wood Buffalo facilities, and Vista Ridge.
 For more information about the LIFT program, please contact Pulse.
