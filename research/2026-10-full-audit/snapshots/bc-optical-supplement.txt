 Optical coverage while on income or disability assistance
 Last updated on August 27, 2026 Everyone who receives income or disability assistance gets specified optical services. Tell the eye clinic you are in receipt of assistance and give them your BC Services Card. They’ll verify your coverage with Pacific Blue Cross.
 Adults
 When you receive assistance or medical services only , you can get:
- Up to $48.90 for routine eye exams once every two years
- New eyeglasses, up to the ministry’s maximum rate
- New lenses if your eye doctor confirms a change in your prescription
- Necessary repairs to lenses or frames
- A case for new glasses or lenses
- Routine eye exams for seniors, covered by Medical Services Plan (MSP), which covers medically necessary eye exams for everyone, regardless of age
 Children
 Children under 19 in families who receive assistance get:
- Routine eye exams for children, covered by Medical Services Plan (MSP)
- New eyeglasses, up to the ministry’s maximum rate
 If you have questions about your optical coverage:
- Call 1-866-866-0800 
- Visit your local office 
 Optical fee schedules
 Money for optical services is paid by the ministry directly to the optometrist or optician.
- Optometrist and ophthalmologist fee schedule (PDF, 417KB) 
- Optician fee schedule (PDF, 432KB) 
 Fee schedules determine the maximum amount the ministry will pay for services. You may be responsible for any additional costs.
