 Apply for a Subsidy
 Free or lower-cost transit and recreation options are available for eligible residents. Check the criteria and apply .  Transit 
- Free local fares for travel within St. Albert. 
- Free local tickets for approved Handibus riders for travel within St. Albert. 
- 65% off monthly adult commuter fares for travel between St. Albert and Edmonton.  Recreation 
- Free six-month membership or 60% off an annual membership. 
- AISH and ADAP recipients are eligible for a free annual membership.  These memberships are available for all City-run recreation facilities including Servis Credit Union Place, Fountain Park Recreation Centre and Grosvner Outdoor Pool.  Children's Programs 
- Up to $150 per child for City-run children's programs .    Who Can Apply
- You must be a resident of St. Albert. 
- You must not be recieving funding from another source for the same option. 
- Your total annual household income (before taxes) must be within the low-income limits set by Statistics Canada.  Number of People in Household Household Income

Other Subsidies
 Canadian Tire Jumpstart The City of St. Albert is a community partner with Canadian Tire Jumpstart Charities. If you require funding for a program involving sport or physical activity and your child is between the ages of 4 – 18, you may be eligible for funding from the Canadian Tire Jumpstart Program. Canadian Tire Jumpstart Prescription to Get Active Servus Credit Union Place is a proud partner of the Prescription to Get Active Program.
