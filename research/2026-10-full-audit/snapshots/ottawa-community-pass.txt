About the Access pass
The Access pass offers full fare on OC Transpo conventional bus and train services. On Para Transpo trips, the pass will let you save one-third of the single-ride fare. The Access pass is the same price as the Community pass. It can offer savings for Para Transpo customers who also ride OC Transpo bus and train service.
If you are a Para Transpo customer who never uses OC Transpo, you should not buy the Access pass. The pass will not offer you any savings.
Access pass eligibility
In order take advantage of Access discount, you need to:
 Be a registered Para Transpo customer
 Live in Ottawa
Helpful tips for customers
The Access pass may be a good option if you:
 Are a Para Transpo customer who uses a combination of OC Transpo and Para Transpo services
 Only use OC Transpo bus and train services in a given month
Whether the Access pass offers savings depends on your travel choices each month.

About the Community pass
The Community pass is available to Ontario Disability Support Program (ODSP) recipients and offers a reduced fare on single-ride and monthly passes. With the Community pass, the fare per-ride is about half the cost of a regular fare.
The Community pass is valid as full fare on all OC Transpo bus routes, the O-Train, and Para Transpo trips. This excludes rural trips. In the rural zone, Community pass holders must pay a flat-rate of $11.00.
Community pass eligibility
You are eligible for the Community pass if you:
 Receive benefits under the Ontario Disability Support Program (ODSP) 
 Live in Ottawa
Renewal no longer required
You no longer need to renew your pass every two years. Once issued, your Community pass will remain valid for as long as you qualify for ODSP or until you turn 65.
Please make sure to advise OC Transpo at 613‑560‑5000 if your Presto card number changes. Presto cards must be registered in the applicant’s name for the Community pass discount to be renewed.

Payment options
Para Transpo fares can be paid with:
Presto Cash O-Payment AboutParaPay
DayPasses and multi-day passes
Para Transpo customers or their companions can buy a DayPass or multi-day pass to use as regular fare on Para Transpo trips.
DayPass exception
 Day Passes apply to regular fares only. They are NOT valid for Para Transpo rural zone fares.
 You can buy a DayPass on a regular (non-Para) bus or at a ticket machine. You can only buy a multi-day pass at a ticket machine.
For more information about DayPasses and multi-day passes, visit our Where and how to pay page.
Presto
Customers can pay for trips on Para Transpo using a Presto card. Load your Presto card with a monthly pass or load funds to pay per ride.
Find out more about where to buy a Presto card and how to load it.
O-Payment: credit card, debit card or mobile wallet
You can pay with a credit card, debit card or mobile wallet if you are paying an Adult fare only. Register your credit card at o-payment.octranspo.com to view your transaction history.
You can't use a credit card, debit card or mobile wallet to pay the fare of another person.
Fare capping
Both Presto and O-Payment offer automatic fare capping. This means that you will not pay more than the maximum fare amount for a time period, no matter how many trips you take.
Fare capping with Presto
Presto offers both daily and monthly fare capping. This means that when you pay for your trip, you will never pay more than the price of a DayPass or price of a monthly pass.
For rural trips, only the base fare amount counts towards the monthly cap. The base fare is the cost of a single urban trip. When you reach the monthly fare cap, you will still have to pay the difference between the base fare and the rural fare.
For example: If you have a Senior discount, $3.28 from your rural fare will count towards your monthly cap, but $7.72 will not. ($3.28 + $7.72 = $11.00)
Fare capping with O-Payment
O-Payment offers both daily and monthly fare capping. This means that when you pay for your trip with a credit card, debit card or mobile wallet, you will never pay more than the price of a DayPass or Adult monthly pass. You can't pay for rural trips with O-Payment. These trips won't count towards your daily or monthly cap.
When you pay for a trip by debit card, credit card, or mobile wallet, your daily charges will automatically be capped at the price of a DayPass ($12.25) if you take multiple urban trips in one day. Daily fare capping for urban trips does not apply to fares paid with Presto.
Rural fares paid with Presto will not be capped, but will be discounted after the daily cap total of a DayPass has been reached.
For customers who take a mix of urban and rural or all rural trips, $4.10 from every trip fare will count towards the daily cap of $12.25. 
Once the $4.10 portion of a customer’s fares have totalled $12.25, rural fares will only cost $6.30.

Tap Tip!
Make sure to pay with the same credit card or mobile wallet each time you pay a fare to benefit from daily fare capping.
Monthly fare capping
Fares paid with Presto or O-Payment will be capped at the rate of a monthly pass. Discounted fares paid with Presto will be capped at their respective monthly pass rate.

For seniors on Para Transpo
NEW! Seniors age 65 and up who use Para Transpo are eligible for four free trips a month when they use a registered Presto card to pay per trip.
Your four free monthly trips will be refunded as a Presto credit in the following month.
The credit cannot be applied to monthly passes. Some seniors who currently buy a monthly pass may choose to start paying per trip instead, to receive the credit.
There is no action required by customers to receive this credit as long as you already have a Senior discount on your registered Presto card. Be sure to use the same Presto card for all of your Para Transpo trips.

What exactly is a Presto card? Does it only work on OC Transpo?
It's a $4.00 reloadable card that never expires, and it covers more than just OC Transpo! You can use it on OC Transpo buses, the O-Train, Para Transpo, and even STO routes and other transit systems across Ontario.
Everything — (single fares, monthly passes, transfers) is stored right on the same card, which makes travelling super convenient.
How Presto ensures you always pay the lowest fare?
When you tap your Presto card, the system automatically tracks your trips and calculates the lowest fare based on how often you ride.
 Daily fare capping: Tap your Presto card all day on the O-Train, buses, and Para Transpo, and you'll never be charged more than $12.25 — the price of a DayPass.
 Monthly fare capping: If you ride often, Presto will stop charging you once you reach the cost of a monthly pass. This means you never pay more than necessary.
 Transfers included
 Presto remembers if you set a discount and only charges you the correct fare.

What happens if I need to transfer between buses or trains?
Transfers are totally free! Depending on the time of day, you have 90 or 105 minutes to switch between buses, trains or Para Transpo without paying again.
Just tap like usual when you board the next ride.
More about transfer times
Do I have to line up at a machine to reload it?
Not at all — you can do everything online. Just go to prestocard.ca or use the official Presto app. You can reload your card, buy passes, set up Autoload and Autorenew, track your trips, and even report a lost or stolen card.
It's quick and secure.

Load funds
If you load your card online or by phone, you need to load at least $0.05 at a time. Your account can't hold more than $1,000. Card readers on the bus are updated at least three times a day. Be sure to leave enough time for your online purchase to get activated on the buses. Once loaded, you need to tap onto a card reader within 30 days to complete the transaction.
Autoload your card
Make sure your card never runs out of money by setting up Autoload:
 Register your card.
 Set-up an Autoload contract at prestocard.ca:
 Choose the balance at which you want to reload
 Choose the amount that will be loaded at each reload
 Activate your Autoload contract by tapping onto a reader within 30 days.
Load a pass onto your Presto card
You can load a monthly pass onto your card up to 14 days before the start of the month. Before you can ride with your pass, you need to activate it by tapping your card onto a Presto reader on or after the first day of the month. If you have not done so, your monthly pass is not valid and does not count as paid fare.
Autorenew your pass
Make sure you always have a pass loaded to your account by setting up Autorenew. Your payments will go through six days before the start of the month. Simply cancel Autorenew before the 20th day of the month to stop the following month’s pass from loading. At the start of the month, you need to tap your card as part of a regular trip to activate the load onto your PRESTO card. Until you have done so, your monthly pass is not valid and does not count as paid fare.
Use your Presto card
Tap and go on OC Transpo buses, the O-Train or Para Transpo. It's that simple.
OC Transpo buses
How to pay:
 Board through the front door.
 Tap your Presto card on the reader.
 Wait for the green light and beep, then take your seat.
O-Train
How to pay:
 Tap your card at station fare gates.
 Enter when gates open.
 Board any train.
Para Transpo
Para Transpo minibuses:
 Tap on the red O-Payment readers. You'll find the reader in the customer area.
 The reader's screen will turn green to show that the payment is successful.
On vans and taxis:
 Hand your card to the driver. They'll tap your card, then return it to you.
 The reader will make a sound if your payment is successful.
Using your card on OC Transpo buses and the O-Train
Once your card is loaded and ready to go, you can use it to pay for your travels. Whether it’s pay per ride or monthly pass, always tap your card when boarding. The readers are located near the doors of buses and on the fare gates at O-Train stations.
When you tap, Presto will instantly calculate the fare and deduct it from your account as needed.
 If the screen goes green, your fare was accepted
 A screen with a yellow exclamation point icon means the reader is rebooting or not ready to accept payment. Please wait before tapping.
 If a red light goes on and you hear two beeps, the transaction did not go through. If this happens, pay the fare with cash and contact Presto
If you ride without tapping (and your card isn’t loaded with a valid pass or transfer), you may be fined.
Tapping every time also helps us better plan our service levels. We review the number of taps on our different routes and factor them in when we develop schedules.
Using Presto on Para Transpo
Load your card with a monthly pass or load funds to pay per trip. On minibuses, tap your card on the red readers. On taxis and vans, hand your Presto card to the operator after boarding. The operator will tap your card on the Presto smartcard reader located at the front of the vehicle.
If you are using a Presto card to pay for a rural trip, make sure to load funds to your card. A monthly pass cannot be used to pay for a rural fare.

Sharing your card
Lend your monthly pass
You can share your monthly Presto pass with someone else, who can use it when you aren't. However, if your card is set for a discount (Senior or Pre-teen) you can only lend it to someone else who qualifies. Presto passes loaded with a Community Pass, Access Pass, or EquiPass can't be shared.
Use your card to pay for a second fare
If you pay per ride aboard a bus or Para Transpo vehicle, you can use your Presto funds to pay the fares of others you are riding with. Here’s what to do:
 Board the bus.
 Let your driver know that you would like to pay an additional fare.
 On Para Transpo, your companion will not receive a transfer.
Current system limitations only allow you to pay one fare at a time at the O-Train station using a Presto card. You can purchase a second person’s fare from the vending machine using cash or a credit card.
Transferring
The transfer period begins from the time your Presto card is tapped on a fare reader. Depending on the time of day, this period is 90 or 105 minutes long.
When you pay with a Presto card, you can transfer between buses, the O-Train and Para Transpo vehicles within this transfer period for no additional charge.
Because Para Transpo trips must be booked 90 minutes apart, most normal trips don't qualify for a transfer.
Using Presto on STO routes
You can use your Presto pass and valid transfer to ride on all STO routes, without a fare top-up. Simply tap your card. You can't use your Presto funds to start your trip on an STO route.
Using Presto with other transit agencies
Presto cards can be used at transit agencies across Ontario. Visit Presto’s Transit Agency Fares page for a list of participating agencies and their fares.
Manage your Presto card online
Managing your Presto account online is simple, secure, and convenient. You can do most tasks at prestocard.ca. Please note: setting up a discount (Senior, Pre-Teen, Community, or Access Pass) must be done in person at approved locations.
Things you can do on the Presto website:
 Order and register a new card
 Load your card or set up Auto-reload
 Check your balance and travel history
 View and download your usage report for taxes
Extra options for registered cards:
 Update Pre-teen/Senior status and renew discount documents
 Transfer your balance to a new card if it is lost or stolen
 Block (hotlist) a lost or stolen card
 Note: Blocking a lost card takes up to 24 hours. Any fares used during that time can't be refunded.
You can also download the official PRESTO app (Android, Apple) for a simple, easy way to manage and instantly load your card on the go.

blockPresto on mobile wallet (Google Pay or Apple Pay)
Presto cards in Apple Wallet or Google Wallet don't work with OC Transpo. They aren't accepted on buses, O‑Train fare gates, or Para Transpo. The Presto app blocks mobile setup to avoid payment issues.
What to use instead:
Use your Presto card to access discounted fares or transfer to STO. Or, tap your debit card, credit card, or phone with O‑Payment for adult fares. With O‑Payment, your fare is capped at $12.25 per day and $138.50 per month, after that, you ride free for the rest of the day or month.
O-Payment
O-Payment makes taking OC Transpo much easier and faster.
Pay your fare by tapping with Interac® Debit card, Visa, Mastercard, or Google Pay or Apple Pay mobile wallets at fare readers on buses and fare gates at O-Train stations.
