 ARCH Program 
 Affordable Recreation for Community Health (ARCH)
 ARCH is a subsidy program that Kamloops residents with low-income access City recreation programs and facility memberships. We understand the value of recreation for healthy living and want to help people in our community to reach their goals. This program is available to permanent Kamloops residents who meet income‑based eligibility or assistance criteria.
The ARCH program provides up to $400 in recreation credits to reduce the cost of fitness classes, activities, and facility memberships or drop-in services. It is an annual program that requires an application each year.
 We also understand that affordable transit is critical to allow people in need to access recreation opportunities. Applying for KamPASS , the City's affordable transit program, is done through the ARCH application.

 FAQs
- 
 Do I qualify for the ARCH program?
 You must be a permanent resident of Kamloops (see the  eligibility boundary map ) and meet ONE (1) of the options below:
- Receive Income Assistance from the Ministry of Social Development and Poverty Reduction (MSDPR)
- Have an annual income that is below the Statistics Canada Low Income Guidelines (see Application Form for Low Income Guidelines)
- Receive a Canadian Pension or Long-term Disability that is below the Low Income Guidelines 
- 
 What will I need to apply?
- Photo ID—BC Driver's Licence or other photo ID
- Second piece of ID—Birth Certificate or Care Card
- Proof of where you live—current utility bill (BC Hydro, telephone, or cable TV bill)
- Proof of income—MSDPR confirmation OR your latest Canada Customs and Revenue Agency’s Notice of Assessment for each adult in the house
- 
 Where can I get an ARCH Application?
 Download Application 
 You can get an hard copy of the ARCH Application at the Tournament Capital Centre (TCC), Westsyde Pool and Fitness Centre, Kamloops Museum and Archives, or ARCH screening agencies ( see the ARCH Agency List ).
- 
 Where do I take my ARCH Application?
 Applications MUST be taken to an ARCH Public Screening Agency or Referral Agency ( see the ARCH Agency List ). ARCH Applications will NOT be accepted at City of Kamloops facilities.
- 
 What is the program cycle?
 The ARCH program runs from August 9 to July 31. People who apply after February 1 receive $200 because the program cycle is half over.
- 
 What can I use my ARCH credit for?
 ARCH credits can be used for City of Kamloops Activity Guide programs and/or to buy memberships for City of Kamloops recreation facilities. ARCH clients pay 15% of the price for Activity Guide programs—your ARCH credit is used to pay the rest.
 TIP:  You can pay more than 15% of the program price to make your ARCH credit last longer.
- 
 What happens if I miss a program that I signed up for?
 If you withdraw from a program, you will have to pay the standard $10 withdrawal/cancellation fee. IMPORTANT: If you miss three classes in a row without letting us know, you will be removed from the ARCH program .
- 
 What happens if the City of Kamloops cancels a program?
 If your program is cancelled, your ARCH credit will be put back into your account.
- 
 Are my family members under my account?
 No, everyone has their own account and ARCH credit. Using another person’s ARCH credit is NOT allowed.
- 
 Can I use my ARCH credit to use the outdoor swimming pools in the summer?
 Yes, but you must buy a punch card or facility pass at the Tournament Capital Centre. Outdoor pools DO NOT have access to your account.
- 
 If I don’t drive, how else can I get to City of Kamloops facilities?
 Phone BC Transit at 250-376-1216 for bus schedules and prices or visit the BC Transit website .
