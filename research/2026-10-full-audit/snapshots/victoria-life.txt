 L.I.F.E. Program 
 Leisure Involvement for Everyone (L.I.F.E.) Program
 The City of Victoria’s Leisure Involvement For Everyone (L.I.F.E.) program improves access to recreation services and programs for individuals and families living on a low income. The City of Victoria and its regional partners are committed to ensuring residents have access and choices in their leisure opportunities.
 Applications for the L.I.F.E. program are processed confidentially and on a first come, first served basis. 
 Crystal Pool Closure Information
 Recreation services are moving to the City’s interim recreation facility, Victoria Recreation Centre, at 851 Broughton St. Repairs and upgrades are underway to prepare the facility for City use, with an opening planned for early 2027. Find answers to frequently asked questions about L.I.F.E. program during the closure period. 
 Open all Can I still apply or renew my pass?
 L.I.F.E. program applications will continue to be accepted during the closure:
- Complete and submit the L.I.F.E Application form . 
- By email: Send the completed application and required documents to LIFEprogram@victoria.ca 
- In person: Submit your application and show your required documents at the Recreation reception desk at City Hall. Starting October 19, a recreation reception desk will provide limited in-person service at City Hall on Mondays and Wednesdays from 10 a.m. to 3 p.m.
- Please complete one application per family residing at the same household. All information provided remains confidential unless required by a Freedom of Information request.
- Please provide proof of residency. Accepted documentation includes current utility bill, phone bill or current rental agreement within the last three months.
- Please provide proof of household income using the most recent Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) Notice of Assessment (NOA) for all household members aged 19 and older. Use the amount listed on Line 15000 of each Notice of Assessment to calculate total household income. Applicants must report the combined household income of all adults aged 19 and older living in the household.
 Access a copy of your NOA or Reassessment through CRA or call 1-800-959-8281.

 Can I visit other regional recreation centres after Crystal Pool closes?
 Yes. Eligible participants may continue to use their 52 free drop-in visits at participating regional recreation centres. Please ensure your 52-visit sticker is current by checking the end date printed on it.
 What if my sticker is expired? How do I get a new sticker?
- Starting October 19, bring your current L.I.F.E. membership card to the recreation reception desk at City Hall. Limited in-person service is available on Mondays and Wednesdays from 10 a.m. to 3 p.m.  
 LI.F.E. Credits
- L.I.F.E. credits are provided for the full two-year term.
- Credits may be used toward registration fees towards City of Victoria recreation programs as well as community and seniors centres within the city. 
 Benefits
 The City of Victoria believes recreation contributes to one’s mental and physical health and encourages the development of healthier and stronger individuals and families. Eligible applicants will receive the following benefits and membership will be valid for two years. The two-year cycle begins upon approval date, not limited to January application.
- Unlimited free drop-in sessions at Victoria Recreation Centre (opening early 2027). Membership passes must be scanned at time of drop-in admission.
- Unlimited access to drop-in public skating at Save-On-Foods Memorial Arena. 
- 52x free drop-in sessions annually to participating regional partners (see municipal recreation partners below). Members in their second year of the L.I.F.E. program will receive a new regional visits sticker based on their application anniversary date.
-   Credit toward registered programming. Credit allotment is for the two-year term. Credit can be used toward registration fees at Victoria Recreation Centre, or the community centres and senior centres found within the municipality. 
- $80 per preschooler (0-5yrs)
- $120 per youth (6-18yrs)
- $80 per adults (19yrs +)
- 50% off 1- year Regional Recreation Pass. L.I.F.E. pass holders have the option to purchase an Annual Regional Recreation Pass at 50% off instead of unlimited access to Victoria Recreation Centre. Credit can be used towards this purchase. The Regional Recreation Pass will need to be renewed following the one-year term. The second year of the membership will return to unlimited drop-ins at Victoria Recreation Centre and Save-on-Foods Memorial Arena.
 Eligibility Requirements
- Only City of Victoria residents with a fixed address may apply for the program. This includes all family members/dependants residing at the same address.
- Applicants must have lived at their address for the previous 30 days and be able to demonstrate proof of residency within the City.
- Income must be at or below the Statistics Canada low-income threshold to qualify:

 Eligibility Support
 If applicants are unable to provide the required documentation, they may still be eligible to apply for the L.I.F.E. program using an adjudicator. Adjudicators are members of the community who have a professional relationship with the applicant. It is a requirement that the adjudicator be familiar with the applicants financial and residential situation as their signature verifies the financial and residential need of the applicant. An adjudicator could be accessed in the following scenarios:
- Notice of Assessment does not accurately reflect applicant’s current income level due to an experience such as an illness or job loss.
- Applicant is unable to provide proof of residency.
- Applicant is unable to provide most recent Notice of Assessment from CRA.
- Applicant is a Refugee or arrived under the Canada-Ukraine Authorization for Emergency Travel (CUAET) in the last year. 
- Note: Please bring landing papers along with proof of Victoria residency to Victoria Recreation Centre. If the applicant is staying with a host family, their proof of residency can be in the host family’s name. If the applicant is a resident in the City of Victoria and does not fall into one of the above scenarios, please contact Victoria Recreation Centre or email LIFEprogram@victoria.ca to explore how we might be able to assist them.
 Adjudicators Can Be:
 •    Social Worker, Case Manager or Family Services Worker
 •    School Principle, Teacher or Counsellor
 •    Doctor or Healthcare Professional
 •    Spiritual Leader
 •    Lawyer or Notary Public
 •    Housing Assistant or Support Advocate

 How to use your L.I.F.E. Pass
 Successful applicants will be notified by phone or email that their application has been approved. Once you have been approved for the L.I.F.E. program, please visit Victoria Recreation Centre to have your photo taken and receive your membership card. Our friendly reception staff are happy to show you where to scan your card for future use.
- Please scan your membership pass at reception when visiting Victoria Recreation Centre. When visiting one of our regional recreation centre partners, please show your pass at reception so they can mark a visit off the sticker on the back of your pass.
- Please present your membership pass when visiting the Save-On-Foods Memorial Arena.
- If you are registering for a program, you can use your credit towards the cost. You can register online or in person. Credit can also be used at community centres and senior centres within the municipality or towards a purchase of a Regional Recreation Pass.
 Helpful Information
- Your photo will be taken and the image will be printed on your L.I.F.E. card.
- The L.I.F.E. card and credit cannot be redeemed for cash or transferred.
- The L.I.F.E. card and credit expires at the end of the two-year term. 
- Credit cannot be used for single admission or merchandise.
- If lost or stolen, there is a $5 replacement fee for a maximum of one card replacement per person per year.
- To use the 52 visits, you MUST have the regional visits sticker on your card and MUST present it at a regional facility or you will not gain admittance. You will require a new sticker each calendar year.
- If you are not a resident of the City of Victoria, you must apply in the municipality you are a resident in.
- Applicants that are denied because they could not meet requirements can reapply if there is a change in their financial or residential situation. Changes must be proven through updated documentation.
