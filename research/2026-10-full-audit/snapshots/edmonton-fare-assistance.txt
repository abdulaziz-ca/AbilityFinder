 Fare Programs
 Ride Transit Program
 The Ride Transit Program offers a reduced-cost Arc card to eligible low-income Edmontonians.
 Senior Passes
 Riders 65 years or older qualify for seniors' pricing but must apply for a concession fare. Seniors who are eligible to ride for free or require a low income annual pass must also apply.
 Transit Access Grant (TAG)
 The TAG Program provides single-fare transit products to eligible agencies to distribute to clients who need short-term transit support and who are in urgent transportation need.
 Providing Accessible Transit Here (PATH)
 The PATH Program provides free pre-loaded Arc cards to individuals who are homeless or are at high risk of homelessness.
