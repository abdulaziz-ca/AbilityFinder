 Seniors 65+
 Eligibility criteria 
 Required proof of eligibility 
 Available discounts 
- Be 65 or older
- Live in Ontario
- Presto card set with the  senior discount 
 You may be asked for proof of age when you use a card with this discount. 
- No-charge service on Wednesdays and Sundays * on all routes (except Para Transpo).
- Discounted monthly pass ($59.75)
- Lower Presto fares ($3.28 per ride)
 Seniors 65+ can ride no charge on OC Transpo buses and the O-Train on Wednesdays and Sundays. If you’re using a fare gate, you  must  use a Presto card set with the  senior discount .

 Youth 13–18
 From July 1 until August 31, 2026, youth age 13–18 ride for free on OC Transpo buses, trains and Para Transpo. 
 To ride for free, you need a Presto card set up with an Youth discount. You can set your fare discount at:
- The Rideau OC Transpo Customer Service Centre
- A ServiceOttawa Centre
- Participating Shoppers Drug Mart, Loblaws or Real Canadian Superstores
 To get set this discount on your Presto card, bring proof of your age.
 Learn more about the Youth Discount 
 If you have an Adult Presto card set to auto-renew with an Adult monthly pass, don't forget to cancel auto-renew when you get your Youth discount set on the card.
 Beginning September 1, Youth ride free on weekday evenings (after 5 pm), weekends and holidays for the rest of the year with a Presto card. Outside of these times, regular fare ($4.10) applies. 
 Eligibility criteria 
 Required proof of eligibility 
 Available discounts 
 Be between the ages of 13 and 19 and
 Live in Ontario
- Anyone can buy a Presto card with an age-based discount. You can buy it for yourself or on behalf of an eligible person. You must meet eligibility criteria to use the card with a discounted fare. You also need to prove eligibility if requested.
- Reduced-fare monthly Youth Pass only ($104.00 per month)
- Some students receive a Student Transit pass for free; check with your school before buying a Presto pass!

 Persons receiving ODSP benefits
 Eligibility criteria 
 Required proof of eligibility 
 Available discounts 
- Be an Ottawa resident
- Receive benefits under the Ontario Disability Support Program (ODSP)
- ODSP number (9-digit identification number found on an ODSP document) and a Presto card number OR Para Transpo ID number
- Discounted monthly Community pass ($43.25) or per-ride fare ($1.75) that let you ride on all routes, including Para Transpo, without a top-up
- Rural fare not included
 Read More about the Community pass 
 Para Transpo customers
 Eligibility criteria 
 Required proof of eligibility 
 Available discounts 
 Be registered as a Para Transpo customer 
- Para Transpo registration number
- Discounted monthly Access pass. This pass lets you ride unlimited on the O-Train and all non Para Transpo buses and lets you save one-third of the cash-fare price (per ride) on your Para Transpo trips and ride for only $43.25.
 Read More about Para Transpo Fares 
 Eligible persons on a low income
 Eligibility criteria 
 Required proof of eligibility 
 Available discounts 
 Have a family income below the low income threshold set by the Government of Canada
 and
 DO NOT receive transportation benefits from Social Services 
- Completed EquiPass Application and Consent Form 
- Notice of Assessment (NOA) from the Canada Revenue Agency for the most recent taxation year
 If you don’t have a NOA, other documentation (question #28) can be used to apply. 
 Discounted EquiPass , which costs approximately half the cost of an Adult pass
