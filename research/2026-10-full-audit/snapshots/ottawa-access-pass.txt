 Para Transpo fares 
 Para Transpo service costs the same as trips on buses or the O-Train, making it easy to travel across the system. Para Transpo trips from an urban to rural, or rural to urban location cost extra. 
 Your fare allows one support person to ride with you at a time. Support persons must be registered in advance.

 Payment options 
 Para Transpo fares can be paid with: 
 Presto Cash O-Payment About ParaPay 
 DayPasses and multi-day passes 
 Para Transpo customers or their companions can buy a DayPass or multi-day pass to use as regular fare on Para Transpo trips. 
 DayPass exception 
 Day Passes apply to regular fares only . They are NOT valid for Para Transpo rural zone fares. 
 You can buy a DayPass on a regular (non-Para) bus or at a ticket machine. You can only buy a multi-day pass at a ticket machine. 
 For more information about DayPasses and multi-day passes , visit our  Where and how to pay  page. 
 Presto 
 Customers can pay for trips on Para Transpo using a Presto card. Load your Presto card with a monthly pass or load funds to pay per ride. 
 Find out more about where to buy a Presto card and how to load it . 
 O-Payment: credit card, debit card or mobile wallet 
 You can pay with a credit card, debit card or mobile wallet if you are paying an Adult fare only. Register your credit card at o-payment.octranspo.com  to view your transaction history. 
 You can't use a credit card, debit card or mobile wallet to pay the fare of another person. 
 Fare capping 
 Both Presto and O-Payment offer automatic fare capping. This means that you will not pay more than the maximum fare amount for a time period, no matter how many trips you take. 
 Fare capping with Presto 
 Presto offers both daily and monthly fare capping. This means that when you pay for your trip, you will never pay more than the price of a DayPass or price of a monthly pass . 
 For rural trips, only the base fare amount counts towards the monthly cap. The base fare is the cost of a single urban trip. When you reach the monthly fare cap, you will still have to pay the difference between the base fare and the rural fare. 
 For example: If you have a Senior discount, $3.28 from your rural fare will count towards your monthly cap, but $7.72 will not. ($3.28 + $7.72 = $11.00) 
 Fare capping with O-Payment 
 O-Payment offers both daily and monthly fare capping. This means that when you pay for your trip with a credit card, debit card or mobile wallet, you will never pay more than the price of a DayPass or Adult monthly pass . You can't pay for rural trips with O-Payment. These trips won't count towards your daily or monthly cap. 
 Tap Tip! 
 Make sure to pay with the same credit card or mobile wallet each time you pay a fare to benefit from daily fare capping. 
 For seniors on Para Transpo 
 NEW! Seniors age 65 and up who use Para Transpo are eligible for four free trips a month when they use a registered Presto card to pay per trip. 
 Your four free monthly trips will be refunded as a Presto credit in the following month. 
 The credit cannot be applied to monthly passes. Some seniors who currently buy a monthly pass may choose to start paying per trip instead, to receive the credit. 
 There is no action required by customers to receive this credit as long as you already have a Senior discount on your registered Presto card. Be sure to use the same Presto card for all of your Para Transpo trips.

 How to pay your fare 
 O-Payment Monthly Passes Cash fare DayPass and Multi-Day Pass 2-for-1 Passes 
 O-Payment 
 O-Payment is an easy and convenient way to pay an Adult fare. It's now accepted on all buses and O-Train fare gates. Tap your debit card, credit card or mobile wallet on the fare reader and be on your way! You can check your trip history or download a receipt at  o-payment.octranspo.com . 
 Payment methods we accept on transit. 
 Use the same card on transfers and you won’t be charged twice. 
 Price capping on cards at $12.25 per day and $138.50 per month. 
 When transferring between trips on buses and the O-Train, use the same card to pay each time. You won’t be charged again if you're within the regular transfer window. On debit and credit card payments, daily fares are capped at the price of a DayPass ($12.25). Monthly fares are capped at the price of an Adult monthly pass ($138.50). 
 ❗  O-Payment remembers the card itself and not the account. Don’t switch between the physical card and its mobile wallet version or you’ll be charged twice. 
 ❗  O-Payment is not available on STO. If you regularly transfer between OC Transpo and STO, consider paying with a Presto card or cash. 
 O-Payment will only charge an Adult fare. If you're eligible for a discounted fare , use a monthly pass, or transfer regularly to STO, Presto is still your best option.   
 Monthly Passes 
 Monthly passes go on sale 14 days before the start of the month. A monthly pass is only valid during the calendar month for which it is purchased. Passes are open to Ontario residents only. 
 For discounted passes, you must set your discount before loading your pass. All discounts can be applied at our Customer Service Centres. You can set your age (Child, Youth or Senior) at participating Shoppers Drug Mart locations  as well. 
 Cash Fare 
 Cash lets you pay for your trip without buying your fare in advance. It is the most expensive payment option. If you expect to make 3 or more one-way trips on the same day, opt for a DayPass instead. 
 Using cash on the bus: 
 Make sure you have the exact amount on you. The driver and fare box can’t make change. 
 Board by the front doors. 
 Deposit your money in the fare box. 
 Wait for your transfer to print before making your way to your seat. 
 To pay using cash an O-Train station, you will need to use a ticket machine. 
 DayPass and Multi-Day Pass 
 The one-day DayPass and multi-day pass lets you ride on OC Transpo, Para Transpo and STO from the time of purchase until it expires. To ride, just tap your one-day DayPass or multi-day pass at the fare gate or show it to the operator as you board the bus. 
 A one-day DayPass is valid for unlimited travel all day, for one person. On weekends and specified holidays, use a one-day DayPass as a 2-for-1 DayPass! 
 On weekends and statutory holidays, one DayPass can be used as a 2-for-1 DayPass (valid for two customers). A single DayPass or two DayPasses may be presented as a 2-for-1 DayPass. A Multi-Day Pass is NOT valid as a 2-for-1 DayPass. A DayPass can be purchased on a conventional bus or at a ticket machine. A Multi-Day Pass may be purchased at a ticket machine only. Day Passes apply to regular fares only and are NOT valid for Para Transpo rural zone fares.

 Example: If you buy a 7-Day Pass on Monday, it will expire the following Monday morning 
 Where to buy your one-day DayPass and multi-day pass 
 The one-day DayPass and multi-day pass are available for sale at ticket machines . Ticket machines accept credit, debit or cash. 
 The one-day DayPass is also available on buses. Just ask your bus driver as you board. 
 2-for-1 Passes 
 On Saturdays, Sundays and select statutory holidays (see below), two people can ride all day on a single DayPass ($12.25)! 
 You can use the one-day DayPass as a 2-for-1 DayPass on these holidays: 
 New Year’s Day 
 Family Day

 You can only use the one-day DayPass as a 2-for-1 DayPass on the holiday itself, even if the holiday falls on a weekend and is celebrated on another day. The one-day DayPass cannot be used to pay the fare of two people aboard STO routes but it can be used to pay a single person’s fare. 
 The 2-for-1 DayPass is available for sale at ticket machines. Ticket machines accept credit, debit or cash. 
 Notice on payment for Para Transpo Users 
 All OC Transpo payment options are accepted on Para Transpo. Fare prices are the same too, except: 
 Seniors must pay the fare on Para Transpo, even Wednesdays and Sundays 
 Trips starting or ending in the rural zone have their own special fare 
 Taxis don’t have self-serve O-Payment readers. To pay by card, hand your Presto, credit or debit card to your driver. 
 Please pay your fares 
 OC Transpo has fare inspectors on the trains and buses. Pay your fares to avoid a fine.

 About the Access pass 
 The Access pass offers full fare on OC Transpo conventional bus and train services. On Para Transpo trips, the pass will let you save one-third of the single-ride fare. The Access pass is the same price as the Community pass. It can offer savings for Para Transpo customers who also ride OC Transpo bus and train service. 
 If you are a Para Transpo customer who never uses OC Transpo, you should not buy the Access pass. The pass will not offer you any savings. 
 Access pass eligibility 
 In order take advantage of Access discount, you need to: 
 Be a registered Para Transpo customer 
 Live in Ottawa 
 Helpful tips for customers 
 The Access pass may be a good option if you: 
 Are a Para Transpo customer who uses a combination of OC Transpo and Para Transpo services 
 Only use OC Transpo bus and train services in a given month 
 Whether the Access pass offers savings depends on your travel choices each month. 
 Best value pass for you 
 Access pass

 13 or more trips per month on OC Transpo

 You cannot buy an Access pass through ParaPay. Instead, purchase your Access pass on a Presto card. This will allow you to use your pass on OC Transpo buses and the O-Train. Calling an OC Transpo Customer Service Representative is the quickest way to set the Access discount on your card.
