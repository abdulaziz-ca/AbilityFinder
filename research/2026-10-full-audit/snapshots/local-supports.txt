 Search tips

 Not recommended: "I need to find a place for a blood test" or "legal advice for immigration" 
 Recommended: "blood test" or "immigration legal"

 New Hours – 211 British Columbia 
 Starting June 30, 2025, 211 British Columbia Resource Navigators will be available by phone, text, or web chat from 9am to 9pm, Monday to Friday, excluding statutory holidays. 
- 
 211 Day: Get to Know the Resource Navigators Behind Canada’s Leading Helpline   
 February 11 (2/11) is National 211 Day! To mark this important day, we spoke with 211 Navigators from across Canada to learn more about what they do, the issues they’re hearing about, and what they love most about their jobs.
