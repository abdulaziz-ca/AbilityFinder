 Recreation Assistance Program
 Pathway to Potential (P2P) recognizes and promotes the full participation of all Windsor-Essex residents in recreation and culture, regardless of financial circumstances. The Recreation Assistance Program provides a range of affordable recreational, cultural and artistic opportunities for Windsor-Essex residents living in low-income households to improve their quality of life and to participate in their local community. These affordable recreation programs include the 90% Recreation Discount, Third Party Partnerships, Targeted Initiatives and Universal Programs. 
 To learn further about the Recreation Assistance Program in your community, please contact your local municipal recreation department. 
 90% Recreation Discount
 The 90% Recreation Discount is a fee subsidy that provides affordable access to recreational, artistic and cultural programs for Windsor-Essex residents and households living on a low income. The program subsidizes eligible residents for 90% off the program registration fee (before taxes). Before applying, please review the program guidelines and eligibility criteria below. Please note that program eligibility may differ across municipalities.
 To apply for or further inquire about the program, residents can contact their local municipal recreation department.
 Eligibility Requirements
 Eligibility for the 90% Recreation Discount is based on the income of all members within the household, as well as family size and availability of funding. To apply for the program, applicants must meet the eligibility criteria and submit required documents. Please refer to the Eligibility Requirements web page for detailed information.
 Eligible and Ineligible Programs
- Funding is allocated to sustained programs that involve sport, physical, cultural or artistic activity.
- Programs should be a minimum of five weeks in duration and include at least one session per week. In the case of day camps or specialty camps, the program must last at least five consecutive days for consideration.
- Ineligible programs include competitive activities, travel teams, fitness centre orientations, personal training and private lessons.
- Funding covers program registration fees. Funding does not cover equipment and material costs.
 Funding Details, Restrictions and Parameters
- Funding availability is based on a first-come, first-served basis.
- Funding is available per eligible person per calendar year.
- Responsibility for informing their local municipal recreation department of changes to financial, residency or other circumstances pertaining to the 90% Recreation Discount program application and eligibility is the responsibility of the program participant. The program participant must notify their municipality of changes within thirty (30) days of the change taking effect.
- Program eligibility is non-transferable between individuals.
- Approved funding must be used within the calendar year. Funding is not transferable from one year to the next.
- All approvals will be determined by the local recreation department and are subject to local demands and budgets.
- The subsidy extends to eligible programs and services directly delivered by municipal recreation departments and approved service organizations within Windsor-Essex County. (To check if a service organization is approved, please contact your local recreation department.)
- Payment is either issued directly to the program provider or you can request a reimbursement if the registration fees were paid in advance. (Note: If you are requesting a reimbursement, it must be done within thirty [30] days of payment.)

 Eligibility Requirements 
 The Affordable Pass Program and Recreation Assistance Program (90% Recreation Discount) utilize income testing to determine an applicant’s program eligibility. To be eligible to receive subsidized program rates, applicants must demonstrate living in a low-income household and submit a copy of a government-issued income document for the most current tax base year. Eligibility Requirements Eligibility for the Affordable Pass Program and 90% Recreation Discount is based on the income of all members within the household, as well as family size and availability of funding. To apply for the program, applicants must meet the eligibility criteria and submit the required documents identified below. Eligibility Criteria: 
 Residents who have lived within Windsor-Essex County for at least 30 days and who are able to demonstrate residency. 
 A combined household income that is at, or below, the Market Basket Measure (MBM) thresholds. For further details on MBM income thresholds, please contact your local municipal transit or recreation department. 
 Required Documents: Proof of Income Residents applying for the Affordable Pass Program and/or the 90% Recreation Discount must submit their proof of income document(s) for each member of the household earning an income for the current tax base year. Preferred documents include: 
 Canada Child Benefit (CCB) notice (Note: This is preferred for 90% Recreation Discount applicants.) Notice of Assessment (NOA) Proof of Ontario Works (OW) or Ontario Disability Support Program (ODSP) benefits 
 Proof of Residency Provide one of the following documents for the household: 
 Ontario Driver’s Licence Ontario Health Card 
 Ontario Photo Card 
 Current Utility Bill Property Tax Bill 
 Lease/Rental Agreement 
 Mortgage Statement 
 Current Bank Statement Please note: Additional documentation may be requested by the service provider when applying for programming. Letter of Endorsement The Letter of Endorsement may be used in the circumstance that the applicant is unable to provide the required supporting documentation for their P2P application (i.e., income documentation, proof of residency). Additionally, the Letter of Endorsement may be used if an applicant is facing recent changes to their circumstances that do not reflect their submitted documentation. Acceptance of the Letter of Endorsement is at the discretion of the municipality. Please check with your local municipal transit/recreation department to determine if a Letter of Endorsement is acceptable.  The Letter of Endorsement should: 
 Be drafted on professional letterhead; 
 Include contact information from the referring agency, including email and phone number; 
 Include the client(s) full name(s); 
 Outline the benefits that the subsidy would provide for your client (for example, increased access to higher education, employment opportunities, medical services, etc.); and 
 Indicate the barriers that prevent your client from applying to the program (for example, lack of income documentation).
