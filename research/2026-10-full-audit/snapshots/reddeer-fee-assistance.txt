 Fee Assistance Program
 We’re accepting Fee Assistance applications, but processing may take over 10 business days due to high volume. Please submit only once. A staff member will email you about eligibility and next steps.
 x 
 The Fee Assistance Program offers financial support to people who are currently facing financial challenges and want to use City-operated recreation facilities or take part in City offered Recreation or Culture programs or activities.
 Pending available funding, the program provides a subsidy of up to $200 per year to each eligible person for Safe & Healthy Communities program registrations, plus a Recreation Pass Card to access drop-in activities at City operated recreation facilities.
 How to Qualify
 You may automatically qualify for the Safe & Healthy Communities Fee Assistance Program if you are a Red Deer resident and one of the following applies to you. 
 If you have the documentation for one of the following qualifiers, you can bring your qualifying documentation, Government issued ID, and proof of residence if your ID does not show your current Red Deer address to the Customer Service Representatives at the Collicutt, GH Dawe, Recreation Centre, or Michener Centre for same day enrollment.
- You are on AISH (Assured Income for the Severely Handicapped): Please provide a copy of your current direct deposit statement from your AISH that shows the expiry date and the names of the individual(s) covered under your health benefits card, or a dated letter from your Public Trustee Representative confirming that you are currently on AISH. 
- You are on an Alberta Works program: Please provide a copy of your letter stating the expiry date along with either your dated Adult/Child Benefit Card or direct deposit statement.
- You are a Bridges Community Living Tenant (formerly Red Deer Housing Authority) : Please provide a copy of your most recent rent receipt from Bridges Community Living, or your current rental housing contract from Bridges Community Living. Confirmation of Tenancy letters from Bridges Community Living will also be accepted.
- You are on Alberta Income Support: Please provide a copy of your current Alberta Income Support letter, direct deposit statement, or Health benefits card with expiry date that shows the names of the individual(s) covered under your health benefits card. This is not Guaranteed Income Supplement. Guaranteed Income Supplement is not a qualifier. 
- You have refugee status: Please provide a copy of your Protected Person Status document (For Refugee Claimants, provide a copy of your Refugee Protection Claimant document), or Single Journey Document for Resettlement to Canada.
- You have been referred by a Referral Agency: Please provide a confirmation number from the agency.
 If you are not a recipient of any of these programs or benefits you may still apply online with your Canada Revenue Agency Notice of Assessment to find out if you qualify as low income. Canada Revenue Agency Notices of Assessment cannot be accepted by the Customer Service Representatives at City Operated recreation facilities, and must be processed by qualified staff through the online application process.
 Applying for Funding
 In Person – Same Day Processing and Enrollment with a Qualifier 
 For same day processing and enrollment, apply in person at the Collicutt Centre, G.H. Dawe Community Centre, Don Moore Recreation Centre, or Michener Aquatic Centre with your ID, proof of residence, and documentation for one of the qualifiers listed above.  
 Online – Low Income with Canada Revenue Agency Notice of Assessment, or Other Qualifier 
 Fill out the online form in its entirety, including information about yourself as the primary applicant and all family members. 
 In your online application, you are required to provide either proof of income (most recent tax year Canada Revenue Agency Notice of Assessment for each adult applying) or proof of automatic qualifier as listed above. Also required is proof of identification (Government issued ID), and proof of residence if your current address is not listed on your ID or qualifying documentation. Applications will not be processed until all documentation is obtained. We will reach out through the email address you provide in the form if we need to gather more information to process your application.
 Fee Assistance Program Application Form 
 To talk to a staff member for more information regarding the program, call 403-309-8411 .
 If you are unable to submit an application online, you may be required to set up an in person appointment at one of the facilities listed above to determine your eligibility (dependent on staff availability). Please be prepared to provide your current tax year Canada Revenue Agency Notice of Assessment, proof of identification, and your current contact information at any in person appointments. Call 403-309-8411 or send an email to feeassistance@reddeer.ca   for more information. 
 Other Community Recreation Subsidy Options
 To find out how you can access other non-City of Red Deer community programs and subsidies, contact the Red Deer KidSport chapter at  403-314-4737  or visit the Canadian Tire Jumpstart Program web page .

 Transit Fare Assistance Pass
 The Fare Assistance Pass is now a permanent fare option. The program reduces the cost of any monthly pass to $34 for those who qualify. Expiry deadline May 31 for current approved riders – new and renewal application now being accepted.
 Apply Now - New Applicants   Apply Now - Renewals 
 Frequently Asked Questions
 How can I apply for the Transit Fare Assistance Pass? 
 The quickest way to apply for the Transit Fare Assistance Pass is by filling out the online form . You can also apply in person at Sorensen Station if you require support. 
 How can I qualify for the Transit Fare Assistance Pass? 
 To qualify for the reduced monthly pass, riders must meet one of the following criteria:
- Receiving
- Assured Income for Severely Handicapped (AISH)
- Guaranteed Income Supplement (GIS)
- Alberta Income Supports
- Current tenants within the Red Deer Housing Authority Program
- Qualify under the Low Income Cut Off
- Approved for The City of Red Deer’s Recreation Fee Assistance Program
 How much will a monthly pass cost if I qualify for the Transit Fare Assistance Pass? 
 A monthly transit pass for those who qualify for the Transit Fare Assistance Pass will cost $34, no matter the type of monthly pass they purchase – adult, senior or student. 
 What are the regular monthly Transit fares? 
 As of September 1, 2023, the following are the regular monthly Transit rates:
 Unlimited Ride (Monthly Pass)
- Adult - $75
- Senior - $62
- Student - $62
 If I purchased a monthly pass, then qualify for the Transit Fare Assistance Pass, can I get a refund? 
 Yes, if you pre-purchased a monthly pass, then qualify for the Transit Fare Assistance Pass, you can visit customer service at Sorensen Station to receive a refund for the difference in price. Proof of purchase is required for refund (receipt or registered MyRide card), and refund will go onto original method of payment. 
 Can youth apply for the Transit Fare Assistance Pass? 
 Yes, however, we do recommend the household parent/guardian submit the application on their behalf. We recognize there may be exceptions, if this is not possible youth are encouraged to apply with additional notes. 
 How long will it take to find out if I qualify for the Transit Fare Assistance Pass? 
 Once an application is submitted, it could take up to 10 business days to process and notify applicants. Applicants are asked to include all required documentation to allow for faster processing times; not including documents can lead to delays. Applicant will be notified about qualification by email. 
 If I already have a monthly pass and have qualified for the Transit Fare Assistance Pass, what do I do? 
 Once you have been notified that you qualify for the Transit Fare Assistance Pass, you will need to visit Sorensen Station the first month to verify eligibility and reload your monthly pass. Once you have done this, you can reload your monthly pass at one of our City of Red Deer reload locations (Sorensen Station, City Hall, Collicutt Centre, G.H. Dawe Community Centre, Recreation Centre, Michener Aquatic Centre). 
 If I qualify for the Transit Fare Assistance Pass, and have not held a monthly Transit pass previously, what do I do? 
 Once you have been notified that you qualify for the Transit Fare Assistance Pass, you will need to visit Sorensen Station to verify eligibility and purchase your monthly pass on to the card. Once you have done this, you can reload your monthly pass at one of our City of Red Deer reload locations (Sorensen Station, City Hall, Collicutt Centre, G.H. Dawe Community Centre, Recreation Centre, Michener Aquatic Centre). 
 Do I have to re-apply after a certain amount of time? 
 Yes. If you qualify for the program, you will need to apply every year. For 2025, passes will expire at the end of June, so be sure to get your renewal application in to ensure there is no interruption in your access to the reduced fare rate.
