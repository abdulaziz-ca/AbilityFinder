 Learning Disability Assessment Bursary 
 Back to all programs 
 This bursary program helps part-time or full-time students with the up-front costs of the learning disabilities assessment.

 What is available?
 Up to $3,500 is available to eligible students. If you have a learning disability, you need a recent learning disability assessment to determine eligibility for services and/or equipment. 
 Am I eligible?
 You may be eligible if you:
- qualify for student funding through StudentAid BC 
- are enrolled in full-time or part-time post-secondary level courses at a designated public post-secondary institution in B.C.
- have been recommended to have a learning disability assessment by the accessibility services office at your school
 How do I apply?
 Please contact the accessibility services office at the post-secondary institution you are planning to attend.
