 Affordable Transit with Fare Assist
 Effective January 1, 2026, save 50% on your transit fare with Fare Assist. 
 Hamilton residents with household incomes below the Low-Income Measure qualify for a 50% Fare Assist discount on single-ride PRESTO fares for themselves and their family. Eligibility is based on Total Household Income, regardless of income source.  
 Apply today & start saving 
 The Fare Assist program will be permanent effective January 1, 2026 , and two other programs will be suspended: the Affordable Transit Pass for Ontario Works recipients and the Temporary Transit Fare Special Program for persons who use a personal mobility device (PMD), such as a wheelchair, scooter, walker, and CNIB cardholders.
 Hamilton residents with household incomes below the Low-Income Measure qualify for a 50% Fare Assist discount on single-ride PRESTO fares—for themselves and their family. Eligibility is based on Total Household Income, regardless of income source.
 Current Fare Assist recipients will automatically receive the new 50% discount starting on January 1, 2026.  
 This includes individuals and families with employment income, as well as those receiving support payments such as Ontario Works (OW), the Ontario Disability Support Program (ODSP), the Canada Pension Plan disability benefit (CPP Disability), Employment Insurance (EI), or the payments from Workplace Safety and Insurance Board (WSIB) . 
 Recent refugees, newcomers to Canada, and individuals aged 18 or 19 who have not yet filed a tax return may also qualify. This program provides a 50% discount on current single-ride PRESTO fares for one year following application approval.
 We estimate approximately 88,000 Hamiltonians will benefit from the program.
 Key Features of the Fare Assist Program
- Hamiltonians who live at or below the Stats Canada Low-Income Measure (LIM) can apply. The LIM by family size can be found below.
- 50% discount on current single-ride PRESTO fares for one year following application approval. HSR fare pricing can be found at Hamilton.ca/HSRFares .  Note:  The discount does not apply to monthly or annual passes.
- The discount applies to both conventional and accessible transit. This means it also applies to trips taken on DARTS vehicles. 
- Applicants can re-apply each year.
- Funds can be loaded to a PRESTO card as-needed, and riders will not be charged an additional fare after 11 paid PRESTO trips in the same week (Monday 12:01 AM to Sunday 11:59 PM). 
- The discount applies to all eligible members of the household, including a spouse and children ages 13-17. Children 12-and-under ride free with PRESTO.

 When does the permanent program take effect? 
 The permanent Fare Assist program will begin on January 1, 2026. 
 What’s changing from the pilot program?
 The pilot program offered a 30% discount. Based on strong results and community feedback, the permanent program will increase the discount to 50%. At the same time, the Affordable Transit Pass and Temporary Transit Fare Special programs will end.
 What happens if I’m already enrolled?
 If you are already approved for Fare Assist, your PRESTO card will automatically receive the updated 50% discount starting January 1, 2026. No reapplication is needed until your next renewal. 
 How many people benefit from Fare Assist?
 To date, more than 14,000 residents have been approved for Fare Assist, with ridership growing steadily. The program is helping people travel more often and access jobs, education and community supports.
 How satisfied are users with the program?
 Very satisfied—89% of users say they are happy with Fare Assist and 91% would recommend it to others. Most users also say the program has made daily travel easier and increased how often they use transit.
 How do I redeem my Fare Assist PRESTO voucher code?
 You can redeem your Fare Assist PRESTO voucher code:
- On the PRESTO website at www.prestocard.ca 
- Using the PRESTO app
 Follow the instructions in your PRESTO account to enter and apply your voucher code
 Can I use the same PRESTO card on DARTS/HSR?
 Yes your (one) PRESTO card can be used on HSR and DARTS. 
 Does each member of my family need their own PRESTO card?
 Yes, each family member will need their own PRESTO card in order to receive the Fare Assist discount following approval.
 Can I apply if I’m 18 or 19 and haven’t filed my income tax?
 If you are aged 18 or 19 and have never filed an Income Tax return you can apply. Please include a copy of your most recent three (3) pay stubs for any and all job(s) held in the last 12 months and / or any annual Income Slips (e.g., T4, T4A, or other) you may have received to date.
 Note:  If you re-apply in the future and you are not in receipt of OW or ODSP, you will be required to provide a Notice of Assessment (NOA).  
 Can I apply if I’m a recent refugee/newcomer to Canada but have not filed a tax return?
 Yes, if you are recent refugee/newcomer to Canada, but have not filed a tax return you can apply. Please include a copy of the following documents:
- Record of Landing (Citizenship and Immigration Canada (CIC) form IMM 1000) and Sponsorship agreements or payments, including Refugee Assistance Program cheque stubs, if applicable. 
- Refugee Status Documentation IMM1442 (Forms IMM1263 and 5292 may also be considered). 
- An Ontario Works (OW) or Ontario Disability Support Program (ODSP) benefit stub for the current month. 
- If currently employed, a copy of your most recent three (3) pay stubs for any and all job(s) held in the last 12 months and / or any annual Income Slips (e.g., T4, T4A, or other) you may have received to date. 
- If you are privately sponsored, a letter from your sponsor/sponsoring group is required to confirm your monthly or annual income.
 Note: You will be required to provide a Notice of Assessment (NOA) if you re-apply in the future.
 Can I apply more then one discount voucher code or concession to my PRESTO card?
 You can now have a universal fare (such as Adult or Senior) and a specific agency discount (like HSR Fare Assist) on the same PRESTO card. Your card will automatically apply your specific agency discount when you travel on that transit system. When you travel on other transit systems, your universal fare will automatically apply instead.
 What is the cost per trip for adults/senior?
 Current fare pricing can be found on HSR's Fares & Photo ID's . The 50% discount is off the single ride with PRESTO price for all members of the household.
 I’m a senior and currently use a monthly pass.  Is this program right for me?
 If you are taking less than 40 trips per month, the Fare Assist rate will be the cheaper option.
 Is the Support Person card still valid?
 Yes. The Support Person card allows your support person to board with no charge, but the original passenger still needs to pay a fare.
 How is the PRESTO card identifiable to persons who are visually impaired?
 The card is marked with the brail symbol for the letter P on the top right corner.
 Can you use DARTS and HSR with one fare within the two hour transfer window?
 Yes you can transfer anywhere within our system using the same payment method and take advantage of the 2 hour transfer window.
 How do I use a PRESTO card?
 Find information about using PRESTO at  https://prestocard.ca/en/help-center 
 How do I apply for Fare Assist?
 Residents who wish to participate in the Fare Assist program can apply at  SpecialSupportsBenefits.hamilton.ca .
 Why is HSR suspending the Affordable Transit Pass and Temporary Transit Fare Special Program?
 Fare Assist is a more equitable discount program with eligibility based on financial need.
 Can a doctor’s note exempt me from paying a fare?
 No, a doctor’s note does not exempt riders from paying a fare.
 What happens if I leave Hamilton using the Fare Assist discount?
 If you leave Hamilton using your Fare Assist discount you will be charged full Adult fare by the other transit agency.  They do not honour the HSR discount.
 What happens if I have another transit agency change my fare type while using their service to get a discount?
 If you allow another transit agency to change your fare type for discounts with them, your HSR discount will stop working when you return to Hamilton and refunds on fares paid here will not be offered. We recommend using another PRESTO card when using their services if you are a senior or a youth. 
 What do I do if I’ve allowed my Fare Assist discount to get changed on my card by another transit agency?
 If you have allowed your Fare Assist discount to be changed by another transit agency, you will need to visit the HSR Customer Service Office at 36 Hunter St. E to have the fare type set back to your discount. Adjustments will not be offered for any regular HSR fares you paid.
 Does this Fare Assist discount work on monthly passes?
 No. This discount is for single fares only.
 Can I redeem the voucher on the PRESTO App?
 PRESTO Vouchers can now be redeemed for both physical and digital PRESTO cards on either the PRESTO website  at  www.prestocard.ca or on the PRESTO app.
