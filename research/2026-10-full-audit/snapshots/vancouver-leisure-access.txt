Leisure Access Program
Because of the the volume of applications, we are unable to provide application status updates. You will receive an email if your application is complete, incomplete, or does not qualify. If you have not heard from us after 4 weeks, email lap@vancouver.ca.
Leisure Access Program brochure and application form: Leisure Access Program brochure PDF file (170 KB); Leisure Access Program self-refer application form PDF file (252 KB)
The Leisure Access Program (LAP) supports low-income residents of Vancouver by providing free and reduced-fee access to programs and services at Park Board facilities, including community centres.
Once your application is approved, a Leisure Access membership will be added to your OneCard and linked to your ActiveNet account. This membership allows you to register for eligible programs and access services at reduced rates.
Your LAP pass is for your use only. The Leisure Access Pass is for your personal use only and cannot be shared or transferred. Staff will take your photo and add it to your OneCard account to use the pass. Children and youth aged 16 and under require a signed OneCard photo consent form PDF file (81 KB) from a parent or guardian.
What's happening: Leisure access passes now valid for 3 years. Starting January 1, 2026, Leisure Access passes will be valid for 3 years. Any pass issued in 2026 will not expire until 2029, giving you plenty of time to enjoy your benefits. The current Vancouver Aquarium 1 Free Visit Pass will become a 3 Free Visits Pass.
Mobi by Rogers Community Pass updates: New adult Mobi by Rogers Community Pass applications are now closed for 2026 because capacity has been reached. Applications are still being accepted for: New and renewing youth applicants; Adults renewing a 2025 Community Pass. Review Mobi by Rogers Community Pass details
How to apply
Family in the Leisure Access Program is defined as: One or 2 adult(s), legally married or common-law partners, and their legal dependents aged 17 and under. Grandparents and/or dependants aged 18 or older complete a separate Leisure Access application.

[Section] You receive income assistance or one of the following provincial or federal financial subsidies
Bring the following items to a Park Board community centre: One of the following income assistance confirmations. Individuals only: Ministry of Social Development and Poverty Reduction (MSDPR) income assistance cheque stub; MSDPR confirmation of assistance; MSDPR release of personal information; Guaranteed Income Supplement (GIS); Rental Assistance Program (RAP) letter; Shelter Aid for Elderly Renters (SAFER); Valid red bus pass (Compass Card). Family: Income assistance (MSDPR) application (office stamped form).
Proof of address you are residing in the City of Vancouver dated within 90 days such as: Home utility bill (cable, gas, hydro, internet, phone); Bank or credit card statement; Lease agreement. Identification, such as: Government-issued photo identification (BC drivers license, BC ID card, Canadian passport)

[Section] You are a client of a non-profit or government agency, which can refer you
Ask the non-profit or government agency to refer you to the Park Board Leisure Access program. This is the agency where you are a client. The agency will use a Third Party Referral form. If the agency is unaware of the Leisure Access Program, they can contact the Leisure Access office at LAP@vancouver.ca to request information. Take the original Third Party Referral form completed by the referring agency within 90 days to a Park Board community centre. It must be signed and dated by you. Bring identification, such as: Government-issued photo identification; ID that matches the client ID selected on your Third Party Referral form

[Section] You are an asylum seeker or a temporary foreign worker
(Same referral process as above: Third Party Referral form completed within 90 days, taken to a Park Board community centre, signed and dated by you; government-issued photo ID or ID matching the client ID on the form.)

[Section] You are a family with a child up to 17 years old who qualifies for the Child Disability Benefit.
Important note: Disability status does not determine eligibility for the Leisure Access Program; eligibility is based on economic need, and receiving the Child Disability Benefit verifies that need.
To qualify a family must: Receive the Child Disability Benefit in their Canada Child Benefit notice (Confirmation of eligibility is noted on page 2 of the Canada Child Benefit under "Explanation"); Reside in the City of Vancouver; Complete the application form PDF file (258 KB).
Include the following documents with your application: 2025 Canada Child Benefit; Proof of address in the City of Vancouver dated within 90 days, such as: Home utility bill (cable, gas, hydro, internet, phone); Bank or credit card statement; Lease agreement.
When complete, attach your required documents to the application and place in a sealed envelope and either: Drop off application at a Park Board community centre with envelope addressed to LAP Office; Mail application to: Leisure Access Office, Vancouver Park Board, c/o 453 W 12th Avenue, Vancouver BC V5Y 1V4

[Section] You, your spouse/partner, or dependent, age 18 and older, qualify for the Disability Tax Credit
Refer yourself, and your family.
Eligibility: You and your family are eligible if your family's combined net income is less than or equal to the following amount based on family size and receipt of the Disability Tax Credit. Disability status does not determine eligibility for the Leisure Access Program; eligibility is based on financial need. Your combined family net income is at or below:
1 person $53,166; 2 people $70,972; 3 people $84,645; 4 people $96,171; 5 people $106,325; 6 people $115,506; 7 people $123,948
Leisure Access is not available to individuals or families with a combined: Interest and Investment Income (Line 12100) over $1,500; Net Rental Income (Line 12600) over $2,000; Capital Gains (Line 12700) over $2,000
Apply: Complete the application form PDF file (264 KB). Please provide: A copy of your 2025 Proof of Income Statement (not Notice of Assessment) verifying receipt of the Disability Tax Credit; Proof of address in the City of Vancouver dated within 90 days, such as: Home utility bill (cable, gas, hydro, internet, phone); Bank or credit card statement; Lease agreement. When complete, attach your required documents to the application and place in a sealed envelope and either: Drop off application at a Park Board community centre with envelope addressed to LAP Office; Mail application to: Leisure Access Office, Vancouver Park Board, c/o 453 W 12th Avenue, Vancouver BC V5Y 1V4
Request a Proof of Income Statement from Canada Revenue Agency: Request online or call Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) at 1-800-959-8281, listen to instructions, then press 2, then 1. Have your Social Insurance Number ready, as well as either: Your CRA Personal Identification Number; Your date of birth and Income reported on line 15000 of your 2025 tax return (Form T1) Tax Return. The 2025 Proof of Income Statement will be sent by Canada Post.

[Section] You are a resident with low-income and do not receive income assistance and cannot be referred
Eligibility: You and your family are eligible if your net family income is less than or equal to the following amount based on your family size. (Source: Statistics Canada)
1 person $43,028; 2 people $60,834; 3 people $74,507; 4 people $86,033; 5 people $96,187; 6 people $105,368; 7 people $113,810. For each additional person, add $8,450.
Leisure Access is not available to individuals or families with a combined: Interest and Investment Income (Line 12100) over $1,500; Net Rental Income (Line 12600) over $2,000; Capital Gains (Line 12700) over $2,000
Apply: Complete the application form PDF file (264 KB); 2025 Proof of Income Statement (not Notice of Assessment) is required to complete application; Proof of address in the City of Vancouver dated within 90 days, such as: Home utility bill (cable, gas hydro, phone, internet); Bank or credit card statement; Lease agreement. When complete, attach your required documents to the application and place in a sealed envelope and either drop off at a Park Board community centre (envelope addressed to LAP Office) or mail to Leisure Access Office, Vancouver Park Board, c/o 453 W 12th Avenue, Vancouver BC V5Y 1V4.
Random verification process: Each month a random sample of self-referred declarations are selected to verify income information, marital status, and dependants as a Proof of Income Statement is not required when submitting a declaration. You will be asked to provide: Proof of Income Statement, (Canada Revenue Agency); Canada Child Benefit (Canada Revenue Agency). The 2025 Proof of Income Statement will be sent by Canada Post. If you are a permanent resident or immigrant, send a copy of your confirmation of permanent residence (COPR). If requested documentation is not provided within 30 days of request, Leisure Access will be deleted from your account.

[Section] You are receiving Employment Insurance (EI) benefits in 2026/2027
Eligibility: Projected family income must be at or below the low-income threshold below. (Source: Statistics Canada) 1 person $43,028; 2 people $60,834; 3 people $74,507; 4 people $86,033; 5 people $96,187; 6 people $105,368; 7 people $113,810. For each additional person, add $8,450. Same investment/rental/capital-gains exclusions. Apply: application form; family members' 2025 tax year Proof of Income Statement (Canada Revenue Agency); Service Canada letter outlining Employment Insurance benefits information (start and finish date, benefit rate); proof of address in City of Vancouver dated within 90 days.
