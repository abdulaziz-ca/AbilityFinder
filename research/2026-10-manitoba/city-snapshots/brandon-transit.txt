 Free Transit for Youth!
Beginning September 2026, the City of Brandon is introducing Ride Ready—a free transit program for youth. 
Brandon Transit is committed to helping young people travel safely and independently, whether they're heading to school, work, extracurricular activities, or spending time with friends.
