 CNIB Registered Clients
CNIB Registered Clients are eligible for free transit (excluding Access Transit), visit CNIB for more details.
UPASS Program
The UPASS is a card provided by Assiniboine College or Brandon University that gives you unlimited access to Brandon Transit. Valid Student ID must be provided along with the U-Pass when requested.
Class Pass Program
Our Class Pass Program is available to schools, daycares and other early learning facilities who are looking for group transportation while experiencing their local transit system!
