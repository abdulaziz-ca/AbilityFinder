 Winnipeg Transit Plus 
 Winnipeg Transit Plus is a service of Winnipeg’s public transit system that provides door-to-door transportation for people who are unable to regularly use the City’s fixed route transit system because of a physical disability in accordance with the established criteria.
Winnipeg Transit Plus operates within Winnipeg, transporting individuals who are ambulatory or who travel in a wheelchair. Other mobility aides, such as scooters, canes and walkers, are accommodated (some restrictions apply).
Winnipeg Transit Plus delivers the service through contracts with private transportation service providers.
Vehicles used are:
 - cars and SUVs
 - wheelchair-accessible vans and mini-buses
Drivers physically assist passengers from the ground floor outermost doorway of the pick-up location, to and from the vehicle, to the ground floor outermost doorway of the destination.
Winnipeg Transit Plus is a pre-booked, shared ride service. Being the first to be picked up does not guarantee you will be the first dropped off. However, we try to keep in-vehicle travel times to a minimum and ensure that passengers get to their destinations by the requested times.

Eligibility Criteria
You must qualify under at least one of the following criteria:
 - Requires the use of a wheelchair or scooter:
 - On an ongoing basis
 - Until a specific date – eligibility only until the date specified
 - as a result of dialysis treatment-eligibility only for trips to and from dialysis treatment
 - Unable to walk 175 metres (575 feet) outside:
 - at all times – unlimited eligibility, or
 - during winter months only – conditional eligibility is restricted to the period to start no later than November 1 and to end no earlier than April 15 (recognizing that “winter conditions” can be unpredictable and can occur earlier or last longer than the defined period), the Manager of Client Services has the authority to use the following metrics to adjust the beginning and end dates on the first day of the seven-day forecast predicted by Environment Canada:
 - when a snow accumulation of snow or a depth or 3 centimeters of more exists or is predicted and/or
 - if the forecast is for weather conditions such as adverse snowfall
 - as a result of dialysis treatment – eligibility only for trips to and from dialysis treatment 
 - Has 20/200 vision or less, or a visual field of less than 20 degrees in both eyes (legally blind) not corrected by lenses – unlimited eligibility (i.e. even with glasses or contacts your vision, in both eyes, is still 20/200 or less) 
 - Has Alzheimer’s Disease or Related Dementia (ADRD) which interferes with ability to use the regular fixed route transit system with an equivalent level of independence and safety
Application Process
 - Send in your completed application form
 - We will contact you by letter within 30 business days of receiving your application
 - You will likely be required to attend our office for a functional assessment of your mobility and cognitive transit skills (if applicable) to:
 - confirm your eligibility
 - assess your ability to safely travel independently (if applicable)
 - review how your mobility equipment will influence your access to Winnipeg Transit Plus vehicles as we use a variety of vehicle types and sizes (cars, wheelchair accessible lift vans and mini-buses).
 - We will notify you in writing of the approval or denial of your application.
Application Form
Application for Transit Plus service can be now submitted online. Applying online is the fastest and easiest way to process your application. Complete your application digitally through our new online application system or download a printable version to submit by mail, fax, or in-person.
 - Winnipeg Transit Plus Application Form – Online (New and recommended)
 - Winnipeg Transit Plus Application Form – Printable
Visitor Application Form
If you are visiting Winnipeg and are registered with a paratransit service in your home municipality, you can apply for temporary Winnipeg Transit Plus services. Complete your application digitally through our new online application system or download a printable version to submit by mail, fax, or in-person.
You will need to provide your paratransit registration number from your home city to complete the application.
 - Winnipeg Transit Plus Visitor Application Form – Online (New and recommended)
 - Winnipeg Transit Plus Visitor Application Form – Printable
Note for visitors: Please submit your application at least 7 to 10 business days prior to your trip.

 Fare Structure/ Payment 
 Winnipeg Transit Plus offers the same fares and categories as fixed route transit including the reduced senior, youth, and post secondary fares.
 Fare Summary 
Full Fare: Applies to individuals aged 17 – 64 who are not in high school or attending a participating post-secondary institution.
Youth Fare: Applies to youths 16 and under*, and high school students 17-21 with a GoCARD. Photo ID must be shown to Operator when boarding. 
*Note: Children aged 11 and under are able to ride free when accompanied by a fare-paying customer.
Senior Fare: Applies to persons who are age 65 or older. Photo ID must be shown to the Operator when boarding.
Post-Secondary Fare: Post-secondary peggo cards and validated ID available at participating post-secondary institutions only. Validated student ID must be shown to the Operator when using a post-secondary peggo card. More information is available on the post-secondary pass page.
WINNpass: The WINNpass program is available to Winnipeg residents, aged 64 years and younger, who are part of an eligible household. More information is available on the WINNpass program page.

Operators and Fare Collection
When riding with Winnipeg Transit Plus, you can pay for your fare with cash, tickets, tokens, bus pass, or through fare payment from account. When paying with cash, you must have the exact change as operators do not carry any change.
The expectation is that every rider pays a fare to ride Winnipeg Transit Plus, unless advised otherwise. Please speak to your operator if you do not have the correct fare and he will advise Winnipeg Transit Plus so that any unpaid fares can be collected.
Post-secondary students must show proof of current registration at time of purchase.

Eligibility, No-Show and Late Cancellation, and Bag Policy updates
 We’re improving our service to make it easier, more reliable, and more accessible for everyone.
As part of these continual improvements, our team at Winnipeg Transit Plus is recommending updates to three of our key policies:
- Carry-On Bag
- No-Show and Late Cancellation
- Eligibility 
These improvements – specifically an updated eligibility criteria – were guided by the 2019 Manitoba Ombudsman report.
In January 2026, we presented the recommended changes to customers, stakeholders, and the public through a series of information sessions. You can view our Public Information Summary online.
On July 16, 2026, City Council considered the recommendations.
What's changing first? What's still to come? 
Carry-On Bag Policy 
Council approved the recommended Carry-On Bag Policy. It is expected to start in late 2026 or early 2027. Transit Plus customers will receive more information before the new policy takes effect.  
Read the Council meeting minutes for more details.
No-Show and Late Cancellation Policy and Eligibility Criteria
Council referred the proposed No-Show and Late Cancellation Policy and Eligibility Criteria to the 2027 Budget Update Process. Council will consider funding for these changes as part of the 2027 budget. The proposed changes cannot move forward without funding. 
The proposed changes would be introduced in three phases. Funding for the first phase would include launching the revised No-Show and Late Cancellation Policy. Later phases would include the revised Eligibility Criteria and other service improvements.

 Carry-On Bag Policy 
The new policy allows customers to bring as many items as they can manage on their own within their seat space.
Items are not allowed on our vehicles if they:
- Prevent customers from accessing the vehicle
- Interfere with wheelchair and scooter securements
- Block mobility equipment storage areas and/or aisles
Driver responsibilities
Drivers do not help customers with personal items. Customers are responsible for managing all personal items aboard our vehicles.
Drivers may decline to provide transportation to customers with an unreasonable number of bags or and items that block space for others and/or impact safety.
The new policy is expected to start in late 2026 or early 2027.
 No-Show and Late Cancellation Policy 
Every year, we handle around 100,000 no-shows and trip cancellations. The new policy would separate late cancellations from no-shows and cancellations at the door. These changes would form the first phase of our three-phased approach.
Nearly one-third of cancellations happen late, which makes it hard to give those rides to someone else. No-shows account for about 10 percent of all cancelled trips.
Most customers use our service responsibly. Approximately 84% have fewer than three no-shows in a month. The recommended change would make service fairer and more reliable for everyone who uses our service.
What we heard
In 2023, we asked customers, family members or caregivers, and service providers for feedback on our proposed No-Show and Late Cancellation Policy. Your feedback helped us update the policy to make it clearer, fairer, and more flexible.
How the proposed policy works
No-shows and cancellations at the door are tracked separately from late cancellations. Customers who frequently miss or cancel trips may have their service suspended temporarily.
No-shows and cancellations at the door
This happens when a vehicle arrives, but the trip cannot happen because:
- The customer is not there
- The customer is not ready within five minutes
The customer cancels the trip when the driver arrives
These have a bigger impact because the vehicle is already there or on the way.
Late cancellations
A late cancellation happens when a trip is cancelled less than two hours before the pickup window starts. When tracking late cancellations:
- Counts reset at the start of each month
- Suspensions are tracked over 12 months
- Errors are reviewed and do not count
- Notifications (text, email, phone, or app) alert customers when events occur and as they approach limits
No-shows/cancellations at the door monthly limits:
- 0–2: Notification and education sent to customer
- 3: Warning with information and tips
- 4+: seven-day suspension (longer if issues continue)
Late cancellation monthly limits:
- 0–3: Notification and education sent to customer
- 4–5: Warning with info and tips
- 6–8: seven-day suspension (longer if issues continue)
Suspensions
Suspensions get longer if they happen more than once in a year:
- 1st suspension: 7 days
- 2nd suspension: 14 days
- 3rd suspension: 30 days
- 4th suspension: 60 days
- Rare cases: up to 90 days
Suspensions start at the next booking window, not right away.
Important things to know
- Emergencies, illness, severe weather, and Transit Plus errors are considered
- Occasional issues are expected
- Contact Transit Plus if something was out of your control
- You can appeal suspensions longer than seven days

 Eligibility Policy 
We are changing eligibility. These changes are proposed to come in the later phases of our three-phased approach. 
Our current policy is limited to individuals who fall under defined categories. The updated policy would make our service more accessible to more people. The new draft focuses on people who are unable to use the conventional transit service (some or all the time) due to:
- Physical abilities
- Cognitive abilities
- Sensory abilities
What we heard
Currently, our eligibility is based on a medical model. Over the years, we have heard about the need to revise our policy. We have drafted this update on the feedback we have received from:
- Customers
- Community partners
- Policy Advisory Committee
An independent review of our service also recommended a revised eligibility policy. It noted the need for us to evaluate applicants on their ability to use conventional transit.
Eligibility categories
We used the feedback we received to revamp our eligibility categories. Qualified applicants would now fall under one of three categories:
- Unconditional Eligibility: Individuals are unable to use conventional transit. We provide all trips.
- Temporary Eligibility: Individuals are unable to use conventional transit in the short term. We provide trips on a temporary basis.
- Conditional Eligibility: Individuals with functional limitations who are affected by environmental or physical barriers. They are unable to use conventional transit some of the time. We will assist on portions of a trip. We will also help them access other transit services.
