# Manitoba — status (2026-10-18)

**Built and hidden behind `MB_ENABLED=false`** (`?v=216`). Switching it on is a one-line change plus regenerating
guides; a full rehearsal with the flag on passed every unit and browser test except the two expected checks of
the province list on the guides index (update them at launch, as for Saskatchewan).

## Records (16)

**Provincial (13):** Manitoba Supports for Persons with Disabilities · Employment and Income Assistance · Rent
Assist · Pharmacare · Disability and Health Supports Unit · Children's disABILITY Services · Community Living
disABILITY Services · Home Care · Primary Caregiver Tax Credit · Manitoba Child Benefit · 55 PLUS · Accessible
Parking Permit (Manitoba Possible) · Homeowner Residential Adaptations for Disabilities.

**Winnipeg (3):** Recreation Fee Assistance · WINNpass (low-income transit pass, 64 or under) · Winnipeg Transit Plus.

Each built from its official page (`snapshots/`, `city-snapshots/`) and checked by a separate reviewer.

## Not built

- Children Therapy Network: no eligibility rule or application on the page.
- Visitable / accessible social housing: the page describes design features only.
- Manitoba Enhanced Pharmacare: limited to specific conditions' drugs (out of scope).
- Student aid disability supports: the federal grants are already listed.
- Brandon Transit: the page is a menu with no who-qualifies rule. Other cities (Steinbach, Thompson, Portage la
  Prairie, Winkler, Selkirk, Morden, Dauphin) had no official program pages in the research pass.
