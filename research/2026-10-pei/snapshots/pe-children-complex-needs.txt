Children with Complex Needs Program. Published date: September 9, 2026.
The PEI Children with Complex Needs Program supports children and youth with ongoing health, developmental, behavioural, or mental health needs and the families who care for them. Families often work with many providers, services and programs while caring for their child. The Children with Complex Needs Program provides support based on each child and family's unique needs. This support may include connecting families with resources, assisting with planning, and helping with communication and collaboration when many people or services are involved.
Please note: The Children with Complex Needs Program does not provide medical care, diagnosis, or treatment. Program staff cannot adjust wait times for services or the Patient Registry. If the child does not have a primary care provider, please ensure their name is on the Patient Registry by completing the online application form. Once registered, you can access Virtual Health Care (Maple).
Does my child qualify for the program? A child or youth must: be under the age of 18; and have a health condition that has lasted or is expected to last at least 3 months; and have at least two of the following: an ongoing need for prescribed medication and/or supplements; frequent or ongoing use or need for medical care, mental health services, and/or extra educational supports; frequent or ongoing use or need for medical therapies, such as physical therapy, speech and language therapy, occupational therapy, etc.
How can the Children with Complex Needs Program help? The program helps families understand, access, and coordinate services that support their child's health, development, education, and overall well-being. Support may include: providing information and answering questions about services and support; helping families understand available options and next steps; connecting families with health care, education, community, and social programs; helping families identify priorities, goals, and service needs; helping bring providers and services together when additional collaboration would benefit the child and family; providing support during important transitions, such as receiving a new diagnosis, starting school, changing services, moving to or from PEI, or transitioning to adult services; connecting families with resources that may assist with costs related to medications, medical supplies, equipment, or travel.
How can I contact the program? Before contacting the program, please review the eligibility criteria above to determine whether your child may qualify. Families may self-refer, or a service provider may complete the referral on their behalf. Phone: 902-218-1253 or toll-free at 1-833-664-3461. Email: CWCNnavigator@ihis.org. Online: Complete the Referral Form (https://services.princeedwardisland.ca/en/service/children-complex-needs-navigator-referral). When leaving a voicemail or sending an email, please include: Child's name; Phone number; Caregiver's name; A brief description of the child's condition and the family's current needs. A team member will respond within 5 business days of receiving your message or referral.
