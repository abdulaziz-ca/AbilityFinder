Adult Day Programs. Published date: December 18, 2025.
For seniors, Adult Day Programs offer a social outlet that facilitates community and interaction with others. The environment is safe and supervised, offering seniors a regular social outlet. For caregiving families, Adult Day Programs offer a needed respite from care. They can use it to run errands, work or simply relax, knowing that their loved one is in good hands and is benefiting from the company of others. Programs include activities such as arts and crafts, games, and theme-based activities. Some locations offer personal care services. Contact the program of interest for further information. Nutritionally balanced lunch and snacks are provided.
How can I participate in an Adult Day Program? To participate, contact the Home Care office in your area. Referrals can be made by individuals, family members, care providers, or family doctors using the Home Care Referral Form. A coordinator will complete an assessment to determine your eligibility. You will be responsible for your own transportation.

Hours generally 8 a.m. – 4 p.m. on set days.
The First French Adult Day Program in Abram Village: a community-based service designed to support older adults and their caregivers in the Evangeline area, with activities in French.
