## Checked and found fine

- app.js `_SCOPE_FULL`, `_SCOPE_DEMONYM`, `_SCOPE_ABBR`, `SCOPE_LABEL`, `SCOPE_LABEL_LONG`, `SCOPE_RESIDENTS`, `SCOPE_GOVERNMENTS`, `SCOPE_ORGANIZATIONS`, `SCOPE_REGION_LABEL`, `SCOPE_DESTINATION`: all compose from the enabled-province flags and now produce all ten provinces.
- Privacy page, About page, Support page: use `SCOPE_RESIDENTS` / `SCOPE_GOVERNMENTS`. "Who runs this" (app.js:5499) says "built in Alberta by a small team", which is a fact about the team, not a scope claim. Fine.
- Coverage page (renderCoverage, app.js:5570-5600): "Where we cover" uses `SCOPE_LABEL_LONG`, the city table uses `names` for all ten, the program count is computed (`localCount`), and the 211 line lists all ten. No hard-coded counts.
- Partner overview, Impact page, Professionals page: program, municipality, provincial and federal counts are computed from the catalogue (`CATALOGUE_INDEX`, `impactCatalogStats`); the scope text comes from i18n placeholders `{scopeResidents}`, `{scopeProgramList}`, `{scopeBenefits}`, `{scopeDestination}`, in both EN and FR (i18n.js:892-907) with all ten provinces. No literal "463" or other program count appears anywhere in the checked files.
- i18n.js landing, hero eyebrow, "New to {scopeDestination}", cats.note ("federal, provincial, and local programs"), prob/how/final, orgs and grants ledes, disclaimer, `nav.tag` = "Canada": no province-limited wording. `life.alberta.*` is only an internal key name.
- Residency step in the wizard (app.js:4036-4050): lists all ten provinces plus "Another province or territory".
- RECEIVES_OPTIONS (app.js:4256-4280), `PROVINCE_NAME` (:4678), `PROVINCE_LEVEL_NAMES`, `LOCAL_LEVELS_EXCLUDED`, `citiesByProvince` (:4321): cover all ten provinces.
- BC-only wizard steps and HELP_PAGES entries (MSP, BC assistance, BC autism, "Specific B.C. programs"): correctly gated by `answers.province === "BC"` and labelled B.C. in their own text.
- public/index.html meta description, `<title>`, og:description and twitter:description: list all ten provinces (short forms in og/twitter). `og:title` and `twitter:title` have no province claim.
- public/guides/index.html meta description, og:description, `nav-tag` ("Alberta + BC + ON + SK + MB + NB + NS + PEI + NL + QC") and eyebrow: all ten provinces, generated from `provinceProse` / `scopeTag`. Only the section grouping is wrong (item 1).
- public/orgs-data.js and public/grants-data.js: page intros are in i18n (fine). Individual org and grant records are province-specific by design (`coverage` field). Org list has entries for all ten provinces except that Quebec and a few provinces have fewer; not a copy issue.
- public/404.html, public/embed.js, public/dbManager.js: no province or count claims.
- scripts/gen-guide-pages.js: `enabledProvinceNames`, `provinceProse`, `scopeTag`, guide-page desc/eyebrow compose from flags. Only line 406 (item 1) is hard-coded. Comment at lines 129-132 ("With BC on and Ontario off...") is out of date but not user-visible.
- Alberta-specific wizard/unmet text at app.js lines 475, 2698-2909 sits inside Alberta program records and is fine.
