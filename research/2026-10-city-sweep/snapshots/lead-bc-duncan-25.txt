 Leisure Access Program Application
 The Leisure Access Program grants annual access for the applicant(s) to attend drop-in programs offered at the Cowichan Aquatic Centre and Fuller Lake Arena. The program is available to residents of the Municipality of North Cowichan, Cowichan Valley Regional District, City of Duncan, Cowichan Tribes Reserve Lands, and the Town of Ladysmith.
 Eligibility requirements: 
The Leisure Access Program is specifically designed for economically disadvantaged residents who live within the boundaries of the geographical areas listed above. In order to qualify for this program, individuals or families must provide proof of residency and income verification.
