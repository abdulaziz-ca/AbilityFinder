 Recreation Facility Access Program (RFAP)
Half price activities for low-income families in Rocky Mountain House, AB
As a family with or without children, you may qualify for the Recreation Facility Access Program (RFAP)! Keep reading to find out who qualifies and how to apply for this Town of Rocky Mountain House program.
 Who is eligible for the RFAP? 
 Answer the RFAP Eligibility Questionnaire and find out if your family qualifies!
 Eligibility for the Recreation Facility Access Program (RFAP) depends on your family's total yearly income and the number of people in your household (including adults and children under 18).
 Your family is eligible if your total family income (the sum of all household members' line 150 on your most recent Income Tax Return) is equal to or less than the following:
 Family Size

 $35,000

 More than 5 people

 How do you apply for the RFAP? 
 Eligible families will go to the Clearwater Regional Family and Community Support Services (CRFCSS) Office to be approved and be given one (1) Access Card per person with an expiry of one year from time of issue.
 Without Referral 
Eligible families or individuals will have to confirm total family income by line 150 of their most recent income tax return(s).
 With Referral 
Eligible families or individuals will have a referral form from a referral agency that confirms the eligibility of the applicant.

 What can Access Cards be used for? 
 Each Access Card provides a 50% discount on a set number of Town of Rocky Mountain House drop-in admissions, program/lesson registrations, and memberships.
