 *CNIB Pass ($35.25): A discounted annual Sarnia Transit Bus Pass for riders with a valid CNIB card. Passes available at City Hall, Customer Service Department. 
 **Bus Passes for Refugees – Special Relief Pass – The City of Sarnia offers Sarnia Transit passes at no cost to visitors from Ukraine who have been displaced by the current war and identified refugees from any country who have recently relocated to Sarnia. The Special Relief passes require an  application form  filled out and the applicant must provide the “Canada Authorized Emergency Travel Number” (issued by federal government). *This pass is valid for 1 year from the date of application. 
 Please contact Customer Service at 519-332-0330 ext. 3131 or  customerservice@sarnia  for more information or to complete an application for a CNIB or Special Relief Pass.
