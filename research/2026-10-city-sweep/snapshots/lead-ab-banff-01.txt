Banff Access Program 
 The Banff Access Program helps eligible Banff residents pay less for recreation programs, transit passes, and other participating services. If you meet the income guidelines and residency requirements, you can apply for a free Banff Access Card.
 Before you apply + 
 Before you apply
 You may be eligible if you:
 live in the Town of Banff
 meet the household income guidelines
 meet the need to reside requirements
 can provide the required documents
 Adults age 18 and older, and their children under age 18 who live at the same address, may apply together.
 If you live in staff accommodation at the Rimrock Hotel or on Juniper Way, apply through the ID9 Access Program instead. These locations are outside the Town of Banff boundary.
 How long  you must live in Banff before applying
 You must have lived at a street address in the Town of Banff for:
 at least 3 full months before applying, or
 at least 6 full months before applying if you have an eligible open work permit.
 P.O. boxes and General Delivery are not accepted as proof of address.
 Open work permit applicants
 If you have one of the following open work permits, you can apply after living in Banff for 6 months: 
 Working Holiday visa
 Post-Graduate work permit
 Partners of Principal Applicant Open Work Permit
 International Student Work Permit

 How to apply + 
 Apply for the Banff Access Program 
 To apply or reapply, email your documents to Community.Services@banff.ca with the subject line: Banff Access Application 
 1. Proof of income 
 Provide:
   Three current pay stubs from each job you work ,
or
 Proof of government issued benefits (such as:  AISH, Employment Insurance (EI), Disability benefits)
 You must also provide:
 A copy of your most recent Notice of Assessment  showing your line 15000 total income. 
 You can find your Notice of Assessment in your  Canada Revenue Agency account .
If you need help, call the CRA at 1-800-959-8281 .
 2. Proof of current Banff  address 
 You must show proof that you have lived at a street address in the Town of Banff for:
 3 full months, or
 6 full months if you have an open Visa application
 Your proof must show your current Banff street address.
 P.O. boxes and General Delivery are not accepted. 
 Accepted documents include:
 Utility bill
 Lease agreement
 Financial document
 3. Proof of need to reside in the town of Banff 
 Please provide one of the following:
  Your employment contract
  A letter from your employer. The letter must be on company letterhead, dated, and signed by the employer.
 4. Proof of residency status 
 You must provide proof that you are one of the following:
 Canadian citizen
 Permanent resident (PR)
 Closed work permit holder
 Accepted documents include:
 Canadian passport
 Canadian birth certificate
 Permanent resident card
 Closed work permit
 Canada-Ukraine Authorization for Emergency Travel (CUAET)
 Reapplying
 If you are reapplying, you must also provide: 
 a Notice of Assessment from your previous year's tax return.

 Important information + 
 Important Information
 Sending in documents does not guarantee access to the program
 The Banff Access Card cannot be used in combination with other subsidies or discounts
 Cards are not transferable
 Your current card will be cancelled if you no longer qualify, such as if you move out of the Town of Banff.
 Subsidized programs and services can be subject to change
 It is a criminal offense to make a false statutory declaration of eligible residency under the Parks Canada National Parks Lease and License of Occupation Regulations. 
 Need help with taxes?
 If you need help preparing your taxes, you can or attend one of our workshops or see if a volunteer can help. Residents with low income and simple tax situations may be eligible for support.
