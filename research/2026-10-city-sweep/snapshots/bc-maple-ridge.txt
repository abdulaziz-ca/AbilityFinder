 Financial Access 
 Last updated on September 1, 2026 
 The City of Maple Ridge believes that all residents should have access to community recreation (and sport), regardless of cost and/or financial barriers. Maple Ridge offers the following financial subsidies. 
 Access Pass
 The City of Maple Ridge is committed to making recreation more affordable and accessible for all! 
 The Financial Access and Support for Recreation offers eligible Maple Ridge residents three options to help reduce the costs of City-based recreational services, based on residents' individual circumstances.  Please review each of the opportunities below and choose the service that best fits your needs/eligibility.
 Access Pass | Financial
 This type of Financial Access allows the City to strive to ensure that all Residents who meet the eligibility criteria have reasonable access to Maple Ridge community recreation services and Programs.
 Open all Eligibility is Based On
 Residency: be a Resident of Maple Ridge, (not applicable to persons with a study, or visitor visa);
 Income: have a household combined income at or below the Low Income Cut off (Before Tax) for a community size of 500,000 and over, as set by Statistics Canada 
 Eligible Residents Will Receive
 75% off admissions (Drop in, 10-pass, 20-pass, 1-month or 3-month passes), or 
 90% off admissions | If income below $18,000.00 (Drop in, 10-pass and 20-pass) (excluding post secondary students)
 and 
 50% off registered program fees to a maximum spending of $300.00
 Access pass is valid for one year from date of approval. Applicants can re-apply annually if they meet the residency and income verification thresholds. 
 Access Pass | Children and Youth in Care/Guardianship 
 This type of Financial Support is for Children and Youth who are in the care (guardianship) of the Ministry of Children and Family Development, (or other government care {guardianship} agency) to help them build healthy lifestyles, habits, and meaningful community connections.
 Open all Eligibility is Based On
 Residency: Maple Ridge Resident(s) (not applicable to persons with a study, and/or visitor visa ); and  
 Guardianship:  They must be in the care (guardianship) of:  The Ministry of Children and Family Development (Youth between the ages of 0–18 years) or 
  Any Province of BC affiliated Child/Youth in Care (guardianship) program/service (Children/Youth between the ages of 0–18 yrs)
  Please note:  this service is only available for the individual in care/guardianship, and not the family unit; one applicant per application 
 Eligible Residents Will Receive
  50% off Admissions (single admission, 10-pass, 20-pass, 1-, and 3-month pass); 
 and 
 25% off program fees to a maximum spending of $150.00 annually
 Access pass is valid for one year from date of approval. Applicants can re-apply annually if they meet the residency and income verification thresholds. 
 Access Pass | Individuals Diagnosed With Temporary or Permanent Disability
 This type of Financial Support embraces the uniqueness and potential in every Resident with a diagnosed temporary or permanent Disability who is interested in participating in the City's recreation services and Programs.
 Open all Eligibility is Based On
 Residency: Maple Ridge Resident(s) (not applicable to persons with a study, and visitor visa)
 and  
 Be diagnosed with a temporary and/or permanent Disability(ies) with required documentation
 or 
 Provide Third-Party Verification/ adjudication demonstrating that they meet the above criteria, in a form satisfactory to the City (ex. medical or para-medical referral, etc.). 
 Please note: this service is only available for the individual diagnosed with a temporary or permanent disability, and not the family unit; one applicant per application. 
 Eligible Residents Will Receive
 50% off Admissions (single admission, 10-pass, 20-pass, 1-, and 3-month pass)
 and 
 25% off program fees to a maximum spending of $150.00 annually
 Access pass is valid for one year from date of approval. Applicants can re-apply annually if they meet the residency and income verification thresholds.

 How to Apply for the Access Pass 
 Open all Step 1: Complete the Application Form (Three Options)
 Option One | Online Application Form and Online Submission
 Please complete all relevant and required fields within the Online Application Form 
 Prior to submitting your required document(s), please rename these documents with your last name
** Required documents include one of each of the following: Address-related bill (hydro, gas, internet, cable, landline, cell phone) |  Please Note: PO Box addresses are not accepted
 Notice of Assessment for each person contributing to the household. Get a copy of your notice of assessment or reassessment from the Canada Revenue Agency 
 Disability benefit
 Once the upload of the required documents is complete, return to the application form by using the tabs at the top of your browser
 Please review your application form to ensure it is complete and accurate
 Press Submit
 You will receive a confirmation email or phone call within ten (10 days), with further instructions once your application has been reviewed and/or processed.
 Option Two | Print Application Form and In-Person Submission
 To complete a paper application, print and complete all relevant and required fields within the application forms:
 Financial 
 Children and Youth in Care/Guardianship 
 Individuals Diagnosed With Temporary or Permanent Disability 
 Alternatively, you can email AccessPass@MapleRidge.ca or call 604-467-7422 to schedule an appointment to pick up a printed application at the Maple Ridge Leisure Centre 
 Attach a copy of the required documents (see **Required Documents above, under Option One)
 Return your completed application and documents to the Maple Ridge Leisure Centre at 11925 Haney Place
 Please Note: Documents can be dropped off at the Maple Ridge Leisure Centre during  Operational Hours . Staff will:
 Review your documents to ensure they are complete
 Photocopy and return your original documents
 Submit your application and supporting documents for final review and processing which can take up to ten (10) days.
 You will receive a confirmation email or phone call, with further instructions once your application has been processed.
 Option Three | Third Party Adjudication (Partnership) Application
 Third Party Verification/ Adjudication means a community agency/organization or professional that has a vast understanding of the individual/family financial needs, disability, or Youth in care needs of the person seeking Financial Access or Financial Support from the City, and who can directly verify those needs to the City to streamline the process and avoid unnecessary steps that may act as a barrier for that person.
 Children/Youth in care are required to use the Third Party Adjudication process to apply.
 If you are receiving support through a third party government and/or community agency, complete the Primary Applicant and Partner/Dependent(s) section on the appropriate form:
 Financial 
 Children and Youth in Care/Guardianship 
 Individuals Diagnosed With Temporary or Permanent Disability 
 Adjudication Letter 
 Have your adjudicating partner complete the adjudicator section of the application form. An adjudicator can be one of the following:  Community organization
 Family support services
 A professional in social work or family services
 Immigration services
 Financial case worker
 Medical or para-medical provider
 A school principle
 Counsellor
 Third Party Adjudicated applications can be submitted either online or in-person by following the instructions in Option One or Two above. You do not need to include your income information, nor confirmation of address.
You will receive a confirmation email or phone call within ten (10) days, with further instructions once your application has been reviewed and/or processed.
 Please Note:  Applications requiring additional screening can take up to 14 days.

 Frequently Asked Questions

 Open all What are the three options for Financial Support?
 Financial Access, an income based subsidy for residents
 Children and Youth In Care, a guardianship based subsidy for residents
 Persons with Disabilities, an ability based subsidy for residents. 
 Who is eligible for Financial Support?
 Eligibility for Financial Access is based on individual or household need. Those who apply for Financial Access must meet the following eligibility criteria:
 Be a Resident of Maple Ridge, (not applicable to persons with a study, or visitor visa); 
 The primary applicant must be 19yrs or older, and 
 Have a household combined income at or below the Low Income Cut off (Before Tax) for a community size of 500,000 and over, as set by Statistics Canada; or 
 Have completed Third-Party Verification/ adjudication
 Eligibility for Financial Support for Children and Youth in Care (guardianship) is based on individual need, and the person must meet the following eligibility criteria: 
 Be a Resident of Maple Ridge; (not applicable to persons with a study or visitor visa); 
 And: they must be in the care (guardianship) of: the Ministry of Children and Family Development (Youth between the ages of 0-18 years); or, Any Province of BC affiliated Child/Youth in Care (guardianship) Program/ service (Children/Youth between the ages of 0-18 yrs) 
 Eligibility for Financial Support for persons with Disability(ies) is based on individual need and the individual must meet the following eligibility criteria: 
 Be a Resident of Maple Ridge; (not applicable to persons with a study, and visitor visa); and 
 Be diagnosed with a temporary and/or permanent Disability(ies) with required documentation.
 What Services will I receive on Financial Support?
 A person meeting eligibility requirements as outlined above will receive the following annual benefits from time of approval (annual being 12-month calendar year):
 Financial Access: 
 75% off Admissions (drop in, 10-pass, 20-pass, 1- and 3-month pass); or,
 90% off Admissions (drop in, 10-pass, 20-pass) if income below $18,000 (excluding post-secondary students (and)
 50% off registered programs to a maximum spending of $300
 Persons with Disability(ies) and Children/Youth in Care (Guardianship): 
 50% off Admissions (drop in, 10-pass, 20-pass, 1- and 3-month pass); and,
 25% off Program fees to a maximum spending of $150
 What Documents are Required for the application?
 Each program requires proof of eligibility.
 Residency: A utility bill with the applicant’s home address listed that is current within the last 3 months. 
 Income: Total combined income, before tax for all family members contributing to the household income, regardless of employment location (out of town, province, overseas):
 Notice of Assessment / income statement; and/or 
 Government Assistance documentation
 Guaranteed Income Supplement (GIS)
 Shelter Aid for Elderly Renters (SAFER)
 Employment Insurance (EI)
 Resettlement Assistance Program (RAP)
 Government-Assisted Refugee (GAR)
 Ministry of Social Development & Poverty Reduction (MSDPR) or Ministry of Employment and Income Assistance (MEIA)
 Guardianship: Requires the Guardian to apply on the Child/Youths behalf as a Third-Party Adjudicator.
 Temporary or Permanent Disability(ies):  
 BC PWD designation confirmation;
 CRA Disability Tax Credit approval;
 CPP Disability approval letter;
 Veterans Affairs disability benefit documentation;
 WorkSafeBC disability-related documentation; or
 What if I don’t have the required supporting documents?
 The 3rd Party Verification/adjudicator or agency partner may verify that you are eligible based on the criteria for each program. Please ensure that the community professional completes the Adjudicator section of the application form.
 What is a 3rd Party Verification/ Adjudicator or Agency partner?
 A third Party Verification/ Adjudicator or agency partner is someone who has a professional relationship with you/your family and is familiar with your financial need.  This individual can verify your residency and income based on the LICO scale, by completing the Adjudicator section of the application form. 
 (Please note: If you have a third Party Verification/adjudicator or agency referral, you do not need to include proof of residency or income verification/ documentation.) 
 Examples of Eligible community members that can adjudicate the City of Maple Ridge Access Pass application on behalf of residents are (but not limited to):
 A professional in social work or family services, 
 Immigration services,
 School Principles, and/or counsellor
 Social Workers
 Medical and Paramedical Providers (Physicians, Physio and Occupational therapists)
 Community agency(ies) 
 Ministry of Social Development & Poverty Reduction Staff
 Ministry of Employment and Income Assistance Staff
 How long does it take for my application to be processed?
 Applications are reviewed within ten (10) business days of submission, and the primary applicant will be notified once completed by either email or phone.
 How Long does the approval last?
 Approved applicants will receive the benefits for one (1) year from the date of approval.
 Why do I have to re-apply every year?
 Circumstances can change over time. The Access Support programs are for Residents of the City of Maple Ridge and re-verification of both residency and income/guardianship, or ability are required each annually.
 Are there any exclusions?
 Please note the following programs are not eligible for reduced fees:
 Private Swimming Lessons
 High Performance Training Camps
 Contracted/partner programs greater than $299.00
 Licensed Afterschool Care
 Employment Certification Course
 How do I start using my Access Pass once Approved?
 To pick-up your Access Pass for the first time, please visit the Maple Ridge Leisure Centre or the Albion Community Centre and provide the customer service staff with the name or phone number associated with your account.  Staff will provide you with a membership card.  When purchasing services, please ensure you provide your membership card to customer service staff to ensure you receive the appropriate discounts as outlined in this document.
 How do I purchase membership(s) or register for programs?
 Membership sales and/or Program Registration can be completed in person at the Maple Ridge Leisure Centre or the Albion Community Centre, by calling the registration line at 604 467 7422 or online at Program Registration | Maple Ridge, BC .   
 Please note: Discounts will be automatically applied to any online purchases and/or registration.  To receive your online sign-in code please email registration@mapleridge.ca or call 604 467 7422. 
 What is the Program Withdraw/Cancelation policy?
 If you need to withdraw or cancel a program the following will apply:
 Seven days prior = No Fee and Full Refund
 Less than Seven Days but greater than 48 h = $10 Withdrawal fee and Program Refund
 Less than 48 hours = No Refund
