 The Town of Hinton respectfully acknowledges that it is located on the First People’s traditional lands. We recognize this traditional territory to show respect and understanding to those who walked this land since time immemorial.  Today, we uphold our ongoing responsibility to work together in the spirit of the intent of the Treaties with all the First Peoples and Nations that call this place home. The Town expresses gratitude for the opportunity to build a better community on these sacred lands for generations to come.
