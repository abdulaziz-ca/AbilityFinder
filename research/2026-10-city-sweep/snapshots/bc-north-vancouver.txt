 Activities for everyone!
 North Vancouver Recreation & Culture together with our partners Parkgate Society, North Shore Neighbourhood House, and Capilano Community Services Society are committed to providing recreation and arts & culture opportunities for everyone in our community.  In support of this goal, we offer a variety of free and low-cost activities throughout the year, and provide financial support to children and adults through our Financial Assistance Program .
 Free & low cost activities & experiences
 Below is a guide to free and low cost programs, services, and experiences offered by NVRC, our partners, and the City and District of North Vancouver.  
 Arts & culture and events
 North Vancouver values and supports arts & culture opportunities.  As a community we acknowledge the power of creative activity to inspire minds, encourage expression, improve the quality of life and health outcomes, and contribute to the vitality of the region. In support of this, NVRC has the following free and low cost arts & culture opportunities, offered year round!

 Low cost drop-in activities - swimming, skating, open gym, parent participation indoor playtimes 
 Swimming - $2 public, family and lane swimming times are offered at all of our aquatic facilities throughout the week.  Find a time and location that works for you
 Skating – Check out our schedule to find $2 skate times.  Skate rental fees are extra. 
 Open Gym – all of our open gym times are just $3 admission.
 Parent Participation Playtime - Unstructured playtime in the gym for parents and preschoolers to have some active fun - riding toys, slide and basic tumbling equipment available. Parent/guardian must stay to supervise. Each family must pay admission for a minimum of 1 child regardless of age (siblings under 6 months are free). Maximum 4 children/parent or guardian.
