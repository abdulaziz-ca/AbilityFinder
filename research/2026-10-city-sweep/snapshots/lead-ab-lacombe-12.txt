 General Funding Information
PASS Program (FCSS)
Local recreation fee assistance
 • Helps families access:
 • Swim lessons
 • Recreation programs
 • Youth activities
 • Who: Children & youth (0–18)
 • Funding: Up to $100 per child/year
Apply through Lacombe & District FCSS:
 • 403-782-6637
 • lacombefcss.net

KidSport Lacombe County
Sport & physical activity grants for kids
Helps cover:
 • Swim lesson registration
 • Sport program fees
 • Who: Children & youth (≤18)
Apply online:
 • kidsportcanada.ca/alberta/lacombe-county

Every Kid Can Play (Alberta)
 • Provincial sport & activity support
 • Helps reduce registration fees
 • Funding up to $350 per child
 • Accessed through partners like KidSport
More Information:
alberta.ca/every-kid-can-play-program
