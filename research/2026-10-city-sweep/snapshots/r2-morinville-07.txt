 Helping individuals and families in Morinville participate in recreation
 The Wellness & Recreation Access Program (WRAP) helps eligible low-income individuals and families in Morinville participate in recreation, fitness and community programs.
 From Morinville Leisure Centre memberships and fitness classes to children's programs, sports and FCSS camps, WRAP helps make it easier to stay active, connected and engaged in our community.
 Qualified applicants can receive up to $250 per person.
 Applications must be renewed every calendar year.
 Apply for WRAP
 WRAP Program Details
 Who is eligible
 Resident of the Town of Morinville
 Applicants are required to submit the following documents along with their applications:
 Alberta ID showing a Morinville address (e.g. driver's license or utility bill)
 Either a current tax assessment or the last 90 days of bank statements to verify income
 For applicants receiving AISH, Income Support or Alberta Seniors Benefits:
 Proof of receipt of AISH, Income Support or Alberta Seniors Benefits
 Extraordinary circumstance as assessed by FCSS Social Worker
 To be eligible for funding, individuals must first meet specific financial criteria as per Revenue Canada’s Low-Income Cut-Offs (LICO) chart see below. Please see below for references.

 What does the WRAP offer?
 The WRAP offers subsidy for the following: 
 1) Morinville Leisure Centre  memberships 
 2) Town of Morinville programs 
 **Community Recreation Programs 
 Children and Youth Fitness/Sport Programs 
 Adult/Older Adult Fitness Classes 
 3) Family and Community Social Services (FCSS) 
 ** Summer Camps 
 ** PD Day Camps 
 ** Fall and Spring Break Camps 
 4) Community Care pass 
 If you are a local Morinville Agency offering residential support you may be eligible for day passes for clients.
 ** For Community Recreation and FCSS Program please visit our  Community Program Guide .
