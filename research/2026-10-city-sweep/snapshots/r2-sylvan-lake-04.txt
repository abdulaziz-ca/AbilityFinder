 Application For Funding
The Town of Sylvan Lake is committed to improving the quality of life and well-being of our
citizens and creating a strong and healthy community. Families, individuals and seniors who
might not be able to afford programs, memberships and admissions can apply for subsidized
fees through the “You Can Play Too!” program to help contribute to their personal growth and
community involvement. This program is only open to residents of the Town of Sylvan Lake.

Application Process:
Completed applications may be submitted at any time, however, applications will only be
reviewed once per month, with a submission deadline of the second Monday each month.
Please ensure your application is complete as only completed applications with required
financial documentation will be considered and in-complete applications will be returned for
completion. If you have any questions or need assistance completing the form, please contact
Karmen Early at the NexSource Centre Recreation, Culture and Tourism office at (403) 887-
2199

Additional Information:
  Applicants must be residents of the Town of Sylvan Lake
  Applicants are encouraged to pay an affordable portion of the program or
 membership fees.
  Applicants must not have received subsidy for the program through another
 source.
  If the Applicant does not attend a registered course or program, or utilize their
 subsidized passes or membership, future applications for assistance may be
 affected.

You must also include at least one of the following with your application in order that we
may verify your earnings:
  CRA Proof of Income Statement- Option C Form
  CRA notice of assessment
  Current pay stub
  Financial aid/EI confirmation or payments
  Proof of income

Please complete this application form, and return in a sealed envelope to:
