Leisure Access Program 
 The Leisure Access program provides low-income residents of the City of White Rock with access to recreation programs and services. The program ensures that residents are not restricted from access or participation in a reasonable variety of recreation activities due to financial hardship.
 Residents are provided with a mechanism for accessing subsidies which incorporates confidentiality, dignity, privacy and easy access.
 What are the benefits of the Leisure Access Program?
White Rock Residents
 50% off eligible White Rock Recreation and Culture programs or memberships. Up to $300 per year.
 Free public skating at Centennial Arena
 Grandview Heights Aquatic Centre and South Surrey Indoor Pool Annual pool Pass or 20 Session Pass available at 50% off.
 See the Pool Pass Rates Here 
 South Surrey Residents 
 South Surrey residents are not eligible for the City of White Rock Leisure Access Program, but can apply to the City of Surrey Parks, Recreation and Culture Leisure Access Program for access to Surrey's recreation facilities.
Who is eligible?
All permanent City of White Rock residents who meet the annual family income limitations may apply. Total income for all adults in the household must be included in the application.

 How can I apply?
 Download and fill out the application form online along with documents required for submission. 
 Complete one application per family and return it to the registration desk. 
 Call Recreation and Culture at 604-541-2199 or email recreation@whiterockcity.ca to arrange your form submission. 
Canadian Tire Jumpstart Program
Sometimes, families need financial assistance to get their kids into a sport or physical activity. The Jumpstart program is committed to helping all families access a range of activities. Jumpstart funding is open to children aged 4 - 18 years old and the program must be a sport or physical activity. Programs should be a minimum of five weeks in duration and include at least one session per week. In the case of sports camps, the program must last at least five consecutive days for consideration.
 How do I apply for Jumpstart funding?
 Visit the Jumpstart website to access the Jumpstart granting system (the City of White Rock is unable to apply on behalf of community members). Once the registration process is complete you will have the ability to apply for funding. Visit the Child Grant webpage.
