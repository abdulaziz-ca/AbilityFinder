 Access Support Pass 
 Individuals requiring support can apply for an Access Support Pass which allows the participant to bring a support worker to drop-in programs free of charge. For information call 604.465.2452 or email parksandrec@pittmeadows.ca . 
 External Trainers 
 All external trainers must submit an application to the Recreation Fitness Coordinator for review and approval in advance of working with clients for physical therapy, rehabilitation or personal training. Contact Jackie at jdidrikson@pittmeadows.ca .
