 Fee Assistance in Recreation
 Section Menu 
 The City of Cornwall works to ensure that you can access our pools, programs and facilities regardless of your income. If you are facing financial barriers, we have options to help make recreation affordable for you and your family, including our aquatics, sports and arts and culture programs.
 Fee Assistance In Recreation (FAIR)
 You can access affordable recreation with our Fee Assistance In Recreation (FAIR) subsidy. We'll cover 75% of the cost, up to $300 annually, for you to participate in swimming lessons, City sports, and other recreation programs. You pay only 25%. This support also extends to pool memberships and public skating passes.
 To qualify for FAIR, you must:
 Be a resident of the City of Cornwall
 Be eighteen years of age or older to apply
 Have an annual combined family income at or below the Statistics Canada before the Low Income Cut-Off (LICO) 
 Have not exceeded the maximum funding amount for the current year
 Applying for FAIR
 Please email us your completed FAIR application Client Application Form FAIR Updated August 2026 and make sure to include identification for each of your family members. Make sure that you include a photocopy of one of the following:
 Canada Revenue Agency Personal Income Tax Notice of Assessment (NOA) including the address portion for each adult in the household
 Ontario Works Statement of Deposit including address
 Ontario Disability Support Program Statement of Deposit including name/address
 Canada Revenue Agency Canada Child Benefit notice (CCB) including address with eligible children if listing children on application
 Guaranteed Income Supplement notice (GIS) including address
 Each year on June 1, you will need to submit the previous year's NOA and Canada Child Tax Benefit statement.
 Acceptable identification
 You must include a photocopy of one piece of identification for each member of your family, which can include:
 Ontario Driver’s Licence
 Passport
 Birth Certificate
 Ontario Photo Card
 Canadian Citizenship Card of Permanent Residence Card
 Immigration and Refugee document issued by Citizenship and Immigration Canada
 Certificate of Indian Status
 Proof of residency
 We accept the following as proof of residency within the City of Cornwall:
 Lease or rental agreement
 Utility or phone bill
 Driver’s Licence
 Ontario Photo Card
 Canadian Tire Jumpstart
 You can also apply for Canadian Tire Jumpstart Individual Child Grants to help cover registration, equipment and transportation costs. These funds are limited and awarded on a first-come, first-served basis. Apply early, as your family could be eligible for subsidies to help with the cost of youth sports activities in our community.
 Apply for Canadian Tire Jumpstart
 You can apply for Canadian Tire Jumpstart support online , or call us at 613-938-9898 ext. 4035 or visit Canadian Tire Jumpstart for more information.
