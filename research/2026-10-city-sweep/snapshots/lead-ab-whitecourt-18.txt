 Facility Features 
 The Allan & Jean Millar Centre offers recreation and leisure activities for all ages and skill levels. It provides a venue for tournaments, trade shows, and cultural events and has become Whitecourt’s community meeting place.
 Fieldhouse
 The fieldhouse offers:
 a multi-use sport area that can be configured for a variety of sports, including: soccer, volleyball, basketball, badminton, and other indoor activities and events.
 second floor running/walking track
 Gannett Publishing & Alberta Newsprint Company Courts with one that can be converted for racquet ball
 squash court
 dressing rooms
 a second floor viewing area with seating for approximately 188 people
 After school youth drop-in sports and evening adult drop-in sports are offered Monday through Friday at a reduced rate. Please see the Community Spectrum for the schedule.
 Pembina Gas Infrastructure Aquatic Centre
 The aquatic centre offers:
 25 metre main pool
 tot pool
 zero depth entry leisure play pool with Infratech Water Play Park
 “lazy river” water feature
 34 person hot tub
 water slide
 steam room
 meeting room and classroom area for programming and parties
 change rooms, including a family change room
 a second floor viewing area with seating for approximately 175 people
 Pembina Fitness Centre
 The fitness centre offers:
 state-of-the-art cardio equipment
 weight training area
 bright fitness studio
 spacious change rooms
 registered fitness classes and complimentary member fitness classes
 McDonald's Whitecourt Go Active Zone
 The designated drop-in indoor play area provides supervised children under the age of 8 with a host of fun activities and opportunities to play with other children. The Go Active Zone contains a permanent indoor playground structure and climbing wall to encourage play!
 Community Rental Spaces
 The meeting room and classroom areas offer a variety of opportunities for parties, special events, business meetings, conferences and community programming.
 A Part of Your Life
 Become a member today! We want to help you stay active and enjoy life and all the amenities we have to offer. There are several membership packages available for the Allan & Jean Millar Centre: annual, monthly, and 10-visit packages for all age groups. Rates are discounted for families and corporate groups. For your convenience annual memberships can be automatically withdrawn from your bank account monthly.

 Corporate Membership 
 Reduce employee absenteeism and increase overall productivity by taking an active role in workplace wellness.
 Active living has been proven to reduce illness and sick days while boosting employee productivity, energy, and ability to manage stress. The Allan & Jean Millar Centre is ready to help you and your employees reap the benefits of a healthy, active lifestyle.
 If your corporation, association, or society registers a minimum of 5 people with us for annual memberships, the Allan & Jean Millar Centre will give a 15% discount off the price of an individual membership. We offer this discount as a thank you to our corporate community and as an opportunity for you to reward your valuable employees. Memberships come complete with access to all areas of the facility and great rates on fitness and activity programs.
 For employees, Corporate Wellness Programs translate into few injuries, less human error, and a more harmonious office environment. It also affirms to employees that their organizations cares about their general health and well being.
 For more information, or to sign up your company today, contact our Guest Services Desk at 780-778-3637 or email Ashley Ellis at  ashleyellis@whitecourt.ca .
 NOTE: This Corporate Membership Plan cannot be combined with any other promotion or membership plan, including family discounts.
 Subsidy and Fee Assistance Programs
 Subsidy may be available for families or individuals living within the Town of Whitecourt or Woodlands County. Circumstances must first meet the financial criteria in order to assist those who are unable to pay the full fee.
 KidSport Whitecourt and JumpStart Programs 
 A great funding opportunity for youth. Parents having difficulty funding sporting opportunities for your child, and teachers and coaches who know of a child who is eager to play sports, but their parents/guardians are financially unable to afford the opportunity. JumpStart may even consider subsidy for equipment and transportation.
 For more information or to obtain an application, please call 780-778-3637 or email whitecourt@kidsport.ab.ca.
 Recreational Fee Assistance Program 
 The Fee Assistance Program provides subsidies for adults and their dependents under the age of 18 years, living within the Town of Whitecourt and Woodlands County. Fee assistance can be applied for each year and each member in the family may be eligible for 2 programs per session or a 3 month membership. Families must have first applied to KidSport for applicable programs. All applications will be held in strict confidence.
 To apply visit Guest Services in the Allan & Jean Millar Centre. For more information call 780-778-3637.
 To find out more about the facility and memberships, please contact Guest Services at the Allan & Jean Millar Centre at 780-778-3637 or email  guestservices@whitecourt.ca .
