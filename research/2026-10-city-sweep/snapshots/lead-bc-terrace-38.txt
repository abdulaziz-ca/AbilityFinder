 CITY OF TERRACE – PARKS, RECREATION & CULTURE
 AFFORDABLE RECREATION ACCESS PROGRAM

The City of Terrace offers the Affordable Recreation Access Program to individuals
who receive funding or benefits through the following programs:

Ministry of Social Development and Poverty Reduction
 • Regular Income Assistance Benefits (must be renewed every six (6) months)
 • Persons with Disabilities (PWD) (must be renewed annually)
 • Persons with Persistent Multiple Barriers (PPMB) (must be renewed every six (6) months)

Ministry of Employment and Social Development Canada
 • Canada Pension Plan Disability (CPPD) (must be renewed every six (6) months)

How to Prove Eligibility
Persons wishing to apply for the Affordable Recreation Access Program must submit a completed copy of the
Affordable Recreation Access Program form and proof of funding or benefits.
Persons receiving benefits from the Ministry of Social Development and Poverty Reduction (Regular Income
Assistance Benefits, Persons with Disabilities (PWD), or Persons with Persistent Multiple Barriers (PPMB)) must
provide a Release of Personal Information (Form HR0095). This form can be found at Service BC or through the
My Self Serve portal at www.myselfserve.gov.bc.ca.
Persons receiving benefits through the Canada Pension Plan Disability (CPPD), the Guaranteed Income
Supplement for Seniors (GIS), or the Child Disability Benefit (CDB) must provide a copy of the standard benefit
letter provided by the City of Terrace’s Park’s Recreation & Culture department, available at the front counter
at the Sportsplex or the Terrace & District Aquatic Centre.

What Will I Receive?
Persons who qualify for the Affordable Recreation Access Program will be issued a discount card with an
expiry date following the above guidelines. This card will allow qualifying persons to receive a 50% discount on
drop-in fees, a 10 Visit Recreation Membership, a 1 Month Recreation Membership, or a 3 Month Recreation
Membership. Memberships must be purchased within the expiry date on the discount card. Fitness class
registration, facility rentals, swimming lessons, or other registerable activities are not included in this program.
Persons receiving regular income assistance benefits will receive a discount card for themselves and up to 3
dependent children living in the household.

How Do I Apply?
 1. Pick up the application and accompanying letter from the front desk at the Terrace and District Aquatic
 Centre
 2. Complete the application form and bring to the appropriate verifying department – Service BC or
 Service Canada
 3. Bring completed paperwork to the Terrace & District Aquatic Centre

There is no limit to the number of times a person can apply for the Affordable Recreation Access Program.
