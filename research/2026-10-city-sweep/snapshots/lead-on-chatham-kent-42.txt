 Affordable Bus Pass Program
 Page Content
 ​​​​​​The Affordable Bus Pass Program provides a 30-day rolling transit pass for urban routes and services to residents of Chatham-Kent who meet specific income eligibility requirements. The Affordable Bus Pass allows for unlimited rides beginning the first time it is scanned on a bus and continues for 30 consecutive days before expiring. You are able to purchase 30, 60, or 90 consecutive days each time you load the card. This card is non-transferable.
 Eligibility
 To be eligible for the Affordable Bus Pass Program, you must be a resident of Chatham-Kent with an annual combined family income at or below the  Statistics Canada before-tax low-income measures (LIM) table for your family size. In order to be eligible for the Affordable Bus Pass, a completed application form along with supporting documentation will need to be submitted to the 
 Chatham-Kent Civic Centre . 
 Cost​​
 Cost - $41.99 / pass This pass provides access to Chatham and Wallaceburg urban transit services, including specialized transit.

 Supporting identification documents for the applicant and each family member (birth certificate, driver's license, baptismal certificate, passport, Ontario Health Card showing birthdate)
