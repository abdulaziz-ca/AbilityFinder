 Sylvan Link Card 
 Sylvan Link is a New Program Aimed at Helping Residents Financially While Supporting Local Businesses
 The Sylvan Link Card is an initiative that contributes to the overall wellbeing of Sylvan Lake and area residents by providing low-income families and individuals access to a variety of services at a reduced rate.
 Residents Information:
 Qualified Link Card holders can receive discounts at participating businesses, increasing access to affordable transportation, recreation, programs, and other everyday essentials. To qualify, you must be within the above income requirements.
 Apply for a Sylvan Link Card 
 Business Information:
 The Sylvan Link Card encourages local businesses to give back to the community by offering select discounts that support their business model. Although aimed to help residents, The Sylvan Link Card encourages card holders to shop locally – bringing in additional business, supporting Sylvan Lake and area businesses.
 What could I offer?
 Discounts 5%, 10%, 20% Off
 Free beverage
 Reduced admission
 BOGO
 Please consider becoming a part of this amazing program which provides our vulnerable residents with the opportunity to shop in their own town and feel like they are part of a community that truly cares about its neighbours.
 Apply:  Sylvan Link Application for Businesses/Organizations

 Care-A-Van
 Helping groups attend events 
 The CARE-a-van assists local organizations, for profits, non-profits, and businesses to attend social, cultural, arts & recreation events (CARE)
 The CARE-a-van is not used for transportation of individuals and/or families for personal use, medical appointments, and/or personal events
 About the van 
 Maximum capacity of 22 persons not including the driver
 Space for up to four (4) wheelchairs, and an electric lift for access
 Seatbelts
 Fire extinguisher, basic first aid kit, an emergency kit, emergency response information kit, spare tire/tire change kit, extension cord, and roadside assistance kit
 The van is not equipped to carry ambulatory care equipment that cannot be locked in and secured (scooters, walkers, etc.)
 The vehicle cannot transport infants or children requiring child seats
 Booking  
 How to book 
 Complete the  CARE-a-van booking request form  
 Schedule an appointment with the Transportation Administrator, 403 887 1137 Ext. 400 |  Email  
 Take the printed copy of your form to your appointment at the Wellness & Community Connections Centre, 4725 - 43 Street
 Review and agree to the booking requirements below 
 Care-a-van Booking Requirements 
 Driver must be verified by the Transportation Administrator or designate 
 Valid Class 4 Driver’s License
 Criminal/Vulnerable Sector Checks indicating a negative record
 Current Driver’s Abstract indicating a negative record
 Registered with the Town of Sylvan Lake Volunteer Centre To register, contact the Volunteer Coordinator, 403 887 1137 Ext. 433, or  email 
  Fees 
 For profits and businesses 
 $65.00 per hour not to exceed 8 hours per day billing for a total of $490.00 per full day rental, plus $0.40 per kilometre
 Non-profit groups 
 $47.50 per hour not to exceed 8 hours per day billing to a total of $350.00 per full day rental, plus $0.20 per kilometre
 Gas: all users 
 A full tank of gas at the time of pick-up, with the user group responsible to return with a full tank
 If the group fails to fill the tank, a charge is applied (cost to fill the tank + an extra $0.05 per litre)
 Deposit: all users 
 3 hour minimum deposit required at time of booking
 Deposit is refundable if more than 24 hours' notice is given for cancellation
