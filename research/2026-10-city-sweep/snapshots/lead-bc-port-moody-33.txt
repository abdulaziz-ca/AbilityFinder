Total Family Income means total combined income, before taxes, by all family members in Canada and abroad who contribute to the
household income and expenses. This includes income from: employment of all types, all investments, pensions, child support
payments, foster child support payments, rental revenues, funds in trust, disability benefits, and other financial sources.

Students living at home must include parents and other family member’s income. Students who live alone and are supported in whole
or part by parents or any other individual must consider that support as income. Students must also include all sources of income from
employment, grants, bursaries, and allowances. Loans which must be repaid are not considered income.

 APPLICATION PROCESS

 Please complete the Leisure Card Application Form on reverse, attach items noted on Checklist, and take
 the form to one of the following facilities:
  Recreation Complex, 300 Ioco Road
  Kyle Centre, 125 Kyle Street

 Supervisory staff will review the application and on acceptance, each member of the applicant’s family will
 be required to have their picture taken at the Recreation Complex for their Leisure Access Card.

 For residents with a disability and who qualify financially, and require the use of leisure facilities not
 available in Port Moody, (for example, aquatic centre), the completed application form must be submitted to
 the Recreation Complex office with the Checklist documents, and a doctor’s note.

1. CONDITIONS OF USE

 The Leisure Access Card is effective for up to one year from the date of issue. Card holders must re-apply
 annually (at least one month before expiry of current Leisure Access Card) and provide proof they meet the
 eligibility criteria. Card holders may be required to provide further proof of eligibility criteria if application is
 made later in the taxation year.

 The card holder must present the card each time they purchase an admission or register for a program for
 themselves or a family member.

 The card is NOT transferrable for use by other persons.

 The card holder may be required to show proof of identity as requested.

 Abuse of the terms or conditions of use may result in the loss of privileges.

2. TERMS OF USE

 Facility Admissions

  Card holders will be entitled to admission to Recreation Facilities at 50% off the posted admission rate
 for their age category (child, teen, student, adult, senior).

  Card holders can purchase the following at 50% off the posted admission rates:
  Fitness passes (11-visit, 1 month, 3 month, 6 month or 1 year), providing the pass term does not
 extend beyond the Leisure Access expiry date. Scheduled monthly payments for the 6 month and
 1 year memberships are available.
  Strip tickets/seasons passes for skating/aquatics.
  Single drop-ins.

 Registered Programs

  A maximum of four programs* allowed for each eligible family member per year for any registered
 program, including swim or skate lessons, workshop, special event or activity, at 50% off the advertised
 price for the program.
 *Not included: private/semi-private lessons; party packages.

  A maximum of two weeks of summer day camp programs** allowed per eligible family member, at 50%
 off the advertised price for the program.
 **All-day camps only (9:00am-3:00pm).

 Equipment Rentals

 Card holders will be entitled to free skate and helmet rentals at the time of admission or during the
 registered program.
