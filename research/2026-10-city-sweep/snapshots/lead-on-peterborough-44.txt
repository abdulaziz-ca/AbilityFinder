 Section Menu 
 The City of Peterborough offers financial assistance to eligible children, families and adults for a variety of programs offered within the City limits. For county residents, discounted membership options are available, which provides access to a number of drop-in programs and discounts on certain City-run recreational programming.
 Do you need assistance with your application? 
 If you require assistance with your application email  rec@peterborough.ca or call 705-742-0050 ext. 1574# to set up an appointment with one of our Recreation Program Assistants.
 Recreation program subsidy  
 The Recreation Program Subsidy assists low-income individuals, and families, and those receiving social assistance with registration costs for recreational programs offered, such as day camps, sport, and recreational activities. You can apply for multiple programs at once, for one or more participants.  The maximum subsidy per person is $100 or $200 per year depending on the low-income cut-off per household.  Applicants are required to be residents within the City of Peterborough. 
 Children’s Assistance (max age 17):  this program will provide up to $200 per year (based on household income) per child for sport, aquatic, recreational and camp programs offered by the City of Peterborough, and by other organizations within the City limits. 
 Adult Assistance (age 18+): this program will provide up to $200 per year (based on household income) per individual for sport, aquatic, or recreational programs that are offered by Peterborough Recreation at City facilities such as the Sport & Wellness Centre, Healthy Planet Arena, and McDonnel Street Community Centre.
 Subsidized membership
 The Peterborough Sport & Wellness Centre offers subsidized membership rates for eligible participants to access the facility. Eligibility is based on household income, with applications accepted for City and County residents. Membership options include: 
 Individual Membership (age 25 to 84) : this membership provides you access to the Sport & Wellness Centre, drop-in programs such as fitness classes, public swimming, and sports, and discounts on qualifying registered adult programs taking place at the Centre. 
 One Adult/Family Membership : this membership provides adults and dependents (max age 22, must reside at the same address) with access to the Sport & Wellness Centre, drop-in programs such as fitness classes (age 12+), public swimming, and sports, and discounts on qualifying registered programs taking place at the Centre including Stop N’ Play, swimming lessons, family programming, and more.
 Telus Cup Legacy Fund 
 Supported by Peterborough Tourism, the Telus Cup Legacy Fund program brings hockey into the lives of children who might not have the opportunity to play. In collaboration with the City of Peterborough’s Recreation Fee Subsidy Program, funding can be provided to children who wish to participate in skating and hockey programs within the City of Peterborough. 
 Hockey Canada’s TELUS Cup was hosted in Peterborough from April 20 to 26, 2026. From the beginning, the Host Organizing Committee wanted the tournament to leave a lasting legacy in the Peterborough community, before, during and well beyond the event itself. Leading up to and throughout the tournament, the committee focused on creating opportunities for the community to be part of the TELUS Cup experience. Initiatives included TELUS Cup Fan Skates, participation in the Peterborough Santa Claus Parade, Parent and Tot Skates with the TELUS Cup, a TELUS Cup Ball Hockey Tournament, and Hockey Canada Dreams Come True program. These events gave children, families and hockey fans across the community an opportunity to connect with the tournament and be part of the excitement of hosting a national championship.
 With the tournament now complete, the focus has shifted to ensuring its impact continues through the TELUS Cup Legacy Fund. In addition to providing financial assistance to help make hockey more accessible, the Legacy Fund can provide participants with hockey equipment to help remove another barrier to playing the game. More than $7,000 worth of equipment is available through the fund, including helmets, protective equipment and skates.
 Together, the hockey subsidy and equipment program will help ensure the legacy of the 2026 TELUS Cup continues in Peterborough by giving more local children the opportunity to get on the ice and experience the game.

 City of Peterborough Recreation Programs
 You may apply to receive the Recreation Program Subsidy prior to program start, to support registration fees. Fees beyond the approved $100, or $200 are required to be paid up front prior at the time of registration.
 Completion and/or approval of this application does not guarantee a reserved spot in a City of Peterborough Recreation registered program.
 Participants are responsible for registering for the programs they are interested in, according to standard registration dates and procedures.
 If the application is approved, you are responsible for completing the registration for the programs you have requested by visiting the Sport & Wellness Centre and registering at the front desk. The amount of subsidy that you are approved for will be applied directly to your registration. You are responsible for paying the difference owing after the subsidy has been applied at the time of registration.
 You may apply to receive the Registration Program Subsidy after program start, within 30 days of registering with proof of receipt.

 If you live in Peterborough County and meet the low income eligibility requirements (see chart above), or receive Ontario Works or ODSP, you can apply for:
 Subsidized Membership at the Peterborough Sport and Wellness Centre
 Jumpstart (children must be between the ages of 4 and 18)

 Frequently Asked Questions
 Can I receive a subsidy for a Parent and Tot class? 
 Yes, subsidy can be used for Parent and Tot classes such as skating lessons, gymnastics, swimming, etc.
 If I already paid for a program, can I be reimbursed? 
 Yes, you may be eligible for a reimbursement, based on availability of funds. You must submit your application and reimbursement within thirty (30) days of payment.
 Can I receive subsidy for After School programs? 
 No, you must contact Children's Services to inquire about Child Care Fee Subsidy or call 705-748-8830.
 Can the subsidy be used for programs offered outside the City limits? 
 No, please visit  YMCA Membership - Financial Assistance for information or call 705-748-9642 ext. 259.
 Can the subsidy be used to purchase equipment, such as bikes or skates?
 No, the Recreation Program Subsidy can only be used to assist with registration fees.  Canadian Tire Jumpstart may be able to provide assistance with equipment.
 Can I receive the funds directly and then pay for the program fees?
 No, payment is either issued directly to the Program Provider or you can request a reimbursement if the registration fees were paid in advance.
 Note: if you are requesting a reimbursement, it must be done within thirty (30) days of payment.

 Canadian Tire Jumpstart
 Canadian Tire Jumpstart Charities  is a registered charity dedicated to removing financial barriers so kids across Canada have the opportunity to get off the sidelines and get into the game.
 Peterborough Recreation Services has been a major benefactor of the Canadian Tire Jumpstart program. Jumpstart funding has not only led to an increase in participation across our programs but has enabled our department’s ability to provide staff with comprehensive training that is required for specific programs. As a result, our staff is more equipped than ever to provide the services needed to ensure our participants have a positive and enjoyable experience. Additionally, we have been able to utilize Jumpstart funding in a manner to improve the quality of our programs. More specifically, the funding has allowed us to improve the quality of our equipment and alleviate the supply costs associated with operating those programs.
 Most recently, Recreation Services was awarded funding to support our Winter 2023 Inclusive Youth Dance Medley class to build capacity for all abilities to participate and lead in recreational activities, sports, and leisure. This example epitomizes why Canadian Tire Jumpstart has been an integral component of the recreational programs offered by the City of Peterborough, and the Recreation Services Division.  #SupportedByJumpstart 
 Jumpstart's core purpose is to enrich the lives of kids (from 4 to 18) in need through sports and physical activity. We assist with the costs associated with registration, equipment and/or transportation.
 The Peterborough Chapter receives support from the two local Canadian Tire stores and consists of several organizations committed to helping children.
 Note: Individual Child grant applications open in January and close when the funds have been depleted for the year. 
 For more information or to apply online visit  Canadian Tire Jumpstart . If you require assistance with your application and live in Peterborough, contact the Recreation Division at 705-742-7777 ext. 1827. If you live in Peterborough County call the YMCA at 705-748-9642 ext. 225.

 The PHA wants everyone to have the opportunity to enjoy the sport of hockey.  Should financial circumstances become a barrier for a child’s involvement, the PHA will try to provide support to local families. This applies to both house league and rep league individuals.
