Information & Application Form



What is the Recreation Access Program (RAP)?
RAP allows Squamish residents who are in financial need to
access Recreation Services and registered programs at a 50%
discount. The Recreation Access Card is valid for one (1) year
from date of issue.

What is a Household?
A household is defined as all those living at the same address – including all extended family members of all ages who
are related by blood, common law, adoption, or marriage. The same address can include separate living areas in the same
house, such as different floors of a multi-unit house or single rooms in a house.

What is Gross Household Income?
Gross household income is the combined amount of money earned before taxes of all members living in a “household”,
in Canada and abroad, who contribute to the household. This includes employment income of all types, investments,
pensions, spousal and child support payments, rental revenues, funds in trust, disability benefits, overseas income, and
all other sources of income.




If you answered YES to all the self-assessment statements, and are within the parameters
for Gross Household Income, please continue as you may qualify for RAP.

How to apply:
Complete and bring this Application Form to Brennan Park Recreation Centre
with the required documents below.
Required documents:
1. Government Issued ID for primary applicant

2. Proof of Financial Eligibility (one (1) of the following for each adult over 18 in the house hold):
 • Notice of Assessment
 • Proof of Income statement
 • Income Assistance statement
 • Disability Support statement

3. Proof of Residency (one (1) of the following documents):
 • Current Lease / Rental Agreement
 • BC Housing Approval Letter
 • Government Document
 • Canada Child (CCB) Notice
If you do not have any of the requested documentation, please call 604.898.3604 to discuss how we can help you.
