 Affordable Recreation 
 L.I.F.E. Program Information
 Everyone needs a bit of fun in their life!
 The L.I.F.E. (Leisure Involvement For Everyone) program promotes access to healthy lifestyle opportunities for residents of the City of Campbell River who face financial challenges.
 If lack of funds blocks your participation in recreation and culture programs, register your family for the L.I.F.E. program for discounted program fees.
 The L.I.F.E. program includes:
 52 free drop-in admissions (Centennial Pool, weight room and drop-in programs and classes)
 a 50 per cent reduction in fees for three (3) City of Campbell River Recreation programs (some exceptions apply, including Summerside Express summer camps)
 Application Process
 Applications are available at the Sportsplex and Campbell River Community Centre, or you can print our L.I.F.E. application form. [PDF/385KB] 
 The L.I.F.E. application form must be completed in full and submitted to a Sportsplex or Community Centre office staff person. Proof of residency with your current address will be required. A driver's licence or current utility bill can be used for proof of residency.
 Applicants must meet the income requirements outlined on the application form. A Notice of Assessment, EI, Disability or Pension Stubs can be used for proof of income.
 L.I.F.E. cards will be cancelled if information provided on the application is false.
 Each member of the family is entitled to one L.I.F.E. card. Cards are good for one calendar year.
 L.I.F.E. Card Use Procedures
 Card must be presented for the reduced fee to be applied.
 Cards are non-transferable, not for cash and not for credit.
 KidSport
 At KidSport we believe that no child should be left on the sidelines. That's why KidSport eliminates the financial barriers to sport participation. KidSport is a community-based sport charity that provides grants for Canadian children ages six to 18 to participate in a recognized sport, led by a qualified coach. The sport must be affiliated with a member organization of Sport BC and must be a member of the Campbell River KidSport organization.
