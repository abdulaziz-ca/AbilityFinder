 Financial Assistance 
 We believe recreation should be accessible to everyone. 
 Access Pass
 Access Passes are available to people who have a permanent cognitive or physical disability. Access Pass holders are entitled to a 25% discount off 10 ticket, 1 month, 3 month, 6 month and 12 month passes to the fitness room, pool or combo fitness room & pool (including promotional deals).
 Download your Access Pass application form and access more information.
 LIFE Program (Leisure Involvement for Everyone)
 LIFE is designed to provide free and low-cost recreation opportunities through the Penticton Community Centre to residents of Penticton on a limited family income. View the LIFE Pass Information Form and Application Form online or visit the Community Centre for a hardcopy. Return your completed form, along with all required documentation, to the Community Centre, 325 Power Street.
 Other Community Financial Assistance Programs
 Jumpstart – Canadian Tire
 Jumpstart helps eligible families cover the costs of registration, transportation and equipment. Apply online at www.jumpstart.canadiantire.ca 
 KidSport Penticton
 Funding is available to provide assistance for children 6-18 years old who live in Penticton, Kaleden, Naramata and the Penticton Indian Bands to participate in Sport BC recognized leagues and programs. 
 For more information, email KidSport or check out the FAQs .
   KidSport application form . Drop off your completed application form at the Community Centre, 325 Power St.
