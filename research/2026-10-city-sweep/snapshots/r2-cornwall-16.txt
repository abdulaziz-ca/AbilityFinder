 Community Passes
 Section Menu 
 The Community Bus Pass offers affordable transportation if you get benefits from the Ontario Disability Support Program and Ontario Works Assistance. We have 175 passes available on a first-come, first-served basis. If you have qualified for the Specialized Service, you can use your Community Bus Pass for that service as well.
 Buying a Community Pass
 You can buy a pass at the Cornwall Transit office (863 Second St W). Please bring photo ID with an original or photocopied Ontario Works or ODSP cheque stub, or drug or dental card. You will need documentation every time you buy a pass.
