 Program Guide & Registration
 Get ready to stay active and prioritize your wellness! Explore our upcoming programs and learn how to register below. We've got all the information you need to embark on a journey of health and happiness. Don't miss out on this fantastic opportunity to take care of yourself. Get started today!
 Programs for Everyone
 The Town of High River offers many programs for all ages, young and young at heart. Children will thrive in a nurturing environment with our qualified instructors, and you will see them build their confidence, self-esteem and creativity.
 Our wide array of programs ensures that adults remain actively engaged and energized. We offer a range of activities focused on promoting physical fitness, expanding knowledge, and nurturing mental well-being. We recognize that mind and body work together, so we try to provide a little of something for everyone.

 Fee Assistance 
 Explore fee assistance programs that make recreation more affordable for individuals and families.
 Missed Classes 
 If a participant is unable to attend a class, no make-up class(es) or credit will be offered.
 If the Town of High River cancels a class, participants will receive a refund in the amount of the individual missed class or a credit to their account.
 Transfers 
 Transfer requests from one program to another are permitted if the program has availability. If a transfer results in a difference in fees, arrangements will be made at the time of transfer.
 Cancellations 
 All programs are subject to cancellation due to insufficient registration. Participants will be notified should a cancellation occur. If a program is cancelled by the Town of High River, registrants will be entitled to a full refund.
 Withdrawls 
 Withdrawals made more than seven days prior to the program start date will be subject to a 15 per cent administrative fee per registration, which will be deducted from the refund or account credit. Withdrawals made within seven days of the program start date, or after a program has commenced, are not eligible for a refund or credit, except in cases of medical withdrawal supported by a physician’s note.
 Medical withdrawals submitted after a program has begun will be effective as of the date indicated on the physician’s note and must be received prior to the program’s end date. A prorated refund will be issued for the remaining classes in the program.
 Read the complete waiver and assumption of risk information carefully before using facilities or participating in programs.
