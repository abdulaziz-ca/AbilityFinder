 The Nelson & District Community Complex is multi-purpose recreation facility.  The facility includes a Fitness Centre, Aquatic Centre, ice arena and multi-purpose rooms.  We also host a variety of drop-in recreation programs for your whole family each week that are included with general admission.

 Active Pass
 The Active Pass is a time-based membership that gives pass-holders unlimited general admission to: Public Swim, Lane Swim, Hot Tub, Fitness Centre, Included w/Admission group fitness classes, Public Skate, and Shinny Hockey. Prices do not include tax.

 Registration for included-with-admission programs within 24 hours of the program start time is available to those who have a 1, 3, or 6-month Active Pass time-based membership, are a Golden Guest, or register online with a credit card.
 For users paying with cash, registration for included-with-admission programs is only available in person up to 24 hours before the program start time.
 For users paying with a punch pass, registration for included-with-admission programs is only available in person on the day of the program.
 In order to receive the second adult or youth Active Pass discount, you must purchase the initial pass at the same time.
 Leisure Access Pass
 The Leisure Access Program helps residents of all ages living in the RDCK who are in financial hardship access RDCK recreation services. This program provides further subsidy on general admission, membership services, and registered programs. Individuals and families are welcome to apply.
 Click here  to for more information and to apply.
