 About the B.C. children and youth disability supplement
 Effective July 2027 , the proposed B.C. children and youth disability supplement will be paid as part of the B.C. family benefit to support families with children under the age of 18 with a severe and prolonged impairment in physical or mental functions.
 This disability supplement will be administered with the B.C. family benefit by the Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) for the Province.
 Each month, eligible families with children under the age of 18 with an approved Disability Tax Credit Certificate (T2201) on file with the CRA will receive the disability supplement as a combined payment with the Canada child benefit , child disability benefit and B.C. family benefit  beginning in July 2027. The B.C. family benefit and the B.C. children and youth disability supplement are funded by the Province of British Columbia.
 Learn about the new B.C. Children and Youth Disability Benefit which provides direct funding for children and youth under 19 who have a lifelong disability resulting in significant and/or complex support needs.
 Eligibility for the B.C. children and youth disability supplement
 You're eligible to receive the disability supplement for a month, if you're a resident of B.C. at the beginning of the month and during the prior month, and you're primarily responsible for the care and upbringing of a child who:
- Is under the age of 18
- Is approved by the CRA for the disability tax credit 
- Lives with you
 How to get ready for the disability supplement
 If your family is already eligible for the B.C. family benefit and the CRA has approved a  Disability Tax Credit Certificate (T2201)  for 2027 for your child, you do not need to apply for the disability supplement. You will get your first payment automatically in July 2027 after filing your 2026 tax return. No other action is required.
 If you have filed your return but are not receiving the Canada child benefit or the B.C. family benefit , ensure your child is registered for the Canada child benefit. For more information, see the CRA's How to apply for the Canada child benefit .
 If you are not currently receiving the federal child disability benefit and you're caring for a child who has severe and prolonged impairment in physical or mental functions, you'll need to submit the Disability Tax Credit Certificate (T2201) certified by your medical practitioner to the CRA and receive CRA approval. For more information, see CRA's How to apply for the disability tax credit .
 If you move to B.C. after you file your 2026 tax return, your eligibility for the disability supplement will be determined once you update your information with the CRA . If you move outside B.C., ensure you advise the CRA as soon as possible to avoid any potential overpayment that you will be required to repay.
 How much you can expect to receive
 Your disability supplement payments are calculated using the following information:
- Number of eligible children
- Adjusted family net income 
 Disability supplement payments will follow the same benefit year as the B.C. family benefit. Your disability supplement payments will be calculated based on your adjusted family net income from the base year (the calendar year before the July in which payments begin). The July 2027 to June 2028 benefit year will be based on your adjusted family net income from your 2026 tax return.
 Even if you do not receive an amount for the B.C. family benefit because your income is too high, you may still be eligible to receive an amount for the disability supplement.
 The maximum annual disability supplement amount you may receive is $6,000 for each child.
 Your monthly payment is your annual disability supplement amount divided by 12.
 For the July 2027 to June 2028 benefit year
 You will receive the maximum annual disability supplement amount if your adjusted family net income is below $50,000.
 You will receive a reduced annual amount if your adjusted family net income exceeds $50,000. The reduction to the disability supplement amount is calculated as 4 percent of the amount that your adjusted family net income exceeds $50,000.
 The disability supplement will be fully phased out when:
- Your adjusted family net income reaches $200,000 if you have one child eligible for the disability supplement
- Your adjusted family net income reaches $350,000 if you have two children eligible for the disability supplement
- Your adjusted family net income reaches $500,000 if you have three children eligible for the disability supplement
 For each additional child the income phase-out threshold is increased by $150,000.
 The adjusted family net income threshold will be indexed to inflation each year.
 Use the online B.C. children and youth disability supplement estimator to estimate how much your monthly and annual disability supplement payments for July 2027 to June 2028 will be based on your 2026 adjusted family net income.
 Families with shared custody
 If a child only lives with you part of the time, you need to determine if you are considered to have shared custody .
 If you're a shared-custody parent of your eligible child or children, you will be entitled to half of the disability supplement you would have received if you were a full-custody parent for that child or those children.
 If you have different custody arrangements for different eligible children, you will receive the full disability supplement you're entitled to for your full custody eligible children and half the disability supplement for your shared custody eligible children.
 To estimate how much you will be receiving, follow these 4 steps using the B.C. children and youth disability supplement estimator :
 Step 1:  Enter your estimated 2026 adjusted family net income. Enter the total number of eligible children you have, including all eligible children you have full or shared custody of, and choose Full Custody. Write down your estimated annual disability supplement for 2027.
 Step 2: Enter your estimated 2026 adjusted family net income. Enter ONLY the number of eligible children that you have full custody of, and choose Full Custody. Write down your estimated annual disability supplement for 2027.
 Step 3: Add up the numbers from Step 1 and Step 2.
 Step 4: Divide the number from Step 3 by 2. This number is your estimated annual disability supplement. Your monthly payment is 1/12 of that number.
 You must notify the CRA if your custody situation changes.
 See the CRA’s website for more information, and scenarios, for how benefits are paid if you have a custody arrangement .
